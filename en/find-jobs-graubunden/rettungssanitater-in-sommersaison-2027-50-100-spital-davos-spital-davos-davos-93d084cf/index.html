Full description

Live and work in the Destination Davos Klosters? We look forward to reinforcement! ##STR1##

Our hospital-bound ambulance service is IVR-certified and provides around 1,500 primary and secondary missions with its emergency vehicles. Our summer is characterized by numerous events, but we also take care of motorcycle enthusiasts, travelers and sportsmen.

As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable, but for the region extremely important company. We maintain a collegial working atmosphere and enjoy the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos.

To strengthen our rescue team, we are looking for a summer season 2027 (May - October 2027):

Paramedics: in summer season 2027 (50 - 100%) ##

Your responsibilities

  • Competent action and ensuring primary medical care
  • Patient care according to competence delegation, algorithms and instructions
  • Patient assessment and situational monitoring during deployment
  • Compliance with guidelines and sharing responsibility for quality assurance
  • Organization divided into departments, participation in a department as desired

Your profile

  • Dipl. Paramedics: in HF or recognised foreign diploma
  • Commitment and responsibility with professional and social competence
  • Category B/C1/D1 driving licence present

Our offer

  • Motivated and professional team in a well-known international holiday destination
  • An exciting environment with emergency protection at various sporting events
  • Personal development with interesting internal training opportunities
  • Attractive employment conditions and benefits
  • Own staff house
  • Plenty of sports and leisure activities in the highest city in Europe

For further information, our co-management of the rescue service Marcel Schawalder and Achim Thamm is at your disposal under the telephone number as well as by email. Of course, our HRM recruitment team will answer your questions by phone or email.
