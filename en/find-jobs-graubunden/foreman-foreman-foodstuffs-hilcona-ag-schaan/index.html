Full description

As a foreman, you are responsible for a smooth and safe process in our sterilization department. You run a small team, operate our pasteurization and sterilization facilities and ensure that quality, hygiene and safety standards are met at all times. Tasks:

  • Assign, guide and support the staff assigned to you in the sterilization department
  • Operation, adjustment and monitoring of equipment for pasteurization and sterilization of our products
  • Carrying out and documenting process and quality controls according to quality assurance specifications
  • Recording / registering the manufactured articles in the system
  • Ensuring hygiene, order and safety at work in the area of responsibility
  • Active participation in the continuous improvement process (optimization of processes and processes) Requirements:
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing (at least level B1)
  • Technical understanding and experience in the operation of production or sterilization equipment
  • Experience in the management of employees (e.g. as foremen, shift manager or team leader)
  • Readiness for 3-shift operation (early, late and night shift)
  • Teamwork, reliability and a structured, responsible way of working
  • Safe handling of MS Office (especially Word and Excel)
  • Own travel option or Driver’s license and the ability to reliably reach the place of work
