Full description

Introduction

For a customer in the field of sanitary and building technology, we are looking for a sanitary installer to support installation, maintenance and maintenance work. The activity includes a wide range of tasks in the field of sanitary facilities, line management and assembly work. We are looking for an expert person who works reliably and safely implements technical processes in day-to-day business. It is used in a professional environment with clear work processes and a varied range of tasks. The employment relationship is permanent and is aimed at candidates who have practical experience in the sanitary sector and would like to contribute their knowledge in a stable employment relationship.

Tasks

  • They install sanitary facilities, valves and piping systems according to technical specifications.
  • They undertake maintenance and maintenance work on existing sanitary installations.
  • They carry out assembly work on construction sites and in conversions professionally and reliably.
  • They shall check the operation, leakage and proper condition of lines, connections and components.
  • They solve disruptions and support the professional repair of sanitary systems.

Profile

  • You have completed training as a sanitary installer or a comparable qualification.
  • They bring practical experience in the installation and maintenance of sanitary facilities.
  • They work carefully, technically and adhere to specified workflows.
  • They can safely implement technical drawings, plans or assembly instructions.
  • They are physically resilient and like to work in the artisanal environment with changing tasks.

Advantages

  • Remuneration mainly by collective agreement, with transparent and reliable billing.
  • Varied activity with different uses in the sanitary sector.
  • Permanent employment contract with a long-term employment perspective.
