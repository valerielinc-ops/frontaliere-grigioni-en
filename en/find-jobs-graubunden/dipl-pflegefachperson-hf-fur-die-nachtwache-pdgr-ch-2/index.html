Full description

Your surroundings

The youth centre is aimed at young people over the age of 13 and offers a protected place for crisis interventions, diagnostic investigations and therapeutic stays. It can be run open or closed and has seven treatment places.

Their tasks

  • You are responsible for supervision and care at night
  • You are jointly responsible for the milieutherapeutic offer on the station
  • They are involved in the development, implementation and ongoing adaptation of treatment planning in the sense of solution-, goal- and system-oriented social pedagogy and nursing.

Our requirement profile

  • You are Dipl. Nurse HF or Dipl. Care professional HF
  • They ideally have professional experience in child and adolescent psychiatry and work patient-oriented
  • They are flexible, resilient, capable of conflict and are happy to take on responsibility

We offer you

  • Varied interdisciplinary cooperation in a professional team
  • We work according to solution- and resource-oriented systemic approaches, as well as milieu- and sociotherapeutic principles
  • Professional and personal training opportunities – we also support your external training needs
  • Advanced working conditions
  • With part-time work models, early service planning and unpaid holidays, an optimal work-life balance
