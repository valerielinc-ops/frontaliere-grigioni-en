Full description

For an established customer in the automotive industry, we are looking for a dedicated specialist as a tire practitioner/in EBA. The position is located in a practical environment and includes activities related to the assembly, replacement and control of tyres as well as the proper handling of wheels and tyre material. It is used in a professionally organized company with clear processes and reliable support. We are looking for a person who works carefully, brings craftsmanship and carries out the daily tasks in a structured team.

Tasks

  • They fit and dismantle tyres properly on passenger or commercial vehicles.
  • They check tyres and wheels for visible damage and for their general condition.
  • They carefully prepare wheels for storage, transport and further processing.
  • They provide support in tire storage and in the proper handling of customer materials.
  • They work according to operational specifications and ensure a clean, safe execution.

Profile

  • You have completed training as an EBA tyre practitioner or equivalent practical experience.
  • They have craftsmanship and work precisely in physical activities.
  • They have a good understanding of order, security and clean work processes.
  • They work reliably, resiliently and can implement tasks in a structured team.
  • They are ready to perform practical tasks in tire service responsibly and carefully.

Advantages

  • Takeover option by the customer operation with appropriate suitability and performance.
