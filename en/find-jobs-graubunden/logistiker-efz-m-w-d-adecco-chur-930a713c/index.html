Introduction

For a customer in the field of logistics, we are looking for a logistics operator (m/f/d) at the earliest possible time. The position is located in a professionally organized environment in which the smooth management of goods movements, warehouse processes and internal processes plays a central role. The company works with clear structures and attaches importance to reliable processes, adherence to deadlines and careful working methods. Through the experienced HR services partner, this position is filled in close coordination with the customer. In addition, there is a takeover possibility by the customer operation. We are looking for a person who practically supports logistics processes, assumes responsibility in day-to-day business and contributes to the stable workflow in the company with a structured working style.

Tasks

  • They take over the acceptance, testing and forwarding of goods in the daily logistics process.
  • They ensure proper storage and traceable storage organisation.
  • They support the internal transport of materials and ensure smooth processes.
  • They shall check stocks and report deviations or discrepancies to the competent authorities.
  • They prepare goods for shipment and ensure correct packaging and labelling.

Profile

  • They have experience in logistics or in a comparable field with practical warehouse operations.
  • They work carefully and keep track of recurring activities.
  • They have a good understanding of organizational processes and material movements.
  • They are reliable, resilient and work structured according to specifications.
  • They have a team-oriented way of working and communicate clearly in everyday work.

Advantages

  • Experienced HR services partner with professional support of the entire application process.
  • Possibility to take over by the customer
