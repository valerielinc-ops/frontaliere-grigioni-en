Full description

The White Arena Group in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and an enthusiastic community for unique mountain adventures. With mountain railways, hotels, gastronomy, sports and rental shops and the LAAX School, we create unforgettable experiences for our guests - and attractive workplaces for our employees. | Berghaus Nagens, located at 2'127 meters above sea level in the ski resort LAAX, is one of the largest mountain houses in the Swiss Alps. With its cozy and rustic ambience, a hostel with 82 beds, 560 indoor and 700 terrace spaces, a café and evening bar and a team of 45 employees, we offer our guests a unique mountain experience. In the integrated freeride base, we attach particular importance to an innovative plant-based cuisine that pampers our guests with vegan and vegetarian dishes as well as a varied salad buffet. | Imagine you are our:e employee: in coffee bar/all-rounder in Freeride Base, with a view over Flims. What You Move:

  • You conjure warm and cold drinks and hand them to our guests
  • You provide hearty and sweet snacks – fresh and appealing
  • You actively approach guests, advise them and make them feel welcome.
  • You prepare everything for a smooth daily routine and set up the buffet
  • You work routinely with the iKentoo POS system
  • You pay careful attention to compliance with all hygiene regulations. What makes you:
  • You have experience as a barista and know how to inspire guests with drinks
  • You have good knowledge of German and English
  • You are enthusiastic about a professional but relaxed approach to guests
  • You remain communicative and flexible – even if things get hectic | Benefits: An attractive workplace in the middle of an extraordinary mountain world is certain. You become part of a dynamic, bright team and can help shape the further development of LAAX. With us you will find an environment where professionalism, speed, pragmatism and humanity go together. Look forward to numerous employee benefits, such as free mountain rides, perks in our shops, hotels and restaurants, and benefits for your family.
