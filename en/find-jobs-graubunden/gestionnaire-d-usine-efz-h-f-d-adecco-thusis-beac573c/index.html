Full description

For a customer in the field of industrial production, we are looking for a plant operator (m/f/d) to support ongoing production operations. The position is located in a technical environment where safe handling of machines, plants and production processes is expected. As a HR partner, we accompany the entire application process and establish contact with an experienced customer company. The focus is on reliable working methods, technical understanding and the willingness to carefully monitor and control production processes.

Tasks

  • They operate and monitor production plants reliably and carefully during the ongoing production process.
  • They shall ensure that machinery and equipment are properly equipped and operational.
  • They control the production process and react at an early stage to malfunctions or deviations.
  • They carry out simple quality controls and ensure consistent manufacturing standards.
  • They support cleaning, setup and maintenance work on the plants in the production environment.
  • They document work processes, production data and special events in a comprehensible manner.

Profile

  • They have technical understanding and can safely operate production facilities.
  • They work concentrated, responsibly and keep an overview even during ongoing production.
  • Ideally, you have experience in machine or plant operation.
  • They are ready to work reliably and structured in the industrial environment.
  • They are characterised by teamwork and a careful and practical way of working.
  • They are ready to learn and can quickly familiarize themselves with technical processes and production processes.

Advantages

  • Takeover option by the customer after successful cooperation and appropriate suitability.
  • Support by an experienced expert for HR services throughout the assignment.
