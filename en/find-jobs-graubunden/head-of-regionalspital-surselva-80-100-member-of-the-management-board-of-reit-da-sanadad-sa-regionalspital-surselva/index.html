Full description

Head of Regionalspital Surselva 80-100% | Member of the Executive Board of Reit da sanadad Surselva SA (RDSS) The Regionalspital Surselva in Ilanz ensures the extended basic and emergency care for around 22,000 inhabitants and seasonally around 20,000 holiday guests of the tourism region of Bündner Oberland. Our approximately 320 employees treat approximately 2,600 inpatient and 16,000 outpatient patients each year with modern infrastructure (CT, MRI, IMC) in the clinics of surgery and orthopaedics/traumatology with neurosurgery and urology, internal medicine with cardiology, oncology, gastroenterology and pneumology, gynecology and obstetrics, pediatrics and anesthesia. The hospital bears the label "Swiss Olympic Medical Base approved by Swiss Olympic" and offers an extended range of services thanks to the cooperation with the Center Hospital in Chur. The Regional Hospital is part of Reit da sanadad Surselva SA (RDSS). Together with eight health institutions – the Regional Hospital, five retirement and nursing homes and two Spitex organizations – and eleven municipalities, a health network is being created that ensures high-quality medical and nursing care from a single source. As of January 1, 2027, the companies will be united under the umbrella of the RDSS. The advertised position is thus a position of the RDSS with place of work in the Surselva Regional Hospital in Ilanz. Further information is available at www.rds-surselva.ch.
