Full description

Getreu der Vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives" liefern die Hamilton Unternehmen seit 1950 Lösungen für die Gesundheitsbranche. Wir sind ein innovativer Vorreiter was Beatmungsgeräte, das automatisierte Pipettieren und Lagern von Proben sowie die Entwicklung von Prozesssensoren anbelangt.

Die Business Unit Robotics entwickelt, produziert und vertreibt Pipettier-Roboter, um Liquid Handling Prozesse für die Pharma-, über die Nahrungsmittelindustrie bis hin zur Zell- oder DNA-Forschung zu automatisieren.

Das kannst du bewirken

We are looking for a Leadership role responsible for Digital Products, Software Architecture, UX/UI, and AI solutions. The position leads a small multidisciplinary team and drives the development of scalable, customer-focused digital products and AI-enabled services.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead Software Architects, Product Owners, and UX/UI Engineers
  • Define digital product and AI strategy aligned with business goals
  • Establish AI solution architecture standards and technology roadmaps
  • Drive innovation, modernization, and adoption of emerging AI technologies
  • Foster collaboration, engineering excellence, and talent development.

Das bringst du mit

  • 10+ years in software engineering, architecture, or digital product development.
  • 3+ years in leadership roles.
  • Strong expertise in software architecture, cloud platforms, APIs, DevOps, and AI technologies.
  • Proven experience delivering complex software solutions and leading cross-functional teams.
  • Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, AI, or a related field (or equivalent experience). Preferred• Experience scaling digital product organizations and integrating AI into commercial products.
  • International and multidisciplinary collaboration experience.
  • Mentoring and coaching mindset
  • Curiosity and continuous learning attitude
  • English (C1) and as a plus German (B2) written and spoken
