Full description

Würth AG supplies craftsmen of all industries with quality products and coordinated system solutions for workshops, warehouses and on the road. The versatile range comprises over 150,000 items: from screws, dowels and fittings to tools, machines, chemical products and occupational safety to vehicle equipment and warehouse management. Proven since 1945 and at home in Switzerland since 1962, Würth AG offers tailor-made solutions with the aim of making everyday life easier for its partners.
