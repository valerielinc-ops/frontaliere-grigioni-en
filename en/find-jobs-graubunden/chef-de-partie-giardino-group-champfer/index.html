Full description

Giardino Group is looking for a Chef de Partie for Giardino Mountain in Champfèr. The Giardino Hotels are a charming Swiss luxury hotel group with locations in St. Moritz, Ascona and Locarno.

Tasks The three restaurants at the hotel provide culinary highlights individually; inspired by modern creations from Asia, Orient and Occident at the Hide & Seek, from Engadin specialities in the rustic ambience of the Stüva, to the high art of star cuisine at Ecco St. Moritz. In this function, you are responsible for the smooth operation of your work area at the Restaurant Hide & Seek and our Engadine Stüva. These include ordering, as well as controls on quality, hygiene and safety.

You work in a powerful and dynamic team and cook at a high gastronomic level.

Requirements

  • A completed professional training as a chef in the catering/hotel industry
  • Experience in a comparable position
  • You keep a cool head even in hectic situations
  • A well-kept appearance and polite manners
  • Independence, reliability and duty-consciousness
  • Loadability, flexibility and enthusiasm
  • A respectful handling of colleagues and superiors

The opportunity to participate actively in the opening, business life and corporate development - Open and transparent communication - Regular training and further training as well as numerous employees of the company's operating system.Both of these are part of a contemporary and characteristic Swiss hotel group.
