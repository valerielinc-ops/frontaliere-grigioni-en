Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Graubünden?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Graubünden's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Graubünden's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
