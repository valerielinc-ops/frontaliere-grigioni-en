Full description

We are looking for a committed personality with pleasure in architecture in an alpine context - someone who thinks about projects, takes responsibility and likes to work in a small, motivated team.

With us you work on conversions and new buildings with a strong connection to the place, the crafts and the careful implementation.

Your tasks

  • Design, pre-project, construction and plant planning
  • Visualization and presentation of projects
  • Participation in all planning phases
  • Direct exchange with builders, specialist planners and craftsmen

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed architecture degree (Master ETH or FH)
  • Professional experience in Switzerland
  • Independent, structured and team-oriented working method
  • Strong design and constructive understanding
  • Vectorworks 2D, 3D and Renderworks, Photoshop, InDesign, Excel
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Driving licence Cat. B

What we offer

  • Demanding reconstruction and new construction projects in the Alpine area
  • A small, collegial and dedicated team
  • Flat hierarchies with plenty of space for personal development
  • Careful architecture with proximity to craftsmanship
  • Direct insights into the entire planning and construction process
  • An inspiring workplace in a converted wool ceiling factory
