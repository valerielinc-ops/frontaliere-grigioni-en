Full description

As a specialist building office of the Canton of Graubünden, the Building Construction Office represents the interests of the canton as property owner and/or building owner and is responsible for all services of real estate management. This includes the structuring of the real estate portfolio, the planning, realization and management of real estate as well as consulting.

Tasks

  • Responsibility for energy/supply management to ensure economical and sustainable procurement, through sound decision-making through the preparation, preparation and monitoring of data
  • Responsibility for contract management including procurement as well as professional examination and assessment of offers/contracts in operational building management
  • Participation and coordination of maintenance, repair and implementation projects
  • Responsibility for furniture/removal management with demand assessment, economic procurement and ordering of furniture according to internal standards and specifications as well as tendering, awarding and implementation of removals in coordination with ongoing projects
  • Catering management contact person with responsibility for monitoring quality and contracted services
  • Interface function between operational implementation and strategic planning
  • Participation in the integration of operational requirements into the project and construction phase to ensure sustainable real estate management (pbFM)

Requirements

  • Completed studies or further education in the fields of facility management or building, environmental and energy technology – or comparable experience as a technician in building technology
  • You have pleasure and skill in dealing with different stakeholders
  • They communicate clearly and like to work in a team in projects
  • The safe use of digital tools is part of everyday life for you
  • You think entrepreneurially and networked
  • You work engaged, independent and service-oriented
  • Your appearance is safe, friendly and convincing
  • You have a stylistically impeccable expression
  • A broad general education and ideally knowledge of Italian round off your profile
