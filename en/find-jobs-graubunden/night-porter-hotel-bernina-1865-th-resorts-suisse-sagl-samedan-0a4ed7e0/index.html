Full description

Would you like to help create unforgettable experiences for guests and build your career in a company that enhances talent?

TH Resorts, a highly expanding Italian hotel chain with over 30 hotels, villages and resorts in marine, mountain and art cities, is looking for a night porter for the winter season 2026-2027 for the TH Samedan - Hotel Bernina 1865. Need fluent knowledge of English, German and French.

  • What are you gonna do?
  • As Portiere di Notte, you will take care of:
  • Close the hotel’s daily accounting.
  • Manage guest check-in and check-out during the night.
  • Respond to call and customer needs at night.
  • Watch the tranquility and sleep of guests.
  • Who are we looking for?
  • A person with:
  • Responsibility and attention to detail.
  • Good organizational and practical skills.
  • Excellent communication ability.
  • Experience as a night watchman, preferably in the hotel industry.
  • High school diploma.
