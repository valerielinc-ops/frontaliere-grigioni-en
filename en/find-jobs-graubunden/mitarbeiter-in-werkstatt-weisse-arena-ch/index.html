About us

The Weisse Arena Group in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and an enthusiastic community for unique mountain adventures. With cable cars, hotels, gastronomy, sports and rental shops as well as the LAAX School, we create unforgettable experiences for our guests - and attractive jobs for our employees.

Weisse Arena Leisure AG is a subsidiary of the Weissen Arena Group, Laax. In winter we rent and sell ski and snowboard equipment. LAAX Rental Shops and the Buron Stores are distributed throughout the ski area, at the valley stations in Flims, Laax and Falera as well as at the mountain station Crap Sogn Gion. In summer, the LAAX Rental Flims becomes the TREK Test Center, where enduro, trail, electric and kids bikes can be rented or bought.

In our own workshop, the rental items are not only serviced, our guests can also have their private bicycles, skis and snowboards prepared and repaired.

The body

For the winter season 26.27. we are looking for reinforcement in the workshop in a part-time job of 60 - 80%.

Tasks

  • Service and repair work on skis and snowboards - maintenance, maintenance and cleaning work on machines and equipment - transport of skis and snowboards to various shops in Flims, Laax and Falera

Requirements

  • You are skilled in craftsmanship
  • With high working pressure you keep calm and stay helpful
  • You work with a lot of work, you are reliable, you are flexible
  • Working in a team makes you happy
  • Having the guide card of category B is absolutely necessary

We offer

You will find an attractive place to work in the midst of an exceptional mountain world. You will become part of a dynamic, alert team and will be able to influence the further development of LAAX. We will provide you with an environment where professionalism, speed, pragmatism and humanity belong together. Enjoy numerous employee discounts, such as free mountain rides, discounts in our shops, hotels and catering establishments as well as benefits for your family. Start your professional adventure at the White Arena Group with your complete online application.
