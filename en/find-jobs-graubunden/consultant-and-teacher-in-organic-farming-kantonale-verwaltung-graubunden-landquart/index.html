Full description

The Plantahof is an office in the Department of Economics and Social Affairs and the competence center for agricultural education and advice in the Canton of Graubünden. In addition to the agricultural vocational school, the Plantahof offers various further courses, further education courses and conferences and is responsible for comprehensive advice and the provision of specialist knowledge for the benefit of Grisons and Glarner agricultural and Alpine farms and other circles involved in agricultural topics. For these purposes, he also runs his own experimental and estate operation at four different locations together with two farm shops. Finally, it has a conference centre with boarding school, lecture hall and a catering facility. Tasks

  • Specialist advice in organic farming in the cantons of Graubünden and Glarus
  • Specialist lessons in organic farming in basic education and in further courses
  • Consulting and further development of Plantahof organic farms
  • Participation and advice Board Bio Grischun
  • Networking with cantonal and national bodies and commissions of organic farming
  • Further tasks in the project Klimaneutrale Landwirtschaft Graubünden as well as in the climate strategy of Plantahof Requirements
  • Agronomist (Bachelor or Master); or equivalent education
  • Additional qualification as a teacher and counsellor (minor education and counselling)
  • Joy and knowledge in the field of organic farming and climate
  • Experience in counselling and training
  • Team capable, reliable and established personality
  • Willingness to assume responsibility as a topic leader /-in organic farming
  • The common working language is German. Knowledge of another cantonal language is an advantage
