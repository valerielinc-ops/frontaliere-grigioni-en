Full description

St. Moritz Tobogganing Club AG aims to operate the oldest skeleton train in the world in St. Moritz. The train is available to your members and outsiders every winter from December to March.

Their tasks

As Deputy Managing Director, you will lead the operational operations of St. Moritz Tobogganing Club AG in close cooperation with the Managing Director and Board of Directors. This essentially includes the following, non-exhaustive tasks:

  • Efficient management of Cresta Run (Skeleton) operations in compliance with high safety standards for drivers and participants
  • Monitoring and ensuring an optimal railway construction that meets the high safety standards. Coordination with railway construction companies and other suppliers. Ensure functioning infrastructure timekeeping and cameras. Monitoring operations vehicles
  • Communication and contact point for members, participation in various club events, implementation of prize distributions
  • Examination and coordination of membership applications
  • Local crisis management
  • Staff management of the Tower Boys/Girls in the company

Your profile

  • You have successfully completed studies in management and finance or a comparable degree programme
  • You have at least ten years of managerial experience in a similar establishment
  • You are a skeleton driver yourself and have the necessary knowledge to correctly assess the risks involved in operating a skeleton train
  • You know the Tobogganing Club and understand the needs of your members
  • You are an inclusive, dynamic, self-confident and self-reliant personality with persuasion and assertiveness
  • You have the necessary leadership competence to make the right decisions even in pressure situations
  • You have knowledge of the cash system R&BS "Riding and Billing System"
  • Your mother tongue is English
