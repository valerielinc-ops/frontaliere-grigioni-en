Production workers

Introduction

We are looking for a production worker for our electroplating immediately or by appointment.

Tasks and responsibilities

  • Operation and monitoring of production and electroplating systems
  • Equipment and emptying production plant
  • Assembly work
  • Grinding metal parts
  • Sandblasting metal parts
  • Washing metal parts
  • Packaging work
  • Performing simple cleaning and maintenance work
  • Compliance with safety, quality and environmental regulations
  • Support the team in general production activities

Requirements

  • Experience in production (preferably in the metal industry) or construction
  • Craftsmanship and technical understanding
  • Good physical strength and careful work
  • Readiness for shift work
  • Basic oral knowledge in German
  • Reliability, teamwork and responsibility

We offer

  • A varied activity in a modern production environment
  • Careful introduction and support
  • Fair employment conditions according to Swiss standards
  • Good social benefits and contemporary remuneration
  • A secure workplace in an established company
