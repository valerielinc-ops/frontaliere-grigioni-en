Co-Lead Guest Experience — Weisse Arena Gruppe

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The Weisse Arena Gruppe in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and a passionate community for unique mountain adventures. With mountain railways, hotels, restaurants, sports and rental shops, and the LAAX School, we create unforgettable experiences for our guests - and attractive workplaces for our employees.

Three properties, three different worlds, one shared aspiration: exceptional guest experiences with personality. At the signinahotel, rocksresort and Riders Hotel, alpine lifestyle, a modern hotel experience and personalized hospitality come together. Whether a stylish hotel, high-quality serviced apartments or an urban meeting place with a restaurant, yoga, gym and club – each property has its own character and offers our guests something special. As the Guest Experience Circle, we connect these worlds and make our guests' stay personal, uncomplicated and unforgettable.

At the rocksresort, signinahotel and Riders Hotel, you are jointly responsible for the Guest Experience Circle together with the Co-Lead Guest Experience Operations. You ensure that our guests receive a consistent, high-quality experience at every touchpoint and continuously develop the area. In this role, you are responsible for the guest experience at the front and throughout the resort, as well as for Value Generation along the Guest Journey.

Responsibilities

What you will do

  • Joint overall responsibility for the Guest Experience area in the co-lead model
  • Ensuring a consistently positive guest experience across all hotels and touchpoints
  • Developing and implementing measures to increase guest value, particularly through upselling and offers during the stay
  • Managing, coaching and developing the Welcome Hosts, as well as promoting an independent and service-oriented way of working
  • Further developing and ensuring service standards, the entire Guest Journey and a professional approach to complaints and difficult situations
  • Responsibility for review and feedback management as well as the continuous improvement of guest satisfaction
  • Managing and further developing the systems and digital applications used along the Guest Journey
  • Close collaboration with Hospitality Services, Housekeeping, F&B, Revenue Management and other relevant areas
  • Mutual representation within the co-lead model, as well as joint decisions on operational matters and measures to resolve guest complaints
  • Planning, implementation and responsibility for Hospitality, Guest Experience and digitalization projects

Requirements

What sets you apart

  • Several years of experience in the hotel or hospitality industry, ideally with some leadership experience
  • Strong understanding of Guest Experience, service quality and operational processes
  • A passion for developing employees and successfully implementing change
  • A high degree of initiative, decisiveness and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Analytical thinking and confidence in working with metrics and digital systems
  • Strong communication skills and the ability to coordinate across departments
  • Resilience, composure and a clear overview, even in challenging situations
  • Very good German and English skills

What we offer

An attractive workplace amid an exceptional mountain landscape awaits you. You will become part of a dynamic, vibrant team and can help shape the further development of LAAX. Here you will find an environment where professionalism, pace, pragmatism and humanity go hand in hand. Look forward to numerous employee benefits , such as free mountain rides, discounts at our shops, hotels and food-service establishments, as well as benefits for your family. Start your professional adventure at the Weissen Arena Gruppe with your complete online application. We look forward to welcoming you.
