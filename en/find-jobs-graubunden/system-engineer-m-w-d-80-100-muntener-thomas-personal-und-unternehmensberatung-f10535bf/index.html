Full description

Keeping infrastructures running, thinking ahead of solutions – does that sound like your world? ####

With an innovative ICT company and managed service provider, you are actively involved in shaping on-premises, cloud and hybrid environments - with a lot of personal responsibility, exciting projects and a team that lives digITal.competence.

The main tasks ####

  • Operating and further developing on-premises, cloud and hybrid infrastructures
  • Planning and implementing migrations, releases and system changes
  • Analyze disruptions and optimize system security, performance and availability
  • Automate processes using PowerShell
  • Managing or shaping IT infrastructure and workplace projects

The requirement profile ####

  • Completed IT training
  • Several years of professional experience in System Engineering
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft 365, Windows Server, Active Directory/Entra ID and Intune
  • Experience with virtualization, networking, backup and security
  • Ideally, knowledge of Powershell, ITIL or Change Management
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method
