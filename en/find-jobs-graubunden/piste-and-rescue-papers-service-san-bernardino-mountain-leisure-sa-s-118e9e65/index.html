Full description

For our ski area in San Bernardino (GR), we are looking for Piste and Soccorso/Pattugliatori for the next winter season.

Main responsibilities

  • Opening, control and landing of the slopes: Inspection and daily control of the tracks before opening to the public; correct positioning of the official Swiss signage and protection devices (nets, mattresses); disembarkation of closed or non-agible tracks.
  • First Aid and Rescue Service: Emergency intervention in case of accidents on the ski demanio, medical stabilization of the patient on the spot, safe transport of the wounded downstream via toboga or snowmobile and technical coordination of rescues with the elixoccorso partners (e.g. Rega).
  • Valanghe and Security Service: Active collaboration with the company avalanche committee, snow mantle profile monitoring and active participation in preventive artificial detachment operations of avalanches through explosives or fixed systems.
  • Legal accident reports: Detailed and rigorous drafting of Swiss official accident records for the collection of evidence in the event of legal disputes.
  • Closing service: Inspection and final control of all tracks at the end of the day of exercise to ensure the total absence of users on the ski demanio before the night beat.

Required Requirements (Switzerland Regulation)

  • Patents and Official Skills (Switzerland - SBS):
  • Swiss patent by Pisteur-Secourista (Patentino A, B or C SBS) [2] or, alternatively, equivalent foreign title with recognition procedure in Switzerland already started or finished.
  • Certificate of training for the Valanghe Service (Level 1 or higher SBS) is a preferential qualification or binding requirement based on avalanche allocation.
  • Certificate of Relief / First Aid in progress (e.g. BLS-AED certification or higher recognised by IRC/IAS).
  • Ski Skiing certified: Excellent level in alpine skiing or snowboarding (capacity to conduct safely in any nivological and slope condition, even when transporting heavy equipment or toboga with wounded on board).
  • Psychophysical fitness: Perfect medical fitness for high-mountain work in extreme winter weather conditions, marked psychological balance for stress management during critical first aid interventions.
  • Flexibility and Turns (LDL): Absolute availability to work on weekends and holidays of the winter season. The programming of working hours fully respects the Federal Law on the duration of work (LDL).
  • Language skills: Excellent knowledge of the Italian language (official language of radio and exercise coordination). German knowledge is considered an important plus for safety and communication with customers.

What we offer

  • Swiss seasonal work contract complies with the federal and cantonal regulations in force, with structured wage frame according to the category tables for the staff of the slopes.
  • High visibility protective technical equipment and rescue/radio equipment complying with Suva and UFT safety standards.
  • Possibility of long-term integration within a project of relaunching and developing the area among the most dynamic of Alpine Switzerland.
