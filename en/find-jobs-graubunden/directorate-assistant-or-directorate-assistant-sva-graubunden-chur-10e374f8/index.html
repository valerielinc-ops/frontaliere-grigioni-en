Directorate Assistant or Directorate Assistant

Pensum: 40% \- 50 %

With around 220 employees, SVA Graubünden is the centre of competence for social insurance and offers a guarantee for the competent implementation of the tasks assigned to it. For the implementation of our demanding service mandate, we are looking for active employees like you who work independently in direct customer contact and are willing to take on responsibility.

For this versatile function, we are looking for a committed and reliable personality. As a directorate assistant, you are the directorate’s organizational hub. You support the director in selected assistance tasks and at the same time ensure reliable coordination of the management-wide business. With foresight, discretion and a sure sense of priorities, you control appointments and information, prepare meetings and business professionally and follow orders and pendencies consistently.

Tasks

  • You support the management organizationally and administratively and coordinate management-wide business
  • You prepare management meetings and technical meetings before and after and keep the minutes
  • You follow orders, resolutions, pendencies and deadlines of the management consistently
  • You prepare the Administrative Commission meetings administratively and coordinate business in the CMI
  • You plan and organize employee information, \- and retired events as well as other director-wide events
  • You will process the business correspondence independently and create letters and presentations for the management
  • You manage the director’s agenda and prepare his or her appointments and the required documents in advance
  • Coordinate requests, contacts, business trips and reservations and set the right priorities
  • You prepare and control the director’s expenses and credit card statements
  • You keep the business documents of the management confidential and continue to develop the assistance procedures

Requirements

  • You have a basic commercial training EFZ or an equivalent qualification; a Swiss certificate as a directorate assistant or directorate assistant is an advantage
  • You bring several years of experience in a demanding management or management assistant
  • You have very good knowledge of German and a stylish written expressive ability
  • Knowledge of other cantonal languages is desirable
  • You master MS Office very well, especially Outlook, Word and PowerPoint
  • You convince with strong organizational skills, forward-looking planning and secure prioritization
  • You coordinate different stakeholders and run parallel transactions reliably
  • You work independently, solution-oriented and flexible and bring a high service orientation and resilience
  • You are characterized by absolute discretion, loyalty and trustworthiness as well as a binding and tactful appearance
