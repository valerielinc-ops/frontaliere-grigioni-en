What to expect

About Holcim

Holcim (Switzerland) AG is one of Switzerland’s leading providers of innovative and sustainable construction solutions in the areas of building construction, civil engineering and infrastructure. At 55 locations throughout Switzerland, the company produces concrete, gravel and cement and recycles demolition materials into resource-saving products.

Sustainability is at the heart of business activities: As a pioneer in the development of sustainable solutions, Holcim Switzerland has set itself the goal of producing climate-neutral and fully recyclable building materials by 2050.

To achieve this vision, Holcim focuses on the circular economy and reduces CO2 along the entire value chain. Holcim Switzerland is a subsidiary of globally active Holcim Ltd and employs around 1200 people in three cement plants, 16 gravel plants, 36 concrete plants and several recycling centers.
