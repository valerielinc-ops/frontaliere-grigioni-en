Full description

Introduction

For a professional customer, we are looking for a heating installer for use in a versatile working environment. The focus of the activity is the professional installation, maintenance and maintenance of heating systems as well as the reliable implementation of technical works in the building and plant sector. We are looking for a person who works in a structured way, safely implements technical processes and carefully handles tools, materials and equipment. The position offers a long-term workplace with clear operational areas and varied tasks. It is used in an environment where craftsmanship, precision and reliability are important. If you have experience in heating construction and are looking for permanent employment, this position can fit your profile well.

Tasks

  • They install heating systems professionally according to technical specifications and applicable standards.
  • They perform maintenance and maintenance work on heating and supply systems reliably.
  • They check equipment for functionality, leakage and proper operation.
  • They repair faults and carefully replace defective components as needed.
  • They assist in the preparation, assembly and connection of pipelines and plant parts.
  • They document work carried out and coordinate with the relevant team.

Profile

  • You have completed training as a heating installer or in a comparable craft-technical profession.
  • They bring practical experience in the installation and maintenance of heating systems.
  • They work carefully, responsibly and strictly adhere to technical specifications.
  • They have craftsmanship and a good understanding of technical connections.
  • They are capable of teamwork and can carry out work orders reliably and independently.
  • They have a structured way of working and act safely even when changing tasks.

Advantages

  • The remuneration is largely above the applicable collective agreement and is applied fairly.
  • They undertake varied activities in the field of heating installation and technical building equipment.
  • A permanent employment contract is offered.
