Full description

Start on January 1, 2027 as a senior physician / senior physician i.V. vascular surgery 100 - 100%

Vascular surgery to think ahead and shape the future.

The Clinic for Vascular Surgery fulfils a central function for a large catchment area, not only for Graubünden, but for the whole of Southeast Switzerland. A wide range of vascular surgery (open and endovascular) is offered. The department has a SIWF continuing education facility recognition category B for vascular surgery and is part of the USGG-certified vascular center.

These are your tasks

  • You treat and supervise outpatient and inpatient vascular surgery patients and accompany them through the entire treatment process
  • You are actively involved in the office hours and operations
  • You are committed to research projects and support the further development of the department
  • You regularly participate in internal and external training

You bring that with you

  • You have completed the Swiss State Examination or an equivalent education
  • You have basic surgical training (common trunk) and have at least one year of experience in vascular surgery
  • You enjoy interdisciplinary cooperation and the variety of vascular surgery activities
  • You enjoy working in a motivated team and convince as an integrative, communicative personality with high social skills
