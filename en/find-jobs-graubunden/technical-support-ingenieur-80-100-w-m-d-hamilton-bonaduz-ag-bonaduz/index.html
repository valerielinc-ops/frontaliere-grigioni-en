Full description

True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

The Robotics Business Unit develops, produces and distributes pipetting robots to automate liquid handling processes for pharmaceutical and food industries up to cell or DNA research.

You can do that

One of your exciting tasks with a travel activity of about 20% is:

  • Technical advice for our globally operating organization
  • Remote or on-site investigation, solution and support of complex technical issues
  • Close cooperation with numerous internal offices (R&D, Product Care, Product Management, Production, etc.)
  • Link and main contact for concerns and questions of our external partners
  • Coordination and processing of customer complaints and support requests
  • Active participation in projects, definition and supervision of processes
  • Development and monitoring of data analyses
  • Support for new products, responsible for the knowledge transfer to the partners
  • Provision of service procedures, documentation, spare parts and training and service concept
  • Organisation and implementation of training for internal and external partners
  • Maintaining and expanding the knowledge base
