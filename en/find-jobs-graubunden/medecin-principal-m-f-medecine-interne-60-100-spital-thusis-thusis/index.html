The Stiftung Gesundheit Mittelbünden is the main institution for basic medical care in the Mittelbünden region. In addition to the hospital Thusis, the Spitex, the rescue Mittelbünden and a basic care practice are part of our supply network. Developments in the healthcare sector are constantly changing the needs of patients. Our focus is therefore not only on inpatient treatment, but especially on future-oriented, integrated emergency and internist basic care for the entire region.

In order to strengthen our team of cadre doctors, we are looking for a committed personality as

Strengthen together with us the care of the health region, and work constantly on the further development. We are open to various employment modalities.

Your tasks

Your profile

  • Specialist title Internal Medicine (FMH or equivalent), second specialist title / focus desired

  • Strong interprofessional and interdisciplinary understanding
