Full description

The InnHub La Punt InnHub La Punt is an innovation campus currently under construction and scheduled to open in 2028. It is dedicated to co-creation and innovation, designed by the architect Sir Norman Foster and brings together a hotel with collaborative work and meeting spaces for companies, startups, athletes, cultural professionals and the local community. Designed as a so-called third place, seasonal main location and catalyst for transformation, the campus offers seminar and learning spaces, sports facilities, retail spaces, 3 restaurants, 1 bar and 1 fuel bar, 62 hotel rooms and 8 apartments with a total of 26 rooms. Instead of a classic hotel, a program-driven environment is created in which curated content, collaboration and individual contributions shape the experience. The InnHub operating system is essentially a hotel. In addition to the extensive hospitality operation, a professionally oriented health and performance offer as well as a partner and content program for the operating company are supported by a common operating logic of the three areas. With its opening in December 2028, a long, complex ramp-up lies ahead of us, which does not allow for shortcuts. We are looking for an experienced hospitality personality as a sparring partner for our GM Hospitality – someone who has already been responsible for or co-responsible for a pre-opening and a ramp-up in this order of magnitude in a management role, who helps to build the organization from the ground up and decisively decides how we design the operation, how we equip ourselves and who will join us in the future.

Position in the organisation

Reported to: GM Hospitality | Close collaboration with: Head F&B/Head Chef, Lead Sport & Health, Lead Content & Partnerships, Lead ICT Architecture, Lead Construction, Overall Project Management | Leadership responsibility: Growing beyond the ramp-up — from a small core team to a share of the ~65 RTD Baseline Hospitality | Location: La Punt, Engadin, Graubünden, Switzerland — on site | Duration: Temporary or Permanent — Function Director F&B & Stv. of GM Hospitality possible from opening at the end of 2028 | Start: By arrangement — earliest possible entry

Core of the task As an operationally involved sparring partner at eye level, this role accompanies the entire development of the InnHub: It translates the hospitality concept into a realistic course of construction and interior design. plans and monitors all procurements and builds up the operational organisation. This also includes building up the future team and ensuring that the InnHub working method also bears under the conditions of real operation.
