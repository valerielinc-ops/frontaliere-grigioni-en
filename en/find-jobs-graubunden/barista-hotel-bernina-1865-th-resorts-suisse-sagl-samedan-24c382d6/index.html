Full description

TH Resorts, Italian hotel group leader in the leisure segment, with over 30 Hotels & Resorts located in marine, mountain and urban locations for a total of about 6,800 rooms, look for Barista for the TH Samedan - Hotel Bernina 1865, for the winter season 2026-2027 with possibility of work also in the summer season 2027.

What are you gonna do?

  • Preparation of cocktails, long drinks, aperitifs and coffee.
  • Product sales management at the bar.
  • Organization and supervision of the warehouse.
  • Coordination of the team and management of work shifts.
  • Promotion of professional development of staff.
  • Requirements

Who are we looking for?

A bartender with

  • Excellent knowledge of mixing techniques and a deep culture of drinks.
  • Creativity, organization and leadership skills.
  • Explain relational and communication skills.
  • Good mastery of English, German and French.
  • Hotel diploma and/or specialization courses.
  • Experience in tourism or catering.
