The White Arena Group in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and an enthusiastic community for unique mountain adventures. With mountain railways, hotels, gastronomy, sports and rental shops and the LAAX School, we create unforgettable experiences for our guests - and attractive workplaces for our employees. | The Riders Hotel is not a hotel like any other. In the middle of LAAX, close to the halfpipe and the snowpark LAAX, riders, creatives, music enthusiasts, families and friends meet here. The Riders Lobby is living room from early morning until late in the evening and offers a curated menu right at the bar. The restaurant eats together, the club celebrates until the night. Reduced to the essentials: snow, music, community. The freestyle center of LAAX. | You are one of the first people our guests meet on a club night at the Riders. Welcome them, check tickets and guest lists, and ensure that admission is quick and easy. You are open and sympathetic, but can remain just as clear if something does not fit. Together with Security and Club Host, you ensure that the right mood for a good night is created at the Entrance. What You Move:

  • Welcome our guests, check tickets and guest lists and ensure fast and organized admission.
  • You manage the box office, process payments correctly and keep track of many guests at the same time.
  • You take care of stamps and make sure that the defined entry procedures are adhered to.
  • You answer questions, help guests easily and are an important contact person during admission.
  • You work closely with Security and Club Host and pass on situations that need their support directly. What makes you:
  • You are open, sympathetic and like to approach people – even late at night.
  • You work quickly, attentively and reliably and can use money and cash systems responsibly.
  • You also keep track of a queue outside the door and do not let yourself get out of peace.
  • You can be friendly and straightforward, but just as clear and determined when the situation demands it.
  • You are a team player and have a desire for club culture, music and the special atmosphere of a Riders club night. | An attractive workplace in the middle of an extraordinary mountain world is certain for you. You become part of a dynamic, bright team and can help shape the further development of LAAX. With us you will find an environment in which professionalism, speed, pragmatism and humanity go together. Look forward to numerous employee benefits, such as free mountain rides, perks in our shops, hotels and restaurants, and benefits for your family. Start your professional adventure with the Weissen Arena Group with your complete online application.
