Case Manager in OKP

This is waiting for you:

  • You accompany customers in challenging multiple problems
  • You coordinate along the medical treatment path
  • You will have challenging discussions with insured persons, medical specialists and other stakeholders – clear, solution-oriented and at eye level
  • You advise the customers on social security-related issues and connect with other positions (employees, IV, etc.)
  • You contribute ideas and actively develop our processes in Case Management Private

You bring this with you:

  • Completed basic paramedical training
  • CAS Case Management and/or Social Security Card
  • Several years of experience in case management desirable
  • Good network of service providers in the canton of Graubünden
  • You work in a structured, independent and solution-oriented manner and keep an overview even in complex situations
  • You combine empathy with the necessary assertiveness and communicate confidently even in demanding situations
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Mobility and a valid driving licence category B
