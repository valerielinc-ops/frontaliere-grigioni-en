Full description

As a university, FH Graubünden focuses on dynamic thinking and proactive action. Our more than 2400 students in education and training develop into responsible personalities. In our interdisciplinary and transdisciplinary research, we focus on applied future technologies, development in the Alpine region and entrepreneurial activity. As an employer, we promote equal opportunities and diversity. For the Department of Central Services, we are looking for an HR team to be strengthened immediately or by agreement. In this role, you support the HR team in various administrative and organizational tasks along the entire employee lifecycle. You work in personnel administration as well as in various HR processes such as recruitment. You also organize and coordinate small HR events as well as various workshops. In addition, you support the Head of Learners in a wide variety of areas and work on digitalization projects.
