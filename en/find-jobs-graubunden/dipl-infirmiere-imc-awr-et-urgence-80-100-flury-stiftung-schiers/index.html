Full description

COMPETENT AND WARM With more than 600 employees in the Schiers Hospital, the three retirement homes in Schiers, Jenaz and Klosters, the Spitex, Wohnen mit Service, the daycare center, the parent advice center and the Klosters Medical Center, the Flury Foundation ensures comprehensive and integrated health care in the Prättigau between Grüsch and Klosters. Our hospital in Schiers is a modern acute primary care hospital with an interdisciplinary ward with 41 beds for the surgery, medicine, gynecology and obstetrics clinics, an outpatient day clinic, the emergency department and the certified Intermediate Care Station (IMC). For our recovery room (AWR), the IMC and emergencies, we are looking for a qualified nurse IMC, AWR and emergency 80 - 100% immediately or by appointment.

Your responsibilities include

  • Postoperative care and monitoring of patients in the area of ​​AWR
  • Information and instruction for outpatients about behavior after the operation
  • Comprehensive care and monitoring of high-risk patients in the IMC area
  • Comprehensive initial care for emergency patients

Your profile

  • Min. 2 years of professional experience in the acute sector, an advantage in one of the areas of AWR, IMC or emergency

and willingness to complete the IMC course

  • You have good organizational skills and look forward to being actively involved in your work.
