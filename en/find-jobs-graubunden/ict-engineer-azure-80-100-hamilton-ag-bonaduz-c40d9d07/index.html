Description

Guided by the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", the companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer when it comes to ventilators, automated pipetting and sample storage, as well as the development of process sensors.

As part of AG's Business Unit Shared Services (SHS), this division handles central tasks for the companies. Teams in HR, ICT, Corporate Communication, Facilities and Finance ensure that processes run smoothly and employees worldwide receive optimal support.

What you can achieve

  • Operation and further development of our Microsoft Azure platform
  • Involvement in exciting cloud\- and infrastructure projects
  • Implementation and optimization of Azure services
  • Automation of infrastructure\- and operational processes
  • Development and maintenance of Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and CI/CD\-pipelines
  • Support for cloud\-migrations and modernization initiatives
  • Monitoring, analysis and optimization of the cloud\-environment
  • Involvement in 2nd\- and 3rd\-level\-support
  • Evaluation and introduction of new cloud\-technologies

What you bring

  • Completed vocational training or degree (FH/HF/Uni) in computer science
  • Initial practical experience with Microsoft Azure or a strong interest in developing further in this area
  • Basic knowledge of cloud, networking or server\-infrastructures
  • Interest in automation, scripting and modern cloud\-technologies
  • Knowledge of PowerShell, Terraform or Azure DevOps is an advantage
  • Interest in security\-, governance\- and cloud\-operations topics
  • Very good German skills and good English skills
  • Independent, reliable and structured working style
  • Teamwork skills, willingness to learn and enthusiasm for new technologies
