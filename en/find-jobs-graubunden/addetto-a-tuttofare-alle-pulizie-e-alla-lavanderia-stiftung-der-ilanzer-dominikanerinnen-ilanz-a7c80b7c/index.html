Full description

THE KLOSTER ILANZ – the largest women’s monastery in the canton of Graubünden – is also an employer and host in Ilanz, the first city on the Rhine. As a community of women with today still around 54 religious sisters, we were and are an important size in the region. With our almost 70 employees, we look after our elderly sisters in our retirement and nursing home, welcome our diverse guests in our guest house and host our employees and guests of all stakeholders.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for

ALLROUNDERIN CLEANING - LAWHERE 60 - 80%, amount by agreement

We offer you

  • An attractive workplace in an exciting and extraordinary environment
  • a versatile activity to gain experience
  • a family working atmosphere in a committed team
  • An appreciative corporate culture
  • various benefits such as free parking, discounts, employee events etc.

Your duties

  • General basic and maintenance cleaning work in all areas of the monastery operation incl. Guest House
  • Assistance in the laundry (holiday representation, etc.)
  • Implementation of the hygiene concept, the proper and professional handling of all machines, as well as their care and maintenance

This is what you do with

  • Experience in a similar position
  • Joy, in all cleaning work and contact with people
  • Readiness for weekend assignments
  • Good knowledge of German, manners and empathy
  • Good physical constitution and resilience
  • Teamwork, resilience, independence and a sense of responsibility
  • Good mood - even in hectic times
