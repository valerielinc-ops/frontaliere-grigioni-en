Start on 1 December 2026 as station manager: in nursing visceral surgery 90 - 100%

Our 28-bed ward with a focus on visceral surgery is looking for a strong-leading personality who accompanies their team with heart, clarity and expertise and actively shapes the further development of care.

You like to take responsibility, give orientation and enjoy developing people and care together? Perfect, then we look forward to your application!

These are your tasks

  • You lead, motivate and develop your team with clarity, appreciation and commitment and create an open feedback and communication culture
  • You are responsible for the organization of the station – from service planning and personnel deployment to economic management
  • You support your team in challenging nursing situations and are active in direct care

You bring that with you

  • A diploma in nursing tertiary level as well as several years of professional experience in acute care surgery
  • Ideally, leadership experience and further education are advantageous
  • Strong communication and reflectivity as well as resilience, independence and high self-efficacy
