Economist/\-in public transport ordering procedure

Office of Energy and Transport \| 80\-100% \| Chur

Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the Cantonal Administration Graubünden together with us.

The Office for Energy and Transport is the cantonal competence centre for energy and public transport issues. In the area of public transport, we are responsible for the strategic planning and the mutual coordination of all public transport in the canton of Graubünden. We coordinate the public transport of trains and buses and are responsible for the planning, project planning and implementation of projects for future-oriented public transport. Good connections to the national and international rail network as well as regular and frequent connections in regional and agglomeration traffic are of central importance for the maintenance and further development of the life, tourism and economic area of Graubünden.

We are looking for the Office of Energy and Transport per 1\. December 2026 or by agreement an economist/\-in public transport ordering procedure.

Your responsibilities

  • Implementation of the regional public transport (RPV) procurement procedure for individual public transport companies (road and rail)
  • Processing of challenging business and financial issues relating to public transport
  • Development of performance agreements with transport undertakings, monitoring and monitoring of their implementation
  • Dealing with complex strategic and conceptual issues relating to public transport in the Canton of Graubünden
  • Responsibility for concluding target agreements with transport companies
  • Representation of the Canton of Graubünden in cantonal and national working groups on public transport

That is what we want from you.

  • Enthusiasm for strong and economic public transport in the Canton of Graubünden
  • University or university of applied sciences degree in business or economic orientation
  • Strong interest in strategic issues
  • Experience working independently on conceptual issues
  • Knowledge of public transport or a comparable complex industry (energy or health) is an advantage
  • Structured and reliable working methods with a high degree of personal responsibility and a high degree of quality awareness
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation, assertiveness, negotiating skills and skill in dealing with different stakeholders
  • Confident mastery of the German language in words and writing
  • Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is an advantage

Our advantages

Flexible working time models and where possible incl. Home office and mobile work\-life\-balance with mind. 5 weeks holidays and service leave Special social allowance in addition to child allowance and support for third-party childcare Attractive wage and social benefits Various training and further education opportunities to shape the future of the canton
