Full description

People and culture. The High Mountain Clinic Davos is growing – and our HR is growing with us. That is why we are expanding our human resources work step by step: more modern, digital and future-oriented. Our HR team is in the middle of an exciting development phase: We build processes and digital tools, sharpen structures and create standards that work in the long term. This means that not everything is already perfect – but that is exactly where the opportunity lies. You can help shape, contribute ideas and actively promote change. If you enjoy taking responsibility, finding pragmatic solutions and building something together with a motivated team, then you are at the right place. With us, you not only shape HR processes – but the future of our HR work. | Tasks:

  • Advise and accompany : You are a competent contact person for employees and a sparring partner for leaders. Personnel development from apprentices to managers is a matter of the heart for you.
  • Designing instead of working : You start by building a “Best-Class Human Resources Management” – with ideas, pragmatism and passion. Employee Self Service and Management Self Service are familiar terms. You bring your experience solution-oriented.
  • Numbers and processes: You like to optimize processes and get involved with numbers flair in payroll accounting (ABACUS).
  • System affinity: You supervise and advise about the time recording system Polypoint and contribute to smooth processes.
  • Teamplayer: Holiday representation, transparency, openness and mutual support are a matter of course for you. | Requirements:
  • Well-founded know-how: You have a university education and training in the field of human resources management or are a proven HR specialist.
  • Practice in health care : Experience in health care (clinic or similar environment) is desirable.
  • Expertise: Several years of professional experience in HR management, a pronounced service-oriented attitude.
  • Calm and Overview: Even in hectic moments, you remain focused, solution-oriented and never lose your sense of humor.
