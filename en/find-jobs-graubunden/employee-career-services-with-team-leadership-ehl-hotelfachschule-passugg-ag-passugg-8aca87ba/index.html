Employee Career Services with Team Leadership (m/f/d)

For 60 years we have been preparing young people for their careers. We offer networked training courses from basic education to high school up to Bachelor. Since 2013, we have been part of the EHL Group, which is one of the most renowned academic education providers in the hospitality management sector in the world.

We search immediately or by agreement

It ensures the effective cooperation of the team, uniform processes and professional support of students/learners and employers and develops the area in accordance with the objectives of the EHL Hotel School Passugg. The position includes a 100% commitment, with the team leadership including 10%.

Your main tasks

  • Planning and implementation of traineeship instruction
  • Advice and support of learners and students in the application phasesCo-organization of Career Events at EHL Passugg
  • Administrative tasks, contracting and data maintenance Control, evaluation and documentation of internship tasks and \-proofCommunication and correspondence with different stakeholders (companies, learners / students, internal departments, authorities)
  • You lead the Career Services division and further develop its employees
  • You expand strategic employer partnerships and strengthen the hotel school’s network
  • You are responsible for processes, key figures, budget and the continuous optimization of services and processes

Your profile

  • You have completed training in the hospitality sector or a comparable qualification and bring experience from the hospitality industry, catering and / or educational environment
  • You already have experience in a leadership position
  • You like to support people in their professional development and are familiar with the areas of consulting, coaching, recruiting or career services They convince through organizational talent, communication strength and instinct in dealing with different stakeholders.
  • Digital tools and systems use you naturally and efficiently in everyday work
  • They communicate stylishly in German and English
  • Other languages are beneficial
  • You are familiar with employment and training law topics or are motivated to deepen your knowledge in this area

In addition to an exciting activity at our school with international students, we offer our EHL family members interesting benefits, career and development opportunities. Try some EHL family air already.
