Role overview

The Department of Infrastructure, Energy and Mobility (DIEM) covers energy, hydropower, natural hazards, public transport, civil engineering, procurement, forestry, hunting, large-scale predators and fisheries.

It is divided into five departments as well as the departmental secretariat, which, as the central staff body, provides comprehensive support for the departmental head in the technical and political leadership of the department.

The departmental secretariat provides its services in the new administrative centre "sinergia" immediately next to the Chur West station in a modern, open and digital working environment.
