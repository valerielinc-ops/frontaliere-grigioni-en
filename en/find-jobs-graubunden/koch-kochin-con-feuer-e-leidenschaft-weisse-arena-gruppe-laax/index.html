Full description

The White Arena Group in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and an enthusiastic community for unique mountain adventures. With mountain railways, hotels, gastronomy, sports and rental shops and the LAAX School, we create unforgettable experiences for our guests - and attractive workplaces for our employees. The Grandis Vinoteca & Grill restaurant in Laax with a clear focus on quality and hospitality is a place that delights wine lovers and gourmets alike. Our concept combines a first-class vinothek with a modern barbecue restaurant that meets the highest culinary standards. For our restaurant, which is completely dedicated to regional, seasonal cuisine and a conscious choice of meat, we are looking for a committed and creative personality who lives the craft of cooking in its most original form: only with fire. What you do move:

  • You maintain a cuisine that focuses on the highest quality products from the region – as natural as possible, sustainably produced and carefully selected
  • You use the playground where cooking is centered on the fire
  • You are looking for a small, passionate team with great demands on quality, craftsmanship and authenticity
  • You appreciate the opportunity to become part of a special culinary project that creates awareness and combines pleasure with responsibility. What makes you:
  • A personality with character, attitude and attention to detail. Someone who engages with enthusiasm, thinks creatively and carries the fire not only in the kitchen, but also in the heart
  • You are a trained chef, have experience in the upscale kitchen and share our values in terms of sustainability, animal welfare and product quality
  • You love to cook with the fire – over an open grill or with the embers
  • You work with respect for the ingredients and know how to get the most out of regional and seasonal products
  • You value simplicity as much as sophistication and bring your own ideas. An attractive workplace in the middle of an extraordinary mountain world is certain for you. You become part of a dynamic, bright team and can help shape the further development of LAAX. With us you will find an environment where professionalism, speed, pragmatism and humanity go together. Look forward to numerous employee benefits, such as free mountain rides, perks in our shops, hotels and restaurants, and benefits for your family. Start your professional adventure with the Weissen Arena Group with your complete online application.
