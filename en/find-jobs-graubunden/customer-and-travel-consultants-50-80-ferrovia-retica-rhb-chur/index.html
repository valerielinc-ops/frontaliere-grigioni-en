Ti piace stare a contatto con le persone e sei esperto/a di viaggi? In qualità di consulente clienti e viaggi presso la RhB, aiuterai la nostra clientela nella pianificazione, darai consigli e prenoterai esperienze che rimarranno impresse nella memoria.

Cosa puoi fare

  • Customer and travel advice at the POS (switch, telephone, e-mail)
  • Exchange with local partners and service providers (information centres, hotels, station stopping stations)
  • Preparation, implementation and post-processing of various events in the region
  • Tasks within the framework of defined third-party business such as tourism, postal services, mountain railways etc.
  • Administrative activities as well as customer support via all channels and support the learners

Ecco perché sei la persona giusta’

  • Completed basic training in the commercial sector or in the retail trade (with advantage in public transport or tourism)
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German as well as good oral knowledge in a 2nd national language and English (both level B1)
  • Geography skills (Switzerland/Europe)
  • Good IT knowledge, especially in the area of distribution systems (applications and channels)
  • Heart blood and commitment as well as safe and winning appearance

Cosa ti offriamo’

  • 2. Class General Subscription
  • Free tickets and discounts on rail travel abroad
  • Paid mobile phone and subscription for business and private use
  • Attractive social benefits and pension fund with very good benefits
  • Additional purchase of holidays
  • Possibility for internal and external training with financial support from RhB
