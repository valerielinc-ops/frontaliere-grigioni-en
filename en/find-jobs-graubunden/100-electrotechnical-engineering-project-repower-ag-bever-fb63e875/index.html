100% Electrotechnical Engineering Project

We are looking for an immediate or date from the beginning

Repower is one of Switzerland's leading energy companies. The history of Repower, which lasts for over 120 years, begins in the extreme south of the Graubünden, in Valposchiavo. In addition to the headquarters of Poschiavo, we have other offices in Landquart, Zurich, Ilanz, Küblis, Disentis, Grono and Bever. As an employer, Repower focuses on personal initiative and confidence to promote the creativity of its approximately 730 employees. Because tomorrow's energy economy needs new ideas. We offer you a challenging working environment, where you can develop future-oriented solutions with your team for sustainable energy.

The electrical engineering department consists of 15 employees and is responsible for the technical design of new plants, as well as for extensions, transformations and renewals in the electrical engineering sector. Employees operate in different locations: I could've, Ilanz, Bever, Landquart and Küblis.

Main tasks

  • Management and responsibilities of substation projects, from design to implementation
  • Technical design of new plants, extensions, modifications and renewals in the field of high and medium voltage (substations/electric power stations)
  • Autonomous design of systems, components and concepts
  • Calculation, definition and evaluations concerning the sizing of plants for the transport of electricity
  • Drafting of competition notices, requests for offers and evaluation

Profile required

  • Degree or continuous training in electronics (SUP/SSS) or equivalent qualification
  • Multiannual experience in the design of substation plants and in the management of projects in the energy distribution sector
  • In-depth knowledge of electrotechnics in the field of medium and high voltage plants
  • Great mastery of German (at least level B2\) and preferably with basic knowledge of Italian
  • You are characterized by autonomy and reliability
  • Interconnected personality, teamwork skills and great availability
