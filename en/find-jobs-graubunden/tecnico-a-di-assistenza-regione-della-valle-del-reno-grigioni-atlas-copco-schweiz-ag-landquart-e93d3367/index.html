Servictechniker\*in \- Region Rheintal / Graubünden

Bring your curiosity to work as a service technician and become part of our service technician Team East. In this you carry out maintenance, repairs and commissioning of compressors.

Your daily work

  • Maintenance and maintenance services of industrial compressors and accessories
  • Performing repairs, revisions and overhauls
  • Commissioning of new machinery
  • Use within the framework of the Picett Organisation (periodic Picett Service)
  • Sale of customer services such as service kits, oil etc.
  • Reporting to the regional manager or relevant departments on customer observations

Your skills and experience

  • Basic technical training (e.g. polymechanic, mechatronics technician, automatic technician)
  • Valid category B driving licence for journeys with the service vehicle
  • Secure use of digital service and reporting tools as well as common Microsoft Office applications
  • Ideally, experience in a repair environment and customer service
  • Good electrical knowledge is advantageous
  • Very good knowledge of German (C1/C2\)
  • Open, solution-oriented personality with a sense of customer needs

We look forward to your application, even if you do not meet every single requirement. Different experiences and perspectives are valuable to us, and we look forward to seeing what you bring to them.

  • Cost coverage for car parking space
  • Very good social benefits
  • An open feedback and you culture
  • Self-employed and flexible work with the possibility of part-time work
  • Local and international development opportunities
  • Free online training and language courses
  • Workplace social services

Your place of work

This is why it is worth working with us
