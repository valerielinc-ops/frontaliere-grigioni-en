Your future workplace is the year-round clinical school for 28 students at the child and adolescent psychiatric clinic, which opened in Chur in spring 2026.

Your tasks

  • You teach the basic subjects (German, mathematics, English) and other foreign languages and also design thematic teaching units

  • You communicate regularly with the treatment team

Our requirements profile

  • You have a teaching diploma for (primary or upper school) as well as a degree in school-based curative education (or the willingness to complete this degree in a timely manner)
  • You are flexible in use at the upper and primary level
  • You enjoy working in a motivated team of teachers and are characterized by very good contact and communication skills
  • You are experienced in interdisciplinary collaboration

We offer you

  • An exceptional and exciting workplace at the interface between school and child and adolescent psychiatry
  • Varied interdisciplinary collaboration in a professional team consisting of specialists from child and adolescent psychiatry, psychotherapy, specialist therapies, social pedagogy and nursing
  • Meaningful work because you make an important contribution to the mental health of children and young people
  • Diverse internal training and further education offerings and team supervision – we also support your external training needs
  • Advanced working conditions
  • An optimal work-life balance with part-time working models, regular working hours and unpaid holidays
