Full description

They are curious about an extraordinary experience in a historic house where theaters are played and exciting people walk in and out. The Piz Mitgel is a cultural house without overnight accommodation next winter. We open a small trattoria with Italian and German dishes for the evening service. At the same time, we spoil the theatre audience at selected dates with refined Apérohäppchen and sweet biscuits. Under the direction of our host, you are responsible for a high and constant quality of the culinary offering, producing the components for our dishes in the à la carte operation as well as for aperitifs and snacks, sometimes also for other outlets. You also ensure compliance with legal regulations and HACCP standards.

What you bring

  • Completed culinary apprenticeship EFZ or many years of experience
  • Passion, creativity and passion for the culinary arts, if possible

Also for the patisserie

  • Independent work with great personal responsibility
  • Joy in the cultural environment

What you can expect from us

  • Multifaceted work in an inspiring environment
  • Highly motivated team that tackles and moves
  • Modern and well furnished kitchen
  • Employee accommodation at fair prices
  • Opportunities for leisure activities in an attractive cultural and winter sports destination
  • Free access to all stage productions by Origen

Further information is available at www.origen.ch

Origen is a visionary, vital cultural institution in Graubünden. The Foundation has a broad cultural portfolio. It realizes cross-division stage productions, enables concert series and develops an innovative open-air play that seeks dialogue with nature. Origen is committed to a high-quality building culture, preserves and revitalizes listed structures, creates iconic cultural buildings and makes a significant contribution to the digital transformation in construction in cooperation with ETH Zurich. Origen cooperates with important universities, conducts research on cultural-historical topics, is committed to the Romanesque language and teaches in depth in various disciplines. Origen is committed to inspiring hospitality, renovating and managing historic hotels and developing new culinary concepts in its own restaurants. The foundation is committed to a culturally shaped, inspired revitalization of the Alpine cultural landscape, creates lively places, promotes quality tourism and creates meaningful jobs in one of the most beautiful regions of the Alps.
