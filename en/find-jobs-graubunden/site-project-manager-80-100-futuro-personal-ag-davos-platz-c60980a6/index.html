Full description

Site manager/project manager/in 80-100 %

For a company that combines architecture, timber construction planning and construction management under one roof, we are looking for an operationally strong personality for the Davos site as a project manager responsible for the location (80-100%).

Their tasks

  • Overall responsibility for several construction projects at the site
  • Ensuring quality, deadlines and efficiency
  • Organisation and coordination of temporary buildings in connection with major international events
  • Operational management of the site, clear structures and responsibilities
  • Establishment, leadership and development of the team on site
  • Representation in the regional environment, maintenance of customer and partner relationships

Your profile

  • Experience in construction project management and/or construction management
  • Background in architecture, construction management, civil engineering or timber construction planning
  • Enjoyment in controlling complex construction projects
  • Structured, entrepreneurial way of working
  • Technical, economic and organisational understanding
  • Experience in the environment of major events or willingness to build a network

We offer

  • Varied role with responsibility and scope for design
  • Dynamic environment with projects in construction, innovation and sustainability
  • Short decision-making paths, high independence
  • Development prospects within the group of companies

Applications by email please with reference 493EBQ in the subject. Thank you.
