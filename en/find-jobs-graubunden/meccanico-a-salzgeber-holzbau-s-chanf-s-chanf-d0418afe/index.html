Introduction

Holzbau S\-chanf stands for versatile and innovative services in the areas of carpentry, carpentry, transport and crane work. In addition, the company operates a district heating network unique in the Engadin, which has been developed completely independently and is operated for a constantly growing number of customers.

To strengthen our team in S\-chanf, we are looking for a committed and reliable personality as a mechanic.

Responsibility

  • Maintenance, repair and maintenance of machinery, equipment and vehicles (PW and trucks)
  • Error analysis and independent correction of technical failures
  • Perform periodic checks and preventive maintenance
  • Support for repair and maintenance work in the field of timber construction, transport and energy center
  • Ensuring reliable and safe operation of facilities
  • Cooperation with internal specialists and external service providers

Requirements

  • Completed training as a mechanic, agricultural machinery mechanic, construction machinery mechanic or equivalent qualification
  • Professional experience in maintenance of machinery and equipment
  • Good knowledge of mechanics, hydraulics and pneumatics
  • Independent, careful and solution-oriented working method
  • Technical understanding and enjoyment of varied tasks
  • Teamwork, reliability and high operational readiness
  • Category B driving licence
  • German skills are advantageous

We offer

  • A varied activity in an innovative company
  • A motivated and collaborative team
  • Modern equipment and technologies
  • Long-term employment
