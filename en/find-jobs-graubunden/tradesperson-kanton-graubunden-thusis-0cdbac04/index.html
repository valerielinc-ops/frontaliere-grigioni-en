Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the cantonal administration Graubünden together with us.

The civil engineering office is responsible for the design, construction, maintenance and operation of the cantonal roads as well as the hydraulic engineering and slow transport in Graubünden. We also take care of the operational maintenance of the national roads on the cantonal territory. The canton’s road infrastructure comprises over 1,450 art buildings, including bridges at airy heights of up to 90 meters and tunnels of up to 3 kilometers in length. The challenges that the nature of our mountain canton brings in and with it makes our work challenging and versatile. As a Friendly Work Space certified office, the health and satisfaction of our approximately 480 employees are particularly important to us. For this reason, we are committed to systematic occupational health management (BGM).

The Technical Section operates the entire operational and safety equipment of the cantonal and national roads, as well as the more than 40 tunnels of the canton Graubünden. It consists of a modern and complex IT/OT system and network landscape. The environment includes communication infrastructure, video surveillance, event ventilation, tunnel sensors, lighting and traffic control of tunnels and road sections. It is controlled by means of a modern control system.

We are looking for the department of road maintenance in the section Technology per 1\. January 2027 or by agreement a/\-n craftsman/\-in
