Full description

For our restaurant in the beautiful Sertigtal in Davos, we are looking for a committed, resilient and team-capable personality as a buffet-and-service all-rounder.

You like to work independently, keep an overview even in hectic moments and support our team wherever help is needed. Kindness, a positive attitude and a respectful approach in the team are a matter of course for us. At noon we serve classic Swiss dishes in a lively daily operation. In the evening, the mountain guide turns into a restaurant with upscale cuisine -- without stars, but with a lot of passion. We make a lot of things ourselves -- from fresh pasta and ravioli to homemade sauces to our desserts and glaces -- and we attach great importance to regionality and quality.

Your responsibilities *

  • Preparation and dispensing of cold and hot drinks, coffee specialities and desserts
  • Support in the service when serving, clearing and caring for our guests
  • Establishment and dismantling of the buffet and service area including Mise en Place
  • Compliance with cleanliness, order and hygiene standards (HACCP)
  • Assistance in general work such as goods clearing, snow clearance, lingerie and other activities in the restaurant
  • Close cooperation with the kitchen and service for a smooth operation \\

You bring that with you? \

  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Friendly, well-groomed and cordial appearance
  • Positive basic attitude as well as enjoyment of working in a team
  • Self-employed, reliable and forward-looking work
  • Resilience and willingness to take part in cross-divisional work
  • Experience in gastronomy or service of advantage \\

Important note German skills are mandatory for this position. \ Applications without appropriate language skills cannot be considered. \*

What we offer? \

  • Regulated working hours
  • Accommodation available
  • Attractive and fair distribution of tips
  • Bonus regulation
  • Young, motivated team with strong cohesion
