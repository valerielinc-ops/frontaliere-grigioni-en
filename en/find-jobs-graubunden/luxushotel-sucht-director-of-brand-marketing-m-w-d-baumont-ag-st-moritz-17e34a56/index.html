Full description

A renowned luxury hotel is looking for an experienced and strategically minded leader as Director of Brand & Marketing (m/f/d) to strengthen its management team.

In this key position, you assume overall responsibility for brand strategy, marketing and communication. They further develop the brand identity, control all digital marketing activities, take responsibility for the social media and content strategy and strengthen long-term customer loyalty. You will also lead the Brand & Marketing team and coordinate external agencies and strategic partners.

Your profile

  • Several years of leadership experience in brand and marketing management, ideally in the premium or luxury segment.
  • In-depth knowledge of digital marketing, social media, content, CRM and brand strategy.
  • Experience in leading teams and dealing with decision-makers.
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, strategic skill and strong implementation competence.
  • Fluent knowledge of English, knowledge of German beneficial.
  • Passion for luxury, aesthetics and first-class guest experiences.

The offer

  • Permanent annual position in an exclusive management position.
  • Membership of the Executive Committee.
  • Direct influence on brand strategy and guest experience.
  • International working environment in the luxury hotel industry.
  • Attractive long-term career and development opportunities.

If you are attracted by a demanding leadership position in the international luxury hotel industry and want to strategically develop a renowned brand, we look forward to your full application.
