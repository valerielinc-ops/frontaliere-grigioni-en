Full description

Introduction

For a client in the building materials and industrial sector we are looking for a technician: in HF in the field of electrical engineering or system technology with a focus on electrical design, industrial plants and technical documentation. The position includes challenging tasks in an environment where energy efficiency, standard implementation and the further development of electrotechnical infrastructure are the focus. We are looking for a person who safely assesses technical connections, accompanies projects from the concept phase to commissioning and supports cooperation with internal and external specialist bodies in a structured manner. The activity offers a wide range of technical interfaces and requires both independent work and close coordination with different disciplines.

Tasks

  • They take over the responsible electrical design and dimensioning of switchgear in industrial plant construction.
  • They ensure the standardized implementation of technical requirements in all project phases.
  • They systematically create, maintain and develop electrical documentation.
  • They accompany the electrical project management from the concept phase to commissioning.
  • They evaluate services and installations of external companies professionally and comprehensibly.
  • They work closely with process engineering, mechanics, construction, IT and other specialist areas.

Profile

  • They have completed training in electrical engineering, automation, energy engineering or a comparable qualification.
  • You have experience in the planning, realization or commissioning of industrial plants.
  • They have knowledge of low-voltage systems and their technical requirements.
  • You work independently, structured and solution-oriented in a technical environment.
  • They enjoy interdisciplinary cooperation and interest in innovative solutions.
  • They communicate safely in German and English in word and writing.

Advantages

  • professional support throughout the application process.
  • Permanent employment contract with a long-term perspective in the company.
