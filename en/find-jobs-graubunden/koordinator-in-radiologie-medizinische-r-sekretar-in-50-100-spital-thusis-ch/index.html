Full description

Are you not just looking for a job, but also a team? Then you are exactly right with us.

In our regional hospital you still know each other personally. We work together instead of next to each other, support each other in everyday life and meet with appreciation, respect and a good dose of warmth. Here you can ask questions, contribute ideas, take responsibility and above all be human.

For our radiology, we are looking for a committed personality as soon as possible or by agreement.

Full-time employment is preferred, but we are also happy to check corresponding part-time models.

Your area of responsibility

You are the central point of contact of our radiology and together with the team ensure that everything runs smoothly. You are often the first voice on the phone and the first friendly face at the reception.

Your tasks include

  • Responsibility for a friendly and professional reception in radiology
  • telephone and personal contact with patients: inside, assignees and internal bodies
  • Coordination and appointments
  • Acceptance of investigation notifications
  • Opening and management of investigation cases
  • Ensuring complete and correct patient data
  • Support for our day planning
  • Reduction of radiological specialists in administrative tasks
  • Assistance in the further development of administrative processes

In short: You hold many threads together and make a significant contribution to ensuring that patients, referees and employees feel in good hands with us.

Your requirement profile

We care more about who you are than meeting every single requirement.

You are a good fit for us if you...

  • like to organize and keep the overview
  • enjoying contact with people
  • Work reliably, responsibly and independently
  • Stay calm and friendly even in lively situations
  • enjoy working in a team and appreciate open cooperation
  • safely handle computer systems and administrative tasks
  • have very good knowledge of German in words and writing

Commercial training or training as a medical secretary is an advantage. Experience in health care or radiology makes us happy, but is not a must.

We offer

  • A warm and family team that is there for each other
  • an open and appreciative working atmosphere
  • short paths and uncomplicated cooperation
  • Regular working hours from Monday to Friday
  • a varied activity with a lot of personal responsibility
  • modern infrastructure and an exciting working environment
  • contemporary employment conditions with 42-hour week and at least 25 days holiday
  • Training and development opportunities
  • Attractive leisure activities
  • a job in one of the most beautiful regions of Switzerland

Do you feel addressed? We look forward to your application. Maybe you're a perfect fit for us. And maybe we're right for you.
