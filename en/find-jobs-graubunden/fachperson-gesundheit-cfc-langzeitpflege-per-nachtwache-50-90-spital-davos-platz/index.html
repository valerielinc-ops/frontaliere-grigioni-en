Live and work in the Destination Davos Klosters? We look forward to reinforcement! ##STR1##

With your expertise in nursing or old-age care and your winning nature, you are an important support for the residents of our nursing home.

As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable company, but important for the region, we maintain a collegial working atmosphere and appreciate the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos.

For the night watch in our nursing home Alberti we are looking for an agreement

Health professional EFZ long-term care for night watch (50-90%) ##

Your responsibilities

  • Joyful and appreciative care and care of the residents of our nursing home at night
  • Vigilant attention to the well-being of residents during the night and use of the right nursing handles as needed
  • Ensuring accurate and complete electronic maintenance documentation
  • Making responsible decisions in demanding situations
  • First contact person in emergency situations for residents, relatives and the medical profession

Your profile

  • Completed health EFZ training or equivalent with SRC recognition
  • In-depth expertise and several years of professional experience in long-term care in order to master the special requirements at night competently and calmly
  • Self-employment, responsibility and flexibility
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • High social skills, enthusiasm and commitment to work with residents
  • Calmness, empathy and the ability to act safely and calmly alone
  • A Heart for Older People and Their Stories

Our offer

  • Independence with a lot of self-responsibility and trust
  • Meaningful task: They give our inhabitants security at night
  • Careful introduction and pleasant cooperation in the care team
  • Attractive employment conditions and good social benefits

For further information, Ms. Bea Heeb, Head of Care and Cross-Sectoral Disciplines, as well as the HRM recruitment team, is at your disposal under or via email.
