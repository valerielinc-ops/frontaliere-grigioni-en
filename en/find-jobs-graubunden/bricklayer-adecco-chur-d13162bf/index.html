Introduction

For our customers in civil engineering we are looking for an independent and robust personality as a mason. You help ensure that construction projects are implemented precisely and on schedule.

Tasks

  • Masonry and concrete work
  • Shells, reinforcements and casting of components
  • Participation in new buildings, renovations and renovations
  • Reading of building plans and implementing the specifications
  • Operation of tools and smaller machines
  • Cooperation with various trades on the construction site

Profile

  • Experience as a mason or completed training in the construction industry
  • Good physical fitness and craftsmanship
  • Self-employed and responsible work
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Team-ready, reliable and resilient
  • Enjoying a variety of activities on the construction site

Advantages

  • Modern work equipment and safety equipment
  • Development opportunities in the building industry
  • Contemporary and fair employment conditions
  • Pleasant, motivated team
