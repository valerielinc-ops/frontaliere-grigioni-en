Full description

Teacher SHP/DAZ Kindergarten, SE Türligarten With 41'000 inhabitants, Chur is the urban center of Graubünden. As an attractive and versatile city, it provides customer-oriented and modern services with its 1,400 employees.

The Chur City School is the largest public school in the canton of Graubünden. Its offer covers all school levels from kindergarten to secondary level I. Special offers are bilingual classes German/Italian and German/Romanian, language integration classes, timeout classes and talent classes Music and Sports at secondary level I. The city school also includes daycare centers and school social work.

The Chur City School teaches more than 3000 students from around 450 teachers in 15 school units. Each school unit is led by a school principal, who together with the school directorate form the overall school management.

The Türligarten School House is one of the smaller primary school houses in Chur and is located on the edge of the old town. In the corresponding 3 kindergartens and 6 primary classes, around 170 children are taught by about 22 teachers.

The professional, targeted and uncomplicated cooperation of all participants is of great importance in the Türligarten school unit. Small but fine, so the school house can be briefly described.

To complement our team for the three kindergarten departments Bodmer and Kornquader 1+2, we are looking for a new one at the beginning of the school year 2027/28 (1 August 2027).
