Full description

Cerchi un'esperienza stimolante presso un datore di lavoro versatile e desideri mettere in pratica le tue conoscenze? La RhB offre interessanti tirocini e, su richiesta, sostiene anche tesi di laurea con riferimento alla pratica. È la tua occasione per unire la teoria alla realtà e ottenere una panoramica dello sviluppo continuo della RhB.

Cosa puoi fare

  • Processing of oral and written customer concerns in customer dialogue
  • Support in quality management and participation in processes in the areas of customer dialogue and rail service, also cross-divisional
  • Deployment at the reception in the administration building (phone center)
  • Advising and selling the premium products Bernina Express and Glacier Express as well as the special offers of RhB in the rail service
  • Service and support services for online customers (by telephone and email)

Ecco perché sei la persona giusta’

  • in training (HF/FH level) in tourism, hospitality or communication
  • Experience in customer service/complaint management is an advantage
  • Service-oriented, passion for guest contact and feeling for the customer
  • Enthusiasm for mobility and public transport
  • digital affinity, good comprehension and eloquent
  • Enjoyment of participation in process improvements

Cosa ti offriamo’

  • 2. Class General Subscription
  • Paid mobile phone and subscription for business and private use
  • Attractive social benefits and pension fund with very good benefits
  • Versatile internship with exciting projects and insight into the entire company
