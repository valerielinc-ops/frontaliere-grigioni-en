Full description

The civil engineering office is responsible for the design, construction, maintenance and operation of the cantonal roads as well as the hydraulic engineering and slow transport in Graubünden. We also take care of the operational maintenance of the national roads on the cantonal territory. The canton’s road infrastructure comprises over 1,450 art buildings, including bridges at airy heights of up to 90 meters and tunnels of up to 3 kilometers in length. The challenges that the nature of our mountain canton brings in summer and winter make our work challenging and versatile. As a Friendly Work Space certified office, the health and satisfaction of our approximately 480 employees are particularly important to us. For this reason, we are committed to systematic occupational health management (BGM).

We are looking for department services as of 1. December 2026 or by arrangement a manager/in land acquisition.

Tasks

  • Management and organisation of the field of land acquisition with two employees
  • Representation of the Civil Engineering Office and the Department of Infrastructure, Energy and Mobility in negotiations and public deeds in connection with the acquisition of land for cantonal road construction
  • Preparation of contracts for land register registrations and adjustments
  • Application for judicial safeguards
  • Examination and assessment of claims for damages
  • Application and organisation of expropriations
  • Responsibility for the measurement and marketing of the completed projects as well as for the updating of the parcel register
  • Support and advice to municipalities and authorities in the field of land acquisition

Requirements

  • Completed training as a real estate trustee with land register management patent or comparable training
  • Professional experience in a similar capacity
  • assertiveness, communication and negotiation skills and high social skills
  • Structured, independent and exact working method
  • Analytical and networked thinking
  • Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is advantageous
