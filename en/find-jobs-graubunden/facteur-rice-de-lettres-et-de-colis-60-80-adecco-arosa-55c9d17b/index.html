Full description

Introduction

Do you like getting up early and looking for a physically challenging activity? Then you fit perfectly with us! With you, tomorrow’s post will keep moving.

Also lateral entrants: insiders are welcome with us!

Tasks

  • Early in the morning (about 06:00) you load the delivery vehicles together with your team and prepare your delivery tour.
  • Through your work, you shape the streetscape of the Arosa region and embody the values of reliability and quality.
  • You are responsible for the punctual and impeccable delivery of packages and letters in your area.
  • On your tour, you answer questions from our customers and contribute to customer satisfaction.
  • You work regularly on Saturdays, with services being split up in the team.

Profile

  • With your physical fitness, you can easily load and lift packages up to 30 kg on a regular basis.
  • You like to be on the road, have the category B driver's license and ideally A1 - or are ready to catch up before starting your job.
  • You appreciate the work in the team, but you also like to travel independently and feel comfortable with vehicles on the road.
  • You are reliable and work fast.
  • Good oral German skills as well as an experienced use of digital devices round off your profile.

Advantages

You benefit from attractive additional services

  • 6 weeks holiday
  • 18 weeks maternity leave and 4 weeks leave for the second parent, participation in family-supplementary childcare
  • Staff vouchers
  • Job in the fresh air
