Full description

We are looking for the secretariat and practice medicine at 1. January 2027 or by agreement

Tasks

Main tasks

  • Administrative processing of inpatients and practice patients (terminal coordination, preparation of medical reports, performance recording, etc.)
  • Organizational skills for a smooth operation of the outpatient practice
  • Coordination and if necessary Assistance in medical-technical examinations (ECG, ergo/spiroergometry, long-term ECG, 24 blood pressure measurement, pacemaker/ICD controls, respiratory polygraphy, nocturnal oximetry, small lung function, echocardiography, ultrasound, etc.)
  • Communication with referring physicians
  • Management of Secretariat staff
  • Active contribution to rational and efficient operations

Requirements

Requirement profile

  • Training as medical/practice assistant (MPA) or equivalent (commercial training with further training as medical secretary)
  • Several years of professional experience in practice and/or secretarial services
  • Competence in computer application
  • Organisational talent
  • Job sharing possible

Our offer

  • Interesting patient spectrum with emphasis in cardiology, pulmonology, gastroenterology and oncology
  • Competent introduction and gradual familiarisation
  • Internal and external training opportunities
  • Regular working hours
  • Good social benefits
  • Pleasant working atmosphere

Under the umbrella «Center da sandà Engiadina Bassa» (CSEB) / «Health Center Unterengadin» key regional providers in the areas of health care and wellness are taking a common path. In the national park region, a wonderful environment awaits you with versatile recreation and sports opportunities.

As a regional hospital, the Ospidal in Lower Engadin is responsible for basic and emergency medical care in the region. It offers state-of-the-art school and complementary medicine and plays a pioneering role in the field of patient safety in Switzerland. The areas of surgery, traumatology, orthopedics, gynecology and obstetrics, internal medicine and palliative care, integrative medicine, anesthesiology and pain therapy including an IMC ward are offered. The hospital has state-of-the-art infrastructure with KIS, RIS, PACS including numerous advanced telemedical solutions as well as a modern surgical tract.
