MÖBELMONTEUR / SCHREINER 60\-100 %

linea R54, based in Chur, designs and furnishes interiors. Depending on the order, the company takes over the interior design, the furnishings or the entire project: from the initial discussion about the concept, material selection and furnishings to delivery, assembly and handover. Customers include private individuals with apartments and holiday homes, hotels and catering companies, companies as well as retirement and care institutions.

For warehousing, delivery and assembly, linea R54 is looking for an independent and careful personality as a furniture installer (m/f/d).

Your tasks

Warehouses and logistics

  • They control the goods receipts, store the goods and keep the warehouse and inventory list.
  • They coordinate the route planning, prepare the vehicles and load furniture, materials and tools.
  • In the camp you ensure order.

Delivery and assembly

  • They deliver independently and assemble high-quality furniture for our customers.
  • If on site something is different than expected, a wall is not in the air or a stairwell is narrower than expected, find a clean solution.
  • They process complaints promptly and carry out repairs professionally.

Clients and Studio

  • On site, you are a friendly and competent contact person for our customers.
  • They coordinate closely with our sales.
  • When we refurbish our Studio Chur, you help shape it.

What you bring

  • A completed basic training, preferably in a craft profession, for example as a carpenter or kitchen fitter
  • Category B driving licence (requirement)
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • A quick understanding and a very careful working method
  • A sense of duty and pleasure in contact with people
  • Flexibility in special operations and short-term changes

What we offer you *

  • Variety between warehouse, vehicle, construction site and studio
  • Projects from the single armchair to the furnishing of an entire hotel
  • Insight into how a project is created from concept to handover

Appropriate remuneration, good social benefits and cooperation based on trust are a matter of course at linea R54.

We look forward to your application.
