Full description

The Regional Court Plessur is a first instance civil and criminal court based in Chur. It is affiliated with the Cantonal Coercive Measures Court and the Intermediary Office with the Arbitration Authority for Rental Matters of the Plessur Region. The business burden is approximately 2,200 cases per year (including cases of the coercive measures court), which is handled administratively by the court office with ten people.

We are looking for the Regional Court Plessur as a result of retirement on 1. January 2027 or by agreement a law firm employee.

Tasks

  • Administrative case management from case receipt to billing and archiving (postal processing, deadline and cost controls, general contact with parties, lawyers and authorities, etc.)
  • Organisation and follow-up of negotiations, hearings and hearings
  • Independent writing of various correspondences, orders of procedure and decisions
  • Telephone and counter services

Requirements

  • Completed basic commercial training (further training in the field of paralegal is advantageous)
  • ideally professional experience in a court, in the profession or in public administration
  • very good knowledge of German, knowledge of Italian advantageous
  • structured, forward-looking, independent and precise working method
  • Team capable, reliable and established personality
  • Readiness to take over the piquetting services
