Full description

True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

Hamilton Storage develops, produces and distributes robots for storing up to ten million chemical substances for pharmaceutical companies. We also produce robots for storing biological samples at -80 degrees Celsius, which are used in biobanks for clinical research purposes.

You can do that

After familiarization with the basics of our products, our development tools and the internal processes, you will test and improve electromechanical components and functions for customer-specific plants and series products in a development team. You ensure that the facilities meet our standards in terms of quality and cost and are in close contact with internal and external suppliers and customers.

Your main tasks include

  • Drive product optimization of biobanking systems to meet the ever-changing market needs
  • Developing, procuring, testing and documenting new electromechanical assemblies according to the Hamilton innovation process in an interdisciplinary project team
  • Creation of requirements, testing and project documentation of improved modules in English
  • Implementation of simulations and evaluation of measurement data and derivation of sound conclusions
  • Creation and improvement of installation, production and service documents
  • Conduct technical discussions with internal and external suppliers and customers
  • Ensuring product quality as well as cost and manufacturing goals in accordance with our high standards

This is what you bring

  • Engineering degree (BSc/MSc) in the field of mechanical engineering, systems engineering or a comparable technical discipline
  • Several years of experience in product development, manufacturing engineering or product lifecycle engineering
  • Experience with MCAD program, preferably Creo Parametric
  • Enthusiasm for robotics, automation technology and innovative technical solutions
  • Creative and open personality with initiative and pleasure in cooperation in interdisciplinary teams
  • Ability to analyse complex issues and present relevant conclusions to an interdisciplinary team in an understandable way and then implement them efficiently
  • Independent, reliable and structured working method
  • Good knowledge of German and English

You can look forward to this

You can expect a versatile role with close proximity to the business and great creative scope. You will have the opportunity to work on highly automated robotics systems for the pharmaceutical and biotech industries, actively developing the team and the organization and shaping sustainable change. You are part of a professional interdisciplinary development team that focuses on cooperation, mutual support and continuous development. Look forward to exciting projects around reliability, innovative technologies and lean management, as well as a wide range of opportunities for professional and personal development.
