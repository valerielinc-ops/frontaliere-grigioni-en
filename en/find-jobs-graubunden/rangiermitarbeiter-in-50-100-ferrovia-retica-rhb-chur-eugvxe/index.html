Full description

Ti piace dare una mano e apprezzi la varietà? Nel nostro team sarai corresponsabile del buon funzionamento di tutto. Il tuo impegno affidabile garantisce il regolare svolgimento delle attività.

Cosa puoi fare

  • Together with your team, you ensure that our passenger and freight trains are ready on time and correctly equipped.
  • Releasing brakes, disconnecting couplings, maneuvering wagons, preparing trains for departure and technical checks on trains are among the countless tasks that will make your day varied
  • With the completed training Kat. A40 you are entitled to carry out shunting trips with our large locomotives
  • You work in shift work, work outdoors and under high safety requirements

Ecco perché sei la persona giusta’

  • You have completed a basic education or bring experience in the artisanal, logistical area
  • Safe knowledge of German in word and writing is mandatory (minor level B)
  • You are willing to learn, have a good understanding and are ready to familiarize yourself with the exciting world of traffic.
  • A family working environment and a responsible, varied activity meet your expectations
  • You have the PW driver's license Kat. B

Cosa ti offriamo’

  • 2. Class General Subscription
  • Free tickets and discounts on rail travel abroad
  • Paid mobile phone and subscription for business and private use
  • Flexible part-time models
  • Attractive social benefits and pension fund with very good benefits
  • Additional purchase of holidays
  • Possibility for internal and external training with financial support from RhB

During our internal training (cat. A40) you will gain the necessary train, station and track knowledge.
