Full description

You want to not only shape products, but really promote them? ####

At an international company, you play a central role in the further development of innovative solutions and products. You work closely with different teams, contribute your ideas and actively shape processes – in a dynamic environment with an international focus and a wide range of challenges.

The main tasks ####

  • Implement epics, features and stories
  • Structuring, prioritizing and detailing backlog
  • Act as a contact person for technical customer inquiries
  • Working closely with project managers, product owners and stakeholders
  • Promote and improve agile principles in the team

The requirement profile ####

  • Completed degree in computer science or engineering
  • Several years of experience as a product owner or as a requirements engineer in the agile environment
  • Sound knowledge of agile software development and requirements engineering
  • Flowing knowledge of German and English
  • Strong communication and moderation skills in the team as well as leadership skills
