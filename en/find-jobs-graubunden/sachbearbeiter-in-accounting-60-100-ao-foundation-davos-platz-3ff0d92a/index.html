Are you a financial juggler with an eye for detail and want to participate in an international environment? Perfect! We provide a platform to unleash your skills and realize your passion for numbers. Welcome to us where we not only keep accounts but also promote personal growth.

Activity

  • Active processing of current accounting including debtor and creditor management, payment processing, account reconciliation etc.
  • Help with monthly and annual accounts and ensure the deputy of other team members.
  • Collaboration in cross-departmental projects.
  • Extension of know-how in Excel and other tools (e.g. Microsoft PowerBI)
  • Detective work: When inconsistencies hide, you are the Sherlock Holmes of accounting.

Requirements

  • You have a commercial education (e.g. commercial apprenticeship or equivalent training such as a commercial diploma or accounting clerk)
  • Multi-year professional experience in accounting and financial accounting
  • A passion for numbers
  • They like challenges, want to continuously optimize processes and processes and like to move in an international environment
  • Very good German language skills in words and writing as well as English language skills complement their profile, whereby development potential may exist, because AO Foundation actively supports English courses
  • A resilient personality with initiative and team orientation with nerves of and one of gold

  • Modern infrastructure
