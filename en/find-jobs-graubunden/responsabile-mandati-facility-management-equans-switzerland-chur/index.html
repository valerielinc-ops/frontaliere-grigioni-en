Full description

We are looking for an independent, leadership and service-oriented person in the Sargans-Chur region, who is developing our Facility Management team with commitment and expertise.

Tasks You lead our Facility Management team and, together with your colleagues, you will experience the diverse operational services in technical and infrastructural building management. You will be responsible for such tasks as:

  • Management and promotion of your team
  • Monitoring of cost and contract compliance
  • Planning and management of work
  • Preparation of analyses and reporting
  • Customer consulting and support
  • Optimization and development of processes and subprocesses
  • Support for acquisition of new man data
  • Enforcement of safety requirements of legislators and operations
  • Ensuring ongoing training and training of your team
  • Technical participation and support in operational operation and maintenance (HLKS/Electro)

Requirements You are a service provider with conviction:in, think with and bring adequate optimization suggestions. Positive feedback encourages you to achieve maximum performance. You also bring the following:

  • Basic technical training in the field of HLKS or electro-advisable
  • Bachelor of Science in Facility Management with technical and commercial background or similar training with relevant multi-year professional experience
  • Professional experience in comparable management position
  • Well-founded business management understanding
  • Very good computer skills (MS Office, ideally Planon CAFM, RTM, SAP Fiori, Baseware)
  • Stylish German knowledge and English of advantage
  • Interest and affinity to real estate, technology and maintenance
  • Joy of team work
  • Engaged, communicative

and flexible personality

  • Expressed customer orientation and responsibility
  • Affinity for numbers
  • Residence in the Sargans-Chur region

About us We are specialized in comprehensive services in the field of facility management for every phase of the life cycle of commercial property and areas. Buildings and infrastructures are complex systems. Whether technical operation, strategic planning or infrastructural services – we ensure maximum availability, increase operational safety and improve the comfort of buildings. This way we minimize costs and create optimal conditions for our customers to concentrate on their core business.

As a company of E Quant Switzerland, the leading comprehensive provider of holistic building and energy solutions in Switzerland, we are also driving the transformation and digitalization of our customers forward and working together at the energy transition.
