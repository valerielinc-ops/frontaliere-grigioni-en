Full description

Start on 1 August 2027 with the four-year vocational apprenticeship Computer Scientist: in 100 - 100%

Field of expertise: Platform development

Computer scientists are in demand professionals – today more than ever. With the four-year basic training in the field of platform development, you lay the perfect foundation for your future in the digital world.

If you want to delve deep into IT, understand systems, develop solutions and take on responsibility in an exciting environment, then you are at the right place. With us you will receive a sound education, a lot of practice and the chance to develop your skills step by step.

These are your tasks

  • You plan, manage and maintain physical and virtual server systems and services
  • You plan local area networks (LAN), select suitable network components, install and configure them and monitor ongoing operations
  • You ensure the security and availability of systems and services as well as the performance of the entire network
  • Ensure that information is protected and can be transferred securely

You bring that with you

  • You bring good grades at secondary school
  • You like to analyze, think networked and find clever solutions
  • You have technical skills and interest in new technological trends
  • You enjoy working in a team and communicate openly and respectfully.
  • You are interested in science subjects and would like to deepen your knowledge further

Can you do something that others can’t? You make the difference? Then you belong to us!
