Full description

Are you a morning person and do you like working outside? Then you are exactly right with us, because Schiers starts the day with a laugh!

Your advantages in the post office.

We offer a fair wage, which ranges between CHF 57’700 and CHF 67’700 per year (100% fee) depending on experience and skills. Also applications of talents with different competencies are welcome, in such cases your wage can be slightly below or above the bandwidth.

With us, you work in a future-oriented, dynamic environment in which you are involved and with which you continue to grow. With us, you can expect various benefits, with which you can flexibly design your everyday life according to your needs.

Holidays

We will send you 6 weeks on holiday.

Family friendly

We offer 18 weeks of fully paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of leave for the second parent. We also contribute to the costs of family-supplemented childcare.

Total employment contract

The collective employment contract protects, strengthens and creates clear rules – for fair employment and working conditions.

Mobility offers

We offer you a free half-tax subscription, a discounted GA or a commuter bonus. You only pay CHF 10 per year at PubliBike or Velospot.

PostMobil and PostFinance

We offer you an attractive employee subscription for CHF 12.95 per month through PostMobile. With PostFinance, you also benefit from numerous advantages, such as a free private account.

Vouchers

We give you up to CHF 500 personal credit, which you can use for all products in the PostShop (including gift cards) as well as for products from SBB and Reka.

You can do that.

  • Early in the morning (about 6 a.m.) you load the delivery vehicles together with your team and prepare your delivery tour.
  • Through your work, you shape the streetscape of the Prättigau region and embody the values of reliability and quality.
  • You are responsible for the punctual and impeccable delivery of packages and letters in your area.
  • On your tour, you answer questions from our customers and contribute to customer satisfaction.
  • You work regularly on Saturdays, with the services divided into the team.

You bring that with you.

  • With your physical fitness, you can easily load and lift packages up to 30 kg.
  • You like to travel, have the driver’s license of category B and ideally A1 (motorcycle up to 125cc) or are ready to catch up before starting your job.
  • You appreciate the work in the team but you also like to travel independently and feel comfortable with large vehicles in road traffic.
  • You are reliable and work fast.
  • Good oral German skills as well as an experienced use of digital devices round off your profile.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Graubünden?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Graubünden's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Graubünden's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
