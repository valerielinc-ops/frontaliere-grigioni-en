Live and work in the Destination Davos Klosters? We look forward to reinforcement! ##STR1##

Their nursing expertise, competent care and planning assistance are appreciated not only by our patients in the acute care unit, but also by our medical team.

As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable, but for the region extremely important company. We maintain a collegial working atmosphere and enjoy the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos.

For our interdisciplinary acute care unit we are looking for agreement

Dipl. HF/FH 60-100% - annual contract Winter 2026/27 and Summer 2027 ##

Your responsibilities

  • Professional care of our patients
  • Independent and patient-oriented work in the shift operation of the acute area (medicine, surgery, orthopedics and obstetrics)
  • Active participation within the team in close interdisciplinary cooperation

Your profile

  • Completed training as a nurse (HF/FH, DNII or AKP)
  • Professional experience desired, preferably with good knowledge in the acute field
  • Flexibility and resilience with high social skills and responsibility
  • empathy and patient-oriented thinking and action

Our offer

  • Varied and responsible task in a collegial team
  • Attractive employment conditions and benefits
  • Staff house available
  • Personal development with interesting internal training opportunities
  • Plenty of sports and leisure activities in the highest city in Europe

For further information, Ms. Bea Heeb, Head of Department Care and Cross-Disciplines, is at your disposal, as well as our HR recruitment team under the number or by e-mail.
