Housekeeper/100% With 41,000 inhabitants, Chur is the urban center of Graubünden. As an attractive and versatile city, it provides customer-oriented and modern services with its 1,400 employees. With almost 170 employees in the Projects/Construction Maintenance, Management and Facility Services departments, the Real Estate and Management department is responsible for managing the municipal properties in the financial and administrative assets and for the residential development cooperative of the city of Chur.

In order to strengthen the Facility Services department, a new, committed team member will be looking for:

Housekeeper/100%
