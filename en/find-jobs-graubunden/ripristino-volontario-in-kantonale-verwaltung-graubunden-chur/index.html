Volunteer restorer — Kantonale Verwaltung Graubünden

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

As of January 1, 2027, we are looking for a committed and reliable person for a volunteering in the field of conservation/restoration (80%) for the duration of one year. Due to the renovation and expansion of the Cultural Property Protection Facility, the entire collection will be temporarily transferred to an external art camp at the beginning of 2028. As part of the volunteer program, you support us in particular in the conservation and organizational preparation of the works of art for outsourcing.

Tasks

  • Conservatory and restorational investigation of works of art in the context of depot relocation
  • Preparation of state protocols and photographic documentation
  • Determination of suitable types of packaging for transport and professional packaging of works of art
  • Organisational cooperation in the preparation and implementation of depot outsourcing
  • Properly plan and carry out restorations

Requirements

  • Completed university degree in conservation and restoration (Bachelor or Master)
  • Practical experience in the conservation and restoration of works of art
  • Safe and careful handling of works of art
  • Independent, reliable and structured working method
  • Readiness for cooperation and professional exchange in the team
