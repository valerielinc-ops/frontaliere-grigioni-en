Full description

The Karlihof Medical Center in Malans GR offers outpatient primary medical care with integrated specialists from various disciplines. The team currently comprises 16 doctors and 18 employees, who together ensure a broad range of treatment for the holistic and interdisciplinary care of patients. In particular, the center has specialized medical services in the fields of gynecology, orthopedics and traumatology, neurology, angiology and oncology. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an MED. PRAXISASISTENT/PRAXISASISTTENTENT 100% You can expect a varied and versatile activity in a motivated environment with modern structures and development opportunities.

Their tasks

  • Office hours organisation incl. Reception and care of patients
  • Assistance in medical treatments (e.g. wound care, bandage change, blood sampling, lung function test)
  • Working in the laboratory, on the digital X-ray and support of the practice pharmacy
  • Administrative activities (ordering, performance recording, billing and insurance clearance)

Your profile

  • Completed training as MPA (EFZ), with laboratory and X-ray skills
  • Good computer skills
  • Kind, empathetic and well-groomed appearance
  • Resilience and teamwork ability – even in hectic situations You have the overview
  • Motivation to actively participate in the further development of our center

We offer you

  • An appreciative and collegial working atmosphere
  • A modern, well-equipped workplace
  • Qualified training and support by a motivated team
  • Varied tasks with development possibilities
  • 5 weeks holiday
