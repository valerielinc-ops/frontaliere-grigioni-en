Full description

Company Hamilton AG

True to the vision -We drive innovation to improve people-s lives- Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

The Robotics Business Unit develops, produces and distributes pipetting robots to automate liquid handling processes for pharmaceuticals, food industry and cell or DNA research.

*You can do it *

  • Management of projects of varying complexity in customer-specific plant construction within the area of liquid handling/laboratory automation
  • Leading project teams in a matrix organization
  • Development of the project plan
  • Coordination of tasks and responsibilities
  • Responsibility to monitor and comply with the project quality, time and cost
  • Interface between internal development and external project stakeholders (sales manager, supplier, field project leader, application specialists and customer)
  • Supporting the sales team during the pre-sales phase and requirements acquisition
  • Preparation of effort estimates and tenders

You bring that with you

  • Completed technical or scientific studies (Bsc or Msc)
  • Professional experience in the field of customer-specific plant construction or the automated life science area is an advantage
  • This position is also suitable as an entry point for an applicant with completed vocational apprenticeship (technical or scientific) and at least. 5 years of professional experience
  • Further development, i.e. Certification in Project Management desirable or the willingness to complete this further education
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Independent, exact working method with a quick understanding
  • Flexible, initiative and creative personality
