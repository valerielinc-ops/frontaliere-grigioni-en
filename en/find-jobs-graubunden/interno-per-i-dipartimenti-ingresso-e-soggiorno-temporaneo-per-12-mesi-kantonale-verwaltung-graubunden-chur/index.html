Full description

The Office for Migration and Civil Law currently has around 230 employees at more than 20 locations and is responsible for the entire area of responsibility in the area of migration, i.e. in particular granting and withdrawing permits, accommodation and support, executing expulsions and integration. In addition, ID cards are issued to Swiss nationals, naturalisations are made and supervision of the civil status authorities is exercised. The headquarters of the Office of Migration and Civil Law is in Chur.

We are looking for an intern immediately or by arrangement to support our two departments of entry and stay. The position is limited to 12 months (shorter assignments possible by agreement).

As an intern, you will have the opportunity to gain practical and in-depth insights into the foreigner permit system. They support the ministries in administrative and organizational tasks and get to know the various processes from the application to the approval or any foreigner law measures.

The internship offers you the opportunity to gain valuable professional experience in a versatile and demanding administrative environment and to gain a comprehensive insight into the activities of the departments of entry and stay.

Tasks

  • Support for employees of the departments Entry and stay in day-to-day business
  • Collection, examination and maintenance of data and documents incl. Post processing and scanning
  • Processing of applications under foreign national law (L, G, B, C permits) incl. Online business
  • Correspondence (mail and telephone) and general administrative work
  • Research work
  • Cooperation with internal and external bodies

Requirements

  • Age of majority and completed maturity (gymnasial, commercial or technical)
  • Rapid comprehension, resilience, teamwork
  • Good computer skills
  • Self-employment, flexibility and discretion
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is advantageous
