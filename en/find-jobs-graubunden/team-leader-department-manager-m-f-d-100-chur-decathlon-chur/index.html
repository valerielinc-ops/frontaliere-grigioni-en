Full description

Mission

Are you looking for a job where you don't spend half your life behind a desk? Do you dream of supporting a team every day and achieving goals together? Would you like to be the boss of your own small business without taking on the risk of being a sole proprietor? Then the position as Team Leader in Chur is just right for you.

Why?

  • Start date : Immediately
  • Standort : Chur
  • Salary range : CHF 5’350 - 6’800 (depending on professional experience)
  • Store size : approx. 2’500 m²
  • Employees : 15 and 25 people
  • Workload : 100%
  1. You spend 80% of your time on the sales floor in the Store

Your department is your territory. Whether you spend time with your team, ensure that the merchandising standards are in place, or directly delight our customers. You don't sit on the bench. Strategy is important, but we're looking for a hands-on personality for the sales floor.

  1. Your success is your team's success

Your job isn't to control everything, but to make everything possible. A team member is passionate about their sport and has an innovative idea for the department in the Store? Let them run with it! Success or failure? Doesn't matter. The main thing is that you learn together and grow from it.

  1. You are in the driver's seat

Here, you run your department like your own start-up. From precise inventory planning through in-depth KPI analysis to recruiting your talents and commercial strategy. You are firmly in the driver's seat. In Schaffhausen, you run your business independently and do everything you can not only to achieve your goals, but to exceed them.

What a Team Leader does here

Your mission is simple: The success of your department – both in terms of people and business.

  • Store Life: You coordinate the flow of goods, lend a hand, and, as “Captain,” are regularly the point of contact for the entire store.
  • People Management: You hold monthly coaching conversations (INDI), create staff schedules, and seek out the best talent for your team.
  • Strategy: You analyze your metrics and plan the next big thing. Which sports will we push next season? How do we further develop your team's skills?
  • Business Ownership: P&L and KPI analyses are not dry theory for you, but your daily tools. You run your department like your own company and make bold decisions for greater profitability.

What we offer you

  • Responsibility from day 1 – we and your team trust you completely.
  • Career without limits – from Team Leader to Store Manager, Ecosystem Leader. Here, neither you nor your ambition has any limits.
  • 30 days of vacation – so you have enough time for your own sporting adventures.
  • Profit sharing & shares – You have the exclusive opportunity to invest up to 25% of your gross salary in company shares and thus participate directly in our growth.
  • Sports & Mobility Bonus – we get you moving.
  • Team Bonus - Together with your team, you have the chance to earn a monthly bonus of up to 10% of your gross salary.
  • A kick-start to family happiness – Because the first moments are priceless, we support you with 16 weeks of maternity leave as you embark on your greatest adventure away from the sales floor.

Profile

Is this job really right for you? Let's be honest - the perfect job without any rough edges doesn't exist. Our teams will tell you what you really need:

  • #1: Love of people - If you don't enjoy communicating with people, this isn't for you. You have to love your team and want to support it.
  • #2: Courage to take risks - We want proactivity. You may make mistakes, but you have to dare to jump in at the deep end.
  • #3: Sports DNA - You don't just sell products, you sell passion.
  • #4: Winner's mentality - We love competition and business performance. You should want not only to achieve goals, but to chase them.
  • #5: Language match - German and English are not an obstacle for you, but your home advantage. You are proficient in both languages (German C1, English B2) to inspire our international team and our athletes every day.
  • #6: Retail foundation - You learned the trade from the ground up. With your training in retail (or a comparable qualification), you know the rules of the game and bring the necessary skills to make the decisive difference on the sales floor.
  • #7: Saturday spirit - Saturdays are our final games. While others relax, you perform at your best. Because on weekends, the heart of sport beats loudest, and as a team we are there for our community.

Your path to us

Don't feel like writing complicated cover letters? Show us your potential!

  • After your application, you will receive an online assessment. To be on the safe side, please also check your spam folder. The questionnaire is intended to give us an idea of how you work. There is no right or wrong answer. Please complete it to continue the process
  • Digital start: As soon as your profile has sparked our interest, Jana (Talent Acquisition Partner) will get in touch to talk to you via video call about you and your professional background.
  • Personal meeting: Then it's straight to the front line! You'll meet our Hiring Manager on site in the store.
  • Strategy check: After the Hiring Manager's “Go”, you'll have a discussion with our Ecosystem Leader, who will explain Decathlon's regional context to you.
  • Finish line: You've almost made it! The final interview with our HR Business Partner confirms your start with us.

Are you ready not just to manage, but to inspire? Then start your adventure at Decathlon Chur now!
