Role overview

Nella tua Coastore/costruttrice di binari contribuisci in modo alla puntualità e alla sicurzza del traffico ferroviario nei Grigioni. Lavori con la tua forza fisica o lasci Che siano macchine all'avanguardia a svolgerekito lavoro per te. L'apprendistato per adulti, Che realizzamo insieme al nostro partner di formazione login, ti consente di affrontare il prosssimo passo professionale in un??e affidabile.

Tasks

  • You work safely and precisely on secured tracks and while running the railway
  • You replace existing tracks or switches with your calculations and good planning with millimetre accuracy
  • The idea of working only with a hack and shovel is outdated. Supported by state-of-the-art track construction machines, you continue to build the rail network, control it with precise measuring devices and keep it up with small machines and equipment

Requirements

  • You are a real team player and like to be outside in wind and weather
  • track builders can rely on each other, because team spirit is just as important as safety regulations
  • you like to be outdoors and on our route network
  • you are motivated to learn something new and to take your next professional step

We offer

  • An advanced pay system (CHF 3900.- in the first year of apprenticeship, CHF 4100.- in the second year of apprenticeship, CHF 4300.- in the third year of apprenticeship, including 13th month's salary)
  • 2nd class General subscription
  • 30 days holidays
  • employee discounts
  • Various career opportunities after apprenticeship

Contact
