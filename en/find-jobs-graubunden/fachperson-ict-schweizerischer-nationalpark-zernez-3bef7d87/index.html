ICT specialist

Expert ICT (m/f/d) at the Swiss National Park

The Swiss National Park was founded in 1914 as the first national park in Central Europe to preserve a piece of untouched nature, observe its development and show its value. Today it is a nationally and internationally recognized nature reserve and in its form unique in Switzerland and the Alpine region. The goals of the National Park form a triad: human-unaffected development of nature, research into the processes taking place and information for visitors, with research and information oriented towards the conservation goals. In this context, the National Park also contributes to the sustainable development of the region.

Your tasks

As part of our small ICT\ team, you ensure the reliable, secure and sustainable operation of our ICT\ infrastructure. These include:

Ensuring data security and integrity of our data center

\- Maintenance and operation of the Linux\ and Windows\ server landscape as well as technical support of laptops and smartphones

\- Implementation, operation and development of the Microsoft\-365\ environment

Maintenance, operation and expansion of network infrastructure and security (especially Fortinet, HP ProCurve, Netgear)

\- Operation and further development of the XEN\-based virtualization environment

\- Operation of our ownCloud\ environment and other ICT\ services – both on the intranet and for externally accessible offers

First and second level support for all our employees as well as ICT support for the visitor centre and decentralized ICT components

Integration and support of a smaller Apple-based infrastructure

\- Cooperation with those responsible for the spatial information infrastructure and taking into account their specific requirements

You not only ensure the stability of the ongoing operation, but you are committed to a long-term and clever strategy and further development.

Your profile

You have a completed vocational apprenticeship or a university degree in computer science or an equivalent education and bring professional experience in several of the mentioned areas.

You are a person who

\- is characterized by a pronounced service\ and user orientation

\- quickly integrates into existing systems and complex relationships and keeps an overview even in a heterogeneous environment

\- works independently, solution-oriented and responsible and at the same time maintains the coordination in the team and with the superiors

\- also reacts pragmatically to short-term problems and keeps an eye on the stability of the overall system

\- recognizes potential for improvement, considers changes, tests them beforehand and then implements them stepwise

\- Enjoy a thematically diverse environment and have the willingness to constantly familiarize themselves with new questions

\- is communicative, proactive and open and has a strong team capability

\- has very good oral and written German\ and English skills

\- A flair for spatial data and an understanding of spatial data infrastructures are beneficial

An exciting task in a diverse working environment.
