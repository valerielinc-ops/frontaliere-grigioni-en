Full description

The Office for Prisoners (AJV) Graubünden is a department of the Department of Justice, Security and Health of the Canton of Graubünden. The AJV is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the implementation of the various types of detention. In Graubünden, the execution of penalties and measures is thus regulated as a uniform topic and holistic task, in which different professions work interdisciplinary with people who have become delinquent in order to prevent further delinquency.

Tasks

  • Supervision, supervision and management of instructed persons from different cultural areas within the daily structures
  • Coordination and ensuring the smooth running of the individual day-to-day execution of the Special Implementation Group of 20 instructed persons
  • Co-creation of everyday life on residential groups
  • Reference work within defined responsibilities
  • The focus in execution setting is the risk and resocialization orientation according to validated concepts (e.g. ROS) as well as activation, motivation work and personal development of the instructed persons.
  • Writing rapports and documenting events
  • Ensure and coordinate an orderly operation taking into account the legal basis as well as enforcement and safety-related requirements and rules
  • Early, late and weekend services

Requirements

  • Completed training as a dipl. Nursing specialist HF – field of psychiatry
  • First work experience in a forced context is beneficial
  • Professional experience accompanying people with stressful biographies as well as mental impairments and/or addictions.
  • You see dealing with people from different cultures who are in difficult life situations as a positive challenge.
  • Strong, conflict-prone, physically and psychologically resilient personality with high social competence
  • Enforcement, segregation and willingness to cooperate constructively in a coercive context
  • Enjoying teamwork and interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Good written and oral expression in German (at least C1); additional knowledge of cantonal and foreign languages is an advantage
  • Cat driving licence B
  • Readiness to complete the necessary training
  • An impeccable reputation (no entries in the criminal record and the enforcement record)
