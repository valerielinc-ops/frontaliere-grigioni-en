Specialist: in network economy \& Regulatory Accounting 80\-100 %

We are looking for an immediately or by agreement

Repower is one of the leading energy companies in Switzerland. The 120-year history of Repower begins in the very south of Graubünden in Valposchiavo. In addition to the headquarters in Poschiavo, we have further locations in Landquart, Zurich, Ilanz, Küblis, Disentis, Grono and Bever. As an employer, Repower relies on initiative and trust to promote the creativity of its approximately 730 employees. The energy industry of tomorrow needs fresh ideas. We offer you an inspiring working world in which you and your team develop future-oriented solutions for a sustainable energy future.

The asset management network and supply consists of approximately 15 employees and is responsible for the strategic planning, control and optimization of the network and supply infrastructure throughout the entire life cycle in order to ensure supply security, efficiency and regulatory compliance. The employees work at the locations Landquart, Küblis, Bever, Ilanz and Poschiavo.

You will do this

  • Ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements in the area of grid economy
  • Analyze regulatory requirements and derive the need for action
  • Creation of evaluations, analyses and decision-making basis for management and departments
  • Further development and optimization of reporting, analysis and accounting processes
  • Collaborating or managing regulatory and cross-divisional projects
  • Advising internal departments and working with external partners
  • Cooperation in the implementation of regulatory requirements as well as the creation and further development of network cost accounting in the service business.

You bring that with you

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship in the electrical sector with further training for / to the Dipl. Technician: in HF or university degree (FH/uni) in electrical engineering, energy engineering, industrial engineering or comparable discipline
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of network economy, regulatory accounting or controlling are advantageous
  • Knowledge of regulatory framework conditions in the energy environment is an advantage
  • Analytical thinking and structured and independent working methods
  • Very good IT\ user knowledge and affinity to data and processes
  • Team-oriented personality with initiative and sense of responsibility
