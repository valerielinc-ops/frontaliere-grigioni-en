Plastic Design Engineer (m/f/d \| 80\-100%)

Are you ready to innovate to improve the lives of millions of people?

You think one step further, see opportunities where others see limits – and want to invest your energy in something meaningful? Then you are exactly right at INTEGRA.

Our solutions are not created by chance – they are the result of passion, precision and team spirit. With every technology we develop, we help people around the world research diseases, make diagnoses and enable new therapies. Whether in diagnostics, research or quality control: Our instruments “Made in Switzerland & USA” make laboratory work more efficient, safer and more sustainable.

What you do with us moves the world.

Sounds like what you’re looking for? Then become part of our team – and help accelerate scientific discoveries.

What you can do *

  • Development and construction of components and assemblies up to series maturity
  • Development of mechanical plastic solutions for our liquid handling products, in particular consumables and their integration into complete systems
  • Selection and evaluation of materials, manufacturing processes and design concepts
  • Technical support for design, material, manufacturing and tool topics
  • Preparation of the necessary production documents incl. Drawings
  • Procurement of components and materials for functional samples and prototypes
  • Close cooperation in the interdisciplinary project team as well as with external suppliers and partners

What you bring

  • Completed basic technical training as a constructor, polymechanic or equivalent
  • Studies in mechanical engineering, plastics engineering or comparable at FH/HF level
  • Professional experience in development and construction
  • Profound experience with plastic components and injection molding technology is advantageous
  • Good CAD\ knowledge, ideally SolidWorks
  • Hands on mentality and enthusiasm for creative solutions and innovative products
  • Good communication skills in German and English

Why INTEGRA / Your Benefits

Your new working environment \- INTEGRA Campus

Welcome to our brand new corporate campus in Zizers – 30,000 m2 of concentrated high tech power, sustainability and Alpine panorama. The building envelope, photovoltaic systems and a smart climate system are designed in such a way that we reduce our ecological footprint to a minimum.

You can look forward to it

  • State\-of\-the\-art workspaces - light-flooded spaces, ergonomic workstations and think\-tanks for creative brainstorming, all with a view of the Grisons mountains.
  • La Pipetta – our in-house restaurant offers daily fresh, discounted menus
  • In the sun, the large terrace attracts you to refuel.
  • Easy arrival – only 3 minutes from the A13, free parking, direct connection to public transport and e-charging stations.
