Full description

The Office of Agriculture and Geoinformation is a department of the Department of Economics and Social Affairs. It comprises the four departments of agricultural measures, structural improvements, surveying and the GIS Competence Center. Tasks

  • Responsibility for the correct accounting of biodiversity and landscape quality contributions
  • Participation in the further development of the agricultural data system LAWIS Plus as well as in technical and technical tasks with the geographical information system ArcGIS Pro
  • Specialised training and support for external specialised offices Requirements
  • Completed studies at a university or university of applied sciences in agronomy or environmental sciences or a degree as an agrotechnician with professional experience
  • In-depth experience with GIS applications, ideally ArcGIS, as well as a high affinity for digital specialist applications and processes
  • Understanding agricultural concerns and the ability to develop practical solutions taking into account agricultural production, enforcement and biodiversity promotion
  • Teamwork, communication strength as well as stylish and addressable mediation of specialist topics
  • Analytical and networked thinking
  • Independent, structured and initiative working methods and organizational skills
