Live and work in the Destination Davos Klosters? We look forward to reinforcement! ##STR1##

In our team, you will receive an insight into our broad spectrum of disciplines as a sub-assistant and expand your expertise with your practical cooperation.

As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable company, but important for the region, we maintain a collegial working atmosphere and appreciate the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos.

Sub assistant: in anesthesia 100% ##

Your responsibilities

You will get the following insights

  • You will work full time and thus gain a profound knowledge of the daily routine
  • Specialised examination and assessment of patients
  • Preparation of the patient for anesthesia
  • Assisting in anesthesia initiation / maintenance / discharge

Your profile

  • Student: in human medicine
  • Knowledge-hungry, positioned young personalities who enjoy working in a team and are interested in learning more about how a regional hospital operates

Our offer

Students actively participate in the daily workflow of the clinic. This includes activities in the department, office hours and operating room, daily participation in reports and training events. After familiarization, a good and broad insight into the field of anesthesiology is thus possible.

For further information, the Department of Surgery & Orthopaedics is at your disposal by telephone or e-mail.
