Chef de Rang (m/f/d) Flims Chef de Rang (m/f/d) as of December or by agreement Pensum: 100% from December or by agreement Hosting is in your blood and service quality is more than a standard for you? Then you are exactly right with us. Our traditional family business stands for warmth, passion and personal responsibility. With us, not only professional competence counts, but above all personality – an open appearance, joy in dealing with people and the proverbial heart in the right place. Our guests are as diverse as our everyday life: from marriage proposals and weddings to incentive travelers to family holidays. We don't get bored. In the summer and winter season, you work together with around 50 dedicated and charming hosts. Several times in succession we were voted the 75 best winter hotels and the 100 best holiday hotels in Switzerland. We see these awards as an incentive to continuously question and redefine our service. Our goal is to always surprise our guests with a real WOW moment. Their tasks In this position, you take responsibility for your own service station and together with the team ensure a first-class guest experience.

  • Independent management of a service station in half-board and à-la-carte service
  • Professional, warm care and advice of guests
  • Expert advice on food, drinks and wines
  • Guidance and support of Commis de Rang and learners
  • Ensuring a smooth service flow and compliance with service standards
  • Collaboration at banquets, weddings and events
  • Preparation and follow-up (Mise en Place) of the service area
  • Compliance with hygiene and quality guidelines (HACCP)

That sets them apart.

  • Warm host personality with charisma and real pleasure in the profession
  • Training and/or experience in gastronomy
  • Very good knowledge of German, other languages are an advantage
  • Positive, open and friendly personality
  • Team player with creativity and calm – even in lively situations

Why you should come to us in the Schweizerhof

  • We are a dynamic, international hotel with over a hundred years of family tradition – here experience and heart meet modern hospitality.
  • An attractive workplace awaits you in the dreamlike destination Flims Laax Falera, with countless leisure opportunities right on the doorstep.
  • Benefit from exciting benefits and discounts in-house as well as special conditions for overnight stays at our partners of Romantik Hotels International and Swiss Historic Hotels.
  • Experience a family working atmosphere characterized by respect, appreciation and a strong team spirit.
  • They come into an intact, well-established team with long-standing hosts, can at the same time reunite and are supported by all sides.
  • We promote your personal and professional development through targeted further education opportunities and training.
  • All hosts are welcome to participate in our active weekly programs (YOGA, Pilates, Shinrin Yoku...) and can use the indoor pool for relaxation in the evening.
  • On Sundays, the restaurant remains closed (high season excluded), which allows for attractive and guaranteed Fridays. Of course, you have two free days per week.
