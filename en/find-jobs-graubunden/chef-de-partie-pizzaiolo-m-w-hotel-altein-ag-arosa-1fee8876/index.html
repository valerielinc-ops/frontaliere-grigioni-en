Full description

Become part of the Hotel Altein and the Alpensand Panorama Restaurant, Casual Italian Cuisine and come to Arosa.

Your tasks

  • Preparation of pizza according to traditional Italian recipes and according to high quality standards.
  • Operation and maintenance of the wood pizza oven.
  • Production of pizza dough, sauces and toppings, as well as their daily freshness control.
  • Creatively design new pizza creations taking into account seasonal and local ingredients.
  • Compliance with hygiene and safety standards in accordance with HACCP guidelines.
  • Cooperation with the kitchen team and active support for general kitchen tasks.
  • Care and cleaning of work utensils and surfaces after each shift.

Your profile

  • Completed training as a chef or pizzaiolo
  • Several years of experience as Pizzaiolo, ideally in an Italian restaurant.
  • Knowledge of wood pizza ovens.
  • Passion for Italian cuisine and an eye for detail.
  • Teamwork, flexibility and resilience, especially in stressful situations.
  • Good knowledge of German or English
  • Knowledge of Italian is beneficial.
  • A high degree of reliability and cleanliness.

This is what we offer you

  • A workplace in a beautiful region
  • A seasonal position in a motivated team
  • A high degree of personal responsibility and independence
  • Staff rate for overnight stays at all Faern Resorts
  • Free use of the fitness and wellness area
  • Various other benefits and special conditions in the hotel

Do you feel addressed? So we look forward to your electronic application by e-mail.
