Team leader: in facilities network Nord Surselva 100%

Repower is one of the leading energy companies in Switzerland. The 120-year history of Repower begins in the very south of Graubünden in Valposchiavo. In addition to the headquarters in Poschiavo, we have further locations in Landquart, Zurich, Ilanz, Küblis, Disentis, Grono and Bever. As an employer, Repower relies on initiative and trust to promote the creativity of its approximately 730 employees. Because the energy industry of tomorrow needs fresh ideas. We offer you an inspiring working world in which you and your team develop future-oriented solutions for a sustainable energy future.

The "Network Nord Surselva" team consists of about 20 employees and is distributed at the Ilanz and Disentis sites. The team is responsible for the construction, inspection and maintenance of production and network facilities in our distribution network.

You will do this *

  • Planning, coordination and monitoring of the maintenance of our facilities and ensuring fast and safe troubleshooting
  • Implementation of repairs and renewals as well as decommissioning in close cooperation and coordination with the internal departments
  • Participation in projects and contribution of operational requirements and regional framework conditions
  • Take over the personnel and professional leadership of the two teams in Disentis and Ilanz and promote the professional and personal development of the employees. You also help recruit new employees.
  • Ensuring compliance with safety regulations and legal requirements, implementing internal processes (IMS) measures and promoting an active safety culture
  • Continuous optimization of workflows and efficient and cost-conscious use of resources

You bring that with you

  • Completed vocational training as a grid electrician:in EFZ, electrical installer:in EFZ or equivalent qualification
  • Completed further training as a net specialist (Federal vocational examination) or equivalent training
  • Further training as a master grid electrician: in or an equivalent higher vocational training of advantage
  • Several years of professional experience (minimum 3-5 years) and at least 3 years of experience in a management function
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Romance skills are beneficial. Further language skills are welcome.
  • Independent, communicative and responsible personality with a strong professional and organizational thinking ability
  • Willingness to travel regularly (about 1-2 days per week)
