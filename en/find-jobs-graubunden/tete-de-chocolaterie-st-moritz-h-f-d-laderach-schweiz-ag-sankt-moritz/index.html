Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family-owned company today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. The “Retail” division comprises more than 250 chocolateries in more than 30 countries. Here we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You lead, motivate and develop a dedicated team and live an open feedback culture
  • You plan the personnel deployment in shift operations and actively participate in the recruitment process.
  • Ensure efficient, smooth branch operations and compliance with the hygiene and freshness concept
  • You are responsible for sales, key figures, billing and achieving store goals
  • You analyze key figures and, together with regional management, derive targeted measures for the further development of the location
  • You will advise customers competently and support them in the selection from our diverse range of confiserie and chocolate products
  • You provide an attractive presentation of goods as well as creative shop design and ensure the availability of goods through ordering
  • You are responsible for the proper handling of the cashier processes and a correct billing

You bring that with you

  • You bring several years of sales and leadership experience in the retail trade and are characterized by a high level of social competence and a strong leader.
  • You have successfully completed an apprenticeship in the retail trade and/or comparable experience in dealing with food
  • You ideally have first experience in dealing with chocolate
  • You always put the highest quality and customer orientation at the top, even in turbulent times
  • You like to take on responsibility, think proactively in the team and promote positive cooperation
  • You have in-depth IT knowledge and enjoy working independently and solution-oriented
  • You value open and respectful communication and master German, English and Italian safely
  • You are not afraid of flexible working hours and are also ready to take responsibility on weekends or holidays

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Attractive sales premium for joint achievement of goals
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate in the workplace
  • Good accessibility of locations

Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh.

Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Elena Marian, Regional Sales Manager, elena.marian@laderach.com, will be happy to help you with any questions.

We ask for your understanding that we do not consider external recruitment agencies when filling this position.
