Full description

The Office for Imprisonment (AJV) Graubünden is a department of the Department of Justice, Security and Health of the Canton of Graubünden. The AJV is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the implementation of the various types of detention. In Graubünden, the execution of penalties and measures is thus regulated as a uniform topic and holistic task, in which different professions work interdisciplinary with people who have become delinquent in order to prevent further delinquency.

The prison (JVA) Realta serves the open prison of men according to the Swiss Penal Code.

We are looking for an employee/in support and security service by agreement

Tasks

  • Supervision, supervision and guidance of instructors from different cultural areas
  • Direct contact person for questions and concerns of the assigned persons
  • Ensure and coordinate an orderly operation taking into account the legal basis as well as enforcement and safety-related requirements and rules
  • Writing rapports and documenting events
  • Early, day, late, night and weekend services

Requirements

  • Completed min. three-year apprenticeship with EFZ or equivalent training
  • Balanced, resilient and conflictable personality with high social competence
  • Enjoying teamwork as well as interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Interest in socio-psychological questions and problems in the prison system are indispensable
  • Very good knowledge of German (at least C1) – knowledge of foreign languages is an advantage
  • Good computer user knowledge
  • Cat driving licence B
  • Readiness to undergo prison training
  • An impeccable reputation (no entries in the criminal record and the enforcement record)
