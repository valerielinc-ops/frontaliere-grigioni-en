This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

On behalf of the municipalities, the cantonal welfare office performs the task of personal social and addiction assistance and supports people in the development of economic social assistance. It provides eight regional social services in the regions of the canton. The regional social service Prättigau/Herrschaft/Five villages is responsible for 18 municipalities with a catchment area of 43 000 people.

Social Advice, Addiction and Financial Support (gr.ch)

We are looking for the Landquart location as of 1. January 2027 or by arrangement a social worker.

Tasks

  • Solution- and resource-oriented social consulting of individuals and families in difficult life situations on polyvalent issues
  • Advice for people with addiction and their relatives
  • Advice and financial support of persons with protection status S from Ukraine in individual accommodation
  • Development of social security benefits and economic assistance
  • Cooperation with social authorities and specialised bodies
  • Other versatile, interesting and responsible activities

Requirements

  • Completed training as a social worker/in BSc/FH or a degree in comparable training
  • Enjoy working with people from different social and/or cultural backgrounds
  • High consulting competence as well as in-depth expertise in addiction counselling, social assistance law and social insurance
  • Sociable, resilient, capable of teamwork, good communicative, organizational and administrative skills
  • Ideally good foreign language skills
  • Independent and solution-oriented work
