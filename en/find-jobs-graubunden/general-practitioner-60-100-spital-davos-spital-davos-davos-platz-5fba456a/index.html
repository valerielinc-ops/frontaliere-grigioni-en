Live and work in the Destination Davos Klosters? We look forward to reinforcement! ##STR1##

With its family practice in Davos, Spital Davos AG makes an important contribution to ensuring health care in the region and supports patients as a reliable partner for all types of acute and chronic health problems with a holistic and modern internal medicine.

This enables family doctors to collaborate interprofessionally and opens up interesting horizons in modern integrated care.

We are looking for an agreement for our GP practice:n

Family doctor (60-100%) ##

Your responsibilities

  • Independent performance of general medical consultation hours, care of acute clinical pictures incl. Emergencies and long-term care of chronically ill patients
  • Close cooperation with a motivated MPA team and the Davos Hospital

Your profile

  • Completed specialist training General Internal Medicine FMH or equivalent foreign title (recognized MEBEKO) and ideally already experience in family practice
  • Willingness and joy to engage and shape the expansion and further development of the practice centre
  • Stylish-confident communication in German, very good English skills desired, every other language is advantageous
  • You see your professional and private future in the attractive destination Davos Klosters

Our offer

  • A professional, dynamic and family team is waiting for you
  • Modern practice infrastructure with wide POCT laboratory, digital X-ray, ultrasound and practice software Ametiq
  • Great scope for design and participation in the strategic orientation of the practice centre
  • Flexible workloads and working time models according to agreement and requirements
  • Versatile training opportunities - Davos is a congress city for medical congresses
  • Living and working in a year-round attractive region with extensive leisure opportunities

Further information is available to you Dr. med. Daniel Ribeiro, Chief Physician and Head of the Department of Internal Medicine at Spital Davos AG, by phone or mail. Our Head of HRM, Gregor Bienz, is also available to you on tel. as well as by e-mail as an interlocutor.
