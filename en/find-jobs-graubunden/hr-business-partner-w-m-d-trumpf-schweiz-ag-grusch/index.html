Full description

As a family-run, high-tech company with nearly 19,000 employees at 71 locations worldwide, we are looking for forward thinkers with unconventional ideas and drive to join our team. Our company culture, which values ​​collaboration and mutual trust, creates the ideal framework for boldly trying new things and questioning the status quo. Our technologies inspire people to develop and produce things that are currently unimaginable. Whether lasers, machine tools, EUV or electronics - TRUMPF is building technological worlds for future generations. Are you ready for new challenges?

Your tasks

  • Advice and support for managers on all HR-relevant topics
  • Support and advice for employees in the area of responsibility across the entire HR life cycle
  • Initiate, support and lead to the success of change and organizational development processes
  • Initiation, support and optimization of the annual HR processes
  • Management of/participation in various HR projects, sometimes across locations with other TRUMPF companies and the headquarters

What we are looking for

  • Completed further training in human resources (minimum level of federal specialist certificate) or (technical) university degree with a specialization in human resources
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in a comparable position
  • Stylish communication skills in German and English
  • Dynamic, emphatic and team player personality
  • Proactive. Solution-oriented and reliable way of working, high level of independence and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Ideally Workday knowledge (CRM)

What we offer

  • Modern working environment
  • Early assumption of responsibility and varied tasks
  • Extensive training and further education offerings
  • Respectful and appreciative corporate culture
  • Various employee benefits
  • Work where others go on vacation
