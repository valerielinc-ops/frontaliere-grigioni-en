Controller (80-100%) With 41,000 inhabitants, Chur is the urban center of Graubünden. As an attractive and versatile city, it provides customer-oriented and modern services with its 1,400 employees.

The Department of Finance, Taxation and Resident Services is responsible for the proper management of the financial budget. Accounting and controlling, tax assessments and the management of the residents and business register of the city of Chur.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for 1. December 2026 or by agreement a dynamic and qualified personality as Controller (80-100%) Their tasks

  • Support of managers in the management and coordination of the services of the departments and participations through information provision and goal-oriented evaluations as well as action plans
  • Further development, maintenance and support of controlling instruments, reporting, investment accounting and the key figures system
  • Support services in the preparation of budget, annual accounts, investment accounts and forecasts
  • Creating analyses and reports incl. Presentation documents and embassies
  • Cooperation and supervision in cash management and treasury
  • Advising and supervising internal departments and supporting the management in the internal organisation

Your profile

  • University of applied sciences/university degree in business administration or administrative management or further training in controlling
  • Professional experience in public administration
  • Several years of experience in cost and performance accounting, reporting and reporting
  • Experience in writing reports and messages
  • Team-capable, flexible, assertive and resilient personality as well as independent, exact, forward-looking and reliable working methods
  • Good ICT knowledge (Abacus
  • Excel, Word)
  • Good German skills required

Our offer We offer you varied and responsible work in a motivated and qualified team. Attractive employment conditions, a modern infrastructure and a centrally located workplace complete our offer.
