Full description

The Office for Primary Schools and Sports (AVS) is part of the Department of Education, Environment and Culture of the Canton of Graubünden.

For the Department of Services and Finance we are looking for 1. March 2027 or by agreement a digitization manager. The Services and Finance department is responsible for internal processes and is responsible for administration, finance, staff and IT.

Tasks

  • Analysis and development of proposals for the digitalization of existing processes with a focus on efficiency and automation
  • Management and coordination of digitalization projects
  • Introduction and further development of digital tools and systems
  • Interface function between departments and IT
  • Training and support of employees in new digital solutions
  • Participation in the implementation of "Digital Administration Strategy 2024-2028"
  • Deputy in the area of 1st-level support for holidays and other absences

Requirements

  • Technical apprenticeship or equivalent training completed
  • Experience in digitalization, project management or process optimization
  • Analytical and conceptual skills as well as a structured working method
  • High affinity for digital technologies and innovations
  • Strong communication and team skills
