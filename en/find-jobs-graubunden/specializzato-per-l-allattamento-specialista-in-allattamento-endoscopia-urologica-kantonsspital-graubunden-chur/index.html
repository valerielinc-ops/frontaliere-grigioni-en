Start on January 1, 2027 or by appointment as nurse / nurse urological endoscopy 60 - 100%

Every year, our Urological Ambulatorium serves more than 9,000 patients with a wide variety of urological questions – from accompaniment to medical consultations and specialized examinations to individual advice on incontinence and self-catheterism. In our interprofessional team, we combine professional competence with personal and sensitive patient care.

Are you looking for a varied job in which you can contribute your nursing skills and develop professionally? Perfect, then we look forward to meeting you!

These are your tasks

  • You prepare diagnostic and therapeutic measures, assist and operate various medical-technical devices
  • You support a smooth and efficient process of urological consultation hours
  • You support and advise our patients and take over administrative activities

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as a dipl. Nurse HF/FH or equivalent and interested in urology
  • An independent, structured and responsible way of working
  • Teamwork, organizational skills and an empathetic approach to patients
