Full description

What to expect

  • Independent coordination of the vocational training administration, maintenance of training documents and optimization of administrative processes
  • Planning and organisation of internal education and training courses as well as intercompany courses (EC) for emax including registration, scheduling and billing
  • Administrative support in recruiting learners, managing applications via Yousty and organising trial apprentices
  • Cooperation in vocational training fairs and events as well as coordination of information exchange with internal and external contacts
  • Complementary support for CV learners as well as support in the planning of training stations and in achieving the training objectives
  • Conducting training and location interviews, preparing educational reports and assisting in preparation for the qualification process

What do you bring with you

  • Completed basic commercial training EFZ and several years of experience in administration or organisation, ideally in vocational training or human resources
  • Vocational training course of advantage or willingness to complete it
  • Very good MS-Office knowledge and pleasure in dealing with digital applications
  • Strong organisational talent and networked thinking as well as a structured, efficient and independent working method
  • High sense of responsibility and a reliable and careful working method
  • Communication strength, contact and service orientation as well as pleasure in working with learners and different contacts
  • Experience in implementing the current CP reform is beneficial

You can look forward to that.

  • Working in the middle of beautiful natural and recreational areas
  • Exciting tasks in an international environment with short decision paths
  • Lots of personal responsibility and attractive management positions
  • Market-oriented remuneration, progressive social benefits and own pension fund with good benefits
  • Staff restaurant, free parking

About us

EMS has 25 production sites in 16 countries, employs around 3,000 people worldwide and, with around 140 apprentices, is the largest vocational trainer in the canton and winner of the National Education Award.

EMS-SERVICES is responsible for the operation of the workshop with other local companies and provides its services in the areas of engineering, energy provision, maintenance, logistics, real estate, information technology, personnel management, accounting and controlling as well as environmental protection and security.
