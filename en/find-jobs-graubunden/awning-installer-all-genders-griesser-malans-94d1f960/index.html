Full description

Storenmonteur:in (all genders)

Malans 100%

Join the Griesser Team! Since 1882 we have been pioneers in the sun protection industry and create favourite places - inspired by the sun. With Swiss quality, we are represented as an internationally active family company in 20 countries. Become part of Griesser and create the perfect balance of sun and shadow with us. This is where your new professional chapter starts.

Your responsibilities

In this role, you are responsible for the professional and timely assembly of our products in the Malans region. The main activities concerned are:

  • Preparation, loading and transport of assembly material
  • Professional installation and installation of sun and weather protection systems (tors, shutters, lamellar doors, etc.)
  • Commissioning, functional testing and minor revisions to existing sun protection systems
  • Independent handling of on-site assembly orders
  • Professional documentation of the work carried out and feedback to internal bodies
  • Compliance with safety, quality and assembly requirements

What we offer

  • Personal & professional development: Various training and development opportunities
  • Work-life balance: 40-hour workweek
  • Security & Future: Comprehensive insurance packages, own pension fund, sickness allowance/accident insurance
  • Recreation & Leisure: At least 23 days of holiday, 9 guaranteed public holidays, 16 weeks of maternity leave and 3 weeks of paternity leave
  • Financial benefits: Attractive monthly flat-rate fees, annual remuneration and preferential conditions on Griesser products

What you bring

  • Completed craftsmanship training with EFZ (ideally as a poly builder specialising in sun protection or alternatively as a metal builder, carpenter, carpenter or similar)
  • Experience in assembly (e.g. store, sun and/or weather protection, facade construction, window construction or metal construction)
  • Technical understanding and strong craftsmanship
  • Physical and vertigo-free
  • Independent, reliable and customer-oriented working method
  • Customer-oriented appearance and professional communication with all contacts
  • Category B driving licence mandatory
  • Lifting platform test and PSAgA course of advantage

We look forward to meeting you. Your contact person is Danielle Roth, HR Business Partner.
