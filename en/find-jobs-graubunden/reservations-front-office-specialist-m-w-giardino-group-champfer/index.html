Full description

Giardino Group is looking for a reservations & front office specialist (m/f) for the Giardino Mountain in Champfèr. Giardino Hotels is a characterful Swiss luxury hotel group with locations in St. Moritz, Ascona and Locarno.

Tasks

Your versatile tasks include the acceptance and processing of room reservations for our currently three hotels, professional and warm telephone support as well as competent advice and active sales. In addition, you are responsible for checking in and out the guests, the care on site and checkout reports. With your authentic appearance and your natural friendliness, you can easily look after our guests at the highest level – personally, professionally and with real enthusiasm.

Requirements

  • Completed apprenticeship in the hospitality or commercial sector
  • Experience in a comparable position in the luxury hotel industry
  • Safe handling of MS Office and Protel PMS
  • A sense of duty, resilience, flexibility and enthusiasm
  • Strong hosting characteristics and a sense of allowing our guests unforgettable 5* holidays
  • Excellent language skills in German (native language or C1) and English, any other language such as Italian or French is advantageous
  • Dealing respectfully with colleagues and superiors
  • A well-groomed appearance and safe appearance

Benefits

  • An innovative and well-organized workplace
  • The opportunity to participate in everyday business and corporate development
  • Open and transparent communication
  • Tipping participation
  • Training according to your interests and future plans
  • Being part of a contemporary and characterful Swiss hotel group
