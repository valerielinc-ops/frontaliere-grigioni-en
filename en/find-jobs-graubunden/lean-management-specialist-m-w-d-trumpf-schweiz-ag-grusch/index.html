Full description

As a family-run, high-tech company with nearly 19,000 employees at 71 locations worldwide, we are looking for forward thinkers with unconventional ideas and drive to join our team. Our company culture, which values collaboration and mutual trust, creates the ideal framework for boldly trying new things and questioning the status quo. Our technologies inspire people to develop and produce things that are currently unimaginable. Whether lasers, machine tools, EUV or electronics - TRUMPF is building technological worlds for future generations. Are you ready for new challenges? ##

  • Responsibilities
  • Autonomous planning, control and implementation of Lean management projects and KVP measures along the entire production chain
  • from value stream analysis (Value Stream Mapping) to sustainable implementation
  • Analysis and optimization of production processes in terms of efficiency, quality and waste reduction, as well as accompaniment of layout plans and reorganizations
  • Planning, conducting and moderating continuous improvement workshops, 5S actions and continuous improvement rounds
  • Creation, introduction and development of work schedule management systems (Shopfloor-Management-Systems) as well as Lean management KPIs with regular reporting to management
  • Training and qualification of employees and managers in Lean methods as well as interdisciplinary collaboration with production management, quality management, procurement and logistics
  • Coordination of production projects ##
  • Requirements
  • Technical or scientific degree (e.g. mechanical engineering, industrial engineering, production technology) or equivalent qualification as well as relevant professional experience in Lean management, production optimization or industrial engineering
  • In-depth knowledge of Lean methods such as Kaizen, 5S, SMED, TPM, Poka Yoke, Kanban and Six Sigma as well as demonstrated experience in conducting value stream analyses and leading KVP projects
  • Safe use of MS Office; knowledge of ERP systems (e.g. SAP) is an advantage
  • Combined analysis and work structure with personal initiative, persuasion and implementation capabilities
  • Communication skills, team orientation and ability to motivate employees at all levels; excellent knowledge of German, knowledge of English is an advantage ##
  • What we offer
  • Modern working environment with state-of-the-art technology
  • Early assumption of responsibility and varied tasks
  • Extended training and development offer
  • Working where others are on vacation
  • Respectful and valuing company culture ## Contact
