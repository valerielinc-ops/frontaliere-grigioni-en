SOMEDIA - MEDIA OF SOUTH EAST SWITZERLAND Somedia is THE leading media company in Southeast Switzerland – innovative, fast and always on the pulse of the times. We use the entire spectrum from print to social media and increasingly rely on data to further develop our products and decisions. You build data models that deliver what they promise? You like to immerse yourself in new systems and want to create a data product that everyone can rely on? Then we are looking for you! For our data team in Chur we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by appointment a dedicated personality as Data Engineer (100%). Your job – varied and exciting You take technical responsibility for our data warehouse and develop it together with the data team into a data product that has an impact throughout the company.

  • Responsible for data warehouse: have architecture, structure and data flows under control and continuously develop them.
  • Promoting Tech Stack: Expanding our data platform based on Microsoft Fabric and Power BI and introducing new tools and approaches.
  • Developing robust solutions: Developing, testing and documenting data models and pipelines.
  • Shape data culture: Together in the data team, anchor data-driven work in the company and in the departments.

This is what you bring – personality and drive

  • In-depth experience in data engineering, data warehousing and data modeling, ideally with Microsoft Fabric, Power BI or similar tools.
  • You work carefully: Clean data models, tests and documentation are a matter of course for you.
  • You work your way into new topics and take responsibility for your results.
  • Structured working methods, initiative and enjoyment of working together in a team.
  • Openness to a wide range of topics and a real desire to build a strong data product.

This is what we offer you – more than just a job

  • Plenty of creative leeway: You actively shape our data platform right from the start.
  • Further education, internal and external, for your personal and professional development.
  • Modern workplaces with the possibility of home office.
  • 40-hour week – for a healthy work-life balance.
  • Attractive employee benefits, exciting company events and a team that is fun.
