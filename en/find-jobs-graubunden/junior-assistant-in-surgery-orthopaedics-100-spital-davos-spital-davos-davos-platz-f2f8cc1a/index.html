Full description

Live and work in the Destination Davos Klosters? We look forward to reinforcement! ##STR1##

In our surgical orthopaedics team, you will receive an insight into our broad range of surgical disciplines and expand your expertise with your practical cooperation.

As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable, but important company for the region. We maintain a collegial working atmosphere and appreciate the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos.

We award positions for May-June 2027 and from 2030 onwards as

Sub-assistant: in surgery & orthopedics 100% ##

You will get the following insights

  • You will work full time and thus gain a profound knowledge of the daily routine in the hospital
  • Collect anamnesis under supervision and guidance
  • Care for patients
  • Attending office hours
  • Being engaged in general medical station work
  • Assist in operations
  • Participation in medical research where appropriate
  • Participate in the 24-hour piquetting service

and we count

  • Student: in human medicine
  • Knowledge-hungry, positioned young personalities who enjoy working in a team and are interested in learning more about how a regional hospital operates

Our offer

Students actively participate in the daily workflow of the clinic. This includes activities in the department, office hours and operating room, daily participation in reports and training events, as well as meetings on operational indicators. After training, the independent care of surgical patients is possible in an emergency or on the department under the guidance of a senior physician.

For further information, the Department of Surgery & Orthopaedics is at your disposal by telephone or e-mail. Jobs shall be awarded at the earliest two years before the start of the traineeship.
