06.10.2026 Schiers

Become part of our team!

We, Raiffeisenbank Prättigau-Davos, are a young and dynamic bank with 44 dedicated employees. For our credit office, we are looking for an independent and committed personality as part of long-term succession planning from now on or by agreement.

What do you expect?

  • They process loan applications from opening to dissolution
  • They evaluate properties and produce financial analysis
  • They manage existing credit positions, ensuring data quality and regulatory compliance
  • You are a valuable support for our credit advisory team with a focus on corporate client consulting
  • You are in contact with customers: inside, offices, insurance companies and other banks
  • You have the opportunity to participate in projects
  • You take on the role as a practice instructor: in for the learners in the credit office

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training or comparable commercial training
  • Good knowledge and proven practice in the area of credit
  • Joy of working in a team and developing joint solutions
  • Service-oriented thinking and action
  • High degree of independence and flexibility
  • Precise and efficient operation
  • Good IT knowledge and openness to digital developments

We move every day. Only in this way do we realize what holds us and where we can develop further. In credit processing, too, we question what already exists and actively shape the digital future – for our internal and external customers. Jolanda Lipp Head of Credit Office Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We give space for personal initiative and participation

We invite our employees to actively participate in their work and to take on responsibility. This creates varied tasks that create real added value – for everyone who wants to make a difference.
