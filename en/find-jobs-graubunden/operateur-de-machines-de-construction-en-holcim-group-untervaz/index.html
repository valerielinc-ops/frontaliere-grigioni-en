Full description

Big machines, challenging terrain and real responsibility - exactly your thing? Then join our team in the quarry and use your skills to ensure that our raw material mining works safely and efficiently.

What you're expecting

  • Ensure the proper operation, maintenance and maintenance of construction machines and systems, including their efficient use
  • consideration of the continuous application of safety, quality and environmental standards
  • Help with troubleshooting all maintenance equipment
  • Ensure a clean, orderly area, workplace and driving slopes
  • Support for detonation preparation for raw material mining

What you're saying

  • Completed vocational training or experience in the construction sector or mechanical sector for several years
  • Experience in operating large construction machines, especially excavators from 30 tons
  • Knowledge of how to send a mobile crusher
  • Relevant experience in quarrying or related areas such as tunnelling, civil engineering or earthworks
  • Technical understanding and knowledge of maintenance of construction machinery
  • Class B driving licence, ideally with training as a construction machine leader (e.g. Campus Sursee; M2, M3 and M7 advantageous)
  • Self-employed, reliable working methods, high safety awareness and team spirit
  • A big plus: You were allowed to gain first blasting experiences, communicate safely in German and have good English skills

  • Attractive compensation: We offer our employees an attractive compensation package with many additional services and a very good pension fund
