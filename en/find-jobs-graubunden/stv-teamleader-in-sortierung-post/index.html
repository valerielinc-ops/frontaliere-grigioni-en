Full description

Do you want to take responsibility and develop further in the area of employee management? Are you enthusiastic about actively shaping everyday work together with your team? As Stv. Teamleader, you take on management tasks and at the same time actively participate in day-to-day business. With you, we strengthen cooperation and develop the team further together.

You can do that.

  • Together with your team leader, you lead a team of about 30 employees in terms of expertise and personnel.
  • As a strong leadership duo, they work hand in hand, promote team development and maintain communication.
  • You represent the team leader during his absences and take on specific leadership topics.
  • You integrate new employees into the team and support them in the introductory phase.
  • You work in a 5-day week usually from Monday to Friday between 10:00 and 05:00. In order to live up to your role model, you actively participate in day-to-day business.

You bring that with you

  • Ideally, you have completed a logistics training course and have several years of professional experience.
  • The roles as a role model and coach give you pleasure and you are happy to take on leadership tasks. Ideally, you already have initial leadership experience and otherwise you are ready to train yourself in the field of leadership and to contribute what you have learned in practice.
  • You communicate precisely, are an organizational talent and maintain a fair, appreciative approach at eye level.
  • Working at night brings you joy, you are physically fit and you are actively involved.
  • Confident knowledge of German in word and writing and advanced MS-Office skills round off your profile.
