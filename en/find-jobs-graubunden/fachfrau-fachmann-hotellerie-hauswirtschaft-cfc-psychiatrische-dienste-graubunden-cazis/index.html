Full description

At the clinic Beverin in Cazis, we train learners and specialists in hospitality housekeeping in the field of housekeeping. Our hotel industry provides services for patients, clients, employees and visitors in catering services, cleaning, laundry care, room management and disposal.

During your training, you work in the hotel industry. This means in the cafeteria, in the kitchen, in the laundry supply and in the room care / room management. You will learn the competent organization of processes in the domestic working environment. Professionals hotel industry housekeeping organize the cleaning with suitable tools and coordinate their assignments for an attractive appearance of the company. Decorating and stocking events, customer contact in the cafeterias, preparing food as well as responding to customer requirements you acquire during the training period.

Our basic attitude in your education

  • We strive for a learning climate characterized by mutual appreciation, tolerance and openness.
  • We offer you space to fulfil your role as a learning specialist in hospitality-housekeeping and a learning specialist in hospitality-housekeeping.
  • We provide you with various forms of learning that promote individual, holistic and self-directed learning.
  • We have clear rules and we define the scope for action together.
  • You learn through practical work and reflection, and we use leadership and training tools systematically.
  • We see learning in practice as a continuous and constructive process.
  • Through differentiated, constructive and well-founded feedback, we achieve learning success and promote your transfer thinking – from theory to practice.

Key points of your education

Duration and education Your apprenticeship lasts 3 years and takes place over three learning fields:

  • School education in compulsory education takes place on one school day per week at the Commercial Vocational School (GBC) in Chur.
  • The intercompany courses (ÜK) support your learning success by providing basic and industry-specific skills.
  • We offer you the professional learning field in the clinic Beverin in Cazis, in the kitchen of the clinic Beverin in Cazis and in an external laundry.

Professional maturity We support you if you strive for the Berufsmaturität (BM) during your apprenticeship. Tell us your interest with the application.

Holidays During basic vocational training, you have five weeks of vacation per year.

Accommodation At the Cazis location, we offer you low-cost accommodation if a daily journey between home and work is associated with a large amount of time.

After completing your apprenticeship, you can work in the profession as a specialist in hotel industry-housekeeping or as a specialist in hotel industry-housekeeping or you can complete one of the further trainings as a division manager in hotel industry-housekeeping or division manager in the housekeeping or household manager with a federal professional certificate.

Selection procedure

After examining your application, you will be informed about the selection decision. If you are shortlisted, the training manager agrees on an interview and selection days. Through selection days you get an insight into everyday work and we get to know each other better. The selection days are organized by us and take place in the Waldhaus clinic in Chur.

The employment decision is made by at least two people, usually by the training manager and his deputy.
