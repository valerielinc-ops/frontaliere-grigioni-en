Full description

As a family-run, high-tech company with nearly 19,000 employees at 71 locations worldwide, we are looking for forward thinkers with unconventional ideas and drive to join our team. Our company culture, which values ​​collaboration and mutual trust, creates the ideal framework for boldly trying new things and questioning the status quo. Our technologies inspire people to develop and produce things that are currently unimaginable. Whether lasers, machine tools, EUV or electronics - TRUMPF is building technological worlds for future generations. Are you ready for new challenges?

Your tasks

  • Management of customer projects from requirements clarification through development to successful commissioning
  • Development of customer-specific solutions for laser marking systems and workstations in close cooperation with our customers
  • Creation of technical documentation (cable drawings, electrical diagram, parts lists, etc.)
  • Be the technical contact for customer and sales companies for support questions
  • Close cooperation with development, production and sales

What we are looking for

  • Sound knowledge of electrical engineering as well as a completed technical degree in the field of electronics, systems engineering or similar (HF/FH)
  • Experience in supporting customer projects or managing smaller projects
  • Experience in developing standard or special machines with ECAD (E3)
  • Passion for technically challenging tasks and developing new automated systems and solutions
  • Strong communication skills
  • Very good knowledge of spoken and written German (at least C1 level)
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written English (at least B2 level)

What we offer

  • Respectful and appreciative corporate culture
  • State-of-the-art work environment with the latest technology
  • Early assumption of responsibility and varied tasks
  • Extensive training and further education offerings
  • Actively promoted work-life balance, e.g. B. with sports and fitness programs
