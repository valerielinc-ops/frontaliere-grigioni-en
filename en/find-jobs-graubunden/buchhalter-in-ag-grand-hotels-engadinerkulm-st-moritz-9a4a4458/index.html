Accountant

People at 1800 meters above sea level characterize the luxury mountain travel. Our dynamic and international team offers our guests smart luxury for an inimitable holiday experience. As a team member of the luxurious Kulm Group, you are part of the key to our success. Together we make every stay inspiring, unique and distinctive. And as such we live our culture: subtle luxury, inspiration and life experience. Together we make this possible. This is your chance to become part of our formative culture:

This moves you with us

Do you love numbers, work in a structured way and want to take responsibility in an exclusive environment? In our Finance team you ensure that two of the most renowned hotels in Switzerland are financially perfectly positioned.

Your tasks – precise, versatile, effective

  • Active participation in monthly and annual accounts – They provide transparent and reliable financial figures for informed decisions.
  • Responsibility for the entire accounts payable incl. Payment transactions, auditing and timely payment transactions.
  • Ensure clean accounts and professional, solution-oriented dunning.
  • Management of rental contracts and billing of our residences via Opera Cloud.
  • Start from 01\. January 2027 or by agreement.

This is you *

  • They have a sound commercial education (or equivalent) and have several years of experience in accounting.
  • Excel is a tool for you that you use efficiently – digital systems inspire you.
  • You work independently, accurately and gladly take responsibility for your area.
  • Your professional appearance, your natural friendliness and your well-kept appearance round off your profile.
  • Languages: very good English, German at least B1, Italian advantageous.

Become part of our Group Finance Teamand actively shape the future of two Swiss hotel icons – the Kulm Hotel St. and the Hotel Kronenhof Pontresina. We look forward to your application! *

Benefits At the Kulm Group, our employees are at the heart of our success. We offer you attractive development opportunities, comfortable accommodation, inexpensive food in the staff restaurant, and numerous discounts.
