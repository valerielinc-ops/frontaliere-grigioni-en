Full description

LOCATION RESPONSIBLE:R PROJECTOR:IN 80-100 % In order to continue and expand our success story, we are looking for an experienced personality as Location responsible:r Project manager:in 80-100 % immediately or by arrangement at our location in Davos INVIAS combines architecture, timber construction planning and construction management under one roof. In Davos we lead projects with a high local presence and close connection to the region. For our location, we are looking for an operationally strong project manager who actively shapes our presence on site. Your role at a glance In this function, you combine project management with location responsibility. You keep track, set priorities and make sure that projects and teams work reliably in Davos. Your tasks

  • Overall responsibility for several construction projects in Davos
  • Ensuring quality, deadlines and efficiency
  • Organisation and coordination of temporary buildings related to the WEF
  • Operational management of the location in day-to-day business
  • Ensuring clear structures and responsibilities
  • Establishment, leadership and development of the team on site
  • Representation of INVIAS in the Davos regional environment
  • Maintaining and expanding sustainable customer and partner relationships

What you bring

  • Experience in construction project management and/or construction management
  • Professional background in architecture, construction management or engineering / timber construction planning
  • Enjoyment in controlling complex construction projects
  • Structured and entrepreneurial working methods
  • Technical, economic and organisational understanding
  • Experience in the environment of the WEF or willingness to build a network in this context

What we offer you With us, you can expect a varied role in which you can take on responsibility and contribute your own ideas with a lot of creative freedom. You work in a dynamic environment characterized by exciting projects in the areas of construction, innovation and sustainability. Our modern way of working, short decision-making paths and high independence offer you the opportunity to make a difference every day and develop yourself personally and professionally. In addition, attractive development prospects within our group of companies open up for you. Do you want to become part of our corporate culture and live our values – enthusiasm, dynamism, sustainability, connection and trust? Then become part of a dedicated team that builds a better future together! Does this role suit you? You are not looking for a next job, but a task with effect. You want to lead projects, shape the location and be visible. Then we look forward to your contact. INVIAS, Selina Zogg s.zogg@invias.swiss Apply for this job Apply now
