Full description

You feel a disturbance before it becomes a problem - and then intervene? ####

At a leading company for sustainable building materials solutions, you use lubrication technology, vibration measurement and craftsmanship to ensure that plants run reliably - in a team that counts on your sense of technology.

The main tasks ####

  • Perform scheduled lubrication and maintenance work
  • Optimize lubrication processes and implement improvement measures
  • Lubricant samples and analysis
  • Mechanical repairs and troubleshooting
  • Coordinate and monitor external service providers
  • Pictet service

The requirement profile ####

  • Completed vocational training in mechanics
  • Experience in mechanical maintenance and maintenance
  • Basic knowledge of SAP and understanding of technology
  • Stylistic German, English is an advantage
  • Independent, reliable working method with team spirit
