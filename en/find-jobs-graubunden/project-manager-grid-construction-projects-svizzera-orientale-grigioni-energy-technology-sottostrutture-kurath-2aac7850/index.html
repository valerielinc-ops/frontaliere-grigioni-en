Full description

Our customer is a leading energy company in Switzerland based in Eastern Switzerland / Graubünden.

Your responsibilities

  • You take over the overall responsibility for subworks projects – from the concept phase to the planning to the successful implementation and commissioning.
  • They draw up technical specifications, concepts and tender documents and control the technical interpretation of the installations.
  • They plan and monitor deadlines, costs and interfaces and ensure a smooth project flow.
  • They lead and coordinate the project team as well as external suppliers and partners.

Your profile

  • Technical training in Bachelor\-/Master's degree (Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Construction)
  • Experience in project management of large infrastructure projects
  • Advantages: Several years experience in planning, project planning and realization of complex infrastructure projects in the energy technology / substationssector
  • Analytical skills as well as a solution-oriented, independent and structured working method
  • Responsible and team-capable personality with social competence
  • Tool Knowledge: MS Office, SAP, Project Management Tools
  • Very good knowledge of German, other national languages are advantageous
  • Your residence is in Switzerland
