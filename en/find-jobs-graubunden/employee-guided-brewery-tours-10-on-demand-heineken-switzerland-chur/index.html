Description

Brewery tours (10% on demand) at Heineken Switzerland (Calanda Brewery) in Chur, Canton of Graubünden, Switzerland. HEINEKEN Switzerland operates the Calanda Brewery in Chur, Graubünden – one of the most traditional breweries in Switzerland, founded in 1780. As part of the HEINEKEN Group, the world’s second largest brewery group, HEINEKEN Switzerland employs around 800 people at several locations in Switzerland. The company brews and sells well-known brands such as Calanda, Eichhof, Heineken and Birra Moretti. HEINEKEN Switzerland offers an international working environment, modern employment conditions and a wide range of development opportunities in the areas of production, logistics, sales and administration.
