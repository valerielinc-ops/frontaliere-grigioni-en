Company description ##

dasteam ag is one of the leading personnel service providers in Switzerland with over 35 years of experience. For our customers in the Rhine Valley, Sarganserland and Chur region, we are constantly looking for motivated and experienced masons for varied assignments on new and renovations.

Job description ##

  • Independent masonry and formwork work on civil engineering sites

  • Concrete, compacting and reworking

  • Reading and implementing building plans
  • General construction work and support of the construction site team
