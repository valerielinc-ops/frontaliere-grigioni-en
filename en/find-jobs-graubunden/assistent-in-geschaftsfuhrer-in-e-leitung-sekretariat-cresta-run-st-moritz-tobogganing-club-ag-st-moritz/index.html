Full description

As an assistant managing director and head of the secretariat, you will manage the operational operations of St. Moritz Tobogganing Club AG in cooperation with the managing director and board of directors. This essentially includes the following, not exhaustively listed, tasks as an assistant:

  • Efficient management of Cresta Run (Skeleton) operations in compliance with high safety standards for drivers and participants
  • Daily accounting and administration of operations in the Riding and Billing System. draw up monthly and seasonal accounts.
  • Communication and first point of contact for members, participation in various club events, implementation of prize distributions.
  • Responsible for entrance control at the Clubhouse.
  • Organization and coordination of social events
  • Training of Tower Boys/Girls in the company (especially in ready timekeeping and cameras)
  • Ensure the clubhouse is kept clean and tidy
