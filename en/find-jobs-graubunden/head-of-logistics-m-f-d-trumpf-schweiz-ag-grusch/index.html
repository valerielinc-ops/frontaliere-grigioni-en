Full description

As a family-run, high-tech company with nearly 19,000 employees at 71 locations worldwide, we are looking for forward thinkers with unconventional ideas and drive to join our team. Our company culture, which values ​​collaboration and mutual trust, creates the ideal framework for boldly trying new things and questioning the status quo. Our technologies inspire people to develop and produce things that are currently unimaginable. Whether lasers, machine tools, EUV or electronics - TRUMPF is building technological worlds for future generations. Are you ready for new challenges?

Your tasks

  • Personnel and operational management of directly subordinate managers as well as their promotion and development of the staff units
  • Responsibility for material deliveries in incoming goods, internal distribution and storage of materials, picking of spare parts
  • Picking and material provision for the production processes in the synchronized flow lines and individual workstations
  • Collaboration in projects, problem solving and SFM
  • Optimizing logistics processes, exemplifying SYNCHRO principles and involving employees
  • Responsibility for sustainably ensuring the standard and the defined work processes
  • Ensure compliance with safety regulations

What we are looking for

  • Completed technical studies or comparable training with further training in business administration
  • Several years of proven leadership experience, ideally also in managing executives
  • Profound experience in the areas of logistics, scheduling, planning and production organization
  • Entrepreneurial personality with strong leadership skills
  • High customer and service orientation and assertiveness
  • Very good communication skills and confident demeanor
  • Your very good English skills and your openness to build trusting international relationships
  • Ideally, good SAP knowledge is desirable

What we offer

  • Respectful and appreciative corporate culture
  • Ultra-modern working environment with the latest technology
  • Early assumption of responsibility and varied tasks
  • Extensive training and further education offerings
  • Actively promoted work-life balance, e.g. with sports and fitness programs
