The High Alpine Institute Ftan (HIF) is a tradition-steeped Swiss boarding school with tailor-made educational paths (bilingual Swiss Matura, international IGCSE\-\&A\-Level\-programme), the renowned HIF Sports Academy and seasonal camps. We belong to Education in Motion (EiM) – a global educational family with over 12,000 people worldwide.

We are looking for a:n Admissions, Marketing & Experience Officer. For many families, the education environment is one of the most important decisions for them. As an Admissions, Marketing & Experience Officer, you help families understand the possibilities at the High Alpine Institute Ftan and to know you as a trusted contact person at their side throughout the decision-making process. You will show how the experience at HIF can become the formative change you are looking for – a change that supports students to continue on their path with confidence and confidence. You work closely with the Head of AMX.

Admissions, Marketing & Experience Officer (50% to 100%)

This might suit you if...

  • you have professional experience in hospitality, tourism, customer service, administration, communication or education
  • you feel comfortable in a school environment and communicate professionally and prudently
  • you communicate clearly and confidently with parents, partners and colleagues in writing and orally:
  • you are organized, reliable and like to keep track
  • you have mastered German and English safely in word and writing
  • Other languages, especially Romansh, are a big plus
  • You enjoy working with different people in an international context

Experience in a school or in the Admissions area is advantageous, but not a requirement.

Your main tasks in this are:*

  • Implement marketing: generate social media, lead, plan and coordinate campaigns and authentic content about school life
  • Continually improving brand presence and impact
  • Developing national and international markets
  • Maintaining and expanding partner network: attracting educational mediators and referral partners, training, supervising and checking their quality and placement success
  • Advising families: qualifying inquiries, explaining educational opportunities, accompanying applications and ensuring a successful handover to the HIF\ community
  • Collaborate and manage: Working with school, boarding and marketing teams; CRM, prospective pipeline, forecasts and reports reliably maintained
