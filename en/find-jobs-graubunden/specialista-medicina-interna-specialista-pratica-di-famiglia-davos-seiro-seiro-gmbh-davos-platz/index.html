Full description

As a specialist in general internal medicine, you take on a versatile and responsible job in an established family practice in Davos in the middle of one of the most attractive regions in Switzerland. They provide long-term support for patients in primary care, work closely with an interprofessional team and benefit from an environment that ideally combines medical quality, personal involvement and a high quality of life. The job is available immediately or by agreement in a charge of 60 to 100%. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • Independent performance of general medical consultation hours in the ambulatory area
  • Care for acute illnesses including medical emergencies
  • Long-term support and treatment of chronically ill patients
  • Close interprofessional cooperation with a motivated MPA team
  • Possibility for active participation and further development of the practice centre
  • Working in a modern medical environment with short decision-making paths

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed specialist training in general internal medicine
  • Ideally, experience in primary care or outpatient care
  • Enjoyment of patient-centered medicine and long-term care
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Interest in interprofessional cooperation and modern integrated care

That sets them apart.

  • Responsibility and high medical quality standards
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Empathetic and professional treatment of patients
  • Motivation to actively participate in the further development of the practice
  • Interest in a long-term career prospect in the Davos Klosters region

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Self-employed family doctor with high medical responsibility
  • Professional, family and interprofessional working environment with short decision paths

  • Great scope for design and active participation in the further development of the practice centre
