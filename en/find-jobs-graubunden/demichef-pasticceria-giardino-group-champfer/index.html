Full description

Giardino Group is looking for a demichef of the game (m/f) for the Giardino Mountain in Champfèr. Giardino Hotels is a characterful Swiss luxury hotel group with locations in St. Moritz, Ascona and Locarno.

Tasks

The three restaurants of the hotel individually provide culinary highlights; of modern creations inspired by Asia, Orient and Occident in Hide & Seek, over Engadine specialties in the rustic ambience of Stüva, up to the high art of star cuisine in Ecco St. Moritz. In this role, you will be responsible for the smooth operation of your workspace in the Hide & Seek restaurant and our Stüva restaurant. This includes ordering as well as controls regarding quality, hygiene and safety. You work in a powerful and dynamic team and cook at a high gastronomic level.

  • A well-groomed appearance and polite manners
  • Independence, reliability and sense of duty
  • Resilience, flexibility and enthusiasm
  • Respectful treatment of colleagues and superiors

Benefits

  • Open and transparent communication
  • Regular training and further education as well as numerous employee benefits
  • Being part of a contemporary and characterful Swiss hotel group
