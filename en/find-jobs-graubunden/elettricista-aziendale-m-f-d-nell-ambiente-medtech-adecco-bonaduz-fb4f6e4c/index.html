Full description

Introduction

For one of our renowned customers in the field (Pharma, MedTech and Lifesciences) we are looking for a dedicated company electrician (m/f/d) to strengthen the technical team at the earliest possible time.

Tasks

  • Performing work according to type. 13 NIV
  • Repair services of buildings and production equipment
  • troubleshooting and repair in particular in machine tools
  • Low- and high-current installation work
  • Revision and updating of electrical diagrams
  • Modernisation and adaptation of fire alarm installations
  • Pictet service and security rounds
  • Using Facility Tasks When Needed

Profile

  • Completed vocational training as an electrical installer, automatic engineer, electronics engineer or equivalent qualification
  • Authorisation by type 13 NIV or willingness to obtain it
  • Professional experience in maintenance, preferably in an industrial environment
  • Sound knowledge of low- and high-current installations
  • Experience in error analysis, troubleshooting
  • Knowledge of building automation or fire detection technology is advantageous
  • Independent, reliable and structured working method
  • Team orientation, high commitment and a sense of responsibility
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Resident in the Chur region or ready to move

Advantages

  • You work in a stable company in the MedTech environment in the Graubünden area with clearly structured processes and responsibilities.
  • You will receive a function directly related to production in the areas of Pharma / Life Sciences / Chemistry / Science with visible contribution to the final product.
  • You can continuously apply your specialist knowledge as a company electrician and deepen and expand it within the scope of the operational requirements.
  • Your remuneration and other employment conditions are discussed transparently in the application process and are based on qualifications and experience.
  • You benefit from an attractive workplace with cooperation in an interdisciplinary team from different technical and manufacturing areas.
  • Very good employment conditions and above-average social benefits are a matter of course
