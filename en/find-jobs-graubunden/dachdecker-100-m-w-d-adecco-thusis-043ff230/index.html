Full description

Introduction

For our customer, we are looking for a skilled person for use as a roofer. The contract covers varied activities in the field of roof, waterproofing and repair work. We are looking for a reliable professional who works safely and feels comfortable in a practical environment. It is used in a professional working environment with clear processes and different tasks on different construction sites. The employment relationship shall be open-ended. The activity is aimed at people who bring precise work, physical resilience and responsible use of materials and tools.

Tasks

  • They cover roofs with various materials professionally and pay attention to clean design.
  • They carefully perform sealing work on roof surfaces, connections and transitions.
  • They undertake repair and maintenance work on existing roof structures.
  • They prepare construction sites, secure work areas and keep order on the work surface.
  • They check roof areas for damage and report conspicuous findings to the competent body.

Profile

  • They have craftsmanship and work precisely on challenging tasks.
  • They bring with them experience or safe knowledge in the field of roofing work.
  • They work reliably, independently and adhere to agreed work processes.
  • They are physically resilient and have no difficulty working at height.
  • They work in a team-oriented manner and coordinate clearly with colleagues in their day-to-day business.

Advantages

  • Remuneration mainly by collective agreement, depending on commitment and qualification.
  • Varied activities in a versatile work environment.
  • Permanent employment contract with a long-term employment perspective.
