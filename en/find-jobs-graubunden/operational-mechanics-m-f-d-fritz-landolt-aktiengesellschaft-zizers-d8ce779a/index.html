Full description

Are you our new team member for our new location in Zizers GR?

Your mission

  • You are THE competent contact person for faults, error messages and repairs of our production and infrastructure facilities and can correct them.
  • You keep the internal machinery, equipment and equipment running through regular inspections and maintenance work and can adjust and rebuild them if necessary.
  • You are also at the forefront of new installations and commissioning of our state-of-the-art plant park such as production plants, high-bay warehouses, mixing stations, etc.

Your talent

  • You have completed an apprenticeship in the field of mechanics (e.g. construction, agricultural machinery, etc.) and bring relevant experience – also from other areas of mechanics.
  • You are quickly able to grasp connections and find suitable solutions, you organize yourself and work independently and exactly. Your bosses and teammates can rely on you.
  • You communicate very well in German and have Office365 user knowledge.
  • You already have the forklift driver’s license or are ready to obtain it.

Our offer

  • We offer you a varied and exciting job with a lot of scope for action and personal responsibility in your area of responsibility.
  • You will be comprehensively trained, trained and supported.
  • Our employees benefit from services beyond the usual e.g. insurance, health & fitness, mobility, further education or gastronomy.
  • A job with interesting prospects at our new location in Zizers, GR. Be a part of our generational project - a brand new factory with state-of-the-art machinery. Take the unique opportunity to help shape this project, set standards and contribute.

Our drive Fritz Landolt AG is the Swiss specialist for temporary surface protection and a renowned manufacturer of technical nonwovens for a wide range of applications. Our premium products such as FloorlinerTM or ALLPROTECTTM reliably protect surfaces from external influences and at the same time contribute to work safety – be it on construction sites, in vehicle, ship or aircraft construction. Those who work with us become part of a down-to-earth team characterised by passion, innovation and cohesion. This has been done over five generations. Together, we not only create optimal solutions for our customers, but also build a future – for us, for the region and the world in which we live. We are committed to this with every fiber. Join the team and be a part of it!
