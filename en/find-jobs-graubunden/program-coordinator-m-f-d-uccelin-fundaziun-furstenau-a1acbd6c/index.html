About us Fundaziun Uccelin belongs to the Caminada Group and was founded in 2016 by Sarah and Andreas Caminada to promote young talents from the kitchen and service professionally and humanely. Our foundation programs are aimed at young and highly gifted chefs: service professionals who want to gain experience in our international partner restaurants and our partner producers.

You move that

  • Organization of foundation programs with scholarship holders: partners, including accommodation and transfer bookings as well as processing of visas and work permits
  • Contact person for scholarship holders: Uccelin partners
  • Planning and publishing content on website, social media and in the newsletter
  • Support for charity events from planning to post-processing
  • Assumption of administrative tasks and support of the Managing Director

You bring that with you

  • Basic commercial training
  • Very good social media and Microsoft Office knowledge
  • Organizational talent, networked thinking and a structured, precise working method
  • Independence, flexibility and team spirit
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, stylish confident in written expression

This is what we offer you

  • A varied task with an international orientation
  • A lot of personal responsibility and scope for design
  • Flexible working hours and opportunities for home office
  • 5 weeks holidays and discounts in our restaurants

Sounds like you? We look forward to your full application. More about the Foundation can be found at www.uccelin.com.
