  • Website & Platform Management: Maintain and update our websites (front-end & CMS), manage content such as packages and events, support SEO hygiene, and ensure a seamless user journey – from inspiration to the booking engine.
  • Online Listings & Reputation: Manage our presence on OTAs, partner platforms, and directories, keep all information consistent, and professionally oversee reputation management on portals such as TrustYou, TripAdvisor, and Google.
  • Digital Asset Management: Actively manage our Bynder system, organize and tag visual assets, and coordinate photoshoots from preparation to final asset handover.
  • Reporting & Performance: Support the consolidation and preparation of data for monthly performance reports.
  • Team Coordination: Provide administrative support to the Brand and Commercial team, prepare meetings, and ensure smooth cross-departmental workflows. | This is you:
  • Education: Successfully completed university degree (Bachelor) with a focus on marketing, digital media, business administration, or a comparable qualification.
  • Experience: Initial practical experience in digital marketing, website management, or online platform administration; basic knowledge of CMS systems (and ideally HTML) is an advantage.
  • Working Style: A structured, precise, and reliable hands-on mentality with an exceptional eye for detail and brand aesthetics.
  • Competencies: Enthusiasm for digital user experiences, strong organizational skills, and the ability to coordinate multiple tasks in parallel and toward clear goals.
  • Languages: Excellent written and spoken English skills
  • German C1. Italian is a strong asset.
