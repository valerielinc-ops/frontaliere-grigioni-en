Full description

With more than 600 employees in the Schiers hospital, the three retirement homes Schiers, Jenaz and Klosters, Spitex, living with service, the crèche, parental counselling and the Klosters Medical Center, the Flury Foundation ensures comprehensive and integrated health care in the Prättigau, between Grüsch and Klosters. Since August 2011, the Flury Foundation runs a daycare center, the Rätikon nursery. The publicly accessible crèche offers 18 childcare places in Schiers every day from Monday to Friday and 12 in Grüsch for children in the preschool area, i.e. from 3 months up to and including kindergarten or school admission. As of 1 August 2027, we are awarding an apprenticeship in our crèches for care EFZ, specialty children

During your education, you will learn

  • Accompaniment and promotion of children in their activities
  • Encouraging creative activities for children
  • Participation in a varied group life

This is what you bring with you

  • Secondary or secondary education
  • Joy of working with children
  • Ability to be creative
  • Team and communication skills

We offer you

  • A varied and exciting education for 3 years
  • Independent and self-responsible work
  • A competent team that accompanies and supports you during your training
  • 6 weeks holiday

First questions can be answered by Mrs. Seraina Kleger, Head of Nursery, Tel. +41 81 308 08 10. Further information about the Flury Foundation can be found on the Internet: flurystiftung.ch About the delivery of the application documents to: hr@flurystiftung.ch we are happy!
