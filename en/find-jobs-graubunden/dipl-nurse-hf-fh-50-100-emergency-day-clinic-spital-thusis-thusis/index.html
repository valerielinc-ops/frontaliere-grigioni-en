Full description

Emergency care with structure, team spirit and without night services

In the course of a succession regulation we are looking for YOU for our emergency / our day clinic in the hospital Sois per 1. September 2026 or by arrangement as:

Would you like to work in a varied environment, professionally accompany patients in different situations and benefit from clear structures at the same time?

Then you are exactly right with us.

Your tasks

  • Competent, emergency medical care and care of patients
  • Co-responsibility for a structured and safe day organization in an emergency, in the day clinic and in special areas
  • Active participation in our lean processes, such as huddles, day control, prioritization and continuous process improvement
  • Interprofessional and interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors, ambulance service, radiology, laboratory, station and other areas
  • independent recording of patient administration, nursing documentation and benefit billing
  • Accompanying and supporting students, new employees and new entrants

This is what you bring with you

  • completed training as a nurse HF/FH or equivalent training
  • Professional experience in acute care, ideally in the emergency area, is an advantage but also motivated young professionals are welcome.
  • Enjoyment of working with patients
  • good observation skills, networked thinking and the ability to quickly assess situations
  • Interest in clear processes, structured cooperation and further development
  • Teamwork, resilience, empathy and responsibility
  • Readiness for early, late and weekend assignments

This is what we offer you

  • a varied and responsible activity in our family regional hospital
  • a dedicated team with open and supportive cooperation
  • No night services
  • Structured lean processes that provide orientation and support everyday work
  • Space for personal initiative, participation and personal development
  • modern infrastructure and internal and external training opportunities
  • Contemporary employment and working conditions with at least 25 days holidays
  • Attractive leisure offers and benefits
  • Working in a region with high leisure value and tourist attractive environment

Ready for your next step? Then we look forward to your application and to getting to know you.
