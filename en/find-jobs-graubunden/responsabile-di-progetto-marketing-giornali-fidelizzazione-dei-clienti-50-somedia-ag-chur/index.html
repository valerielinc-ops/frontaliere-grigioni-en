Full description

SOMEDIA - MEDIA OF SOUTH EAST SWITZERLAND Create the future of reading with us: with experiences that connect. Somedia is one of the five largest media companies in Switzerland. We include over 20 leading media brands, digital platforms, innovative marketing and communication agencies, e-learning service providers and emerging startups. We are an independent family company with flat hierarchies and maintain an open du culture. Our way of working is characterized by the values ambitious, courageous, respectful and responsible - and we act according to the principles: clear, simple and self-confident.

Do you feel addressed? Then become part of our team and move with us what moves people.

For Somedia Press AG in Chur we are looking for 1. March 2027 or by agreement a project manager Marketing Newspapers - Customer loyalty (50%) . What You Expect

  • You develop and realize effective measures for the retention and further development of our subscribers of daily and weekly newspapers and strengthen their long-term connection with our media brands through professional, targeted customer communication.
  • You bring your own ideas, develop existing approaches and make an active contribution to the achievement of our strategic goals.
  • You plan, coordinate and implement holding marketing and telephone marketing campaigns independently.
  • You will develop our tools and processes in the areas of CRM, telephone marketing and newspaper statistics and use digital possibilities to optimize our marketing activities.
  • You work independently on your projects and at the same time work closely with various internal and external contacts.

What you bring

  • A completed basic education with further education or a degree in marketing, communication or a related field.
  • Experience in project management as well as interest in marketing technologies, digital tools and the meaningful use of artificial intelligence (AI).
  • Strong conceptual skills, combined with a structured, independent and solution-oriented way of working.
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, a good feeling for target groups and pleasure in turning ideas into concrete measures.
  • You like to take on responsibility, work independently and at the same time appreciate the exchange and cooperation in the team.
  • You communicate openly, bring different perspectives together and like to find good solutions together with others.

What we offer

At Somedia, you can expect an environment in which personal responsibility and cooperation go hand in hand. We rely on trust, open communication and mutual support. You get freedom to contribute your ideas, develop your own solutions and drive your projects forward independently – with a team that appreciates the exchange and the common progress.

We live quality, ease and humanity and promote your professional and personal development.

You can expect an annual working time with a 40-hour week, the possibility of a 4-day week, flexible home office solutions and at least five weeks of vacation. You also benefit from regular team meetings, joint events, inspiring workshops and attractive benefits such as a discounted Natel subscription, a free newspaper subscription and lunch & learn events.
