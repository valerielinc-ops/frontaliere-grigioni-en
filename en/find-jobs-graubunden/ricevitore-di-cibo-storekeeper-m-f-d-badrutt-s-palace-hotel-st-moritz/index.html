Role overview

Your key responsibilities

As a valued member of our Purchasing team, you will be responsible for a diverse range of tasks across the Receiving & Stores operation.

  • You inspect all incoming deliveries against purchase orders and delivery notes, ensuring compliance with our quality standards.

  • Whenever required, delivered goods are stored appropriately and in accordance with established procedures.

  • All ordered goods are checked for completeness, and you liaise with suppliers whenever discrepancies arise.

  • Delivery notes are processed and posted accurately and in a timely manner.

  • Responsibility for issuing stores requisitions to our internal departments forms an integral part of your role.

  • Internal stock transfers relating to stores issues are recorded accurately and systematically.

  • By monitoring inventory levels and placing replenishment orders independently, you help ensure the continuous availability of goods.

  • In addition, you oversee the organisation of the receiving area, including the garage, storage rooms, empty containers, packaging, cleanliness, and materials management.

• What you bring to the job

  • You have completed vocational training as a Chef or a Restaurant Professional.

  • Previous experience in an administrative role enables you to work in a structured, organised, and conscientious manner.

  • You are proficient in Microsoft Office applications, with advanced skills in Microsoft Excel.

  • Ideally, you have experience working with an inventory management system; knowledge of Kost is considered an advantage.

  • You have an excellent command of written and spoken English; additional knowledge of Italian or German is an asset.

  • A high level of commitment, resilience, and the ability to grasp new concepts quickly enable you to work independently and in a solution-oriented manner.

  • A professional appearance and courteous, friendly manner complete your profile.

Our offer

  • Fixed-term seasonal position from the end of November 2026 to the end of February 2027

  • Average 5-day working week with 43.5 working hours.

  • 5 weeks of vacation and 6 public holidays per year.

  • 13th salary from the start date (pro rata temporis).

  • Accommodation in one of our staff houses.

  • Meals available in our modern staff restaurant.

  • Opportunity to work in an attractive environment with development and career growth opportunities.

If you are looking for an interesting, challenging, and diverse role in a dynamic environment and would like to join a well-established and collaborative team, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
