The White Arena Group in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and an enthusiastic community for unique mountain adventures. With mountain railways, hotels, gastronomy, sports and rental shops and the LAAX School, we create unforgettable experiences for our guests - and attractive workplaces for our employees. | At 1980 meters above sea level is the Stalla Alp Nagens, where the newly converted restaurant Stalla in the rustic cow stable invites you to enjoy all year round. Here you will find regional specialties with their own alpine cheese, fine fondue, raclette and other Swiss classics. The sun terrace invites you to stay and after a day on the slope or a winter hike you enjoy the last rays of sunshine before it goes back to the valley. | For the Alp Stalla in Laax we are looking for motivated support at our Raclette station. You enjoy contact with guests, like to work clean and fast and bring a good mood? Then you are exactly right with us. What you do move:

  • You prepare Raclette and set it up
  • You serve our guests friendly and attentively
  • You keep the station clean and organized
  • You help our guests experience an unforgettable mountain moment | What makes you:
  • You are aware that you are working on the fire all day and feel comfortable doing so.
  • You enjoy gastronomy and guest contact
  • You are reliable and enjoy working in a team
  • You work cleanly and carefully
  • You stay motivated and keep track even on lively days. An attractive workplace in the middle of an extraordinary mountain world is certain for you. You become part of a dynamic, bright team and can help shape the further development of LAAX. With us you will find an environment where professionalism, speed, pragmatism and humanity go together. Look forward to numerous employee benefits, such as free mountain rides, perks in our shops, hotels and restaurants, and benefits for your family.
