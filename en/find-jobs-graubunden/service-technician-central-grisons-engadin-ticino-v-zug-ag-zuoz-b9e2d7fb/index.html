Service technician: in – Region Mittelbünden / Engadin / Ticino

You work with 80-100% from your home base in Malans, with a dedicated service vehicle and an application area in Engadin and adjacent regions

V\-ZUG Field Service Switzerland

If a V\-ZUG device does not run as it should at our customers, our field service comes into play. Our service technicians are on the road every day – competent, solution-oriented and with the aim of turning a service mission into a good customer experience. With 16 service locations throughout Switzerland, we are close to our customers. Whether diagnosis, repair, maintenance or personal advice: We combine technical know-how with a genuine understanding of service. We work independently in our regions, but remain closely connected as a team – digitally, in daily exchange and with the common goal that V\-ZUG inspires even after the purchase.

This gives you the job

  • You are independently on the road in your local application area and bring high-quality household appliances back to work in the kitchen and washroom. From diagnosis and repair to revision to maintenance, you will encounter new situations and different devices every day. Whether oven, dishwasher, washing machine or washing tower – variety is guaranteed
  • In your assignments, your professional assessment counts. You decide when a repair is worthwhile and when an exchange is the better solution, create suitable offers and accompany the customer with an honest and transparent recommendation.
  • Your know-how does not end with technology. In direct exchange with the customer, you give practical tips on operation, care and maintenance, clarify questions about connection and installation and ensure that our devices also provide long-term pleasure.
  • With your tablet you have everything important directly at hand and document your bets, service reports and offers digitally. This way you keep track of your working day and your orders at all times
  • Even if you travel a lot independently, you are part of a team. In the daily exchange with the service center, you share experiences and solutions, while service bulletins and technical updates keep you up to date. So your know-how grows with each use
  • In a well-founded training program at our headquarters in Zug, you gain comprehensive product and service knowledge and are prepared step by step for your assignments

You bring this for the job

  • You have completed basic craftsmanship or electrotechnical training (EFZ or EBA) – ideally as an electrical installer:in, electronics technician:in, automatic technician:in, sanitary installer:in or in a comparable profession
  • You bring solid electrotechnical knowledge and know how to systematically, safely and solution-oriented to an unknown device. Ideally, you already have the NIV\-15\ permit or are ready to complete the required further training with us
  • You enjoy direct customer contact and can explain technical connections in such a way that they are understandable even without technical prior knowledge – friendly, clear and at eye level
  • You work independently and reliably. Even if the schedule is tight once, you will find a way to bring together quality, care and deadlines.
  • You are physically fit and take on challenging tasks – whether in tight installation situations, heavy equipment or complex connections
  • You are sure to communicate in German. knowledge in Italian is advantageous due to the area of operation
  • Other languages are a plus
  • You have a category B driving licence
