Full description

For our customer, an established company in the development industry, we are looking for a motivated plaster B with craftsmanship to strengthen the team.

Their tasks

  • Support for indoor and outdoor plastering work
  • Assistance in mixing materials and mounting floor and surface plaster
  • Preparatory work such as covering, marking and setting up the construction site
  • Transport and provision of materials and tools
  • General auxiliary work in drywall and on construction site

Your profile

  • First experience in the construction industry, ideally in the field of plaster or plaster
  • Craftsmanship and willingness to learn
  • Reliable and operational working methods
  • Resilient, team-ready and flexible
  • Knowledge of German (minimum A1–A2) for understanding on the construction site
  • Advantageous driving licence Cat B

We offer

  • Fair remuneration according to GAV expansion industry
  • Long-term assignments with development opportunities
  • Working in a collegial team
  • Exciting and varied projects in the interior and exterior
  • Personal support during deployment
