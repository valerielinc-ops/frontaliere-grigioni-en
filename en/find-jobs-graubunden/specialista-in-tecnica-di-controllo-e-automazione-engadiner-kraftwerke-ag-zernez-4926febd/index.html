Engadiner Kraftwerke AG (EKW), headquartered in Zernez, is one of the largest hydropower companies in Switzerland. In the heart of the national park region, it operates critical infrastructure that contributes to the country’s power supply. Around 60 employees work every day for reliable and sustainable energy production.

In order to strengthen our IT \& OT\ team in Zernez, we are looking for a:n

Specialist: in management and automation technology (80 to 100%)

Your tasks

  • You supervise the control technology and automation systems of our hydropower plants and ensure reliable operation together with internal specialist offices and external partners.
  • You analyze disruptions and support them in their sustainable correction.
  • You will make adjustments and optimizations to control, automation and control systems.
  • You will work on challenging renewals, extensions and modernizations of our power plant and network control technology, from planning to commissioning.
  • You create and maintain technical documentation.
  • You coordinate and accompany suppliers and external service providers.
  • You participate in the implementation and further development of OT\-Security.

Your profile

  • You have technical training in automation, electrical engineering, system engineering or a comparable field of expertise.
  • You have already gained experience in automation, control or process control technology.
  • You are interested in hydropower and technical plants.
  • You work structured, independent and solution-oriented.
  • You like to be on site in our various power plants and also take part in technical work yourself.
  • Responsibility, teamwork and a reliable way of working distinguish you.
  • You have good knowledge of German.
  • Knowledge of industrial networks and interfaces is an advantage.

Your perspective

With us, you can expect a well-founded and practical training in our power plant plants and control systems. Working in a versatile technical environment with complex and long-term plants You work closely with our operational and maintenance departments. Develop with internal and external training You keep on targeting yourself.

Organizationally, you are located in the area of IT & OT. You work independently, but regularly inform the head of IT & OT about the status of the work and coordinate with him on the priorities.

We offer this to you *

  • More time for you: 5\+ weeks of holidays, with more and more, and the possibility to buy up to 10 days of additional holidays at attractive conditions.
  • Working flexibly: Annual working hours with a time account, flexible design of your working day and up to 25% home office.
  • Further development: We support further training in control technology, automation and OT\-Security with up to 100% coverage of costs and time.
  • More than the wage: monthly household allowance in addition to the salary
  • Good insurance: EKW takes over the premiums for day sickness and accident insurance, including additional insurance with private hospital department. Our pension fund offers very good services and we finance a large part of the contributions.

For substantive questions about the job or the requirements, Enzinger, Head of IT \& OT, is available to you under the number \ or by email.

Would you like to use your expertise to contribute to safe and reliable electricity production? Then we look forward to receiving your application directly via our website or via email.
