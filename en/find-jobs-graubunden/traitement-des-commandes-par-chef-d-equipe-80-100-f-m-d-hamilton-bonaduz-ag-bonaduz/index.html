Full description

True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

The Operations business unit produces parts for all Hamilton companies, from injection moulded parts to pipette tips for liquid handling to metal and plastic parts. Our goal is to produce as many parts as possible in-house. In this way, we remain agile, independent and efficient.

You can do that

  • Leadership and further development of the team of specialists in commercial order processing and shipping
  • Responsibility for the timely order processing process (Sales Order Processing) from order receipt to shipping planning and coordination up to invoicing
  • Coordination and handling of all export and transport tasks to all countries supplied by Hamilton Bonaduz AG, including preparation and maintenance of the necessary export and customs documentation
  • Optimization and further development of the organization, processes, policies and work instructions in the commercial order processing process to increase efficiency, quality and customer satisfaction
  • Analysis and reporting of relevant performance indicators (KPIs) and implementation of continuous improvement initiatives
  • Close cooperation with internal interfaces (sales, purchasing, production, logistics, quality, engineering) to ensure a smooth value chain
  • Contact person for national and international customers regarding delivery status, export issues and service concerns

This is what you bring

  • Commercial education or basic technical training with further commercial education
  • Professional experience in international transport (import-export), foreign trade and customs topics of advantage
  • Several years of management experience in the field of order processing, logistics or supply chain management, ideally in an industrial or technical environment
  • Professional use of ERP system Microsoft D365 or SAP for order processing and Microsoft Office tools
  • Analytical and process-oriented thinking
  • Proactive and service-oriented personality with pleasure in contact with customers
  • Independent, targeted, implementation- and assertive personality
  • Strong communication s and team skills
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English (in word and writing)
