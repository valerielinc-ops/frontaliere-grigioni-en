Role overview

On behalf of the municipalities, the cantonal welfare office performs the task of personal social and addiction assistance and supports people in the development of economic social assistance.

It provides eight regional social services in the regions of the canton.

The regional social service Prättigau/Herrschaft/Five villages is responsible for 18 municipalities with a catchment area of 43 000 people.
