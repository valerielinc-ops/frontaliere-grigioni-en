Full description

Rehabilitation medicine with time, rest and a team working together towards the rehabilitation goal. In Seewis im Prättigau, Canton Graubünden, we are looking for a senior physician or a senior physician psychiatry and psychosomatics in the amount of 60-100% for a specialized rehabilitation clinic. It expects an interdisciplinary environment with flat hierarchies, plenty of room for manoeuvre and an impressive mountain landscape at the entrance to the Prättigau valley. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They provide psychiatric care under the guidance of specialist medical experts
  • They look after inpatient and outpatient patients with psychiatric-psychosomatic clinical pictures, mainly with F-diagnoses, without night and weekend presence services
  • They design medical patient care independently and responsibly
  • They work together in an interdisciplinary team to develop the best solutions according to rehabilitation goals
  • They impart knowledge through systematic internal training and bedside teaching

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title for psychiatry and psychotherapy or advanced training
  • Experience in the treatment of psychiatric-psychosomatic pathologies
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation with care and therapy

That sets them apart.

  • Patient-centred, trusting basic attitude in psychosomatic care
  • Self-responsible, structured working method with high professional standards
  • Communication strength and team spirit in an interprofessional environment

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Responsible medical practice without night and weekend presence services
  • Flat hierarchies with plenty of room for manoeuvre and close exchange with care and therapy
  • Recognized SIWF Training Centre for Psychiatry and Psychotherapy
  • First-class rehabilitation in a well-maintained hotel environment with over 50 years of experience
  • Workplace in an impressive mountain landscape with a high quality of life

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
