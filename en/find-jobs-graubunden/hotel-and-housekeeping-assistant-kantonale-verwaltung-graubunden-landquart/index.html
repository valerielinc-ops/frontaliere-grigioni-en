Full description

As a professional hotel industry housekeeping you clean offices, rooms and general rooms, you work in the service and guest area, you do administrative work, you are familiar with nutrition and food as well as laundry care.

The basis of in-company training is, in addition to professional support, the development of methodological, social and self-competence. This means that during your training you learn the professional knowledge and skills from scratch and are able to master the requirements in the interpersonal and professional areas.
