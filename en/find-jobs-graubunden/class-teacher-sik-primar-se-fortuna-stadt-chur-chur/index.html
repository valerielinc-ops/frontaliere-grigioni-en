Full description

Class teacher SIK Primar, SE Fortuna With 41'000 inhabitants, Chur is the urban center of Graubünden. As an attractive and versatile city, it provides customer-oriented and modern services with its 1,400 employees.

The Chur City School is the largest public school in the canton of Graubünden. Its offer covers all school levels from kindergarten to secondary level I. Special offers are bilingual classes German/Italian and German/Romanian, language integration classes, timeout classes and talent classes Music and Sports at secondary level I. The city school also includes daycare centers and school social work.

At the Chur City School, approximately 3,200 pupils from around 400 teachers in

15 school units are taught. Each school unit is led by a school principal, who together with the school directorate form the overall school management. Due to maternity leave we are looking for the second semester of the school year 2026/27 (from

  1. February 2027 a

Class teacher Language integration class Primary level for the 1st / 2nd class, Pensum approx. 80 %

Classes are divided into five days. On Monday you teach all day, from Tuesday to Friday in the morning. Further employment beyond the deputy is possible at best.

Their tasks In this exciting function, you lead a linguistically and culturally heterogeneous class with clarity and prudence. With language-sensitive lessons, you promote the acquisition of the school language German and prepare the children for the transition to the rule classes. They maintain an appreciative exchange with the parents and work openly and constructively with the teachers of the other language integration classes as well as the teachers of the receiving rule classes.

The following shall apply

  • Tasks according to professional mandate for teachers of the city of Chur in the fields of teaching, learners, school and teacher.
  • Your profile

They have completed training as primary teachers. Ideally, you have completed the CAS "German as a Second Language (DaZ)" or have experience in DaZ teaching. They teach with joy and enthusiasm, appreciate the encounter with people from different cultures and see linguistic and cultural diversity as an opportunity. They build sustainable relationships with the children, accompany them reliably and are the contact person for their parents.

They are open to new things, actively contribute to the team and shape the further development of the school unit. In everyday school life, you show yourself flexible and resilient and maintain your humor even in demanding situations.
