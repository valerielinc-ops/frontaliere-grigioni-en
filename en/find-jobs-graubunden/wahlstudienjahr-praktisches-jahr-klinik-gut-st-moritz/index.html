Full description

As part of the elective or practical year, we offer you the opportunity at our clinic locations in St. Moritz and Fläsch to deepen your knowledge in an exciting, practical environment. Note: All internships are currently occupied at the St. Moritz location until mid-2028. We will not resume further planning from mid-2028 until January 2027, i.e. applications will then be processed again. We do not keep a waiting list and will not hold applications. At our location in Fläsch there are still free places. As a sub-assistant doctor you are an active part of our team and get to know the versatile facets of orthopaedics and traumatology. You work in the emergency and orthopaedic-surgical consultation hours and support our teams on the station. You take on responsibility in everyday hospital life and gain valuable experience – from assistance in the operating room to patient care. Under the guidance of experienced specialists, you will also learn how to carry out small-scale surgery and care. Your training

Interest in orthopaedics, surgery and sports medicine Motivation, initiative and enjoyment of teamwork Enrolled study status in human medicine Apply now Would you like to deepen your knowledge in a dynamic environment and actively shape medicine?
