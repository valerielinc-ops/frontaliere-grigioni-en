Full description

Live and work in the Destination Davos Klosters? We look forward to reinforcement! ##STR1##

In the function as station manager of the acute care department, you are responsible for the technical and personnel management and work practically in your team. They support the employees in ensuring the perfect operation of day-to-day business and in nursing care in the acute area.

As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable, but for the region extremely important company. We maintain a collegial working atmosphere and enjoy the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos.

By agreement, we are looking for a senior care specialist HF/FH as

Station management acute department (80-100%) ##

Your responsibilities

  • Comprehensive nursing care for inpatient and outpatient patients
  • Planning and documentation of the care process in close coordination with the qualified nursing staff
  • Planning, coordination and supervision of care in cooperation with the medical service and interdisciplinary services
  • Responsibility for leadership, commitment and development of directly subordinate employees, conducting regular assessment interviews
  • Support and advice of the care team in complex care situations
  • Ensuring a high quality of care and continuous optimization of work processes
  • Responsibility for the cost centre of the acute care department
  • Regular participation in nursing cadre meetings

Your profile

  • Completed training at tertiary level in the care sector and several years of professional experience in an acute hospital
  • Designated managerial experience or willingness to pursue relevant training
  • Enjoyment of a seasonal operation with intensive phase in winter in the enlarged team, lively summer months and quieter times in the middle season
  • Strong communication and organisational capacity, high social skills, sense of responsibility, empathy and assertiveness
  • Self-responsible, innovative and goal-oriented working method
  • Respectful and appreciative treatment of the team and patients
  • Excellent oral and written knowledge of German

Our offer

  • Demanding, varied and interesting work in a professional and committed team
  • Independence with a lot of personal responsibility and trust
  • Attractive employment conditions and social benefits, hospital crèche up to kindergarten age
  • Plenty of sports and leisure activities in the highest city in Europe

For further information, Bea Heeb, Head of Care and Cross Disciplines (telephone or email) as well as our HRM recruitment team are available to you by telephone or email.
