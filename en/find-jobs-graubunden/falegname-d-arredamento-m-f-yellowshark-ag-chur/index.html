01 Future employer

Seize your opportunity now – For our clients, we are continuously looking for motivated and customer-oriented cabinetmakers for temporary or permanent positions who can start immediately or by agreement.

02 Your duties

  • As a cabinetmaker, you can expect a varied and diverse role.
  • You will mainly work in the workshop and manufacture furniture, kitchens, doors and custom-made pieces.
  • Proper handling of machinery and compliance with all safety regulations are essential.
  • You have sound technical knowledge and are proficient in processing wood and panel materials.
  • Our clients place great value on an open work culture and promote teamwork.

04 Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as a carpenter in Switzerland or an EU country
  • Good knowledge of occupational safety
  • Reliability and ability to work quickly
  • Enthusiasm for working with wood
  • Aged between 20 and 50
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Friendly and customer-oriented manner
