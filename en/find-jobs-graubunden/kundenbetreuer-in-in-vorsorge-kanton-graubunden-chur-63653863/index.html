Pension Fund Graubünden PKGR \| 50\-100% \| Chur

The Graubünden Pension Fund is the largest pension fund in the canton of Graubünden. It provides occupational pensions for its approximately 13,000 insured and pensioners and manages assets of more than three billion francs.

Among our insured are the employees of the canton of Graubünden, the public institutions, municipalities, schools and institutions from the health and social sector. This diversity makes our work varied and creates exciting and challenging questions every day.

For our pension team, we are looking for a committed, service-oriented personality as a customer service provider. In this you are the first point of contact for our insured, employers and pensioners. If suitable and interested, we offer you the opportunity to develop into a specialist. You assume additional professional responsibility.

Your responsibilities

  • You process independently and professionally the pension transactions of our insured (entry and exit, wage and employment degree changes, divorces, home ownership promotion, purchases, retirements)
  • They are committed to supporting our insured, employers and pensioners in German and ideally in another official language.
  • You support the prevention team for disability and death in cooperation with our reinsurance
  • You are actively involved in the further development of processes and digital channels

That is what we want from you.

  • Basic commercial education
  • Experience in occupational pension schemes or social insurance is advantageous
  • High customer and service orientation
  • Very good knowledge of German and knowledge of other cantonal languages
  • Independent, exact and reliable working methods with high quality awareness and resilience in peak times
  • Joy of an open and respectful cooperation in a small team
  • Digital Affinity
