Specialist in paramedicine with specialist responsibility

This is waiting for you

  • You take on professional responsibility in the field of paramedicine
  • You assess and draft recommendations regarding the obligation to pay based on the legal basis of the Health Insurance Act
  • You ensure a uniform and technically sound performance assessment
  • You help with the processing and billing of services in the field of physiotherapy
  • You develop guidelines, processes and assessment criteria
  • You are observing developments in physiotherapy and the Swiss health care system
  • You work closely with internal specialist departments as well as external service providers and partners
  • You bring your expertise to projects and support the further development of performance management

You bring this with you

  • Completed training as a physiotherapist or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in physiotherapy
  • Knowledge of the Swiss health and health insurance system is an advantage
  • Analytical, structured and independent working method
  • Enjoying complex issues and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Good communication skills and safe appearance
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Other national languages are beneficial
  • Good IT knowledge (excel evaluations, creation of processes and guidelines, training facilities, etc.)
