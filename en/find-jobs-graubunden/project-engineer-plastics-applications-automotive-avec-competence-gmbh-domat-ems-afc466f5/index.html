Full description

You lead customer projects developing innovative plastics solutions for the automotive sector and provide technical consulting on materials and injection moulding.

Tasks

  • Lead projects developing innovative solutions with customers in the automotive sector
  • Provide technical consulting on material selection, component and material analysis, injection moulding processing
  • Conduct trials and sampling on modern equipment in the injection moulding technical center and at customer sites
  • Develop technical foundations, feasibility analyses and cost calculations
  • Work in cross-functional teams with research & development, production, material analytics, marketing & sales
  • Monitor the market for new trends, technologies and opportunities
  • Deliver technical presentations and conduct internal training sessions

Profile

  • Completed degree in plastics engineering, materials engineering or mechanical engineering
  • Experience in injection moulding technology and design of technical plastic components
  • Interest in current and future vehicle technologies
  • Decisive and entrepreneurial personality with strong goal orientation
  • Willingness to engage, learn, take initiative and remain flexible
  • Good written and spoken English

Offer

  • 3–6 months interim, then a permanent position
