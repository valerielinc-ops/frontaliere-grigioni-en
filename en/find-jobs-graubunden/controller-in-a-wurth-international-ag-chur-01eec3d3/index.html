Full description

What moves you

  • Check logistics calculations for deviations and anomalies, analyze and establish test and evaluation logics
  • Analyze freight costs and flows of goods and work with the specialist departments to develop opportunities for savings
  • They accompany projects from a controlling perspective and prepare key figures and reports for management and specialist areas
  • The development and further development of the controlling system landscape is part of your field of activity
  • Together with the specialist departments, you optimize controlling processes and reporting structures and act as a competent contact person for freight costs.

What you bring

  • Several years of experience in controlling, ideally with a focus on logistics, supply chain or transport controlling
  • Strong understanding of goods flows, freight costs and logistical connections
  • Very good analytical skills and a safe handling of deviation and cause analyses
  • Experience and pleasure in the development of dashboards and key figures systems
  • Knowledge of business intelligence (BI) or the willingness to become deeply involved
  • Very good Excel\ knowledge
  • Experience with automated interfaces and data models is an advantage
  • Communication strength and the ability to understand complex analyses
  • Confident, solution-oriented approach in exchange with our specialist areas
  • An independent, efficient and implementation-strong working method as well as openness for digitization and AI\-supported applications distinguish you
