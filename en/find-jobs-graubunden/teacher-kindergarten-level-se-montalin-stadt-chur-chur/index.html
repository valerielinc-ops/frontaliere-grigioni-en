Full description

Teacher Kindergarten Level, SE Montalin With 41'000 inhabitants, Chur is the urban center of Graubünden. As an attractive and versatile city, it provides customer-oriented and modern services with its 1,400 employees.

The Chur City School is the largest public school in the canton of Graubünden. Its offer covers all school levels from kindergarten to secondary level I. Special offers are bilingual classes German/Italian and German/Romanian, language integration classes, timeout classes and talent classes Music and Sports at secondary level I. The city school also includes daycare centers and school social work.

The Chur City School teaches more than 3000 students from around 450 teachers in 15 school units. Each school unit is led by a school principal, who together with the school directorate form the overall school management.

The Montalin school unit includes the primary school house and five kindergartens. Are you ready to shape the future of our students together with us?

We look forward to your application. To complement our team, we are looking for a school year 2027/2028 (August 1, 2027).

Teacher kindergarten level for the kindergarten Loë 3 approx. 90 %

Their tasks

  • Teaching with passion: In a positive learning atmosphere, you support the students in a two-stage kindergarten. Your teaching is based on the curriculum 21.
  • Teamwork and exchange: You work closely with a helpful team and a curative school teacher. Joint projects and events are important to us.
  • Cooperation with parents: They maintain a constructive and trusting exchange with parents in order to support the development of the children in the best possible way.

Further fields of activity are described in the professional assignment for teachers of the city of Chur in the fields of teaching, learners, school and teacher. Your profile

  • Education: A completed teaching diploma for the kindergarten.
  • Skills: You bring high didactic, educational and social skills to the team and are open and ready for feedback, innovation and further education.
  • Personality: Joy in dealing with children, commitment, flexibility and a positive, solution-oriented basic attitude.
