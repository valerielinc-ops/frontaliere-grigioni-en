Introduction

For a customer in the field of industrial production, we are looking for a polymechanic (m/f/d) with technical understanding and safe working methods. We are looking for a professionally experienced personality who reliably supports technical processes and feels comfortable in a professional production environment. If you are looking for a responsible job in an industrial environment, this position is a fitting opportunity.
