Full description

Do you enjoy daily contact with people and love advising and selling? Then an apprenticeship in retail is exactly right for you! During the three-year basic vocational training, you will acquire the skills you need for a successful start in the diverse world of retail at Post. Your training position is in Landquart.

Your training

  • As the face of Post, you are in daily contact with our customers at the branch, advising our customers on the entire range of physical and digital products at the counter and on the sales floor
  • Receiving letters and parcels, making deposits and withdrawals, selling products and services or providing needs-based advice – your tasks are varied and diverse.
  • Post is increasingly positioning itself as a service center for partner companies such as insurance companies, banks and health insurers – here you have the opportunity to acquire further industry knowledge.
  • The range of products offered in the branches includes additional products and services relating to topics such as housing, gifting and experiences – you are right in the middle of things when it comes to supporting national and regional campaigns and creating diverse shopping experiences for customers.
  • You will have the opportunity to acquire and deepen your professional skills in a team and at various branches.
  • In inter-company and internal classes, you connect theory with practical experience.

What you bring

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, enjoyment and motivation who want to make a difference and are open to new things. You should meet the following requirements:

  • You have successfully completed the intermediate level of compulsory school with good performance.
  • You have a flair for numbers and understand how to handle money.
  • You enjoy interacting with people, and you can express yourself appropriately for the situation both orally and in writing.
  • Your good organizational skills and your sense of order set you apart.
  • You are open, reliable, and discreet.
  • You are happy to share your professional knowledge.
