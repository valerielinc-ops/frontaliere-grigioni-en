Full description

Responsibilities

  • Be responsible for complex cardiology
  • You will care for inpatients and outpatients with complex cardiological diseases – including heart transplants, LVAD systems, heart failure, cardiac surgery and other demanding internal comorbidities.
  • Leading professionally
  • You work closely with nursing, therapy and other specialist areas and contribute to high-quality patient care.
  • Shaping interdisciplinary medicine
  • You will work on an equal footing with nursing, therapy and other specialist areas and jointly develop individual rehabilitation concepts with a lasting effect.
  • Developing young doctors
  • You train assistant doctors, promote their professional development and actively shape further training in cardiology.
  • Advancing cardiology
  • You will contribute your ideas to the professional, organizational and strategic development of cardiology and actively shape the further expansion of acute, highly specialized cardiological rehabilitation.
  • Contribute your own priorities
  • You have the opportunity to further develop specialist focuses, for example in the areas of heart failure, GUCH, cardiometabolic-renal medicine, device topics, sports cardiology or clinical research.

Requirements

  • Specialist qualification
  • You have the title of specialist in cardiology FMH (or equivalent), ideally supplemented by a second specialist title in general internal medicine.
  • Broad clinical experience
  • You have several years of experience in cardiology and feel confident even with complex patients.
  • Leadership skills
  • You lead appreciatively, make decisions and know how to win people over to common goals.
  • Joy of training
  • You support assistant doctors with commitment and promote their professional development.
  • Creative will
  • You would like to actively develop our cardiology department professionally, organizationally and strategically.
  • Team orientation
  • You value cooperation with other professional groups and are convinced that good medicine is created in a team.
