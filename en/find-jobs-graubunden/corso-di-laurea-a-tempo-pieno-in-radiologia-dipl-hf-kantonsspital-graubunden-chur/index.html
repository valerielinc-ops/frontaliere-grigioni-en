Full description

Start every mid-September the three-year full-time study Dipl. Radiology specialist / Radiology specialist HF 100 - 100%

Do you want to combine your technical curiosity with medical know-how and play a key role in modern diagnostics? Perfect! Studying for Dipl. Radiology specialist HF offers you exactly this opportunity – practical, future-oriented and full of development opportunities.

This training is your starting point to take your career to the next level and to position yourself in a highly modern professional field.

These are your tasks

  • You carry out radiological, nuclear medicine and radiotherapeutic examinations as well as treatments professionally
  • You serve the latest high-tech devices such as CT, MRI, linear accelerator and gamma camera safely and accurately
  • You care for patients empathetically and professionally throughout the entire examination or treatment process
  • You collect and manage investigation data digitally and provide complete documentation

And that's what we offer you.

  • You will be professionally accompanied and supported in your development
  • You can expect a motivated, warm and committed team
  • You work with a modern, medical infrastructure

That's what you used to say.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed a three-year vocational apprenticeship with a federal certificate of ability, an FMS degree or a high school diploma – and have successfully passed the aptitude procedure
  • You bring a good technical understanding and enjoyment of modern devices
  • You have empathy and strong communication and social skills
  • You are psychologically and physically resilient and keep track even in demanding situations

Can you do something that others can’t? You make the difference? Then you belong to us!
