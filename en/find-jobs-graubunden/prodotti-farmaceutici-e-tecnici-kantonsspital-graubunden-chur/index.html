Start on 1 November 2026 or by appointment as an employee: in technical service 80 - 100%

Our hospital technology ensures that everything works smoothly in the cantonal hospital, from building technology to medical equipment to the entire infrastructure. For the care of the hospital Oberengadin, we are building a new department within the KSGR Group – you are there right from the start and actively participate.

Are you interested in versatile building technology and the technical equipment of a hospital? Perfect, then we look forward to your application!

These are your tasks

  • You will take care of maintenance, repairs and improvements to building technology systems (HLKS, electrical, mechanical) as well as to medical equipment
  • You are responsible for troubleshooting and troubleshooting
  • You work closely with internal and external service providers and provide a 24-hour piquetting service with assignments outside regular working hours.

You bring that with you

  • A completed vocational apprenticeship in the field of engineering (building technology, electrical, electromechanical, HVAC or similar), ideally supplemented by further training in maintenance
  • Professional experience in building technology as well as good IT and German skills in words and writing
  • A responsible, independent and service-oriented personality with good manners
