Logistician/\-in suburban warehouse

Chur \| 80\-100 %

You can make this contribution

  • Responsible for the allocation of material for repair and inspection and check the condition and functionality of the material stored above and below ground and ammunition
  • Instruct and train the troops and specialists about the treatment and maintenance of the material mainly in German
  • Lead contact person for customers as well as the troop craftsmen
  • Responsible for the proper management of the work and personnel in the delivery and return of army material and ammunition of the troops in compliance with the prescribed dates
  • The material and ammunition are properly stored, inventoried and provided according to existing regulations and instructions

This makes you unique

  • Vocational apprenticeship as logistician/\-in EFZ
  • Pronounced service concept in contact with and in advising customers, for example the troop
  • Frequent and conscientious work and organisational skills to support a smooth operation
  • Category B, BE C, (CE and F), good SAP knowledge

To the point

As a key function, you will ensure the proper functioning of our acceptance, storage, shipping and delivery, as well as handover of Army equipment in your assigned area of expertise.

Your commitment to security and freedom

The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
