Inspector/consultant/in electrical installations or team leader/in 60%\-100 %

About us

ePowerCon AG offers independent, practical solutions for electrical safety.

In order to strengthen our team in Graubünden, we are looking for a committed personality as an inspector, electrical safety consultant or team leader/in electrical installations.

In this responsible function, you make an important contribution to the safety of electrical systems. You advise our customers competently, solution-oriented and in partnership

Your tasks

They combine professional control, security consulting and operational leadership. In doing so, you take responsibility for the quality of the electrical work, the cooperation with customers and partners as well as the further development of your area of responsibility.

+ Conduct final, acceptance, periodic and random checks on electrical installations + Prepare and test safety evidence and control reports + Assess electrical installations according to applicable standards, regulations and safety requirements + Advising customers, customers and installation companies on electrical safety issues + Document test results and accompany the correction of defects + Maintain customer relationships and contribute to a high quality of service + Organize control tasks independently and coordinate + Depending on the function, take responsibility for the professional leadership and support of the team

You bring that with you

+ Completed training as Federal Dipl. Electrical installer, electrical technician HF (authorized to control), electrical project manager or electrical safety consultant + Professional experience in electrical installation, experience with electrical controls is beneficial + Good knowledge of Swiss installation standards and legal regulations + Self-employed, reliable and responsible working methods + Strong communication and consulting competence + Category B driving licence

This is what we offer you

+ An exciting and flexible activity with a lot of freedom and responsibility + A modern working environment with contemporary digital infrastructure + Independent work and scope for design + Business car with private use and possibility for home office + Profit sharing and attractive employment conditions + Current social benefits + Support for further education and your personal development + A collaborative team, short decision paths and a long-term perspective

Ready for something new?

Then we look forward to your application and to getting to know you.
