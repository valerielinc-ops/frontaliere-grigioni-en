We bring ideas to life with you? *

In Engadin, hotels, residential buildings and commercial projects are being built that have to survive in the mountains. At the Samedan site, you work in the architecture team and transform designs into precise plans. You will develop works and detailed plans, which will be read and built later on the construction site. You work closely with project managers, construction managers and specialist planners. You take responsibility for your plans early on and see how it becomes built architecture. If you draw carefully and enjoy building construction, you will find an environment in which you can develop professionally.

Your tasks

  • Preparation of works and detailed plans
  • Development of design plans in close coordination with the project team
  • Support for project managers and sub-tasks
  • Adaptation and coordination of plan stands with specialist planners
  • Construction site operations for inventories and inspections

Your profile

  • Completed training as a draftsman: in EFZ field of architecture
  • Expertise in construction
  • Structured, careful and independent working methods
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Good MS\Office\ skills
  • ArchiCAD\ knowledge of advantages

We offer

  • Responsible work with real scope for design
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation at the locations Chur, Zurich and Engadin
  • Flexible working time models
  • Training budget and active promotion
  • Regular team events and cross-location activities
  • Working in the heart of Engadine.
