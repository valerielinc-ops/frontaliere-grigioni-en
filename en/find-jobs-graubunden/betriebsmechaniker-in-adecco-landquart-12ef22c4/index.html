Introduction

You have a technical flair and like to ensure that machines and plants run reliably? For our customer, we are looking for a committed personality as an operating mechanic who, with expertise and commitment, makes an important contribution to smooth production operations.

Tasks

  • Maintenance, inspection and repair of production facilities and technical facilities
  • Resolving malfunctions and performing error analyses
  • Ensuring the operational readiness of machinery and equipment
  • Provision of preventive maintenance work
  • Support for conversions, optimizations and commissioning
  • Documentation of the work carried out

Profile

  • Completed training as a polymechanic, production mechanic, plant and apparatus manufacturer, agricultural machinery mechanic or equivalent
  • Experience in the maintenance of machinery and equipment is advantageous
  • Technical understanding and skill
  • Independent, reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Driving licence Category B of Vorte

Advantages

  • Varied and responsible work
  • Modern work equipment and well-equipped workshop
  • Thorough training by a motivated team
  • Long-term applications with development potential
  • Attractive employment conditions
