Full description

Are you outgoing and want to share your enthusiasm for health and fitness? Do you enjoy working in a team and know how to respond to customer needs and motivate them to achieve their goals? Then you are the right fit for us!

Responsibilities

  • You supervise and support our clients throughout the entire training process
  • You create training plans and monitor trainings and goal achievement
  • You ensure cleanliness and hygiene within the studio
  • You conduct introductory trainings and sell memberships
  • You handle various administrative tasks, prepare contracts, and actively acquire future clients

Requirements

  • Desired: experience as a fitness instructor
  • Completed basic or advanced training: education as a fitness instructor with a valid BLS/AED certificate
  • You have a flair for sales as well as strong service and consulting skills
  • You are a motivated and responsible personality who inspires our clients
  • You are flexible and enjoy working evenings as well as weekends and holidays
  • You enjoy working in a team
  • You have a valid work permit
  • German (good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Central workplace: Our studios are located in well-connected central locations
  • Mobile subscription: National flat-rate subscription from CHF 10.– / month (Swisscom / digitec connect)
  • Migros Bank: Free private and investment account with preferential interest rates
  • Employee offers: Various discounts in the areas of holidays and insurance (Hertz up to 10%, Generali 25%, Axa Insurance up to 15%, etc.)
  • Informal culture: We stand for respectful interaction on equal terms
