Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services.

Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You monitor and execute the compaction of customer orders efficiently.
  • You make sure that no goods are damaged during compaction.
  • You control the completeness of the products provided per tour.
  • You manage empty containers reliably and systematically.
  • You comply with all occupational safety regulations as well as the legal QS guidelines.
  • If necessary, you can also support your team in picking or transport.

Requirements

  • You have long-term professional experience in logistics or a completed apprenticeship as a logistics specialist EFZ/EBA.
  • You have an SUVA-approved forklift certificate with experience.
  • You work structured, goal-oriented and take responsibility.
  • You are familiar with warehouse management systems.
  • You are physically resilient and ready for refrigeration (–20 °C to +5 °C).
  • You speak and write good German.
