Full description

Do you value a solid basic education? Are you a well-established and enthusiastic personality who has good manners, an interest in banking and enjoyment of customer contact? Then you may soon be our new team member.

Tasks

During your three-year training, you will gain insight into different departments and will be used in the following work situations:

  • Receive and forward bank customers
  • Advise bank customers
  • Support bank customers via digital channels
  • Assistance in advising customers
  • Prepare and process banking transactions administratively

In the various departments, you will be accompanied and supported by competent and experienced practical trainers. You can complete the training with us either in the EFZ advanced basic education or with vocational maturity.

Requirements

School

  • Completed folk level at top school level with good grades
  • Good knowledge of German, oral & written
  • With professional maturity: Very good performance at top school level

Personal

  • Contactful and communicative
  • Teamwork capability
  • Reliability and exact work
  • Open to new

Interests

  • Delight in everyday banking and customer contact
  • Trends and developments in the professional field and in the industry
  • Application of information sources and use of new technologies

We offer

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.
