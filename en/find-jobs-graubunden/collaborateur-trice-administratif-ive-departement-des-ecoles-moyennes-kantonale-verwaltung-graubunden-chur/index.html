Full description

In the field of secondary schools and tertiary education and research, the Office for Higher Education implements the educational policy mandates of the government and the Department of Education, Culture and Environmental Protection in favour of an attractive educational and research location Graubünden.

The secondary schools department shall be responsible for implementing the legal provisions applicable to secondary schools. The approximately 2,300 high school students in the Canton of Graubünden are divided between the Bundner Kantonsschule at the Chur location and the eight private secondary schools in the regions. Depending on the location, different courses are run – Gymnasium, Handelsmittelschule (HMS), Fachmittelschule (FMS) and Informatikmittelschule (IMS).

In the department of secondary schools of the Office of Higher Education we are looking for 1. March 2027 or by arrangement a case officer/in administration.

Tasks

  • Administrative processing in the field of cantonal entrance examinations
  • Support of websites and applications in the area of cantonal entrance examinations (e.g. browser-based software for examination registration) incl. User support for technical login questions
  • Administrative processing in the area of final examinations in secondary schools (e.g. assistance in the allocation, coordination and settlement of expert assignments)
  • Control and processing of data in the area of student administration as well as maintenance of the application used for this purpose incl. User support
  • General processing and secretarial work to support the department management
  • Deputy Secretariat (representation of holidays) and other tasks

Requirements

  • Commercial basic training with vocational maturity or commercial middle school with vocational maturity, additional further training in the administrative area are advantageous
  • In-depth knowledge of the Graubünden education system (transitions Primar–Sek I–Sek II, Structure Gymnasium/HMS/FMS/IMS)
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office applications (especially Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook) and affinity for digital working methods and database systems
  • Knowledge of the cantonal digital systems CMI and Abacus is an advantage
  • Accurate, reliable and independent work with high quality awareness and resilience in peak times
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Knowledge of several cantonal languages
  • Strong service orientation, teamwork and enjoyment of contact with schools, parents and authorities
  • Readiness for flexible work arrangements, especially during the entrance examination phase from November to April
