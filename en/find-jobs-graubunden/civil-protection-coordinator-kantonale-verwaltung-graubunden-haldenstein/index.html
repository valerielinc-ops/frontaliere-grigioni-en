Full description

The Office for Civil Protection and Military Affairs is the cantonal specialist for all aspects of military, civil protection and civil protection and implements the corresponding laws.

In the area of civil protection, plans and preparations for disasters are prepared together with other services and the necessary assistance is coordinated in the event of damage events.

The Office for Civil Protection and Military is looking for a Civil Protection Coordinator as of 01.04.2027 or by agreement

Tasks

  • Development of concepts and project management and coordination in the area of civil protection
  • Implementation of integral risk management in the Canton of Graubünden in close cooperation with other departments and institutions
  • Support in the field of staff work in the Cantonal Management Staff
  • Participation in the planning, organisation and implementation of education and training for members of municipal leadership staffs and the cantonal leadership staff
  • Representation of the department in regional and national specialised bodies and in interdisciplinary working groups

Requirements

  • University of applied sciences or university degree (Bachelor/Master) or many years of professional experience in the field of civil protection
  • Military cadre function as well as good knowledge of civil protection partners
  • High willingness to provide services and teamwork in an interdisciplinary environment
  • Innovative, networked thinking personality with strategic, conceptual and communicative strengths
  • Skilled organizational, planning and coordination skills
  • Analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is an advantage
