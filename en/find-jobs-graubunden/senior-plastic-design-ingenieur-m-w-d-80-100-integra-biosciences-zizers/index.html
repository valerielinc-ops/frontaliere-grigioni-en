Full description

Are you ready to innovate to improve the lives of millions of people? You think one step further, see opportunities where others see limits – and want to invest your energy in something meaningful? Then you are exactly right at INTEGRA. Our solutions are not created by chance – they are the result of passion, precision and team spirit. With every technology we develop, we help people around the world research diseases, make diagnoses and enable new therapies. Whether in diagnostics, research or quality control: Our instruments “Made in Switzerland & USA” make laboratory work more efficient, safer and more sustainable.

What you do with us moves the world. Sounds like what you’re looking for? Then become part of our team – and help accelerate scientific discoveries. What you can do

  • Construction of individual parts and assemblies and their implementation, from conception to introduction to production
  • Creating mechanical plastic solutions for our liquid handling products in special consumables and their integration into complete systems – with a focus on injection molding
  • Planning, design and development of plastic articles from ideation to series introduction as well as support for device development in material, plastic, tool and production engineering issues
  • Optimizing and simulating injection molded parts and processes
  • Production documents for drawings, parts lists and assembly instructions
  • Purchasing and material procurement for functional samples and prototypes
  • Coordination with internal and external customers, suppliers and service providers

What you bring

  • Basic technical training as a mechanic or constructor with a focus on plastics technology
  • Completed technical studies at FH level or as a technician HF Mechanical Engineering with a specialization in plastics technology or adequate
  • Several years of professional experience as a development engineer in the field of plastics
  • Profound experience in the development of injection moldings and tool design
  • Good CAD knowledge, ideally with Solid Works
  • hands-on mentality, as well as enthusiasm for creative solutions and innovative products
  • Good communication skills in German and English
