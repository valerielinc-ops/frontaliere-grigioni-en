Working Student Technical Writing 60 \- 80 % (f/m/d)

For more than 40 years, Medical has shaped the future of mechanical ventilation. We develop Intelligent Ventilation solutions that support clinicians in critical moments.

Our ventilators are used in intensive care units, during emergency transport, and in MRI environments. They help medical teams care for adult, pediatric, and neonatal patients worldwide.

Here, your work has real impact. Every improvement you make supports better respiratory care for patients around the globe.

How you can make a difference

We are seeking a dedicated, motivated individual to support our small team of information developers who provide the end\-user documentation for our devices and related products. You will support our team by performing and managing documentation releases, helping to manage documentation and software localization, managing the purchase order/invoice processing for the group, and assisting the writing team and related groups in a variety of other ways. These tasks involve working with various groups in the organization (including the writers, product management, product care, marketing, accounting, purchasing, and others) and external suppliers (including translation and print vendors).

You also have the ability and desire to work collaboratively with the team.
