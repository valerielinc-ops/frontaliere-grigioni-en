Full description

Start by appointment as a nurse / nurse specialist recovery room and day clinic 70 - 100%

The Institute of Anesthesiology ensures the anesthesiological and perioperative care of our patients at the Chur and Walenstadt locations – with three recovery rooms and the day clinics Fontana and Walenstadt. In our experienced team, the focus is on personal and professional support: from recovery room to discharge.

We are looking for an empathetic personality who takes responsibility, creates closeness and lives quality. Sounds like exactly your environment? Perfect, then we look forward to meeting you.

These are your tasks

  • You look after and monitor patients in the recovery room and in the day clinic before and after surgical procedures and ensure safe, perioperative care.
  • You carry out individual pain therapy, recognize postoperative changes or complications early and initiate the necessary measures
  • You work closely with the interprofessional team in a 24-hour operation across locations in Chur and Walenstadt and contribute to a high quality of treatment and a smooth process in the recovery room and in the day clinic.

You bring that with you

  • A diploma as a nurse / nurse specialist HF/FH and at least one year of professional experience in the acute hospital
  • Willingness to complete a post-diploma course in supervision care
  • An empathetic and flexible personality with a strong ability to act and make decisions

Can you do something that others can’t? You make the difference? Then you belong to us!
