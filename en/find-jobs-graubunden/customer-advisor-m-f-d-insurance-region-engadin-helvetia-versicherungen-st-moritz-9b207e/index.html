Client Consulting – Sales

Pensum: 50-100% Location: Experience level: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than just completing tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high standards, and bring the courage to break new ground. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand, and where your ideas, initiative, and contribution count. This gives you the opportunity to truly make a difference for our customers, your team, and your personal development.

What you will achieve

  • Consulting & support with foresight: You accompany private and corporate clients in all insurance matters with genuine commitment and ensure tailored solutions for every individual need. As a client advisor, you are there when it matters.
  • Building long-term relationships: Trust is at the center of your work – you cultivate and develop sustainable customer relationships based on your excellent service.
  • Actively shaping growth: You have a good network and contribute your energy to acquiring new customers. Your commitment ensures that our customer base grows steadily. How can we support you in this? By providing you with your own existing customer portfolio of private and corporate clients.
  • Marketing & sales support: With fresh ideas, you support our marketing campaigns, attend trade fairs and events to strengthen our network.
  • Always keeping your finger on the pulse: Through our basic training and continuous further education, you remain professionally up to date and offer the highest consulting competence.

What you bring

  • Experience & expertise: You bring experience in customer consulting or sales – ideally in the financial or insurance sector. Ambitious career changers who are ready to hit the ground running are also very welcome.
  • Communication talent: Inspiring and winning people over is in your blood. You shine through your strong communication and negotiation skills. As a client advisor, you can argue convincingly and have a command of German in writing and speech.
  • Service is your strength: Field service is your passion, and your high customer orientation and service readiness set you apart.
  • Engagement & initiative: With above-average motivation and entrepreneurial spirit, you are ready to achieve great things and exceed targets.
  • Regional anchoring: Your place of residence is in the Engadin region, where you look after your customers. You have a strong network in this region and appreciate building and maintaining new contacts.

Ready to inspire customers with passion and grow with us? Then use your talent purposefully and apply now!
