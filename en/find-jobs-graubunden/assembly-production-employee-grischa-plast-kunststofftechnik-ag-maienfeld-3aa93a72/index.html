Employee/installation (m/f/d)

Three locations. One team. Your future.

The PLAST Group unites Stüdli Plast AG in Romanshorn, Grischa Plast AG in Maienfeld and Küng Plast AG in Uznach. With around 76 employees and 64 machines, we combine many years of experience with modern plastic technology and innovative manufacturing solutions.

From large components and assembly to precision injection molding, toolmaking and 3K technology, to medical technology, micro injection molding and measurement technology, our three locations offer a versatile working environment.

As a certified group of companies, we stand for quality, innovation and continuous development. At the same time, we focus on modern workplaces, flexible working time models, short decision-making processes and a corporate culture in which our employees are valued as people and specialists.

Discover your possibilities at the PLAST Group – and shape the future with us.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed and motivated personality as soon as possible or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • Assembly and assembly of plastic parts and assemblies
  • Performing manual assembly and post-processing work
  • Performance of visual and quality controls
  • Detection and sorting out of defective parts
  • Packaging and provision of the manufactured products
  • Compliance with quality and production specifications
  • Ensuring a clean and orderly work environment
  • Assistance in optimizing workflows and assembly processes

Your profile

  • Technical understanding and skill
  • Good fine motor skills and careful working methods
  • Quality awareness and reliability
  • Self-employed and responsible work
  • Teamwork and flexibility
  • Willingness to familiarize yourself with new tasks and workflows

We offer

  • A varied and responsible activity in an innovative company
  • Modern workplaces and a pleasant working atmosphere
  • Flexible working time models for a better work-life balance
  • Attractive employment conditions and benefits
  • Individual training opportunities
  • A collegial environment with flat corporate structures
