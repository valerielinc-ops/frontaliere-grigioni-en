Full description

Be responsabile dello sviluppo del Glacier Express come prodotto premium nel turismo internazionale. Grazie alla tua sensibilità per mercato, esperienza cliente e qualità, sviluppi offerta, pricing e concetti operativi per B2C e B2B. Coordini partner chiave, gestisci budget e contratti e implementi innovazioni come sistemi infotainment, garantendo un’esperienza coerente e memorabile lungo tutto il viaggio.

Cosa puoi fare

  • You lead Glacier Express AG, assume overall responsibility for results, quality, market position and organisation and report directly to the Board of Directors.
  • You will further develop the corporate strategy and business model, strengthen earnings power and tap into new growth and sales potential for attractive, sustainable guest experiences.
  • You strengthen the international market positioning of the Glacier Express and are responsible for the further development of the brand, product, marketing, sales and customer experience.
  • You control finance, budget, forecast, investments and revenue management, thereby creating the basis for sustainable growth.
  • You lead the six-member team of Glacier Express AG and ensure clear responsibilities, binding cooperation and effective implementation.
  • You control the complex interaction of the Rhaetian Railway, Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn, Panoramic Gourmet AG and other service and sales partners and consistently align the different interests with common goals.
  • You represent the Glacier Express in the tourist and economic environment and maintain a sustainable network with destinations, partners, authorities and media.

Ecco perché sei la persona giusta’

  • You have a higher education or university degree, preferably in business administration, tourism, mobility or hospitality.
  • You have in-depth marketing knowledge, a good understanding of digital developments and know how to use AI specifically for effective marketing.
  • You bring multi-year overall management and earnings responsibility from tourism, hospitality or a comparable service industry.
  • You know international markets and have proven experience in strategic product development and product marketing.
  • You combine entrepreneurial and strategic thinking with strong implementation strength and secure financial understanding.
  • You move confidently in complex partner structures, lead bindingly even without direct authority and can combine different interests into viable solutions.
  • You are a compelling, winning personality that brings people together, builds relationships, and credibly represents the Glacier Express inside and out.
  • An affinity for rail, gastronomy and high-quality tourist experiences as well as knowledge of sales, booking or revenue management systems are advantageous.
  • You communicate with confidence in negotiations in German and English. Other languages are welcome.

Cosa ti offriamo’

  • A key entrepreneurial position with a great deal of room for manoeuvre
  • The opportunity to lead an internationally known Swiss premium brand into the future
  • A versatile field of activity in tourism, mobility, gastronomy, brand and sales
  • Attractive employment conditions and contemporary forms of work

  • The application period shall run until 30. November 2026.

  • In January 2027, an assessment will follow to get to know your understanding of leadership and your impact.
  • In a further discussion, you will answer the questions of selected members of the Board of Directors. The decision is planned until the end of January 2027.
