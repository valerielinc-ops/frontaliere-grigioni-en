Chauffeur / Chauffeur

Main tasks

They supply our agricultural and horticultural customers with high-quality seeds in the Northeast Switzerland region. They work reliably and responsibly in a small, collegial team with fixed tours and mainly in the regional area of operation (no overnight stays).

  • Delivery of seeds and aids to farmers and landscapers by truck of the Cat.C
  • Loading, securing and unloading of goods
  • Operation of the corresponding conveying means for loading and unloading
  • Careful handling of transport goods and vehicles
  • Direct customer contact during delivery
  • Care and simple maintenance work on the vehicle

Requirements

  • The ticket Cat. C and several years of professional practice as a chauffeur
  • Ticket Cat CE is an advantage
  • Forklift driver embodiment
  • Physical fitness and pleasure in practical work
  • Self-employed, responsible and reliable flexible working methods
  • Flowing communication in Swiss German is mandatory, as direct customer contact during delivery with farmers and landscape gardeners
