Full description

As a health specialist, you work closely with clients, their relatives and people from different professions and cultures. The training is very varied due to the active rotation principle in various fields of work. You will complete assignments in the acute hospital, in the Spitex, as well as in the rehabilitation clinic. As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees.
