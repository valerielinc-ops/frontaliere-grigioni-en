Design engineer EFZ 80\-100%\ interface between engineering and production

Our client is a company based in the canton of Graubünden. It has developed over decades thanks to its central in the Alps into a special supplier of valves for hydropower plants. Today, customers worldwide draw on the company’s expertise to deploy reliable safety organs in hydropower plants. For more than 50 years, it has specialized in the development and production of valves for hydropower plants.
