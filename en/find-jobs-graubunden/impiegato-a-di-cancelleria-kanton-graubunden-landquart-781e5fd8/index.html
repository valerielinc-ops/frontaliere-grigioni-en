Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the Cantonal Administration Graubünden together with us.

The Regionalgericht Landquart is a first instance civil and criminal court based in Landquart. As a law firm employee, you are jointly responsible in a small team for a smooth and error-free process of all law firm-related business of the court company.

We are looking for the Regional Court Landquart per 1\. January 2027 or by agreement a law firm employee.

Your responsibilities

  • Administrative management of cases (from their receipt to their archiving)
  • Organisation and follow-up of negotiations, hearings and hearings
  • Writing of hearing and negotiation minutes
  • Making decisions independently or according to specifications
  • Independent writing of various correspondences, orders of procedure and decisions
  • Switch and telephone service

That is what we want from you.

  • completed administrative or commercial apprenticeship
  • Ideally, several years of professional experience in a court or in the profession
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Good knowledge of IT
  • structured, forward-looking, independent and precise working method
  • Team capable and reliable personality
