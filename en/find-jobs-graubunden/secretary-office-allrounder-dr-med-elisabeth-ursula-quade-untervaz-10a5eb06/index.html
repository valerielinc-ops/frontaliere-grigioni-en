Full description

Secretary:in / practice administration (m/f/d) – approx. 30 % Place of work: Untervaz (GR) Job opening: By immediately or by agreement (including careful training)

For our psychiatric-psychotherapeutic individual practice in Untervaz, we are looking for a reliable, discreet and independent personality for practice administration and billing. This position offers a manageable, well-planned time horizon (probably 1 to 3 years, depending on further practical planning).

Their main tasks

  • They are the first point of contact for our patients (telephone and e-mail) and maintain patient dossiers.
  • They take over the ongoing performance recording and billing according to TARDOC. If suitable, you will also be responsible for monitoring the incoming payments (meditating and rejection management with health insurance funds, IV and other cost carriers).
  • They handle general practice correspondence, prepare medical reports administratively and coordinate dispatch to insurers and assigning agencies.
  • They ensure a smooth administrative process in the background, order practice and office materials and support the practice owner in everyday life.

Your profile

  • You have a basic commercial education (KV) or an education as MPA / medical secretary:in. Cross-enterprises from the health insurance, social security or hospital sector are also welcome.
  • They like to work with numbers, understand administrative processes quickly and ideally bring initial knowledge in ambulatory service billing (TARDOC).
  • They work precisely, structured and independently. When dealing with patients, you are characterized by empathy, calmness, secrecy and a well-groomed appearance.
  • You are confident in dealing with the common office programs and quickly work your way into practical software.

What we offer you

  • A versatile task with a foreseeable time horizon – ideal for people who are looking for a meaningful part-time job without years of commitment (e.g. as a re-entry, bridge or until their own retirement).
  • They will be introduced comprehensively and step by step by the previous holder in all processes and in the TARDOC billing.
  • A responsible hub function in a quiet, family setting – without weekend or emergency services and with flexible division of working days.
