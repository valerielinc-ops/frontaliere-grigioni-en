Full description

The Spital Oberengadin AG in Samedan, a company of the KSGR Group, is responsible for basic medical care as well as emergency care for the population and guests. We are looking for a permanent contract for the period from 01.04.2027 to 30.06.2027 or by agreement

Their tasks

  • Care and supervision of patients in complex and unstable care situations
  • Assistance and support of relatives in demanding situations
  • Early recognition of changes and professional action in acute situations
  • postoperative surveillance and accompaniment in the recovery room
  • Co-responsibility in diagnostic and therapeutic measures
  • Interprofessional cooperation with different disciplines
  • Active participation in a professional and human-influenced working environment
  • Electronic patient documentation and administrative activities

Your profile

  • Training as a Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH or a recognised foreign diploma
  • ideally post-diploma certificate IMC / supervision care or the willingness to complete it
  • Professional experience in IPS, IMC, emergency or anesthesia beneficial
  • Enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation
  • Responsible, independent and reliable working methods
  • Calm and overview even in challenging situations
  • Empathetic, reflective and team-oriented personality

Pensum: 60-80 %
