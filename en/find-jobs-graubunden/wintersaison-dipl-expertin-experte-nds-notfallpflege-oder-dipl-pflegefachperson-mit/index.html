Full description

Spital Oberengadin AG in Samedan, a company of the KSGR Group, is responsible for basic medical care and emergency care for the population and guests with the largest hospital in southern Germany. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a colleague for the winter season (December 2026 to March/April 2027).

Their tasks

  • Care during and after small outpatient operations
  • Follow-up checks
