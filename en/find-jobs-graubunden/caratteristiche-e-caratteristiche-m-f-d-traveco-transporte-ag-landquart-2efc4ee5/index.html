The company headquarters Landquart is looking for you as a dispatcher or dispatcher immediately or by agreement.

Disponentine / Disponent (m/f/d)

  • These tasks inspire you *
  • You are responsible for daily transport, deployment and route planning as well as personnel and vehicle management
  • Ensuring optimal utilization and timely delivery
  • Continuous optimization of processes, structures and interfaces in cooperation with adjacent areas
  • Co-responsible for operational decisions, escalation management and management of around 45 employees
  • Participation in projects, in particular the new TMS/WMS landscape
  • If necessary, assumption and responsibility of day-to-day operations

We are looking forward to this profile

  • Several years of professional experience in disposition and advantageous already first experience in a management function
  • Further education in the field of transport and logistics, ideally with a federal degree
  • Sound knowledge of disposition and transport control
  • Strong leadership and social skills as well as a solution-oriented, positive basic attitude
  • High IT affinity and ready to invest in digitalization
  • Willingness to work shifts in the event of holiday absences and staff shortages (05:00 – 14:00 / 13:30 – 22:00) and to perform Sunday service once a month
