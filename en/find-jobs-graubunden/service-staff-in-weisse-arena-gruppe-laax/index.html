Full description

The White Arena Group in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and an enthusiastic community for unique mountain adventures. With mountain railways, hotels, gastronomy, sports and rental shops and the LAAX School, we create unforgettable experiences for our guests - and attractive workplaces for our employees. Casa Veglia – Alpine Classics & Family Joy Casa Veglia is a place where charm and history are celebrated. Here our cuisine combines the best of Swiss food culture: delicate fondue chinoise, classic cheese fondue and classics, such as our homemade capuns. | Imagine you strengthen our team as a service employee: in and inspire our guests with your sense of pleasure, warmth and a service that stays in the memory. What You Move:

  • You look after our guests in the à-la-carte and/or bar area with friendliness, attention and a high degree of professionalism
  • You support our guests in the selection of food and drinks and convince by actively selling our specialties
  • You provide an organized service and a well-kept, welcoming environment
  • You participate in events, themed evenings or special promotions in the restaurant and bar
  • You take care of a perfectly prepared service area (Mise en Place) and ensure cleanliness and order.
  • You operate our checkout system safely and reliably and carry out the debt collection independently
  • You consistently ensure compliance with our quality and hygiene standards (HACCP) | What makes you stand out:
  • You already have practical experience in the service area and know what matters
  • With your positive charisma and your team spirit you ensure a pleasant atmosphere
  • You keep a cool head even in stressful situations and adapt flexibly to different requirements
  • You have good German skills, English skills are an advantage – other foreign languages are a plus
  • You work reliably and responsibly, but you do not lose sight of the details. An attractive workplace in the middle of an extraordinary mountain world is certain for you. You become part of a dynamic, bright team and can help shape the further development of LAAX. With us you will find an environment in which professionalism, speed, pragmatism and humanity go together. Look forward to numerous employee benefits, such as free mountain rides, perks in our shops, hotels and restaurants, and benefits for your family. Start your professional adventure with the Weissen Arena Group with your complete online application.
