Introduction

For our customer, a successful and modern production company, we are looking for a committed and reliable personality as a plant manager. In this responsible position, you ensure the smooth operation of the production facilities and make an important contribution to maintaining high quality standards. If you have technical understanding, are happy to take on responsibility and are looking for a varied job in a dynamic environment, we look forward to your application.

Tasks

  • Operation, monitoring and control of production and production facilities
  • Ensuring a smooth production process
  • Implementation and adjustment work on machinery
  • Resolving minor incidents and supporting technical issues
  • Implementation of quality controls and documentation of results
  • Compliance with safety, hygiene and quality standards
  • Maintenance and cleaning services of equipment

Cooperation in improvement processes to optimize production and efficiency

Profile

  • Completed technical or craft vocational training beneficial
  • Experience as a plant operator, machine operator or in a production plant
  • Technical understanding and interest in production facilities
  • Self-employed, reliable and responsible work
  • Readiness for shift work
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Teamwork as well as high quality and safety orientation
  • Resilience and flexibility

Advantages

Modern production facilities and a safe working environment

Comprehensive induction and training opportunities

Collegial working atmosphere and motivated team

Regular employee events and additional social benefits
