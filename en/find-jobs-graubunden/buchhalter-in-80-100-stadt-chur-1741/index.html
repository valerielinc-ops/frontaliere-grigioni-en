Full description

Accountant (80 - 100%) With 41'000 inhabitants, Chur is the urban center of Graubünden. As an attractive and versatile city, it provides customer-oriented and modern services with its 1,400 employees.

The department Finance, Tax and Resident Services is responsible for the proper management of the financial budget, the debt management including debt collection, the tax assessments, as well as the management of the residents and business register of the city of Chur.

We are looking for an established, responsible and dynamic personality immediately or by agreement to complement our dedicated team as

Accountant (80 - 100%)

Their tasks

  • Create VAT bills and support the VAT departments of the city
  • Accounting and reconciliation of municipal taxes (-konti) and church communities
  • Control and reconciliation of balance sheet and delivery accounts
  • Checking the correct accounting of secondary programmes
  • Participation in financial statements
  • Self-employed management of secondary accounts
  • Support for the Head of Accounting
  • Preparation of statistics and evaluations

Your profile

  • Eidg. Financial, accounting or training certificate
  • Abacus knowledge
  • Sound practical experience in financial accounting and security in final theses
  • Strong understanding of numbers and a very careful working method
  • Ability to set priorities and address multiple topics simultaneously
  • Rapid comprehension and structured and forward working methods
  • Safe oral and written expression
  • Clever handling of MS-Office
  • Enjoying digital work processes and new tools
  • Self-employment, initiative and responsibility

Our offer We offer you varied and responsible work in a motivated and qualified team. Attractive employment conditions, a modern infrastructure and a centrally located workplace complete our offer.
