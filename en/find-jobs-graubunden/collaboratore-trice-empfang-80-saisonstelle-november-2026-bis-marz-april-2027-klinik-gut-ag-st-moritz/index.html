Full description

Become part of our winter team – in the middle of beautiful Engadin! Klinik Gut is an established private clinic for orthopaedics and accident surgery. We also serve our national and international customers at the clinic locations in St. Moritz and Fläsch in the practice locations in Chur, Buchs, Ascona and Zurich. For the winter season 2026/27 we are looking for you as Employee reception 80% (seasonal position, November 2026 until the end of March 2027) You are our warm welcome at the reception and together with our team ensure a smooth routine in the clinic. Your main tasks are

  • Patient admission and organisation of consultation hours for all doctors and emergencies
  • Information provided on reception and telephone
  • Oral and written contact with patients, insurance companies and employers as well as general administration tasks

You bring with you

  • Experience in healthcare (e.g. MPA), in the hotel industry or with a commercial background
  • Reliable, empathetic and flexible personality that does not lose track even in a lively environment
  • Joy of direct patient contact
  • Very good planning and organizational ability with structured working methods
  • Good language skills (D, I, E) and advanced PC skills

You can look forward to that.

  • A varied task in a committed, warm team
  • Current employment conditions
  • Possibility to shape processes and take responsibility
  • And best of all: work where others go skiing – in the middle of Oberengadin with all its leisure opportunities and natural wonders
