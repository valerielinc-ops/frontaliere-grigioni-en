Full description

We are looking for a/- as a reinforcement of our activation team n

Tasks

Main tasks

  • You plan, organize and realize independently resource-oriented group and individual activities
  • You plan and lead excursions, events and seasonal activities
  • You contribute to the design of decorations and the annual activities calendar
  • You work closely with your teammates and carers and actively participate in team meetings
  • You pass on observations professionally and document them value-free

Requirements

Requirement profile

  • You have completed training as a specialist in everyday life design and activation. Specialist care, social care or a comparable education - also lateral entrants with a willingness for further education are welcome
  • You are sociable, resilient and communicative and meet older people with empathy, patience, appreciation and a professional attitude
  • You are creative, have good organizational skills and enjoy teamwork
  • You are ready to work in the various Chüra plants in Scuol & Zernez and are flexible for isolated evening and weekend assignments
  • You have good knowledge of German and ideally also Romanesque

We offer

We offer you

  • a meaningful work with a lot of design possibilities
  • a responsible and varied activity in an appreciative environment
  • Attractive, contemporary employment conditions
  • generous support for training
  • A motivated and very committed team

Under the umbrella of the “Center da sandà Engiadina Bassa” (CSEB) / “Health Center Unterengadin”, important regional providers in the areas of health care and wellness are taking a common path. In the national park region, a wonderful environment awaits you with versatile recreation and sports opportunities.

Long-term care in Lower Engadin is based on a holistic model of care and care, in which the needy people are at the center. In addition to professional care and empathetic care, we attach great importance to a varied and meaningful everyday life.

Is your heart beating for older people? Do you see the opportunity to contribute your skills and experience to us in a meaningful way and thus have a positive impact on the quality of life of our residents? Then a dedicated activation team looks forward to you as a new colleague/colleague.

For further information please contact Agnes Kiesler, Head of Activation, telephone +41 81 861 26 52 or agnes.kiesler@cseb.ch.
