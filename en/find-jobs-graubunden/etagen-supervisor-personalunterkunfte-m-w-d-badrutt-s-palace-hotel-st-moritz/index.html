Floor Supervisor Staff Housing (m/f/d) — Badrutt's Palace Hotel

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Your key responsibilities

  • You lead and motivate a team of up to 5 employees with confidence and flair.

  • Ensuring impeccable cleaning and maintenance standards across up to 300 staff rooms or 150 holiday apartments is a key part of your role.

  • Within the assigned staff areas of the hotel, you ensure outstanding levels of cleanliness and order at all times.

  • As a skilled organiser, you oversee linen management and small inventory, ensuring smooth and efficient operations.

  • Close collaboration with the Technical Services and Human Resources departments enables you to maintain the highest standards of service quality.

  • By actively participating in team training sessions, you contribute to the continuous development of our cleaning standards.

• What you bring to the job

For this varied and exciting position, you ideally bring the following qualifications and qualities:

  • A completed professional qualification in hospitality, combined with relevant work experience, provides the ideal foundation for this role.

  • Previous leadership experience has given you the confidence to support the management of a team.

  • Good spoken English and Italian skills are required, while knowledge of German is considered an advantage.

  • Confident computer skills enable you to handle your daily tasks efficiently.

  • A high level of initiative, commitment, and reliability characterises your way of working.

  • Your strong sense of quality is complemented by excellent attention to detail.

  • With a confident and professional appearance, you demonstrate excellent manners and interpersonal skills.

Our offer

  • Permanent full-time position in St. Moritz.

  • Average 5-day working week with 43.5 working hours.

  • 5 weeks of vacation and 6 public holidays per year.

  • 13th salary from the start date (pro rata temporis).

  • Accommodation in one of our new staff houses at the beginning upon request.

  • Meals available in our new and modern staff restaurant.

  • Opportunity to work in an attractive environment with development and career growth opportunities.

If you are looking for an interesting, challenging, and varied role in a dynamic environment and would like to join a well-established team, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
