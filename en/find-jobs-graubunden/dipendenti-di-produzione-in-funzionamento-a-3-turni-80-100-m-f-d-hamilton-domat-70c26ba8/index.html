Company Hamilton AG

True to the vision -We drive innovation to improve people-s lives- Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

The Operations business unit produces parts for all Hamilton companies from injection moulded parts to pipette tips for liquid handling to metal and plastic parts. Our goal is to produce as many parts as possible in-house. In this way, we remain agile, independent and efficient.

*You can do that

  • Operation and monitoring of production plants towards injection moulding, automation and packaging plants
  • Detecting, analysing and resolving simple faults and taking appropriate immediate action
  • Reporting and documentation of incidents and cooperation with maintenance in the event of technical problems
  • Implementation of quality controls and documentation
  • Documentation of production data and plant states
  • Ensuring compliance with safety, hygiene and quality rules

You bring that with you

  • Completed vocational training in a technical or industrial profession or comparable professional experience
  • Experience as, plant operator, machine operator or production employee
  • Technical understanding and safe handling of process control systems and production facilities
  • Independent, careful and responsible work scope
  • Teamwork, reliability and assertiveness
  • Durable and flexible
  • Readiness to work in 3 - shift operation
  • Good knowledge of German (B1)

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Graubünden?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Graubünden's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Graubünden's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
