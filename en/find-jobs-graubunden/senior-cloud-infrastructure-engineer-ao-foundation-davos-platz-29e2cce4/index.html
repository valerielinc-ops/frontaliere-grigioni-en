Senior Cloud Infrastructure Engineer (ID 2166\)

The AO is a medically guided, not\-for\-profit organization, a global network of surgeons, and the world's leading education, innovation, and research organization specializing in the surgical treatment of trauma and musculoskeletal disorders. We are home to people from all over the world, from different backgrounds, with diverse talents and specialist areas. What binds us together is our passion for excellence, our dedication to our mission of improving patient care, and our understanding that we are stronger together: we are one AO.

Main Responsibilities

  • Conception, design, engineering and implementation of infrastructure projects in the cloud
  • Implement security controls, such as firewalls, security groups, and network access controls, to protect cloud infrastructure and data
  • Troubleshoot and resolve issues with cloud infrastructure in a timely manner
  • Design and implement disaster recovery plans for cloud infrastructure services
  • Automate infrastructure management using tools like Bicep
  • Further development of existing Azure environments
  • Analysis of new cloud services and sharing of the acquired knowledge
  • Development of technical solution concepts and presentations
  • Monitor and optimize cloud\-based infrastructure performance and costs
  • Provide technical guidance and support to other teams
  • Collaborate with cross\-functional teams

Main Requirements

  • You have a degree in computer science or business informatics or comparable qualifications
  • CI/CD, GIT and IaC are not foreign terms for you.
  • You have in\-depth knowledge of Microsoft Azure solution architecture and migration
  • Proven Azure certifications an advantage
  • You have hands\-on experience in cloud automation
  • You have an understanding of agile methodologies and bring an agile work mindset to the table
  • Experience in designing and implementing security controls in cloud\-based environments
  • Strong understanding of security best practices and frameworks such as NIST, ISO 27001, and SOC 2
  • Design, implement and maintain our Azure infrastructure using Bicep, Terraform and ARM with containerization and orchestration tools such as Docker and Kubernetes
  • Understanding of networking concepts and protocols such as TCP/IP, DNS, and VPN.
  • Certifications are an advantage
  • Strong analytical and problem\-solving skills and Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • Have a good English proficiency
  • You're a cloud fan like us!
  • 3 days office presence in Davos HQ
  • An interesting and varied job in an exciting and innovative organization
  • The opportunity to be part of a highly committed international team
  • Modern infrastructure
  • High degree of flexibility regarding working hours and location (depending on operational requirements)
  • Generous pagacke of social benefits, including supplementary vacation days and pension scheme contributions
  • Internal skills training opportunities and support for continued education
