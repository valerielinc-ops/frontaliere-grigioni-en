Full description

Introduction

For a customer from the construction and expansion industry, we are looking for a plasterer to strengthen the team. The position includes classic activities in the field of plastering, stucco and troweling as well as other tasks related to the professional processing of wall and ceiling surfaces. We are looking for a reliable professional who works precisely, brings craftsmanship and moves safely in the daily construction site environment. It is used in a professional working environment with clear processes and varied tasks. Careful execution according to technical specifications is just as important as a responsible handling of materials, tools and work equipment. The position is suitable for people who are looking for a practical job with a craftsmanship focus and like to work in a team-oriented environment.

Tasks

  • They carry out plastering work on walls and ceilings according to specified standards.
  • They carefully prepare substrates to create a clean and viable surface.
  • They take over spatula and repair work on different construction site areas.
  • They apply insulation and cleaning systems in compliance with technical specifications.
  • They work safely, neatly and gently with hand tools and machines.
  • They control the executed areas and correct minor defects independently.

Profile

  • You have practical experience in plaster crafts or in a comparable craft.
  • They work carefully, reliably and consistently adhere to technical specifications.
  • They bring craftsmanship as well as a good eye for clean surfaces.
  • They are physically resilient and suitable for activities on changing construction sites.
  • They can work in a team and, if necessary, coordinate clearly with colleagues.
  • They have a basic understanding of materials, tools and workflows in development.

Advantages

  • Varied activities for exciting working days.
  • Sectoral remuneration based on training and experience.
