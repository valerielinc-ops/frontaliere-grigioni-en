Full description

What you do with us

As Director of Food & Beverage, you are a member of our Executive Committee and carry the strategic, operational and personnel responsibility for the entire F&B area.

  • Together with your management team, you are responsible for up to eleven F&B outlets and the associated support departments.

  • They continuously develop the F&B strategy, implement trend-setting concepts and ensure efficient processes and smooth interface management.

  • Comprehensive budget and earnings responsibility – including revenue development, cost control and sustainable increase in profitability – is in your hands.

  • As a driving force, you further develop our service and quality standards and ensure their consistent implementation in all outlets.

  • You lead and develop F&B executives and their teams, are responsible for personnel planning and recruiting and promote a performance-oriented leadership culture.

  • Despite your comprehensive management responsibility, you are present as a host personality and take care of our guests and their concerns with pleasure.

  • You ensure compliance with all legal and operational requirements and promote close, solution-oriented cooperation with all departments of the hotel.

    • This requires you to bring with you

    Several years of experience as Director of Food & Beverage or Executive Assistant Manager F&B in the international luxury hotel industry.

  • Experience in leading and developing large, international teams and leaders.

  • Strategic and entrepreneurial thinking as well as strong operational competence.

  • Funded knowledge in budgeting, result management, cost optimization and resource planning.

  • When dealing with an international luxury audience, you perform confidently and host-oriented.

  • A decision-making, self-responsible and solution-oriented working method.

  • Excellent communication skills, diplomatic skills and innovation.

  • Very good knowledge of German and English; Other languages are beneficial.

Our offer

  • A permanent annual position in a central management position of the international luxury hotel industry.

  • Membership of the Executive Committee with direct influence on F&B strategy and guest experience.

  • The responsibility for a diverse F&B landscape with up to eleven outlets and international orientation.

  • An attractive working environment in St. Moritz with a long-term perspective and development opportunities on an executive level.

  • If this challenging leadership task in the international luxury environment appeals to you, we look forward to your complete online application.

    • If you appreciate an interesting, challenging and versatile job in a dynamic environment and would like to join a well-established team, then we look forward to your complete online application.
