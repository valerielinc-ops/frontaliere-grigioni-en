Live and work in the Destination Davos Klosters? We look forward to reinforcement! ##STR1##

In our team, you will receive an insight into our broad spectrum of disciplines as a sub-assistant and expand your expertise with your practical cooperation. We attach great importance to a motivating, trusting approach and a good teaching and learning culture.

Davos Sports & Health (DSH) provides athletes with competent and uncomplicated care, promotes health with targeted programs and creates knowledge through research and teaching. Davos Sports & Health is affiliated with Spital Davos AG, which guarantees comprehensive medical care. The interdisciplinary team of specialists in sports medicine, surgery, movement science, physiotherapy, medical massage and research & teaching supervises, among others, the Hockey Club Davos, athletes of Swiss Ski, the Sports Gymnasium and the Davos Talent School.
