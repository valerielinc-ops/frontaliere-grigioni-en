Full description

The Office for Migration and Civil Law currently has 230 employees at more than 20 locations and is responsible for the entire area of responsibility in the area of migration, i.e. in particular granting and withdrawing permits, accommodation and support, executing expulsions and integration. In addition, ID cards are issued to Swiss nationals, naturalisations are made and supervision of the civil status authorities is exercised. The headquarters of the Office of Migration and Civil Law is in Chur. We are looking for the Office for Migration and Civil Law as of 1. September 2026 or by arrangement an administrative assistant. The vacancy is limited to 31. December 2027. Tasks

  • Collection, management and control of asylum data
  • Data collection and maintenance in connection with foreigner ID cards
  • Issuing of work permits
  • Creating tables, statistics and reports
  • General correspondence and opinions
  • Information provided at the reception/switch and on the telephone Requirements
  • Commercial or equivalent vocational training completed
  • Professional experience in the commercial sector with corresponding training
  • Self-employment, personal responsibility and decision-making
  • Teamwork, careful and exact work
  • Adaptness in oral and written communication
  • Empathy in dealing with different cultures and life situations
  • Foreign language skills are an advantage
  • Very good knowledge of MS Excel and MS Word as well as generally high IT affinity
