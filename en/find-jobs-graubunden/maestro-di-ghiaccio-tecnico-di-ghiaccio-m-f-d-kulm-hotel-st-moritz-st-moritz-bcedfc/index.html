This is your chance to be a member of our shaping culture: | This is what you move with us: As Ice Master/ Ice Technician, you are the guardian of our ice rink. You take full responsibility for ensuring optimal ice conditions for ice skating, curling, and exclusive events.

  • Ice Preparation & Maintenance: Professional setup, daily care, and maintenance of the ice rink to meet the highest quality standards.
  • Machinery Operation: Safe operation, maintenance, and manoeuvring of ice resurfacing machines and specialised equipment.
  • Weather Management: Continuous monitoring of weather and temperature conditions, alongside flexible adaptation of preparation times.
  • Safety & Order: Adherence to all safety standards on the ice rink and proper care of associated equipment | This is you:
  • Language Skills: Fluent English is an absolute must-have. Additional languages are a Plus.
  • Experience: Proven experience in laying, maintaining, and resurfacing ice surfaces (natural or artificial ice), as well as routine handling of ice resurfacing machinery.
  • Practical Skills: Technical understanding, a solution-oriented approach, and physical fitness.
  • Attitude & Flexibility: High resilience to all weather conditions, self-reliance, and willingness to work weather-dependent hours (early and late shifts).
  • Hospitality: Friendly, courteous demeanour reflecting the Kulm service culture.
