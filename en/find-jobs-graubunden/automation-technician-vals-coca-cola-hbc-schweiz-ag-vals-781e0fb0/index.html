Automatic: in Vals (m/f/d)

Your technique. Our mineral water. A common future.

Connect your passion for automation with the world of mineral water.

In our modern production and filling plant in Vals, you ensure that our plants run safely, reliably and efficiently. You will analyze disruptions, develop technical solutions and actively shape the future of our location.

Your new tasks:

  • You will maintain and repair our production, filling and infrastructure facilities.
  • You analyze electrical and automated faults and repair them sustainably.
  • You manage and optimize PLC\ controls, sensors, actuators and HMI\ and OT\ systems.
  • You support the networking and further development of our production facilities.
  • You participate in the installation, testing and commissioning of new plants.
  • You work in automation, OT, modernization and CIP projects.
  • Together with the team, you ensure high plant availability.
  • You consistently comply with the applicable regulations on occupational safety, health and quality.
  • You take on assignments in shift and pikettdienst.

ARE YOUR SECRET INGREDIENTS:

  • You have an apprenticeship as an automatic engineer: in EFZ, electrical installer: in EFZ or a comparable technical training.
  • You have experience in the maintenance of automated production facilities.
  • You bring good knowledge of electrical, control and control technology.
  • Ideally, you have experience with Siemens S7, STEP 7 or TIA Portal.
  • Knowledge of HMI, industrial networks and OT\ systems is an advantage.
  • You work independently, structured and solution-oriented.
  • You like to take responsibility and work in a team.
  • You have good knowledge of German. Knowledge of English is an advantage.
  • Readiness for layer and piquette inserts
  • Interest in professional training and personal development
  • Ideally, you will live a maximum of 45 minutes from Vals location

Please apply online and provide us with your complete application documents incl. Letter of motivation and employment certificates. in a single PDF. We do not accept applications via email. For this position, we do not receive dossiers from recruitment agencies.
