Head of Civil Engineering \& Plants (m/f/d) 100% Member of the Management Board

Your leadership for a tourism community with a future.

It stands for a high quality of life and an attractive tourist location. A functioning infrastructure is central to this. As part of a forward-looking succession plan, we are looking for an experienced leader who will lead the civil engineering and works department responsibly and actively shape its future.

Your future field of activity

  • Strategic and operational management of a department with 30 to 40 employees
  • Technical, human and organisational support for the subordinates
  • Promotion and further development of employees as well as active team development
  • Planning, control and implementation of technical and administrative projects
  • Responsibility for budget, financial planning, internal control processes and legal requirements
  • Cooperation with authorities, municipalities, tourism, mountain railways and the population
  • Active participation in the management and representation of the municipality in commissions and working groups

The requirement profile

  • Completed basic technical training with higher education in civil engineering
  • Professional experience in the fields of construction authority, work service or infrastructure management, ideally in public administration
  • High leadership and social competence with appropriate assertiveness and decision-making strength
  • Independent, resilient and flexible personality with a strong organizational talent
  • Stylistic communication in dealing with employees, authorities and external stakeholders
  • Solid IT\ user knowledge is required

Special

They benefit from a responsible position with room for manoeuvre, attractive employment conditions, above-average social benefits and generous training opportunities. In addition, you enjoy a high leisure value in the Arosa region and can count on active support in the search for housing on site.
