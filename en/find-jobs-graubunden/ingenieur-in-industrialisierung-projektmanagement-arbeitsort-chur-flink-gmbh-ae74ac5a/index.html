Engineer/industrialization \& Project management (80\-100%), Chur location

Flink has been developing and implementing sophisticated products for customers at home and abroad since 2005 – from the first idea to design and engineering to production and logistics. Swiss precision, creativity and innovative strength characterize our solutions. With an international network and manufacturing expertise, we accompany companies in the implementation of products for medicine, industry and sports.

In small, interdisciplinary teams, we develop and realize products from the first idea to series production. In this role, you work at the interface between development and production and ensure that innovative product ideas become production-oriented solutions.

Your profile:

  • Engineer FH/ETH, preferably in the field of mechanical engineering
  • Several years of experience in technical project management and industrialization
  • Experience in the design and dimensioning of plastic and metal components
  • Experience with the common 3D\-CAD\ programs
  • Preferably knowledge of programming languages relevant for development (, JS, etc.)
  • Experience working with different departments and external partners
  • Very good knowledge of German and English

Your main tasks:

  • Planning, coordinating and controlling technical projects and industrialization from feasibility to production start-up
  • Implementation of feasibility investigations
  • Developing and implementing production solutions
  • accompanying prototypes, pre-series and production starts
  • Coordinating participating departments and external partners
  • Create specifications and parts lists
  • Leading project documentation

Your personality:

You are a forward-looking, solution-oriented and reliable personality with very good self-management and a pragmatic mindset. You recognize connections and keep track of complex tasks. You tackle challenges independently and in a structured manner and find practical ways. You communicate sovereignly and clearly, actively participate and take responsibility for your tasks. You will enjoy working on new topics and continuously developing existing approaches.

We are:

We live an open you culture with flat hierarchies and short decision-making paths. You get a lot of creative leeway and can contribute your experience directly to exciting projects and products. You can expect a versatile environment in which development, design, engineering and production work closely together. We offer modern working conditions, flexible working hours and a motivated team that values cooperation and mutual support.

We look forward to meeting you. Send us your complete application documents including letters of motivation.
