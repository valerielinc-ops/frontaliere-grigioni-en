Cost of living vs Ticino

The median gross salary in Graubünden is about 16% higher than in Ticino (BFS LSE 2024 regional wage-level data). The median LAMal health-insurance premium is also lower: CHF 329/month vs CHF 425/month in Ticino (23% lower, BAG/UFSP data) — a real extra cost to factor into your net income.

G permit and border zone

Graubünden borders Germany and France, not Italy: the G permit here is typically issued to German or French residents of the border zone. Italian residents considering a role in Graubünden usually apply for a B residence permit instead of cross-border-commuter status, which is reserved for residents of a border municipality in the neighbouring country.
