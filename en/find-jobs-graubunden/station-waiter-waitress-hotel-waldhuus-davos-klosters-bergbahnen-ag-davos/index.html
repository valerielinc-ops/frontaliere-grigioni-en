Full description

Davos Klosters Mountains are five mountain and ski resorts in the Grisons Alps. In addition to the cable cars, Davos Klosters Bergbahnen AG operates over 20 accommodation units under the Mountain Hotels and Resorts brand, from simple group houses to the upscale four-star Superior Hotel.

To support the team at Hotel Waldhuus we are looking for one person for the winter season 2026-27 as:

Chef de Rank 100%

The hotel

Hotel Waldhuus Davos is located in the highest city in Europe (1560 m above sea level) and has been part of Davos Klosters Bergbahnen AG since April 2019. The hotel has 108 rooms, 1 half-board restaurant, 1 bar and 5 seminar rooms, as well as a spa area and kids club.
