Mobile telephony / antenna construction (wireless) 100%

For exciting and varied applications in the field of mobile communications and antenna construction, we are looking for a motivated and reliable installer. Your tasks

  • Assembly and installation of mobile radio antennas and directional beam systems
  • Installation and installation of antenna systems on roofs and mobile radio sites
  • Execution of installation and assembly work in the wireless sector
  • Support for commissioning and on-site technical work
  • Compliance with safety rules and quality standards

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as electrical installer EFZ or assembly electrician EFZ
  • Mandatory driving licence category B
  • Dizziness and no fear of heights
  • Physical Fitness and Joy of Working at Height
  • Technical understanding and skill
  • Independent, reliable and team-oriented working method
  • Good knowledge of German

We offer

  • Varied and interesting activity in mobile and antenna construction
  • Modern work equipment and professional equipment
  • Collegial and motivated team
  • Long-term application possibilities
  • Current employment conditions
