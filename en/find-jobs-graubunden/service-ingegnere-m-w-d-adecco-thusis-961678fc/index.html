Full description

Introduction

For PackSys Global, a Swiss company and business unit of the Brückner Group, we are looking for a dedicated specialist for the worldwide service deployment of innovative packaging solutions. The company develops and manufactures machines that set international standards in plastics processing. At the Haldenstein site, you will be responsible for the electromechanical construction, electrical wiring and the professional installation and maintenance of state-of-the-art plants. The activity combines technical precision with international customer engagement and varied tasks in mechanical and plant engineering. They work closely with customers, accompany test runs and factory acceptances and support commissioning on site. The application takes place in a global environment with a high level of personal responsibility, clear processes and direct reference to practice.

Tasks

  • They take over the electromechanical design and the precise electrical wiring of high-tech plants in the plant.
  • They install and maintain machines professionally and work carefully and technically accurately.
  • They accompany test runs and factory acceptance in close cooperation with customers and internal interfaces.
  • They put plants into operation at the customer’s premises worldwide and accompany them until final acceptance on site.
  • They train the customer staff and provide the safe handling of the facilities in daily operations.
  • You are willing to spend around 50 percent of your working time abroad, in assignments of one to a maximum of five weeks.

Profile

  • They have completed mechanical training, for example as polymechanics, or a comparable qualification.
  • Ideally, you bring electrotechnical knowledge and thus complement your technical profile.
  • They work analytically, goal-oriented and find suitable solutions for complex technical tasks.
  • They speak very well in English and communicate safely in an international environment.
  • They are characterized by customer orientation, a sense of responsibility and organizational skill.
  • They see international travel as a welcome change and professional challenge.

Advantages

  • professional support throughout the application process.
  • Varied activities in service, assembly and international customer assignments.
  • Permanent employment contract with a long-term perspective in the company.
