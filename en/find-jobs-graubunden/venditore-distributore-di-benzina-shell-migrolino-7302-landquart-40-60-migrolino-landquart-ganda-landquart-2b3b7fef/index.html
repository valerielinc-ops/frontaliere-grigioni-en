Full description

The Reichle & staff GmbH has been since 01. February 2019 petrol station tenant of Shell (Switzerland) AG and as such operates the 6 stations in St.Gallen Erlach, Stelz/Kirchberg & Ganda/Landquart (“Migrolino 2.0” shop concepts), Au und Staad and Glarnerland (“MIO” shop concepts by Migrolino).
