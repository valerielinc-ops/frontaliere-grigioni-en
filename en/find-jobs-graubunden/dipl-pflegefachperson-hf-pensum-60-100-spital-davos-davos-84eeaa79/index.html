Full description

You will take on the role of a safe and competent nurse to care for and assist patients on our Monitoring Station and in the Recovery Room. As a regional/acute hospital with an emergency service, nursing home, and Spitex, we are responsible for the basic medical care of the Davos population and guests. However, the well-being of our employees is equally important to us. Working with us means being part of a manageable, yet extremely important operation for the region. We foster a collegial work environment and enjoy the proximity to nature, as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities offered by Davos. ##

  • Responsibilities
  • Care for interdisciplinary patients on the Monitoring Station and in the Recovery Room
  • Conducting ambulatory pain therapies
  • Participation in preventive, diagnostic, and therapeutic measures
  • Accompanying patients and their families in crisis situations
  • Thorough documentation of all nursing activities
  • Professional and close interdisciplinary collaboration with various departments ##
  • Requirements
  • Completed training as Dipl. Health Care Assistant HF or equally recognized training
  • Post-diploma certificate in Monitoring Nursing preferred
  • High sense of responsibility and a pronounced quality orientation
  • Exact and reliable working method
  • Empathetic, motivated, and resilient personality
  • Very good German skills, good English skills, any other language preferred
  • Willingness to work in all shifts (three-shift operation) ##
  • What we offer
  • Varied and responsible work
  • Well-organized, committed, dynamic, and collegial team
  • Attractive employment conditions and social benefits, hospital-owned kindergarten
  • Interesting opportunities for further and continuing education ## Contact
