Service technician for construction machinery (a)

Learn to know us \- That's why Avesco

Avesco AG is a leading Swiss family company with over 150 years of history, offering specialized solutions in the fields of construction machinery, construction technology and energy systems.

Screws, tinkering and keeping construction machinery running, sounds like yours? Then you are exactly right with us!

In close cooperation with a dispatcher, you ensure that our machines do not fizzle out at the customers on site. From high-tech diagnosis to the last screw \ - you are the professional who makes it possible.

Your mission

  • You bring construction machinery back to its best – directly at the customer’s site
  • With state-of-the-art diagnostic technology you will find errors and provide a quick solution
  • You ensure that our machines remain operational reliably
  • You advise our customers, give technical tips and ensure satisfied faces

Your background

  • Training as construction machinery, truck or agricultural machinery mechanic
  • Enthusiasm for large machines and high-tech systems
  • Enjoy carrying out repairs and maintenance independently
  • A reliable, solution-oriented way of working
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Driving licence Cat. B/BE
  • Our values – your plus at Avesco *

With us you will meet a strong team, exciting technology and real development opportunities in an open environment with attractive framework conditions.

Holidays

Your recovery is important to us. Look forward to five weeks of vacation a year. From your 41st birthday, there are gradually more holidays - up to a total of six weeks.

GAV Swissmem

Thanks to the GAV Swissmem, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and additional benefits – for more security and fairness in everyday work.

Flexible working hours

You have a 40 hour week and in many functions you benefit from flexible working hours.

World-class brands

Work with leading brands in the industry, expand your expertise and continuously develop together with us.

Own service vehicle

Your modern service vehicle is equipped with high-quality tools - so you are ready for any challenge.

  • Phone and laptop included

We provide you with the appropriate digital equipment. Your own company phone and laptop accompany you in everyday work and provide more flexibility.

Questions? We are here for you

If you have any questions, it is always there for you. If you have any questions about the application process, please provide information.
