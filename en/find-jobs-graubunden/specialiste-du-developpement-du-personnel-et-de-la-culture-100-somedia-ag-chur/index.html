Full description

SOMEDIA - MEDIA OF SOUTH EAST SWITZERLAND We bring people forward. With our media, we make a relevant contribution to a free and informed society on a daily basis. Well-known brands such as Southeast Switzerland, the Bundner Tagblatt and Radio Grischa provide orientation, promote exchange and connect the people in our region. As an independent family business, we are firmly anchored in Southeast Switzerland. You expect a lot of personal responsibility, short decision paths and an open you culture. In accordance with our Somedia values, we act courageously, ambitiously, respectfully and responsibly. For our People & Culture Team in Chur we are looking for a specialist Personnel Development & Culture (100%) as of 1 September 2026 or by appointment. You are part of our seven-member People & Culture team and actively shape our cultural transformation. In doing so, you develop effective learning and further education formats, organize cultural events and accompany employees in their development. You do that for us.

  • Information with effect: You design and create e-learnings, guides and explanatory videos for employees and leaders.
  • Make culture tangible: You plan, organize and accompany our people & culture events from preparation to follow-up, including welcome day, squad day and service anniversary event.
  • Create orientation: You will design and moderate the annual cultural onboarding workshop for learners and practical trainers with a focus on values, attitude, role understanding and collaboration.
  • Support development: You advise and support employees on development and further education issues.
  • Keep employers up-to-date: You maintain a career website, job platforms and benefit information and ensure that our content is correct, up-to-date and appealing.

You bring that to me.

  • Education with substance: You bring first or several years of experience in learning & development, adult education, personnel development, communication or a comparable environment. What is important is your joy in bringing people forward.
  • A sense for good learning content: You know how to prepare presentations, training materials and digital learning content didactically and visually so that they really arrive. You work with us with Articulate 360, Affinity Publisher and AI-enabled tools.
  • Independent, but not on your own: You organize events and projects in a structured and reliable way from A to Z with support from the P&C team and the event team.
  • Safety in front of groups: You feel comfortable in front of groups and bring your first experience in moderating workshops, training courses or events.
  • AI as a work tool: You use digital and AI-supported tools safely and responsibly.

This is what we offer you.

  • Design leeway from day one: You bring your own ideas for learning content and our People & Culture Events and implement them independently.
  • Room to grow: As a development potential, we offer you the prospect of further developing the position in the direction of employer branding, recruiting or company health management.
  • Tangible advantages: 40-hour week, annual working hours, the possibility of a 4-day week and home office, at least five weeks holidays, discounted Natelabo, free digital newspaper sabo.
