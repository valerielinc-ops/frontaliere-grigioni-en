Senior physician psychiatry/psychosomatics (a) 60\-100 %

Location: Seewis rehabilitation clinic Where your job is doing rehab.

Cardiology, Internal Medicine / Oncology and Psychiatry / Psychosomatics. First-class rehabilitation, embedded in a magnificent mountain landscape. With over 50 years of experience. The environment in the Seewis rehabilitation clinic also benefits the approximately 135 employees.

Here advanced medicine meets flat hierarchies and a lot of room for manoeuvre. Through close exchange with care and therapy, you and your ideas advance.

You can look forward to this

  • Ensuring psychiatric care under expert guidance
  • Caring for outpatients without night / weekend presence services
  • Independent and responsible design of medical patient care
  • Ensure the highest treatment standards
  • Working together in an interdisciplinary team to develop the best solutions according to rehabilitation goals and impart knowledge in the form of systematic internal training and bedside teaching

We are pleased about this

  • Completed medical studies as well as completed specialist title OR in advanced training to the specialist title in psychiatry \& psychotherapy
  • Swiss diploma or recognised equivalent
  • Joy of acting patient-oriented and responsible
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing (at least level B2\)
  • Regular training in recent years
