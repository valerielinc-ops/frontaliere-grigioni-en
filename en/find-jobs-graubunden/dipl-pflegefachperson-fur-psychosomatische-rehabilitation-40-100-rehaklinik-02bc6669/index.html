Dipl. Nurse for Psychosomatic Rehabilitation (a) 40\-100 %

Introduction

Location: Seewis rehabilitation clinic Where your rehab is.

Cardiology, Internal Medicine / Oncology and Psychiatry / Psychosomatics. First-class rehabilitation, embedded in a magnificent mountain landscape. With over 50 years of experience. The environment in the Seewis rehabilitation clinic also benefits the approximately 135 employees.

Here you will be inspired interdisciplinary and work at eye level with doctors and therapists. Subject, leadership, education: Freely shape your career.

You can look forward to this

  • Holistic care and psychosocial support of patients with psychosomatic diseases
  • Promoting self-employment and support in dealing with everyday life as well as structuring the daily routine
  • Support in disease and crisis management as well as the recognition of early warning signs and the initiation of appropriate measures
  • Medication management and ensuring professional medication
  • Conducting supportive, structuring and solution-oriented individual discussions as well as nursing group discussions
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors: inside, psychologist: inside, therapist: inside, relatives and external bodies
  • Regulated working hours from Monday to two-shift operation (\- and late service) without night watches

We are pleased about this

  • Completed training as a certified nurse HF/FH/DN II or FaGe with expertise in psychiatry
  • Open for re-entry: inside and specialists from somatics
  • Strong social and relationship skills
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Active participation and promotion of team spirit
