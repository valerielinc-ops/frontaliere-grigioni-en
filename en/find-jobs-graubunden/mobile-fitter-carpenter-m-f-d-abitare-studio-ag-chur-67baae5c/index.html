Furniture fitter / carpenter (m/f/d)

80-100% \| Design furniture, kitchens \& interior design \| Chur

Craftsmanship, initiative and pleasure in high-quality design?

Abitare Studio stands for high-quality design furniture, individual kitchen solutions and sophisticated interior design. We plan and realize projects for private customers and companies – from individual furniture and kitchens to holistic room concepts.

To strengthen our team in Chur, we are looking for a skilled, reliable personality who likes to work independently, think and take responsibility.

Your tasks

Delivery and assembly

  • Professional delivery and assembly of high-quality design furniture, kitchens and individual furnishing systems
  • Independent execution of assembly work for our customers
  • Minor adaptation, repair and carpentry work
  • Careful handling of high-quality materials and products
  • Professional and friendly customer contact

Warehouse and workshop

  • Together with your assembly colleague, take responsibility for the organization and operation of warehouse and workshop
  • Ensure order, cleanliness and a clear storage organisation
  • Receiving, checking and properly storing goods
  • Prepare deliveries and assemblies as well as provide required materials
  • Maintaining and maintaining tools, machinery and vehicles
  • Monitoring and documenting stockpiles
  • Actively shape and improve workflows

Cooperation in the team

  • Close coordination with our planning, sales and administration team
  • Consideration in the preparation and implementation of deliveries and assemblies
  • Recognize practical challenges independently and approach them in a solution-oriented manner

You bring that with you

  • Completed craftsmanship, ideally as a carpenter, assembly carpenter or in a related profession
  • Experience in furniture assembly, ideally also in kitchen assembly
  • Technical understanding, craftsmanship and a good eye for detail
  • Independent, structured and precise working method
  • A sense of responsibility, organisational talent and initiative
  • Enjoying high-quality products, order and well-functioning processes
  • Careful appearance and pleasure in customer contact
  • Teamwork and mutual support
  • Category B driving licence
  • Good knowledge of German

This is what awaits you

  • Varied projects with high-quality design furniture, kitchens and individual facilities
  • An exciting working environment in the world of architecture and design
  • A small, dedicated team with short decision paths
  • Self-responsible work and partnership in the assembly team
  • Space for self-initiative and the opportunity to actively shape work processes
  • Direct contact with our customers and visible results of your work
  • A long-term perspective in an established company

Interested?

Then we look forward to meeting you!
