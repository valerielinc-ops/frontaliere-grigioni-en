Full description

Are you ready to help develop the digital transformation? As an ICT service and competence center of the cantonal administration of Graubünden, we create the technical basis for a secure, modern and future-oriented digital workplace. Together with you, we will advance the digital transformation of the canton of Graubünden.

We as the Office for Computer Science are looking for a client engineer in Workplace & Endpoint Management immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • You optimize our digital workplace based on Windows 11 and develop our modern, secure and standardized client environment.
  • You are responsible for Mobile Device and Application Management (MDM / MAM) with Microsoft Intune.
  • You automate client and administration processes with PowerShell.
  • You contribute to the provision of applications, configurations and updates using Microsoft Intune and Microsoft Configuration Manager (MCM).
  • You’ll analyze and resolve issues around Windows 11, Microsoft 365, working closely with service desk, support and engineering.

Requirements

  • Education or training in computer science
  • Deep experience in client engineering and patch management in Windows 11 and Microsoft 365 environments.
  • Experience in Mobile Device Management with iOS/iPadOS.
  • Good understanding of hybrid deployment of Windows clients based on M365.
  • Analytical thinking, an independent and structured way of working and the ability to solve complex technical problems in a sustainable manner.
  • Strong team skills as well as good knowledge of German in words and writing.
  • Readiness for occasional assignments outside regular working hours
