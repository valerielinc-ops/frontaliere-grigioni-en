Full description

Start by appointment as a professional health emergency 80 - 100%

At the largest emergency station in Southeast Switzerland, the focus is on teamwork. We provide emergency patients with various illnesses and injuries around the clock. Through the close and interdisciplinary cooperation of our committed nurses, experienced doctors and practice assistants, we treat 26,000 patients every year.

Would you like to contribute your skills to one of the most exciting emergency stations in the region in the future? Perfect, then you are exactly right with us!

These are your tasks

  • You support the care team in a dynamic and varied working environment in the Central Emergency Station
  • You are the right hand of the caregiver and act with her in the team of two
  • Assist in diagnostic-therapeutic activities
  • You take on nursing, domestic and administrative activities

You bring that with you

  • You have a degree as a specialist in health and already gained work experience in an acute hospital
  • You have the willingness to acquire additional skills within the hospital to carry out the activities at the Central Emergency Station
  • Your reliable and forward-looking working method and your quick comprehension characterize you
  • Your open and positioned nature is an enrichment for the team

Can you do something that others can’t? You make the difference? Then you belong to us!

  • Gastronomic offer: All four locations of the cantonal hospital Graubünden offer a wide range of catering options. With us you can eat varied, balanced and healthy.
