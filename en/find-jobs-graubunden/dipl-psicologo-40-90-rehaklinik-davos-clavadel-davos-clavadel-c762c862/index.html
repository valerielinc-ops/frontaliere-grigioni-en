Dipl. Psychologist (m/f/a) 40 \- 90%

A far-sighted approach to rehab and my career – that’s the feeling of life and work at the Valens Clinic Group. Exchange, progress, interdisciplinary teamwork and good prospects are important here.

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an

Your tasks

  • Psychological counselling and psychotherapy in individual, group and family setting for patients with mental, psychosomatic and pain-related diseases
  • Psychological support and support of patients in all departments of our clinic in disease processing and on their way back to more independence and quality of life
  • Active cooperation in the interprofessional treatment team as well as participation in case discussions, reports and rehabilitation planning
  • Responsibility for timely and high-quality documentation as well as the preparation of progress reports and exit reports
  • Interprofessional coordination with referents, relatives and internal and external treatment partners to support rehabilitation success

Your profile

  • Completed university or university of applied sciences degree in psychology with a focus on clinical psychology
  • Started or completed further training in psychotherapy (or willingness to take it in a timely manner)
  • Ideally, work experience in the inpatient psychiatric, psychosomatic or rehabilitative environment
  • Enjoyment of the psychological accompaniment of people with mental, psychosomatic and complex somatic diseases in individual and group setting
  • Self-employed, responsible and reflective working methods
  • High social competence and enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation
  • Good knowledge of German, other foreign languages advantageous

Your future with us

  • Prospective and easy-to-reach workplace – in a collegial environment with clear structures, competent managers and appreciative communication
  • Real benefits instead of empty phrases – with paid changing time, jump-in premium, 28 days of holidays as well as numerous internal and external discounts and offers.
  • Further education that advances you – internal and external training, including through our Rehab Academy, the group-wide \- and further education center
  • Diversity as strength – We rely on thinking, self-confident and critical employees with different experiences, qualifications and perspectives, because we are convinced that this will bring us all forward.
