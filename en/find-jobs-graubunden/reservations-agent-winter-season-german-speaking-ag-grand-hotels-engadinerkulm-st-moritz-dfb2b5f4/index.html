Reservations Agent \- Winter season \- German speaking

Lift your career to a new level at 1800 meters above sea level and become part of the Kulm Group family. Are you ready to redefine the luxury of mountain travel? Become part of our dynamic and international Kulm Group team. Together we offer our guests a unique luxury experience and create unforgettable memories.

Why Kulm Group?

  • Striving for excellence: We focus on excellence. We work every day to achieve the best and to inspire our guests and employees.
  • Passionate team: Work in a vibrant, international team that supports and inspires each other. With us you will experience real cohesion and team spirit.
  • Career opportunities: Develop your potential and grow with us. Your personal and professional development is important to us.
  • Unique culture: Immerse yourself in a work environment that values creativity, passion and authenticity. Here you can bring your ideas and really make a difference.

Become part of something extraordinary!

This moves you with us

  • Competent handling of reservations: You are the first point of contact for our discerning guests and will accept requests for individual and group reservations. You always act with the highest professionalism and forward-looking attention.
  • Data maintenance: You maintain the guest databases with the utmost accuracy and thus contribute significantly to the satisfaction and return of our guests.
  • Complaint management with sensitivity: If there are complaints, process them immediately and always find the best solution for our discreet and efficient guests.
  • Multimedia communication: You are responsible for answering all incoming requests, whether by phone or email.
  • Coordination with other departments: In close cooperation with our dedicated team, you ensure that the occupancy of our luxurious guest rooms and function rooms is optimally coordinated with the offers in F\&B and Spa.
  • Administrative support: You support the Reservations Manager in all administrative matters, ensure compliance with our high standards and contribute to constant product optimization.

This is you *

  • Charism and Kindness: Her personality is characterized by natural friendliness and charisma, combined with a keen sense of the wishes of our discerning guests. They convince with their well-groomed external appearance and their excellent manners.
  • Team spirit and flexibility: You enjoy working in a team, are communicative and adapt flexibly to the challenges of a dynamic working environment.
  • Experience in the upscale hotel industry: With a completed professional training in the upscale hotel industry and at least 2 years of experience in the 5\-star hotel industry, you will bring with you in-depth expertise in the areas of front office or reservation.
