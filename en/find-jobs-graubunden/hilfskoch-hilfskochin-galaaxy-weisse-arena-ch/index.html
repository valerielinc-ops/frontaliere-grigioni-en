Full description

The White Arena Group in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and an enthusiastic community for unique mountain adventures. With mountain railways, hotels, gastronomy, sports and rental shops and the LAAX School, we create unforgettable experiences for our guests - and attractive workplaces for our employees. | The Galaaxy on the Crap Sogn Gion is the hub of the LAAX ski area and much more than a mountain station. Here, enjoyment, lifestyle, encounter and alpine experience meet: from the Main Station and the restaurant Capalari via the Satellite Bar and the Hangar Kids School Restaurant to the Bridge as a seminar and coworking space. With a wide range of gastronomy offers, events and the Mountain Hostel, the Galaaxy connects people during the day and in the evening and creates a place in the middle of the mountains where there is always something to experience. | Reinforce our kitchen team in the Galaaxy – as an auxiliary cook on the Crap Sogn Gion | What moves you:

  • You help prepare our menus for day-to-day business
  • You take over Mise-en-Place work and make sure everything is ready to go
  • You provide the food for the service staff
  • You support in all areas of the kitchen and also take part in general cleaning work
  • You pay attention to consistent compliance with hygiene regulations. What makes you:
  • You have work experience in a similar function and enjoy your work
  • You are communicative, responsible and happy to fulfill the wishes of the guests
  • You are a team player who works cleanly, carefully and reliably
  • You keep your peace and friendliness even in hectic situations
  • You speak good German, further knowledge of foreign languages is an advantage | An attractive workplace in the middle of an extraordinary mountain world is certain for you. You become part of a dynamic, bright team and can help shape the further development of LAAX. With us you will find an environment where professionalism, speed, pragmatism and humanity go together. Look forward to numerous employee benefits, such as free mountain rides, perks in our shops, hotels and restaurants, and benefits for your family.
