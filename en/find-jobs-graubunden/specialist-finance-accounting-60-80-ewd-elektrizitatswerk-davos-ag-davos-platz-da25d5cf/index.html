Specialist Finance \& Accounting 60\-80% (m/f/d)

Working in the tourist destination at EWD Elektrizitätswerk Davos AG – responsible for the power supply community of Davos since 1893.

To complement our team, we are looking for you as Specialist Finance \& Accounting 60\-80%(m/f/d)

Your responsibilities

  • Participation in quarterly and annual accounts (FIBU/BEBU)
  • Support of the budget process, including multiannual planning
  • Preparation of the reporting\- incl. Cost accounting (regulator ElCom)
  • Project evaluation and controlling
  • Active participation in financial projects
  • Development and processing of system requirements and further developments (Abacus)
  • Assumption of tasks and deputies in financial accounting

You bring that with you

  • Basic commercial training with further training in accounting
  • Several years of experience in a comparable position, ideally in the energy industry
  • Abacus experience is beneficial
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills
  • Good MS\Office knowledge
  • Independence, accuracy and confidentiality are also among your strengths

We offer this to you *

We are a modern company focused on the market and its customers with a highly motivated team. Thanks to the ideal company size of 60 employees (including 4 apprentices), we offer you a responsible position with short paths, little hierarchy and a broad and varied field of activity in the energy industry with a future.

  • Market share
  • Flexible working hours
  • Above-average support for education and training
  • Holidays 6 to 7 weeks
  • Miscellaneous advantages
