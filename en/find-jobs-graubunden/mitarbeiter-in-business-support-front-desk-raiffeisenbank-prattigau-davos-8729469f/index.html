Employees: in Business Support / Front Desk (m/f/d)

Raiffeisenbank Prättigau\-Davos is a regionally anchored, modern and successful bank with 44 dedicated employees. To strengthen our Business Support Team, we are looking for an independent, service-oriented and motivated personality as an employee Business Support / Front Desk (m/f/d).

What do you expect?

  • You are the first contact person for our customers at the reception.
  • They provide information on basic products such as maps and support our customers in using the Raiffeisen digital offers.
  • They manage our ATMs and ensure smooth operation.
  • They accept customer concerns, prioritize them and forward them to the responsible bodies.
  • They process customer orders and support our consultants in administrative activities, for example in card, account and securities orders.

What do you bring?

  • Joy of contact with people as well as a friendly and professional appearance.
  • Basic commercial education or comparable professional experience.
  • Reliable, independent and structured working methods.
  • Affinity for digital solutions and modern technologies.
  • Interest in active participation in projects.
  • Regional anchorage or a connection to the region Prättigau\-Davos is advantageous.
  • Readiness for operations at the locations Schiers, Küblis and Davos.

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du\ culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We live diversity and equal opportunities

We promote the different perspectives, experiences and competencies in our teams. Because this diversity makes us special and contributes decisively to our success.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.
