Engadiner Kraftwerke AG (EKW), headquartered in Zernez, is one of the largest hydropower companies in Switzerland. In the heart of the national park region, it operates critical infrastructure that contributes to the country’s power supply. Around 60 employees work every day for reliable and sustainable energy production.

For our realigned business support unit, we are looking for a person immediately or by agreement who wants to show what moves EKW, who likes to try new things \&

Professional communication, events \& Central services (30\-80% \- Job sharing possible)

Your tasks

  • Communication (internal \& external): You are responsible for communication via website, intranet, LinkedIn and media releases and make the events visible directly on site. Strategic development: You bring new ideas and develop our communication activities as well as our communication and sponsorship concept.
  • Brand \& Employer Attractiveness: You develop Corporate Identity and Corporate Design with the help of an agency, accompany the redesign of the website and position EKW as an attractive employer for skilled workers and learners.
  • Central services \& Organization: You are the face of EKW, the central contact person for internal and external concerns and represent EKW internally and externally. You take on responsibility in office management and administration and also get involved so that everything runs smoothly in everyday life.
  • Event Management (internal \& external): You organize employee events and public events and develop new formats that strengthen cooperation within EKW. In addition, you are responsible for the strategic project «Anniversary year 75 years EKW» in 2029.

Your profile

  • Professional background: You have completed training or studies, e.g. in communication, marketing, business administration or in the commercial sector, and initial experience in communication, public relations, event organization or administration.
  • Openness \& Readiness to learn: You like to learn, educate yourself and have none before the unknown. Energy, technology and the Engadin region interest you.
  • Communication \& Appearance: You approach people proactively and openly, whether in the office or in our power plants. You communicate clearly and convincingly and find the right tone. Knowledge of Italian or Romance is a plus, but not a must.
  • Organization \& Initiative: You organize yourself and sometimes others, keep several balls in the air at the same time and keep the focus even in colorful situations. You recognize potential for improvement, bring your own ideas and enjoy implementing them.
  • Digital competence and design: You are IT-affine and use digital tools and AI applications in everyday life to make your and others’ work easier. You have an eye for design, photo or video. Knowledge of Adobe Creative Cloud or TYPO3 is an advantage.

The decisive factor for us is not a straightforward CV, but your motivation to take responsibility and make a difference together with us.

Your perspective

With us, you can expect a versatile task with creative leeway and short decision paths. You have the opportunity to actively shape a newly oriented unit, contribute your own ideas and take on responsibility. With growing experience and commitment, you take on leadership-relevant responsibilities.

You work closely with the Head of Business Support, the Directorate and colleagues from different specialist areas. You can count on an open team that supports each other and develops solutions together.

We offer this to you *

  • More time for you: 5\+ weeks holidays, with more and more, and the possibility to buy up to 10 additional holiday days at attractive conditions.
  • Flexible work: Annual working time with time account, flexible design of your working day and 25% home office.
  • Further development: We support your further education with up to 100% of the costs and working time.
  • More than the wage: monthly household allowance in addition to the salary.
  • For families: 16 weeks of maternity or 3 weeks of paternity leave, 100% pay each.
  • Good insurance: EKW takes over the premiums for day sickness and accident insurance, including additional insurance with private hospital department. Our pension fund offers very good services and we finance a large part of the contributions.

For substantive questions about the job or the requirements, the Head of Business Support is available to you via e\-mail. On request, a first informal get-together can be carried out continuously after receiving an application via MS Teams.

Would you like to use your knowledge and motivation to contribute to the future viability and attractiveness of Engadin power plants? Then we look forward to receiving your application directly via our website or via email.
