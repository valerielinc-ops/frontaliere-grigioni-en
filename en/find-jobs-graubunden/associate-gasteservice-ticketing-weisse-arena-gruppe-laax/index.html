Full description

The White Arena Group in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and an enthusiastic community for unique mountain adventures. The group includes various hotel and catering businesses, sports and rental shops, a ski, snowboard and bike school as well as a management and construction company. Our success? A motivated and dedicated team that lives its passion daily. | Weisse Arena Bergbahnen AG is a subsidiary of the Weisse Arena Group, Laax. It is responsible for the construction and operation of the tourist transport facilities and has 28 facilities (cable cars, gondola cars, chairlifts, ski lifts). In addition, the snowmaking, the preparation of the 224 km slopes as well as the parks and pipes, the rescue service and the lift ticket sale. You enjoy dealing with people and use your friendly manner to make our guests feel welcome. Even if there is a lot going on, you keep the overview and find the right solution for our guests. Then a varied position in the guest service & ticketing awaits you – first for the winter season, with the prospect of a subsequent annual job. What You Move:

  • Support for the smooth day-to-day operation of the designated point of sale
  • Competent advice and sale of all products and tickets
  • Processing of refunds, claims and group requests
  • Billing and control of the individual and total billing of the point of sale (daily, weekly)
  • Control of the coin stock and cash deposits (cash and car park)
  • Assistance with staff training of internal sales outlets
  • Cooperation in the preparation of various documentation
  • Support and control in the investment of products and prices
  • Support for the training of new employees and learners
  • Control of stocks of sales and consumables
  • Support and participation in the introduction of new products and projects as well as improvements | What makes you:
  • You have completed vocational training
  • You speak fluent German, can also communicate in English and preferably bring Italian or French skills
  • You are familiar with acting at the forefront and can positively amaze guests
  • Irregular working hours and weekend assignments are a matter of course for you. An attractive workplace in the middle of a breathtaking mountain world is certain for you. You also have the opportunity to develop in our dynamic, bright team and vibrant company culture. Look forward to numerous employee benefits, such as free mountain rides, perks in our shops, hotels and restaurants, and benefits for your family.
