Full description

Brand of the Planzer Group: Kuoni.

We are summit strikers by profession. Because the Alps show us every day what top performance means. With more than 90 vehicles and direct rail connections, we bring goods quickly, reliably and environmentally consciously to every corner of Southeast Switzerland. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

Tasks

  • You work actively in the goods incoming, goods outgoing and in order picking
  • You ensure that the goods land safely and quickly in the right storage space. Chaos? Not with you!
  • The goods you move mainly with floor conveyors from A to B
  • Together with your team, you are responsible for inventory inventory
  • All material movements are recorded in the system
  • You always keep the camp in check and are open for additional tasks to ensure a smooth process

Profile

  • You have completed vocational training as a logistics specialist or would like to work as a lateral entrant in logistics
  • You are already a proud owner of a forklift card or already have experience in running work equipment
  • If you do not yet have a forklift, you are ready to complete the training.
  • Oral and written you can safely express yourself in German
  • Reliability, accuracy and rapid adaptability to new challenges set you apart
  • Even if it gets stressful, you stay cool and keep track.

Benefits

  • With us you start with 5 weeks vacation and benefit from the 5th year of service even from 6 weeks!
  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Do you love cool events? We can celebrate!

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
