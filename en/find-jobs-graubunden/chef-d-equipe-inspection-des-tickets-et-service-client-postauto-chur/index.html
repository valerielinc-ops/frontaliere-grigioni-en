Full description

Do you enjoy leading and promoting employees? With you, we gain a motivated leader who, together with the team, is responsible for the professional implementation of ticket checks and the advice of our passengers in public transport vehicles.

You can do that.

  • As a team player, you are responsible for the technical and personnel management of about 7 employees in the Graubünden area.
  • Your good operational leadership skills support you in recruiting, introducing and accompanying new employees in everyday work.
  • As a proven organizational talent, you independently take the planning and disposition of your employees into your own hands.
  • Your communication-strong appearance supports you in representing your opinion safely in front of the operating zones.
  • You are open to new things and willing to take on special tasks.
  • You also take on special tasks and help with major events or unforeseen events.
  • Through your professional appearance at the ticket checks, you act as a model for your team.

You bring that with you

  • You have further training as a manager, leadership specialist, leadership SVF, specialist card public transport specialist or have already gained profound leadership experience.
  • You have an affinity for public transport as well as several years of experience in control and customer service.
  • In the world of tariffs and national transport, you are familiar.
  • You are a flexible, resilient personality with a high level of social competence, who has good communication skills and assertiveness.
  • The advantages of irregular working hours incl. You appreciate Sundays and public holidays as well as at night and you are flexible.
  • Cat. B is necessary for this position, Cat. D is advantageous.
  • With your stylish knowledge of German and good knowledge of English and Italian you round off your profile.
