Together we produce high-quality organic poultry products!

We are a Swiss company specializing in the processing and marketing of high-quality organic poultry meat. Quality, sustainability and a respectful relationship with each other are important to us.

To strengthen our production team, we are looking for motivated and reliable employees who like to work practically and enjoy handling food.

Your tasks

  • Cutting, processing and packaging of poultry meat
  • Production and preparation of various meat products
  • Order picking and provision of customer orders
  • Compliance with our quality and hygiene requirements
  • Cleaning and maintenance of workplaces and production facilities
  • Cooperation in other production tasks

You bring that with you

  • Enjoying practical work and dealing with food
  • A careful, hygiene-conscious and forward working method
  • Team spirit, flexibility and readiness
  • Physical resilience and willingness to work in cooled rooms
  • Knowledge of German to understand and implement work instructions

Experience in food production or meat processing is an advantage, but not a requirement. Even motivated career changers are welcome!

This is what awaits you with us

  • A varied and responsible activity
  • A collegial team with short decision paths
  • Careful training
  • Development opportunities and the opportunity to learn new things
  • A fair remuneration and contemporary employment conditions
  • A workplace in a company that values quality and sustainability

Interested? We look forward to seeing you!
