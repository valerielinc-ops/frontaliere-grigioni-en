Full description

The Plantahof is a department in the Department of Economics and Social Affairs and the competence center for agricultural education and advice in the Canton of Graubünden. In addition to the agricultural vocational school, the Plantahof offers various further courses, further education courses and conferences and is responsible for comprehensive advice and the provision of specialist knowledge for the benefit of Grisons and Glarner agricultural and alpine farms and other circles involved in agricultural topics. For these purposes, he runs his own experimental and material operation at four different locations together with two farm shops. The conference centre has a modern infrastructure with accommodation options, spacious seminar rooms and contemporary equipped meeting rooms as well as a wide range of catering options.

We are looking for 1. May 2027 or by arrangement a head of the meeting centre area (member of the management).

Tasks

  • Strategic, human, organisational and financial management of the conference centre with its associated departments
  • Ensuring a professional guest and customer support as well as a needs-based offer and service quality
  • Responsibility for the rental and management of training and seminar rooms and accommodation
  • Ensuring technical infrastructure, quality standards and compliance with legal requirements and relevant hygiene and safety regulations
  • Coordination of construction and infrastructure projects and representation of operational user interests
  • Collaboration in department-wide and higher-level projects as well as representation of the Plantahof in specialist committees and external organizations
  • Need-based internal and external communication

Requirements

  • University degree or higher specialist training in hospitality or business administration with a deeper facility management or comparable training
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience in the hotel industry, catering, accommodation or in a comparable service company
  • Strong customer and service orientation, entrepreneurial thinking and strong planning, organization and communication skills
  • Independent, solution-oriented and resilient personality with an understanding of technical and digital connections
  • Experience in project management and in the implementation of infrastructure projects is advantageous
  • The general working language is German. Knowledge of another cantonal language desired
