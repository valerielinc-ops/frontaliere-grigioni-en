Quality Manager (m/f)

Since 2005, Flink has been developing and implementing sophisticated products for customers in Germany and abroad – from the first idea to design and engineering to production and logistics. Swiss precision, creativity and innovative strength characterize our solutions. With an international network and manufacturing expertise, we support companies in the implementation of successful products for medicine, industry and outdoors.

Quality Manager (m/f) 80\-100%, Chur place of work

In order to complement our team, we are looking for a committed personality who actively shapes processes and competently accompanies our departments in all quality-relevant topics.

Your profile

  • Technical or commercial vocational training completed
  • Several years of professional experience in quality and process management as well as corresponding further training
  • In-depth knowledge of the requirements of ISO 9001 and ideally ISO 13485
  • Experience in planning, implementing and supporting internal and external audits
  • Very good communication skills and a stylish expression in German and English

Your main tasks

  • Responsibility for the support, maintenance and further development of the quality management system including QM\ documentation and quality processes
  • Planning, implementation and follow-up of internal and external audits
  • Analysis and processing of deviations, complaints and risks as well as implementation of sustainable improvement measures (CIP)
  • Implementation and coordination of quality checks and product qualifications
  • Support of projects and production areas in quality-related issues
  • Promoting a sustainable culture of quality in the company

Your personality

You are a reliable, solution-oriented personality with a high level of quality awareness and enjoy actively promoting improvement. You understand how to capture complex topics, identify connections and focus on the essentials. With your clear, appreciative communication on an equal footing, you succeed in involving employees and teams, winning over quality topics and getting enthusiastic about common goals. You combine a high quality standard with pragmatism and a good feeling for the needs of our company.

We are

We live an open you culture and a collegial togetherness with flat hierarchies and short decision-making paths. You can expect a varied activity with a lot of creative leeway and personal responsibility. We offer modern working conditions, flexible working hours and a motivated team that values cooperation and mutual support.

We look forward to your complete application documents.
