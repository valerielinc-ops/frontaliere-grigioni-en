Full description

The Radiology Plus team is an association of several established Radiological institutes in eastern Switzerland including the radiological departments of Thurgau’s cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen under one roof. The Radiologie Plus team also provides teleradiological care for several clinics and medical facilities in the cantons of Thurgau, St. Gallen and Graubünden. Our portfolio includes the complete range of imaging diagnostics, interventional radiology, nuclear medicine and radiation therapy.
