Data analyst: in Smart Grid 100%

We are looking for an immediately or by agreement

Repower is one of the leading energy companies in Switzerland. The 120-year history of Repower begins in the very south of Graubünden in Valposchiavo. In addition to the headquarters in Poschiavo, we have further locations in Landquart, Zurich, Ilanz, Küblis, Disentis, Grono and Bever. As an employer, Repower relies on initiative and trust to promote the creativity of its approximately 730 employees. Because the energy industry of tomorrow needs fresh ideas. We offer you an inspiring working world in which you and your team develop future-oriented solutions for a sustainable energy future.

The Smart Grid department is part of the Asset Management Network and Supply division. The division comprises around 15 employees and is responsible for the strategic planning, control and optimization of the network and supply infrastructure throughout the entire life cycle in order to ensure security of supply, efficiency and regulatory compliance. The employees of the division work at the locations Landquart, Küblis, Bever, Ilanz and Poschiavo.

You will do this *

  • Analysis and cleaning of distribution network and measurement data to gain relevant business insights
  • Cooperation in the expansion, maintenance and operation of the Datawarehouse distribution network
  • Provision of data analytical services for team and in-house stakeholders
  • Development of concepts for the intelligent use of flexibilities in the distribution network, such as heat pumps, electromobility and PV systems
  • Optimization of processes for eliminating disturbances in the distribution network
  • Participation in the construction and integration of future smart grid systems
  • Cooperation with universities and other specialist bodies

You bring that with you

  • University degree in a STEM discipline, such as electrical engineering, physics, mathematics or a comparable discipline (ETH/University/FH)
  • Good programming skills in R and , knowledge in Shiny Apps and Power BI are a plus
  • Good knowledge of query and design of relational databases (SQL); Experience with time series optimized databases is advantageous
  • Strong interest in data analysis as well as energy and technical issues
  • Structured, analytical and independent working method
  • Communication skills and enjoyment of working together in a team as well as with external partners
  • Very good German and English skills
