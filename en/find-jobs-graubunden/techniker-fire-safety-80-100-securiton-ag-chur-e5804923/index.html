Technician Fire Safety 80 \- 100% (m/f/d)

Together for a secure future As a leading company in the field of safety technology, Securiton AG convinces worldwide with reliable alarm systems and safety systems. In Switzerland, more than 800 employees work every day at various locations for the safety of our customers. Working at Securiton is more than just one – together as a dedicated team, we protect human lives and prevent property damage.

What you do

  • Install fire alarm systems, put them into operation, instruct and hand them over to the customer
  • Realization of projects in cooperation with the project manager and the executing electricians
  • Responsibility for the maintenance and extension of existing fire alarm systems
  • Resolving malfunctions in technically demanding fire alarm systems
  • Advice and support of customers in all technical aspects
  • Post-integrated service activities
  • High customer satisfaction and enthusiasm for your actions

What you bring

  • Completed training as an electrical installer, automatic operator or telematician (EFZ)
  • Experience in IT as well as network and building technology
  • Rapid comprehension and flexible and independent working methods
  • Strong customer and service orientation
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Place of residence in Engadin or Ober Prättigau
  • Readiness to travel to Engadin

What to expect

  • You culture across all levels
  • Engaged team that achieves more together
  • Our Leadership Principles as the basis for our cooperation: «We are all leaders»
  • Certified "Great\-Place\-To\-Work®" employer
  • Very well developed training and further education offer
  • Flexible working hours, annual working hours
  • Individual and comprehensive implementation programme
  • Between 25 and 30 days holidays
  • Solid pension fund
  • Various special offers (e.g. private purchases from well-known companies, holiday apartments in Switzerland at discounted conditions)
