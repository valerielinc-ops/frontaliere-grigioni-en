Full description

True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing healthcare solutions since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in ventilation equipment, automated pipetting and storing samples, and the development of process sensors.

As part of Hamilton Bonaduz AG's business unit, the division is responsible for key tasks for Hamilton companies. Teams in HR, ICT, corporate communication, and insuring and finance ensure that processes run smoothly and employees are optimally supported worldwide.

You can do that

  • Further development, ensuring operation and continuous optimization of the DMS, SharePoint, Power Platform and M65 environment
  • Analysis, conception, implementation and professional support of ICT projects in the SharePoint and Power Platform environment
  • Support in automation and digitisation of processes using Power Platform and Scripting
  • Processing and solution of incident in 2nd and 3rd level support
  • Creation and maintenance of technical documentation
  • Close cooperation with internal departments for optimal implementation of requirements

You bring this with you

  • Completed training in the ICT sector or equivalent qualification
  • Well-founded, practical experience with Microsoft 365, especially with SharePoint Online and Power Platform
  • Very good knowledge of PowerShell and JavaScript for automation and expansion of M3.5/Sharepoint solutions
  • Experience in handling structured data formats such as JSON and XML
  • Extensive analytical, structured and solution-oriented working methods
  • Ability to communicate complex technical topics intelligibly
  • Team-oriented personality with independent and reliable working methods
  • Very good German knowledge of word and writing as well as good English knowledge
