Construction machinery mechanic / deputy workshop manager \- repair workshop (a)

Learn to know us \- That's why Avesco

Avesco AG is a leading Swiss family company with over 150 years of history, offering specialized solutions in the fields of construction machinery, construction technology and energy systems.

You want to screw up, tackle and take on responsibility as a deputy of our workshop manager in Landquart? Then you are golden right here! With us: “Gohts nid gits nid” – we make heavyweights fit for use again.

Your mission

  • You carry out repairs, maintenance and repair work on our construction machinery - your main place of work is our workshop in Landquart, supplemented by rare assignments directly at the customer's site (construction site / customer workshop)
  • If your workshop manager is absent, you seamlessly take over the wheel: you coordinate assignments, make competent solutions thanks to short decision-making processes and are the first point of contact for your workshop team.
  • You can troubleshoot with state-of-the-art diagnostic software, repair malfunctions in hydraulics and electrics and also revise attachments and components if necessary
  • You listen carefully to problems, work with brains and use our resources sustainably so that occupational safety and safety come first in every mission
  • We grow together: You share your knowledge, learn curiously from your friends, empower your colleagues and celebrate joint successes just as much as the success you have achieved.

Your background

  • Completed basic training as construction machinery, agricultural machinery or truck mechanic (EFZ)
  • The willingness and joy of taking responsibility and representing your workshop manager sovereignly
  • Solid know-how in hydraulics, electrics and mechanics as well as pleasure in troubleshooting and repairing yellow
  • You think solution-oriented, keep cool even with tricky tasks and appreciate an honest, appreciative word at eye level
  • Category B driving licence (category BE is a clear plus)
  • Our values – your plus at Avesco *
