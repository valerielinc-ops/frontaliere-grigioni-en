Full description

  • There is a lot to move. Let's do it together.

On behalf of our client, we accompany the appointment of the management of an organization in the healthcare sector and are pleased to introduce you to this special task. The clinic is located in German-speaking Switzerland and accompanies patients in several medical fields. It is embedded in a group of healthcare companies that also operates beyond national borders. In this way, it combines the proximity of a manageable building with the support of a strong network and can use cross-border synergies.

WORD YOU MAY BE FREE

  • They lead the clinic with an entrepreneurial eye, are responsible for results and finances and ensure that the operation runs economically and smoothly.
  • Together with the medical management, you sharpen the medical offer and align it consistently to the needs of patients and referees.
  • They make the clinic visible in the healthcare market and are their face to the public and partners
  • They establish and deepen relationships with assigning doctors, hospitals and other partners and thus build up a network that supports the clinic in the long term.
  • In close exchange with the management of the Swiss national company, you translate the strategy into concrete steps and bring the clinic reliably to its destination
  • You lead the clinical team, develop your managers in a targeted way and create a culture in which participation, service awareness and clear processes come together.

WHAT YOU HAVE INCOMING

  • A university degree in business or medicine forms your foundation, supplemented by a sound further education in management
  • You have been successfully responsible for a healthcare organization for several years, ideally in a clinic, and know how medical quality and economic success go together
  • You see change as a design space: you have developed organizations, simplified structures and brought teams together across professional groups
  • You convince in negotiations and maintain your network with real interest in the people behind it
  • They keep an eye on the big picture and still take part in everyday life when it matters.
  • Your leadership style is characterized by respect, empathy and clarity, and even under pressure you remain sovereign and open to feedback

What you expect

She expects a clinic with short decision paths, in which her handwriting quickly becomes visible, and at the same time the opportunity to shape the future of the Swiss national company. Establish processes sustainably, develop managers in a targeted manner and take the entire organization a decisive step forward: This task requires creative will, experience and a secure hand. If you feel like tackling it and leaving visible traces in a clinic, we are very happy to meet you.
