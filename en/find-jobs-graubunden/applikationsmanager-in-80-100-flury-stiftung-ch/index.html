Full description

COMPETENT AND CORDIAL With more than 600 employees at Schiers Hospital, the three retirement homes Schiers, Jenaz and Klosters, Spitex, Wohnen mit Service, the crèche, the parent counselling service and the Klosters Medical Centre, the Flury Foundation ensures comprehensive and integrated health care in Prättigau, between Grüsch and Klosters. We are looking for an Application Manager 80 – 100% with immediate effect or by appointment.

Your responsibilities will include

  • Operation, support and further development of the HIS (ines) as well as adjacent subsystems and specialist applications
  • Interface management and integration between systems
  • Recording of requirements in the specialist departments and development of solution concepts
  • Cooperation with external suppliers and system partners
  • Collaboration in ICT projects, taking over project management if necessary

Your profile

  • Training or studies in the field of computer science or an equivalent qualification
  • Good understanding of application architectures, interfaces and databases
  • Ideally, knowledge of hospital processes and the supporting systems (e.g. HIS, ERP) as well as

Standards such as HL7 and FHIR

  • Structured, independent way of working and analytical thinking skills
  • Strong communication skills and enjoyment of exchanging ideas with different professional groups

We offer

  • Future-oriented and innovative company
  • Modern infrastructure
  • A varied and challenging job with a competent and motivated team
  • Generous social benefits, attractive benefits and our own crèche

If you have any questions, please contact Mr. Maximilian Lang, Head of the ICT/Data Protection Department, at +41 81 308 08 86. Further information about the Flury Foundation can be found on the Internet: flurystiftung.ch Please send your application by e-mail to: hr@flurystiftung.ch
