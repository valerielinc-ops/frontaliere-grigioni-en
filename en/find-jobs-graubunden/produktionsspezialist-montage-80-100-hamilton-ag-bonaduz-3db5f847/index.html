Production specialist Assembly 80 \- 100% (m/f/d)

True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

The Operations business unit produces parts for all companies, from injection moulded parts to pipette tips for liquid handling to metal and plastic parts. Our goal is to produce as many parts as possible in-house. In this way, we remain agile, independent and efficient.
