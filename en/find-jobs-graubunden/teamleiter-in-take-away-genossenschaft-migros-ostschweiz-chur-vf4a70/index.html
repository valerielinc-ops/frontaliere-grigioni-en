Full description

The gastronomy of the cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland is diverse, modern and dynamic. Whether Migros restaurant, Migros take away, shared gastronomy or catering services – we offer every customer a tasteful experience.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Take Away, Gäuggeli (Take Away), Gäugglistrasse 28, 7000 Chur

Mansioni

  • You take responsibility for running smoothly in the takeaway area and provide an environment in which our customers and the team feel equally appreciated.
  • In addition, you are actively involved in day-to-day business – whether it is serving and advising our customers at the checkout counter or preparing hot and cold food.
  • You have control over our specific offer concepts and presentation standards being adhered to in the take-away and ensure an appealing offer that delights our customers.
  • Promote team building and accompany the further development of employees and qualify them in the annual cycle.

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in a comparable function
  • Completed basic or further training: in gastronomy
  • You enjoy customer contact and attach great importance to quality - be it when presenting our offers or in sales.
  • Your reliability, flexibility and loyalty make you a true role model.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Working hours without room hour: 41h week and regular working hours without room hour
  • L-GAV: Attractive Land collective employment contract
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Insurance: Benefit Conditions at Various Insurance Companies
