Full description

Company Hamilton AG

True to the vision -We drive innovation to improve people-s lives- Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

The Operations business unit produces parts for all Hamilton companies, from injection moulded parts to pipette tips for liquid handling to metal and plastic parts. Our goal is to produce as many parts as possible in-house. In this way, we remain agile, independent and efficient.

*You can do it *

  • Mechanical and electrical assembly and testing of complex assemblies and instruments in the field of laboratory automation
  • Commissioning and parameterisation of the assembled units in accordance with the technical requirements
  • Implementation of intermediate and final quality checks to ensure functionality and quality standards
  • Ensuring process flows according to specifications for compliance with specified production standards
  • ESD-compliant assembly according to the electrostatic protection regulations
  • Documentation and reporting of test results to optimise work processes and ensure traceability
  • Error analysis and technical problem solving during assembly and testing
  • Cooperation with the development department to continuously improve product quality and efficiency in assembly

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic technical training, ideally in the mechanical, electronic or mechatronic field
  • Several years of experience in the precise assembly and commissioning of assemblies, instruments or automated systems
  • Good computer competence as well as routine in dealing with software-supported assembly, testing and documentation processes
  • Strong holistic and networked thinking as well as an entrepreneurial way of working
  • Precise, efficient and independent working methods as well as a very high quality awareness
  • Good comprehension and ability to quickly understand complex technical relationships
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as English skills are advantageous
  • Ability to perform assembly work requiring prolonged standing and occasional lifting of components
  • Team-oriented, responsible and reliable personality
