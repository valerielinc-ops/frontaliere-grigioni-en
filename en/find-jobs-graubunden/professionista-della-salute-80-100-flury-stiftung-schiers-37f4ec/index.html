Full description

COMPETENT AND WARM With more than 600 employees in the Schiers Hospital, the three retirement homes in Schiers, Jenaz and Klosters, the Spitex, serviced living, crèche, parental advice and the Klosters Medical Center, the Flury Foundation ensures comprehensive and integrated healthcare in Prättigau between Grüsch and Klosters. Our hospital in Schiers is a modern acute primary care hospital with an interdisciplinary ward with 41 beds for the surgery, medicine, gynecology and obstetrics clinics, an outpatient day clinic, the emergency department and the certified intermediate care station (IMC). We are looking for a health specialist 80 - 100% for the day clinic, recovery room, emergency and IMC areas as of October 1, 2026 or by appointment.

Your responsibilities include

  • Care and support for day clinic patients
  • Assistance with medical activities, e.g. punctures, laser therapies, infiltrations
  • Care of patients as part of the preliminary examinations
  • Assist in the care of patients in the recovery room and IMC
  • Assisting in the admission and care of emergency patients

Your profile

  • Completed training as a FaGe (EFZ) with professional experience
  • Communicative, friendly and empathetic personality with high professional and social skills
  • Quick comprehension, independent, solution-oriented and reliable way of working
  • Willingness to acquire additional skills

We offer you

  • Varied and demanding area of ​​responsibility
  • Working in a motivated team and an innovative company
  • Modern workplace with electronic nursing documentation
  • Attractive, contemporary employment conditions and generous social benefits; own crèche
  • Working without night shifts
