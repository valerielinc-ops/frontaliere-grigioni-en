Live and work in the Destination Davos Klosters? We look forward to reinforcement! ##STR1##

Self-employed living at home despite illness, convalescence or age: This is made possible for our clients with your professional support.

As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable, but for the region extremely important company. We maintain a collegial working atmosphere and enjoy the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos.

The Spitex team is looking for an

Dipl. Care professional HF (pensum 60-100%) ##

Your responsibilities

  • Treatment and basic care for our clients at home
  • Case management and needs assessment
  • Assumption of day-to-day responsibility and contact person for all professional groups involved
  • Accompaniment and promotion of learners

Your profile

  • Training as a Dipl. Nurse HF or equivalent recognised training
  • Resilience, responsibility and teamwork
  • Flexibility and readiness for evening and weekend services
  • Self-employed work
  • Very good knowledge of German and driving licence category B

Our offer

  • Careful introduction to an interesting and versatile area of responsibility
  • Responsible, varied and independent work in a motivated team and exciting environment
  • Attractive employment conditions and benefits
  • Hospital-owned nursery
  • Interesting training and further education opportunities
