Production manager / Operations manager bakery (m/f)

Location: Küblis, GR \| 100% \| Monday to Friday

You are a doer, keep the overview even if several things are running at the same time, and like to take on yourself? Do you enjoy production, technology, people and organization?

Then we are looking for you as production manager / operations manager bakery (m/f).

Your tasks

  • In this versatile function, you are the central contact person for production and ensure that people, machines, raw materials and processes play together optimally.
  • Planning and organization of daily production.
  • Create and coordinate production plans.
  • Management and operational planning of a team of around 20–25 employees. *
  • Create and coordinate service and holiday plans. *
  • Active presence and support in production.
  • Monitoring production processes and ensuring smooth operation.
  • Recognizing potential for improvement and further development of processes and processes. *
  • Procurement and ordering of raw materials and monitoring of availability.
  • Organization and provision of the required materials for production and logistics.
  • Control and filing of delivery notes and other production documents.
  • Implementation and monitoring of HACCP\ requirements. *
  • Support for the implementation and further development of the FSSC\-22000\ system. *
  • Assistance in internal and external controls and in the preparation of audits.
  • Cooperation with management and other areas.
  • Monitoring of machinery and equipment.
  • Implement small technical repairs and temporary solutions independently if the situation demands it.
  • Coordination of repairs and maintenance in case of major technical problems.
  • Assistance in the optimization of machines, workplaces and production processes.
  • Ensuring order, quality, hygiene and safety at work

Your profile

We are not looking for a pure desk leader, but a practical all-rounder who likes to be in the middle of the action.

Ideally, you bring

  • Professional experience in a bakery, food production or comparable production. *
  • Training as a baker, confectioner or comparable training is an advantage.
  • Experience in the management and organization of employees.
  • Understanding and craftsmanship.
  • Enjoy understanding machines and plants and finding a solution for minor problems yourself.
  • Experience with production planning and work organisation.
  • A sense of responsibility and independent working methods.
  • Good organizational skills.
  • Quality and hygiene awareness.
  • Ideally, knowledge of HACCP and FSSC 22000\.
  • Good communication and enforcement skills.
  • Willingness to participate in production, if necessary.
  • Flexibility and the ability to keep track even in hectic situations.

What to expect

With us it will not be boring. The position is versatile and combines leadership, production, technology, organization and quality management.

You have a lot of creative freedom and can contribute your experience and ideas directly. You are not only at the desk, but active in production and close to the team.

We offer you

  • Basically no night work or weekend work. In urgent and rare exceptional cases, short-term use outside regular working hours – also at night or on weekends – may be necessary, for example in the event of technical disruptions or urgent production problems. Such missions are the exception.
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • 13 Monthly Wages
  • Free parking
  • A varied and responsible leadership position
  • Short decision-making processes and direct cooperation with the management
  • Plenty of personal responsibility and versatile task area with production, technology, personnel, logistics and quality
  • The opportunity to actively shape and improve processes
