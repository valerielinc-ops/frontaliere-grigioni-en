Full description

The Alberti Nursing Home of Spital Davos AG with 16 beds will be transferred to the Guggerbach Centre in the first quarter of 2028. As Head of Care in the Guggerbach Center, you will gradually take over the management of the Alberti Nursing Home from 2027 onwards and are actively responsible for the transfer process. As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable, but for the region extremely important company. We maintain a collegial working atmosphere and enjoy the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos. | In cooperation with Zentrum Guggerbach, we are looking for a full-time or part-time search by autumn 2026 or by agreement

  • Personnel, professional and organizational management of the care area I for 40 residents in the center of Guggerbach with all related tasks
  • Co-responsibility and co-creation of the process of transferring the Alberti nursing home to the Guggerbach Center as of 1. Quarter 2028, close cooperation and coordination with the hospital and center management, the affected employees, residents and relatives
  • Take over the professional and organizational management of the nursing home Alberti and lead a team of around twelve employees from 2027 until the transfer |
  • Completed training as a nurse (HF/DNI)
