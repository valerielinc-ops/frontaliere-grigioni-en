This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The Bundner Kantonsschule is an important educational institution with about 1,000 pupils of all three cantonal languages. As a department of the Office for Higher Education, it offers secondary education courses at the long- and short-term high school as well as at the technical, commercial and computer schools. The IT service provides a secure and reliable digital infrastructure for teaching and learning operations, supports learners, teachers and employees with technical concerns and accompanies the school in the further development of its digital work and learning environment.

We are looking for an ICT System Engineer/-in Support & Projects for the IT service as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • Ensuring the operation of the IT infrastructure and the system environment as well as their further development
  • Support of server, network, client and cloud systems based on Microsoft, Apple and Linux technologies
  • Analysis and correction of technical problems in 1st and 2nd level support and support of teachers, employees and learners in technical problems
  • Planning and realization of IT projects, development of technical concepts and introduction of new solutions and technologies
  • Support and development of audio, video and media technology in school premises
  • Creation and maintenance of technical documentation, standards and processes
  • Support for teachers and employees in the use of digital tools and in the technical implementation of new digital forms of learning and work

Requirements

  • Completed training as a computer scientist or equivalent qualification
  • Professional experience in the administration and support of IT infrastructures as well as good knowledge in several of the following areas: Windows Server, Linux, Active Directory, Entra ID, Microsoft 365, network, virtualization and client systems
  • Experience with IT projects, concept work and the introduction of new technologies
  • Analytical, networked thinking, an independent, structured and solution-oriented way of working and the willingness to take responsibility
  • Service-oriented, open personality who enjoys working with people and has the ability to convey technical issues in an understandable way
  • Knowledge of German, knowledge of other cantonal languages and interest in the development of digital media in education are beneficial
