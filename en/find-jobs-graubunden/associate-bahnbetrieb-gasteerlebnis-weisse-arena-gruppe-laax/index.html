Full description

The White Arena Group in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and an enthusiastic community for unique mountain adventures. With mountain railways, hotels, gastronomy, sports and rental shops and the LAAX School, we create unforgettable experiences for our guests - and attractive workplaces for our employees. | Weisse Arena Bergbahnen AG is a subsidiary of the Weisse Arena Group, Laax. It is responsible for the construction and operation of the tourist transport facilities and has 30 facilities (cable cars, gondola cars, chairlifts, ski lifts). Furthermore, the snowmaking, the preparation of the 215 km slopes as well as the parks and pipes, the rescue service and the lift ticket sale. Do you love the mountains and enjoy making a successful day in LAAX possible for people? Then become part of our LAAX Ropeways crew. As a host: you are there for our guests on the train, ensure a friendly and smooth process and serve your facility safely and reliably. We will teach you the necessary technical knowledge. | What moves you:

  • You receive our guests attentively, help with questions and provide a positive experience on the train
  • You support our guests when boarding and disembarking and ensure a safe and smooth process
  • You prepare the assigned railway installation for daily operation and operate it properly
  • You keep an eye on the company and act calmly and responsibly in special situations
  • You ensure order and cleanliness and support in the event of disturbances as well as daily and monthly checks. What makes you:
  • You are warm, attentive and enjoy contact with different people
  • You take responsibility, stay calm even in lively situations and work reliably in the team
  • You speak good German (at least level B2) and can ideally also communicate in English.
  • You are ready to work on weekends and holidays
  • Skiing or snowboarding knowledge is an advantage. An attractive workplace in the middle of an extraordinary mountain world is certain for you. You become part of a dynamic, bright team and can help shape the further development of LAAX. With us you will find an environment where professionalism, speed, pragmatism and humanity go together. Look forward to numerous employee benefits, such as free mountain rides, perks in our shops, hotels and restaurants, and benefits for your family. Start your professional adventure at the Weissen Arena Group with your complete online application. We look forward to seeing you. | Weisse Arena Bergbahnen AG Bahnbetrieb Sabrina Pascher www.weissearena.com I www.laax.com
