Manufacturer driver:in Your contribution with us

  • You serve and guide our road pavers professionally and safely
  • You ensure a precise and uniform installation of asphalt coverings
  • You set up the paver for the respective applications and monitor the installation process
  • You work closely with the installation team, the polisher and other machinists
  • You carry out checks and minor maintenance and maintenance work on the machine
  • You pay attention to a safe, economical and careful handling of machines and equipment
  • You can also support the construction team with further work if necessary

What matters to us

  • Experience as a paver driver: in road and surface construction
  • Safe and responsible handling of construction machinery
  • Technical understanding and good feeling for machines
  • Independent, reliable and careful working method
  • Teamwork and willingness to take part in the construction site
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Category B driving licence is advantageous
