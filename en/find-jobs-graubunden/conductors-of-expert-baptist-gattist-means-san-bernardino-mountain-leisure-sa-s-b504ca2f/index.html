Full description

We are looking for drivers of expert baptists/catters for the next winter season

Main responsibilities

  • Preparation and maintenance of the slopes: Latest generation baptist means for leveling, milling and shaping of the snowy mantle, in accordance with the safety standards of the area.
  • Operations with winch in slope: Execution of beat jobs on high slope strokes, strictly applying UFT technical requirements and Suva safety protocols for anchoring and using the winch.
  • Ordinary maintenance of the vehicle: Daily care, cleaning and technical checks before and after the work shift, providing the correct compilation and signature of the official operating records of the plant.
  • Safety and hazard warning: Constant monitoring of the status of the tracks and timely reporting obligation to the Technical Chapter (or its Replacement) of any anomalies, avalanche hazards, obstacles or need to disembark the tracks.

Required Requirements and Regular Framework

  • Experience and qualifications: Comprehensive and autonomous experience in the conduct of snow cats in alpine ski resorts.
  • Legal skills (UFT / Suva): Possess of regular driving license valid in Switzerland (Cat. B / C).
  • Official training certificate for drivers with winch, in accordance with the safety guidelines for work on the slopes.
  • Psychophysical fitness: Perfect physical and mental fitness (fundamental requirement for critical task safety), prolonged concentration capacity under poor visibility or adverse weather conditions.
  • Flexibility and LDL Standard: Absolute availability at work on mainly night shifts, weekends and holidays. The planning of shifts and rest periods fully respects the Federal Law on the duration of work (LDL) in the transport sector.
  • Language skills: Excellent knowledge of the Italian language to ensure correct radio communication and immediate understanding of the safety instructions of the Chief Exercise. German knowledge is a plus.

What we offer

  • Swiss seasonal work contract complies with the federal regulations in force and the wage tables established by collective agreements in category.
  • Time frame and allowance for night and holiday work regulated by law.
  • Use of technologically cutting-edge vehicles inserted in a certified maintenance plan.
