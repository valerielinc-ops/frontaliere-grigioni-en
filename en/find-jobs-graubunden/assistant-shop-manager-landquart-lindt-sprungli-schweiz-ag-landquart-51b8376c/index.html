Assistant Shop Manager Landquart (60\-80%)

About us

We enchant the world with chocolate! \& Sprüngli (Switzerland) AG is a subsidiary of the global \& Sprüngli Group with headquarters and production facilities in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich. In Altendorf we operate our own logistics centre, while the Cocoa Center in Olten produces cocoa mass for our own use as well as for other European production facilities. Throughout Switzerland, we proudly operate 15 shops, including iconic locations such as the Jungfraujoch and the Swiss Transport House in Lucerne. More than 1000 employees from more than 50 nations contribute to our success.

What you do with us

  • Team and shop management in the absence of the shop manager
  • Support in recruitment and in the creation of shift plans
  • First-class sales advice for our local and international customers
  • Merchandising of sales shelves (including freshness checks)
  • Cash register operation
  • Assistance in warehouse management
  • Co-responsibility for the entire process of shop\ operation

When you fit in with us

  • An advantage is a completed training as a retail specialist EFZ or a comparable training
  • At least 2\-3 years of professional and managerial experience in a comparable function
  • Convincing salesperson with a high level of customer orientation
  • Flexible in time and ready to take on weekend assignments
  • Team-capable, committed and open personality
  • Passion for our premium chocolate and for attention to detail
  • Flowing German and good English skills, other languages beneficial
  • Careful appearance and a friendly and proactive nature

We are all unique!

We recruit free from prejudice and consider skills and qualifications unaffected by personal characteristics such as gender, sexual orientation, origin, religion, marital status, impairments, etc.

Health Health is important to us. With a sports premium and other health initiatives, we support you to stay fit.

Development Learning is written BIG with us. For personal and professional development, various exciting possibilities are available to you.

Family-friendly environment \- properly insured Our social insurance, as well as maternity leave, goes far beyond the mandatory for your own protection and for your loved ones.

Flexibility Depending on the function, flexible working hours, home office and part-time work are possible. We also offer you the option to increase the holiday days.

Advantage Numerous perks such as a top staff restaurant, discounts in the shops or great leisure offers are at your disposal.
