Product Owner Service Management and Support

Cantonal Police \| 60\-100% \| Chur

Together for Graubünden Graubünden is the only trilingual canton in Switzerland and economically, culturally and politically diverse. With 7106 km2, it is the largest canton in Switzerland and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is as versatile as our landscape. Bring your skills and shape the future of the Cantonal Administration with us.

Your surroundings As a service provider, the ICT Service Management team of the Cantonal Police Graubünden ensures the smooth and safe operation of more than 600 workstations and mobile devices. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a motivated personality as an ICT product owner. In close cooperation with the ICT\ operations team and the Office of Computer Science, you ensure that our employees receive the best possible IT\ service.

Your responsibilities

  • Capture, prioritize and implement requirements and needs together with internal and external stakeholders
  • Continuous development of IT\ service management and support processes
  • Coordination and support of releases and changes
  • Identification of problems and risks and development of suitable solutions
  • Leading projects to improve user experience and customer satisfaction
  • Participation in license management
  • Supporting the team in handling incidents

We offer this to you

  • A versatile and responsible job in a committed team
  • The opportunity to actively shape ICT\ service management in the security-critical environment of the cantonal police
  • Flexible working time models, including job sharing
  • Workplace in Chur with good public transport connections

That is what we want from you.

  • Completed training in computer science EFZ, ideally supplemented by further training in computer science or business informatics at the level of HF/FH
  • Several years of professional experience and in-depth expertise in the IT\ area
  • Experience as a product owner or business analyst, ideally in service management and support
  • Strong communication and coordination skills as well as an independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Good understanding of user experience, service quality and information security
  • The ability to communicate complex technical relationships in an understandable and addressable manner.
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of other cantonal languages is advantageous
  • Readiness for periodic cooking (4\-6 weeks per year)

Our advantages

Flexible working time models and where possible incl. Home office and mobile work\-life\-balance with mind. 5 weeks holidays and service leave Special social allowance in addition to child allowance and support for third-party childcare Attractive wage and social benefits Various training and further education opportunities to help shape the future of the canton

Place of work

Cantonal Police Ringstrasse 2 7000 churn

For questions, Donnicola, Head of ICT, is at your disposal.

Application deadline: 29\. November 2026

We look forward to your online application and getting to know you.
