Full description

True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

The Process Analytics business unit develops, produces and distributes state-of-the-art process measurement technology for customers in biopharmaceuticals, chemicals and breweries. Our product portfolio includes sensors for critical process parameters such as pH, oxygen, or sensors for measuring cell density, which are extremely important in biological processes and are unique worldwide with their innovative technology.

You can do that

  • Process-oriented implementation of quality requirements based on applicable standards, legal requirements and customer requirements
  • Independent preparation, implementation and follow-up of internal and external audits (customer, authority and certification audits)
  • Responsibility for compliance and further development of the requirements of ISO 17034 in the field of reference materials
  • Handling general quality assurance tasks such as customer quality assurance arrangements, change management and risk assessments
  • Handling regulatory issues over the entire product lifecycle and supporting the specialist areas in their implementation
  • Preparation and maintenance of quality documentation as well as advice and training of the respective specialist areas
  • Active participation in interdisciplinary projects and ensuring quality requirements

This is what you bring

  • Technical or scientific training with further training in quality management or comparable qualifications
  • Professional experience in quality assurance, quality management or a comparable environment, ideally with experience in carrying out and accompanying audits
  • Good knowledge of ISO 9001
  • Knowledge of ISO 17034 is an advantage
  • Good understanding of customer requirements and regulatory requirements as well as the ability to translate them into workable processes and processes
  • Structured and methodical working methods as well as flexible, tolerant and factual treatment of internal and external contact persons
  • Initiative and communicative personality with hands-on mentality and customer-oriented solutions
  • Due to the international team, in addition to German, very good English skills in word and writing are required
  • Safe handling of MS Office
