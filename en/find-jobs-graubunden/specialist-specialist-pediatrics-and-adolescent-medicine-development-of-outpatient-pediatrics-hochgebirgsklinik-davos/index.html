Full description

Outpatient pediatry in the interdisciplinary clinic of the SIX-H 190 beds, 5 disciplines, over 120 years of history – and 1560 m above sea level.The high mountain clinic Davos is an interdisciplinary rehabilitation centre with a universitary connection – embedded in the spectacular nature of the Grisons Alps. In our clinic's own ambulatory, we care for children and adolescents outpatiently and work closely with various specialist disciplines of the clinic. You do not want to treat simply, but to develop medical care and work interdisciplinaryly?

Then we would like to apply to you.

Tasks

  • As a specialist in paediatric and adolescent medicine, you will become part of a multiprof-unified team that accompanies children, adolescents and their families holistically – medically, psychologically and pädagogisch.

  • Support: In addition to outpatient activities, you will help our somatic patient: in the inpatient area if necessary.
  • Precede interdisciplinary: You work closely with psychology, care and pedagogy – and give impulses for new supply routes. - Contributing: You get involved in the conceptual process and support in the expansion of our unique parent-child offer throughout Switzerland.

  • Practical or clinical experience: You have experience in the medical care of children and adolescents – ideally in outpatient or interdisciplinary in the city of S.A.
  • Appreciate team culture: You feel comfortable in flat hierarchies with open communication. - Pleasure for design: You enjoy building a new structure in the ambulatory and bringing in your own ideas. - Appreciate quality of life: You value an environment with a high quality of life – in the middle of nature and yet well connected.
