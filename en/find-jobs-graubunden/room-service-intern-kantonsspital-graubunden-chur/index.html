Full description

Start by appointment as a cadre intern: in Room-Service 100 - 100%

During an assignment period of six months, we offer you exciting insights into everyday life on the stations in the form of an internship. In addition, we enable you to get to know the management activities of our management team. Does that sound exciting to you? Perfect, then you are exactly right with us!

These are your tasks

  • You receive the patients on the ward and pamper them with a professional meal and drink service, are the contact person for the infrastructure and provide daily cleanliness and laundry in the room.
  • As Guest Relation Manager, you clarify the needs of our additional insured patients and are their contact person during the stay.
  • In the management team you support the team leaders Room-Service in administration and leadership tasks and participate in meetings
  • You will also develop your own small project and gain insights into other interdisciplinary areas of the hospital

You bring that with you

  • You are a student: at the hotel school and ideally bring your first experience in the service
  • In hectic situations, stay calm and work independently
  • You are a motivated and flexible personality with well-groomed manners
  • A high customer orientation is your top priority

Can you do something that others can’t? You make the difference? Then you belong to us!

And that's what we offer you.

  • Flexible working hours / Home Office / Part-time work: If the company allows it, working hours and expenses can be flexibly tailored to your current living situation.
  • Gastronomic offer: All four locations of the cantonal hospital Graubünden offer a wide range of catering options. With us you can eat varied, balanced and healthy.
  • Health: The health of our employees: inside is important to us: The cantonal hospital Graubünden was awarded the quality label Friendy Work Space in November 2022.
  • Accommodation for staff: For our employees, interns and apprentices, we provide staff rooms and apartments at a discount as far as possible.
  • Our values: The excellent quality of our services and the health and satisfaction of our employees are equally our focus. An open, appreciative corporate culture, diversity, tolerance and sustainability are just as much an integral part of our philosophy as progressive employment conditions and diverse development opportunities.
