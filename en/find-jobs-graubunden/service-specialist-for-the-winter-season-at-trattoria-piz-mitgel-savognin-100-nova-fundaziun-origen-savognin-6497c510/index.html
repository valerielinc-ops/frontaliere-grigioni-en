Origen is a visionary, vital cultural institution in Graubünden. The Foundation has a broad cultural portfolio. It realizes cross-division stage productions, enables concert series and develops an innovative open-air play that seeks dialogue with nature. Origen is committed to a high-quality building culture, preserves and revitalizes listed structures, creates iconic cultural buildings and makes a significant contribution to the digital transformation in construction in cooperation with ETH Zurich. Origen cooperates with important universities, conducts research on cultural-historical topics, is committed to the Romanesque language and teaches in depth in various disciplines. Origen is committed to inspiring hospitality, renovating and managing historic hotels and developing new culinary concepts in its own restaurants. The foundation is committed to a culturally shaped, inspired revitalization of the Alpine cultural landscape, creates lively places, promotes quality tourism and creates meaningful jobs in one of the most beautiful regions of the Alps.
