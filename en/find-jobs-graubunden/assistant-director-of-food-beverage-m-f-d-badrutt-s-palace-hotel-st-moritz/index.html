Full description

What you will do

As Assistant Director of Food & Beverage, you will support the Director of Food & Beverage in leading and developing the entire F&B area. Together with the managers, you will ensure an exceptional guest experience and smooth operations in our outlets.

- Working closely with department heads, you will oversee the day-to-day operations of up to eleven F&B outlets and their support departments.

- You will actively support the implementation and further development of the F&B strategy, our concepts and processes.

- Your

  • responsibilities also include contributing to budgeting, revenue growth, cost control and performance management.

- Together with the managers, you will develop the teams and ensure that our service and quality standards are consistently upheld. You will also support workforce planning and recruitment.

- As a natural host, you will be visible, build relationships with our guests and attend thoughtfully to their needs. At the same time, you will coordinate collaboration with the hotel’s various departments and ensure compliance with legal and operational requirements.

- In the absence of the Director of Food & Beverage, you will assume operational responsibility for the department.

What you bring

- Several years of experience in Food & Beverage at an international luxury hotel, ideally in a comparable position.

- Experience leading and developing international teams.

- Strong operational expertise and a good understanding of business operations.

- Knowledge of budgeting, cost control and resource planning.

- A confident, guest-focused personality who enjoys direct contact with guests.

- An independent, decisive and solution-oriented approach to work.

- Very good German and English skills; additional languages are an advantage.

Our offer

- A permanent year-round position in a versatile leadership role in the international luxury hotel industry.

- The opportunity to actively shape the further development of our F&B department.

- An attractive working environment in St. Moritz with individual development and career opportunities.

- Accommodation in one of our new staff residences at the beginning.

- A 13th-month salary from the start of employment, paid pro rata, as well as five weeks of vacation and six public holidays per year.

If you enjoy an interesting, challenging and varied role in a dynamic environment and would like to join a well-established team, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.

Warning about recruitment scams

Badrutt’s Palace Hotel will never request payments or financial information as part of the recruitment process. All official communications take place exclusively through email addresses ending in @ badruttspalace.com . If you receive a payment request or a suspicious message, please ignore it and contact us directly through our official website.
