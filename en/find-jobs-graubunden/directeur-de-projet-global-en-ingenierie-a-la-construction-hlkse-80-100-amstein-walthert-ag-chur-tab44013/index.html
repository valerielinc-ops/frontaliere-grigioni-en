Full description

Amstein + Walthert is an engineering company with around 1100 employees:innen. As engineers, planners and consultants, we work on all aspects of the technology of civil engineering and construction over the entire life cycle. Our solutions are innovative, elegant and sustainable. These are your tasks

  • You assume overall responsibility for all technical trades (heating, ventilation, sanitary, electrical) on our construction projects.
  • You guide and support the specialist construction managers as well as the project managers of the individual trades.
  • You participate in internal and external project meetings
  • You ensure compliance with quality, deadlines and costs and always keep an overview.
  • During 2-3 days a week you are present directly on site in the Engadin, coordinate the trades, make decisions and are available as a competent contact person.
  • 1-2 days per week you work in the home office or at one of our modern locations in German-speaking Switzerland.
  • If you are not working on the Engadin construction site, you will work on further technical projects in the planning of your competence (e.g. as a heating, ventilation, sanitary or electrical planner)
  • you work closely with other departments of the A+W Group You bring that with you
  • Training in building technology (HLKSE) with further education at higher technical school such as FH, TS or equivalent
  • Several years of experience in the management and coordination of construction projects, especially in the field of HVACs
  • Leadership experience and enforcement
  • Strength of organisation and communication as well as own initiative
  • Reliable personality with purposeful work
  • Enthusiasm for building technology
  • You like to work in a team also across locations
  • Enjoyment of demanding work
  • Good knowledge of MS-Office and CAD
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing, English and/or Italian knowledge of advantage
  • Willingness to travel with a focus on Engadin
