Full description

The civil engineering office is responsible for the design, construction, maintenance and operation of the cantonal roads as well as the hydraulic engineering and slow transport in Graubünden. We also take care of the operational maintenance of the national roads on the cantonal territory. The canton’s road infrastructure comprises over 1,450 art buildings, including bridges at airy heights of up to 90 meters and tunnels of up to 3 kilometers in length. The challenges that the nature of our mountain canton brings in summer and winter make our work challenging and versatile. As a Friendly Work Space certified office, the health and satisfaction of our approximately 480 employees are particularly important to us. For this reason, we are committed to systematic occupational health management (BGM).

We are looking for the road construction department as of 1. December 2026 or by agreement a draftsman/in-constructor/in the field of road planning

Tasks

  • Creating drafts for main road projects in the study phase with AutoCAD and Civil 3D
  • Adaptation of the project planning basis in the field of road construction
  • Support of project managers in drawing aspects of subtasks in projects
  • Control of third party plans for completeness and presentation
  • Testing and installing updates of CAD programs

Requirements

  • Completed apprenticeship as a draftsman/in civil engineering EFZ
  • Several years of professional experience in road projecting
  • Experience of AutoCAD and Civil 3D is beneficial
  • Readiness to learn and initiative
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary teamwork
