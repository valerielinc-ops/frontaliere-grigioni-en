Full description

Start by agreement as Automation Engineer 80 - 100%

The team for automation and artificial intelligence is part of IT and shapes the digital development together with the specialist areas. In a modern and interdisciplinary environment, we identify potential for optimization in clinical and administrative processes, develop digital solutions and accompany their introduction into everyday work.

Would you like to participate in the establishment and further development of our competence center for automation and artificial intelligence? Then we look forward to meeting you!

These are your tasks

  • You contribute to the development of the competence center and help establish standards and best practices
  • You design, develop and maintain stable and scalable automation solutions based on the UiPath platform and implement end-to-end process automation with interfaces, data flows, APIs and AI components
  • You will assess and prioritize automation and AI use cases, participate in architectural and design decisions and develop the use of modern technologies such as document understanding, AI services and agentic automation.

You bring that with you

  • Experience in developing RPA solutions, ideally on the UiPath platform, as well as an understanding of software development processes
  • The ability to identify optimization potential in business processes and the will to implement automation from idea to operation independently
  • Interest in professional development in the field of Artificial Intelligence and Agentic Automation
