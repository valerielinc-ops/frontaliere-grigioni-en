Toolmaker/tool maintenance

Toolmaker / tool maintenance (m/f/d)

Three locations. One team. Your future.

The PLAST Group unites Plast AG in Romanshorn, Plast AG in Maienfeld and Plast AG in Uznach. With around 76 employees and 64 machines, we combine many years of experience with modern plastic technology and innovative manufacturing solutions. PP MASTER

From large components and assembly to precision injection molding, toolmaking and 3K technology, to medical technology, micro injection molding and measurement technology, our three locations offer a versatile working environment. PP MASTER

As a certified group of companies, we stand for quality, innovation and continuous development. At the same time, we focus on modern workplaces, flexible working time models, short decision-making processes and a corporate culture in which our employees are valued as people and specialists.

Discover your possibilities at the PLAST Group – and shape the future with us.

Your tasks

  • Production, maintenance, repair and optimisation of demanding injection moulding tools
  • Independent analysis and correction of tool problems
  • Milling, grinding, turning and, if necessary, erosion work
  • Support and assistance in the design and implementation of CAD/CAM\ solutions
  • Programming and operation of CNC\ machines
  • Technical support and coordination within toolmaking
  • Ensuring quality requirements and documentation of the work carried out
  • Active participation in process improvements and technical developments
  • Your profile *
  • Completed training as a toolmaker, polymechanic, or comparable technical training in the field of plastics
  • Professional experience in tool and mould construction
  • Sound knowledge in the areas of milling, grinding, turning and ideally erosion
  • Experience in the field of injection molding tools is advantageous
  • Good CAD/CAM\ knowledge
  • Experience with Mastercam and/or VISI is beneficial
  • Independent, precise and solution-oriented working method
  • High quality and safety awareness
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Teamwork, responsibility and leadership skills

We offer

  • Attractive compensation with 13\. Monthly wage and clear promotion opportunities
  • Flexible working time models and contemporary workplaces
  • Exciting and varied activity with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Flat hierarchies and short decision paths
  • Education and training opportunities
  • Attractive social benefits
  • Possibility to actively take responsibility and shape toolmaking

Application information If you are interested in this exciting position, we look forward to receiving your application via this website or our website.

Please send us your CV and a short cover letter explaining your motivation and suitability for this position.

We look forward to meeting you.
