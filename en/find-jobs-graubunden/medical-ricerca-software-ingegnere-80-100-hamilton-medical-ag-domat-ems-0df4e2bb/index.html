Medical Research Software Engineer 80 \- 100 % (w/m/d)

For more than 40 years, Medical has shaped the future of mechanical ventilation. We develop Intelligent Ventilation solutions that support clinicians in critical moments.

Our ventilators are used in intensive care units, during emergency transport, and in MRI environments. They help medical teams care for adult, pediatric, and neonatal patients worldwide.

Here, your work has real impact. Every improvement you make supports better respiratory care for patients around the globe.

How you can make a difference

  • Implement and evaluate new research concepts using in silico simulations and HIL testing.
  • Develop C\+\+ research features from early prototypes through to software releases.
  • Perform the validation and verification of the research software to comply with regulated software development processes.
  • Contribute to the design and refinement of new ventilation technologies in close collaboration with researchers and medical experts.

That's what you bring along

  • A master's degree in computer science, biomedical engineering, mechanical engineering, control systems engineering, or a related field, and five years of professional experience.
  • Strong C\+\+14 programming skills; experience with MATLAB or is a plus. Experience in software architecture and rapid prototyping.
  • Experience with IBM Rhapsody and Cantata\+\+ is an advantage. Experience with software development processes, particularly verification and validation; experience in a regulated environment is an advantage.
  • Experience with modelling, simulation, numerical methods, or data analysis in a technical, medical, or physiological context is highly beneficial.
  • An interest in mechanical ventilation and closed\-loop control systems. Knowledge of respiratory physiology or physiological patient modelling is beneficial.
  • A structured and analytical working style and the ability to collaborate effectively within a multidisciplinary team.
  • Fluency in English; knowledge of is an advantage.
