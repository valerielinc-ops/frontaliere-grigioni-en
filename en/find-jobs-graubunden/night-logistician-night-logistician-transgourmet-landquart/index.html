Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services.

Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You monitor and carry out the consolidation of customer orders
  • You make sure to avoid damage to goods when compacting
  • You control the completeness of the products provided for each tour
  • You manage the empty containers
  • You collect and distribute the crossdocking articles
  • You take false deliveries and forward them to your superior
  • You ensure compliance with the operational guidelines for occupational safety and accident prevention
  • You comply with the legal QS guidelines

Requirements

  • You have professional experience in logistics or a completed apprenticeship as a logistics specialist EFZ or EBA
  • You have an SUVA-approved truck pass and have sufficient driving experience
  • You work goal-oriented and structured
  • You show your own initiative, are reliable and bring a high commitment
  • You are physically robust and ready to work in refrigerators with temperatures between -20 °C and +5 °C
  • You have good knowledge of German in word and writing
