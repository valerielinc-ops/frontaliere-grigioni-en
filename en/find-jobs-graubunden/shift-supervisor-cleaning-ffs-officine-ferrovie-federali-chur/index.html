Shift manager: in Cleaning 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Shift manager: in Cleaning Chur, 01.12.2026 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

As a shift manager: in Cleaning, you and your team ensure that our customers travel on clean trains. You conduct the layer, clean with it and ensure the cleaning quality. You coordinate the human resources as needed and ordered services and are the contact person for your team and for communication with the stakeholders. You see irregular working hours and shift work as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. Night and weekend work is also paid for, for example, an hourly wage supplement of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night assignments, you get time surcharges of 10 to 30%, which allows you more free time. Motivated and willing to actively invest in your professional development, you will complete the internal cleaning training for shift managers with certification. Interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • Work and human resources are organized and coordinated as needed and based on work plans.
  • You support your team during the shift and ensure optimal working conditions.
  • You ensure the required quality and communicate to the appropriate interfaces.
  • Work safety is your top priority.
  • You support your manager in introducing new employees.

You bring that with you.

  • Working with people gives you pleasure.
  • You are adept at using digital tools.
  • You bring the willingness to work in shift operation.
  • You bring experience in the cleaning industry and have knowledge of railway operations.
  • You have completed an apprenticeship as a specialist in cleaning technology EFZ or an equivalent apprenticeship or are ready to complete this apprenticeship within two years.
  • You have good knowledge of German or are ready to learn it at level B1.
