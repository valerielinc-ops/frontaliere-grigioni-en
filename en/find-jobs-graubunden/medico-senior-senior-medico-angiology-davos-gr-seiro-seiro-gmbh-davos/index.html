Full description

Responsible for and at the same time shaping one discipline: Angiology in the only inpatient angiological rehabilitation in Switzerland. In Davos, Canton of Graubünden, we are looking for a senior physician or a senior physician angiology in the range of 60 – 100% by appointment. In a specialized rehabilitation clinic with 190 beds and five disciplines, you take on the professional responsibility for angiology and shape its strategic development in the cardiovascular team. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • You are responsible for angiology and shape its further development
  • They treat patients with complex angiological diseases in the ambulatory and inpatient setting
  • Together with cardiology, you are driving the expansion of angiology in the cardiovascular team
  • They work closely interdisciplinary with cardiology, pulmonology, dermatology and other disciplines
  • They shape structures and expand outpatient and stationary offers in a targeted manner
  • You will take over the professional leadership and further education of the doctors in your field

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title Angiology
  • Several years of clinical experience in the detection and treatment of complex vascular diseases
  • Leadership experience and the willingness to take responsibility for a specialist area
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

That sets them apart.

  • Entrepreneurial, forward-looking thinking with clear professional leadership
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and the development of employees
  • Strong willingness to shape structures and services

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Management function with real specialist responsibility in the only inpatient angiological rehabilitation in Switzerland
  • Great scope for developing angiology in the cardiovascular team
  • Interdisciplinary Rehabilitation Centre
  • Flat hierarchies, short decision-making paths and a collegial working atmosphere
  • Living environment with a high quality of life
