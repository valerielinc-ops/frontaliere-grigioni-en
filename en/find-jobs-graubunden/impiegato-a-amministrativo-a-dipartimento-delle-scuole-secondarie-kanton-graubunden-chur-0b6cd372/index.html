Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the Cantonal Administration Graubünden together with us.

In the field of secondary schools and tertiary education and research, the Office for Higher Education implements the educational policy mandates of the government and the Department of Education, Culture and Environmental Protection in favour of an attractive educational and research location Graubünden.

The Department of secondary schools is responsible for the implementation of the legal provisions applicable to secondary schools. In the canton of Graubünden, the approximately 2,300 secondary schoolchildren are divided between the Bundner Kantonsschule in Chur and the eight private secondary schools in the regions. Depending on the location, different courses are run – Gymnasium, Handelsmittelschule (HMS), Fachmittelschule (FMS) and Informatikmittelschule (IMS).

In the department of secondary schools of the Office of Higher Education we search per 1\. March 2027 or, by arrangement, an administrator/\-in administration.
