Service technician: in construction machinery for the Graubünden region

Join the market leader!

Avesco AG is the market leader in the field of construction machinery and energy systems and has been family-owned in Switzerland for more than 150 years. Avesco AG employs 500 people. Demanding customers from different industries benefit from a comprehensive selection of high-quality products and solutions.

Screw, tinker, keep construction machinery running sounds like yours? Then you are exactly right with us!

In close cooperation with a dispatcher, you make sure that our machines do not fizzle out at the customers on site. From high-tech diagnostics to the last screw – you are the professional who makes it possible.

(80% \- 100%)

Your mission

  • You bring construction machines back to best form \ - directly at the customer on site.
  • With state-of-the-art diagnostic technology, you will find errors and provide a quick solution.
  • You make sure that our machines remain operational reliably.
  • You advise our customers, give technical tips and ensure satisfied faces.

Your background

  • Training as construction machinery, truck or agricultural machinery mechanic:
  • Enthusiasm for large machines and high-tech systems
  • Enjoy carrying out repairs and maintenance independently
  • A reliable, solution-oriented way of working
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Driving licence Cat. B/BE

Our offer

  • Thorough training so you can get started quickly
  • An exciting self-responsible task with a lot of variety
  • A cool team that sticks together and supports each other
  • Flat hierarchies, open communication and a leadership at eye level
  • Modern work equipment and a dynamic working environment
  • Attractive employment conditions with top social benefits: 13 monthly wages, 40 hours per week, at least 25 days of holidays as well as development and training opportunities
  • Cool employee benefits
