Full description

Live and work in the Destination Davos Klosters? We look forward to reinforcement! ##STR1##

We offer sub-assistants a varied education and competent care by the responsible doctors. We attach great importance to a motivating, trusting approach and a good teaching and learning culture.

As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable company, but important for the region, we maintain a collegial working atmosphere and appreciate the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos.

From April 2029 we will assign a position as

Sub-assistant:in Internal Medicine 100% ##

Your responsibilities

You will get the following insights

  • Care of inpatient internist patients under supervision
  • Assistance in patient admission and dismissal
  • Learning and practicing of specific clinical investigation techniques as well as technical investigations
  • In the emergency department: medical emergency rooms, care of outpatients/medical patients, participation in small-scale surgery (wound care, repositions, plasters)
  • Sub-assistant: insides are integrated into the on-call service

Your profile

  • Sub-assistants:women in the elective year (CH) or in the internship year/PJ (D)
  • very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • independent, reliable working method
  • resilient, flexible, responsible and knowledge-hungry
  • Working in a team and interested in learning more about how a regional hospital operates

Our offer

  • Learning of clinical findings (anamnesis, status) in inpatient and outpatient patients, holistic assessment of a patient with preparation of a clarification and therapy plan
  • Conducting diagnostic tests, learning about educational interviews, participating in specialist examinations (ultrasound, echocardiography, endoscopy)
  • Learning differential diagnostic approach and developing problem-oriented thinking skills

For further information, the Secretariat for Internal Medicine is at your disposal by telephone or e-mail.
