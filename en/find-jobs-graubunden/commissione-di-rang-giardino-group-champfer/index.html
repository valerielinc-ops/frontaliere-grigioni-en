Full description

Giardino Group is looking for a Commis de Rang for the Giardino Mountain in Champfèr. Giardino Hotels is a characterful Swiss luxury hotel group with locations in St. Moritz, Ascona and Locarno.

Tasks

In this function, you are responsible for the smooth service in our à la Carte Restaurant. You work independently from ordering to debt collection and look after and advise our guests at the highest level. Customer-oriented treatment of complaints is also part of your area of responsibility.

Requirements

  • Completed training as a restaurant specialist
  • Experience in a comparable position in upscale gastronomy
  • A well-groomed appearance and polite manners
  • Independence, reliability and sense of duty
  • Resilience, flexibility and enthusiasm
  • Dealing respectfully with colleagues and superiors
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Knowledge of Italian and/or French is an advantage

Benefits

  • An innovative and well-organized workplace
  • The opportunity to actively participate in the opening, everyday business and corporate development
  • Open and transparent communication
  • Regular training and further education as well as numerous employee benefits
  • Being part of a contemporary and characterful Swiss hotel group
