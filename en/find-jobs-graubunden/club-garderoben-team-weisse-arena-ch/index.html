The White Arena Group in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and an enthusiastic community for unique mountain adventures. In the middle of LAAX, close to the halfpipe and the snowpark LAAX, riders, creatives, music enthusiasts, families and friends meet here. The Riders Lobby is living room from early morning until late in the evening and offers a curated menu right at the bar. The restaurant eats together, the club celebrates until the night. Reduced to the essentials: snow, music, community. The freestyle center of LAAX. | At the beginning of the night, the jackets come in almost at the same time – and in the end, all their things want to come back at the same time. You ensure that our wardrobe remains organized, everything has its place and our guests are served quickly and friendly. In between, you keep an eye on your area and support the club team where an extra hand is needed. What You Move:

  • You take jackets and personal belongings of our guests and ensure a fast and organized edition.
  • You manage the cash register and make sure that payments and wardrobe tickets are handled correctly.
  • You keep the wardrobe clean, clear and well organized throughout the club night.
  • You handle the personal belongings of our guests carefully and responsibly.
  • In quieter phases, you can provide flexible support in other club areas and take over breaks if necessary. What makes you:
  • You are friendly, attentive and enjoy contact with our guests.
  • You work quickly and very organized and keep the overview even if many guests come at the same time.
  • You are reliable and responsible and handle money and personal belongings carefully.
  • You are flexible, like to get involved and have no problem supporting outside your direct area.
  • You are a team player and have a desire for club culture, music and the special atmosphere of a Riders club night. | An attractive workplace in the middle of an extraordinary mountain world is certain for you. You become part of a dynamic, bright team and can help shape the further development of LAAX. With us you will find an environment in which professionalism, speed, pragmatism and humanity go together. Look forward to numerous employee benefits, such as free mountain rides, perks in our shops, hotels and restaurants, and benefits for your family.
