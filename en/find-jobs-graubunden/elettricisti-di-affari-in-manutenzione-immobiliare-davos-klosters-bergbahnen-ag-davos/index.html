The Davos Klosters Mountains are five mountain and ski resorts in the Bundner Alpen. In addition to the mountain railways, Davos Klosters Bergbahnen AG operates more than 20 accommodations under the brand Mountain Hotels and Resorts, from the simple group house to the upscale four-star superior hotel. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for a person for the real estate maintenance department as: company electrician: in 100% Your tasks

  • Implementation of new electrical installations and refurbishments
  • Maintenance and operation of our building infrastructure, including catering and hotels
  • Remediation of building and building installations
  • Support of the real estate maintenance team in renovation projects
  • Takeover of Pikettdienst by week
  • Monitoring and ensuring compliance with legal regulations and safety standards Your profile
  • Training: Completed training in electrical engineering or comparable qualification and approval of company electricians
  • Working method: You work reliably and independently, are motivated and have a high level of service and security awareness
  • Personality: You are open-minded, friendly, attentive and enjoy learning new things
  • Language: You have good oral and written knowledge of German We offer
  • Interesting, responsible and varied workplace in the mountains
  • Season ticket and some day tickets of the Davos Klosters mountain railways
  • Provision of staff apartments or rooms (if needed)
  • Participation in employee events
  • Special conditions for fitness subscriptions and overnight stays in the Mountain Hotels If you are interested, we look forward to receiving the application documents.
