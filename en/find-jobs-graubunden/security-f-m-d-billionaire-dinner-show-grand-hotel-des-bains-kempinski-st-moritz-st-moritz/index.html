Full description

The Grand Hotel des Bains Kempinski is hosting the remarkable Billionaire Dinner Show. At the Billionaire St. Moritz, we offer you an unrivalled combination of fine dining and captivating performances in a luxurious setting. Whether our guests are enjoying a meal or watching a show, every moment is carefully curated and promises an unrivalled experience. The event is selecting exceptional new international performing talents and refining the culinary offering so that we can welcome our guests back from the start of December. Our guests can look forward to being whisked away once again into a world of opulence, indulgence and breathtaking entertainment. We are looking for seasonal security staff for the Billionaire Dinner Show for the 2026/2027 winter season. | Key Responsibilities:

  • Ensuring the safety of our international VVIP guests, performers and staff with the utmost discretion.
  • Monitoring entrances and VIP areas during the dinner show and subsequent events.
  • Carrying out regular patrols in the event area and securing escape and rescue routes.
  • Acting with confidence and courtesy in conflict situations whilst adhering to internal service standards.
  • Acting as the first point of contact in the event of medical emergencies or security-related incidents, working closely with the hotel management and local authorities.
  • Providing support to other departments, such as valet parking, where required. | Skills, Knowledge and Expertise:
  • Proven professional experience in security within the luxury hotel industry, at high-end events or in personal protection.
  • An impeccable reputation (a current criminal record check is required) and training in first aid/BLS-AED would be an advantage.
  • Fluency in German and English; knowledge of other languages (particularly Italian, French or Russian) is a strong advantage.
  • Excellent manners, a well-groomed appearance and a strong service-oriented attitude.
  • Willingness to work nights and weekends during the winter season. | Benefits:
  • Living and working in the heart of the Engadin Alps
  • A wide range of opportunities to shape your role
  • A pleasant working atmosphere within a young and dynamic team
  • International career prospects and opportunities for development
  • Outstanding staff benefits, including access to all Kempinski hotels
  • Staff accommodation in the immediate vicinity of the hotel, including laundry facilities, a fitness room, communal lounge and kitchen, and a varied selection of meals in our staff restaurant
  • Free use of the in-house crèche for staff children
  • Monthly staff activities such as skiing, snowboarding and cross-country skiing lessons, hiking trips, spa evenings and German language courses
  • In-house ‘Feel Good Committee’ to help shape all welfare-related matters
