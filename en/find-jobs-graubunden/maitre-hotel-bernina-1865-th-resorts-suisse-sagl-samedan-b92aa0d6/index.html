Full description

Do you have experience in hospitality and want to play in a dynamic and professional environment?

TH Resorts, a highly expanding Italian hotel chain with over 30 hotels, villages and resorts in marine, mountain and art cities, is looking for a Maitre Hotel Bernina 1865 in Samedan.

What are you gonna do?

Like Maitre, you will be the reference point of the restaurant room and you will take care of:

  • Coordinate work and manage staff shifts.
  • Supervision the room before, during and after service, ensuring operational excellence.
  • Collaboration with the kitchen and reception to ensure an impeccable experience to guests.
  • Set decorations and buffet with attention to detail.
  • Present the menu and wine card, recommending guests in the choice.
  • Care for your team's training and professional development.
  • Promote and sell extra services offered by the restaurant.
  • Requirements

Who are we looking for?

A professional with

  • Excellent organizational, management and leadership skills.
  • Ability to make decisions and solve problems effectively.
  • Attention to detail and a strong orientation to service.
  • Good knowledge in gastronomy, enology and techniques of Mise en Place.
  • Familiarity with the various types of service (buffet, veneered, etc.).
  • At least 3 years of experience in the catering industry.
