About us

EMS has 25 production sites in 16 countries, employs around 3,000 people worldwide and, with around 140 apprentices, is the largest vocational trainer in the canton and winner of the National Education Award.

EMS-GRILTECH is a division of EMS-CHEMIE AG, which is part of EMS-CHEMIE HOLDING AG. We produce and sell Grilon, Nexylon and Nexylene fibers, Griltex hotmelt adhesives, Grilbond adhesion promoters, Primid powder coating hardeners and Grilonit reactive diluents.
