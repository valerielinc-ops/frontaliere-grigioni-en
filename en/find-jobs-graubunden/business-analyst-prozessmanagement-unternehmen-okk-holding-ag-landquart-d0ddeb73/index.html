Business Analyst Process Management Company

This is waiting for you:

  • Responsible and versatile at the interface between departments and IT, in which you actively participate in the further development of our processes and applications in the company business unit
  • You will analyze technical questions and requirements and use them to develop viable solutions for processes, data and applications.
  • You identify optimization potential in existing processes, data and applications
  • You evaluate and assess solutions with regard to functionality, quality and cost-effectiveness
  • You work in projects for the further development of processes and systems in the corporate customer sector
  • You create and maintain professional test cases and perform functional, regression and end-to-end tests.
  • You take over the 1st level application support for supervised business applications

This is what you bring:

  • Commercial basic training with several years of professional experience in the analysis of technical issues, processes and operational problems as well as pleasure in developing sustainable solutions
  • Further education in Business Analysis or Business Informatics
  • Sound knowledge in the structured collection, analysis and documentation of professional requirements
  • Experience in the preparation of professional test cases and in the professional testing of business applications, ideally in the insurance environment
  • Teamwork, responsibility and readiness
  • Strong analytical skills and a structured, conceptual and solution-oriented working method
  • High communication strength and the ability to mediate between department and IT
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
