Full description

As a specialist building office of the Canton of Graubünden, the Building Construction Office represents the interests of the canton as property owner and/or building owner and is responsible for all services of real estate management. This includes the structuring of the real estate portfolio, the planning, realization and management of real estate as well as consulting.

We are looking for an object manager in Facility Services regions immediately or by agreement.

As an object manager/in facility services, you can expect an open working environment in Chur as well as in the regions, attractive opportunities for further education and possible freedom for family and leisure activities.

Tasks

  • Ensuring high availability of cantonal infrastructure and buildings as well as efficient and smooth provision of required services
  • Responsibility for coordination, management and personnel planning as well as for billing and control of property maintenance work and services in the assigned portfolio
  • Planning and control of special tasks over the assigned objects
  • Support of the object managers in the technically correct guidance and execution of the cleaning and care work of the building substances as well as outdoor facilities
  • Support of the property managers in the coordination of operations of external service providers (e.g. craftsmen, cleaning companies, etc.)
  • Active participation in the provision of services and in the continuous improvement of existing processes

Requirements

  • Manual or technical training completed
  • Head of Facility Management and Maintenance (HFP) or willingness to obtain this training
  • High sense of responsibility and quality
  • Readiness for action in technical interventions
  • Networked thinking as well as independent and solution-oriented working methods
  • Good computer user knowledge
