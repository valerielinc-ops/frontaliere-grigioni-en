Full description

As a family-run, high-tech company with almost 19,000 employees at 71 locations worldwide, we are looking for forward thinkers with unconventional ideas and drive to join our team. Our company culture, which values collaboration and mutual trust, creates the ideal framework for boldly trying new things and questioning the status quo. Our technologies inspire people to develop and produce things that are currently unimaginable. Whether lasers, machine tools, EUV or electronics - TRUMPF is building technological worlds for future generations. Are you ready for new challenges?

Their tasks

  • Interface function between the purchase of production materials and the interdisciplinary development teams of the respective business unit
  • Representation of the purchasing view in development projects including critical questioning of technical specifications
  • Ensuring systematic and early involvement of purchasing in all phases of the product development process
  • Consideration of all purchasing concerns in the projects, especially in the selection of suitable suppliers acc. of the respective product group strategies
  • Ensuring product cost reduction and target achievement over the entire product life cycle incl. Identification of measures and their implementation or Tracking
  • Cross-location coordination of supply chain aspects in the start-up and discontinuation control of products
  • Preparation and implementation of make-or-buy decisions as well as tendering and awarding of new components acc. of the respective product group strategies
  • Analysis and coordination of optimization of supply chain resilience
  • Data analysis and reporting, use of analytical tools and production of reports for decision-making and performance monitoring
  • Maintenance of master data and coordination of initial samples for new components
  • Support of supplier qualification at new suppliers

What we are looking for

  • Successfully completed study of engineering or industrial engineering
  • At least 7 years of professional experience in international, technical product purchase or in the development sector in the machinery/plant construction, medical or automotive environment
  • Experience in project management and working in matrix structures
  • knowledge of different production methods, preferably in the product groups electrical engineering, electronics, drive technology, machining and surface coating, as well as mechanics
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Conceptual thinking
  • Negotiation skills, ability to conduct contract negotiations and achieve optimal conditions with suppliers
  • Very good communication ability, firm and secure appearance, reliability and enforcement ability
  • Independent and structured working methods, a high degree of initiative as well as teamwork skills are required
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Willingness to travel (about 10%)
  • Very good MS Office as well as SAP (R/3 or S4 HANA) knowledge

What we offer

  • Highly modern working environment with the latest technology
  • Early assumption of responsibility and varied tasks
  • Extensive training and further education offer
  • Working Where Others Vacation
  • Respectful and appreciative corporate culture
