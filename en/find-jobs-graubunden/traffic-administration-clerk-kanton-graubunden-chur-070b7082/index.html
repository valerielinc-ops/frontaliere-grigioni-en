Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the Cantonal Administration Graubünden together with us.

Do you like to work independently, keep an overview even with a high volume of work and appreciate a versatile area of responsibility? Then you are exactly right with us! In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed personality as a case handler/\-in traffic administration.

Your responsibilities

  • They independently process and monitor the administrative processes of speeding.
  • They control incoming payments, create invoices, report speed overruns and forward cases to the competent authorities.
  • They organize legal assistance applications for home and abroad.
  • You take on a wide range of correspondence and secretarial work in different languages and ensure a smooth administrative process.
  • They are in dialogue with internal and external partners such as police departments, authorities and the public prosecutor’s office.
  • You contribute your ideas and support us in continuously improving existing processes.

That is what we want from you.

  • They have completed commercial training and have several years of practical experience in this field of work.
  • They are well-versed in dealing with common IT applications and interested in acquiring extensive knowledge of internal IT programs.
  • They communicate stylishly in German and have very good Italian skills in word and writing. Other language skills are a plus.
  • They work independently, carefully and in a structured manner and keep an overview even with a high volume of work.
  • You are a reliable, resilient and service-oriented personality with pleasure in administrative tasks.
  • You convince through your open, friendly, teamwork and sense of responsibility.
