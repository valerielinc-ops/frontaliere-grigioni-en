Full description

Your key responsibilities

As a valued member of our Purchasing Team, you will contribute to the smooth running of our operations by taking responsibility for the following tasks:

  • Inspect all incoming deliveries against delivery notes and purchase orders, ensuring compliance with our high quality standards.

  • Ensure the proper storage of delivered goods whenever required.

  • Verify that all ordered goods have been received in full and liaise with suppliers in the event of any discrepancies.

  • Accurately process and record delivery notes in a timely manner.

  • Manage the distribution of Economat orders to the various hotel departments.

  • Ensure the accurate and systematic recording of internal stock transfers related to Economat supplies.

  • Monitor Economat stock levels, independently manage replenishment orders and ensure the continuous availability of supplies.

  • Oversee the organisation and maintenance of the goods receiving area, including the garage, storage rooms, empty containers, packaging materials, cleanliness and equipment management.

• What you bring to the job

  • You have completed professional training in Culinary Arts or Food & Beverage Service.

  • You have previous experience in an administrative role and demonstrate a structured, accurate and detail-oriented approach to your work.

  • You are proficient in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel.

  • Ideally, you have experience working with an inventory management system; knowledge of Kost is considered an advantage.

  • You have an excellent command of English, both written and spoken. Knowledge of Italian or German is an additional asset.

  • You are highly motivated, resilient and quick to learn, with the ability to work independently and adopt a solution-oriented approach.

  • You demonstrate a professional appearance, excellent interpersonal skills and a friendly, courteous manner.

Our offer

  • Fixed-term seasonal position from the end of November 2026 until the beginning of April 2027, with the possibility of returning for future seasons

  • An average 5-day working week of 43.5 hours

  • 5 weeks of holiday and 6 public holidays per year

  • 13th-month salary from the start of employment (pro rata temporis)

  • Staff accommodation in one of our employee residences, subject to availability

  • Meals available in our modern staff restaurant

  • An attractive and international working environment with opportunities for professional development


If you are looking for an interesting, challenging, and varied role in a dynamic environment and would like to join a well-established team, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
