Live and work in the Destination Davos Klosters? We look forward to reinforcement! ##STR1##

As a specialist child care specialist, you provide with your empathy and commitment for the well-being of the most valuable treasures of our employees and other Davos parents.

As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable company, but important for the region, we maintain a collegial working atmosphere and appreciate the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos.

By agreement we are looking for a

Specialist Care Specialty Children 80-100% ##

Your responsibilities

  • Support for the leadership of the children’s group
  • Implementation of the educational goals of the crèche
  • Further diverse tasks in daily crib operation

Your profile

  • Completed training as a specialist childcare specialist or an equivalent education recognised in Switzerland, specialists fresh from education very welcome!
  • They treat the children with joy, empathy and commitment.
  • You are a creative, enthusiastic and resilient personality
  • They maintain a respectful, responsible and collegial approach
  • They are flexible and capable of teamwork

Our offer

  • Responsible task in an age-mixed, happy team
  • Attractive employment conditions and good social benefits
  • Staff house available
  • Modern and yet homely infrastructure in a beautiful and quiet location
  • Pleasant working environment with good support
  • Plenty of sports and leisure activities in the highest city in Europe
