Full description

Land register administrator deputy and clerk With 41,000 inhabitants, Chur is the urban center of Graubünden. As an attractive and versatile city, it provides customer-oriented and modern services with its 1,400 employees.

The Chur Land Registry, which is responsible for the management of the land registers of the city of Chur and the municipality of Arosa, is looking for a

Land Registry Deputy and Person handling/in Land Register Their tasks Depending on your employment profile, you will fulfill interesting and varied tasks in the following areas of activity:

  • Land register management
  • Advice and support of customers
  • Issuing of land register extracts and other certificates
  • Writing and auditing of contracts. Land and land register applications
  • Public registration of land register transactions and authentications
  • Introduction of the Swiss Land Registry

Your profile

  • Commercial education, preferably in the field of land register or notary
  • For employment as Land Registry Manager Stv.: Certificate of Competence for Land Registration (cantonal patent) or Master’s degree in Law (advantage with professional experience in Land Registry/Notarial System)
  • Rapid comprehension
  • Precise and reliable operation
  • Discretion, assertiveness and cultivated manners

Our offer We offer you independent, versatile and responsible work in a small and motivated team. A workplace with modern infrastructure and contemporary employment conditions awaits you in Chur and/or Arosa (external office).

If you have a master’s degree without corresponding professional experience, you can of course purchase it from us. Their questions Further information can be provided by Curdin Cavegn, Land Registry Manager, Tel. +41 81 254 47 97 or curdin.cavegn@chur.ch
