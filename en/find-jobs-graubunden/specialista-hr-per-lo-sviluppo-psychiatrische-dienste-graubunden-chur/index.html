Full description

Your surroundings

She expects an appreciative and collegial working environment in a small, committed team with plenty of room for manoeuvre. HR development offers a versatile field of activity in which you can contribute your own ideas, question existing ideas and actively shape the further development of the PDGR.

Their tasks

  • They play a decisive role in the planning, coordination and further development of the internal training programme
  • They organize workshops, seminars and internal events and actively contribute to their content design and moderation.
  • You take on deputy tasks in the areas of e-learning as well as in the preparation of training, further education and training agreements
  • They accompany and implement projects in the field of HR development

Our requirement profile

  • You have several years of professional experience in HR, preferably with a focus on HR development
  • You have ideally completed a higher business education with a deeper HR or an equivalent qualification
  • They like to work conceptually, are strong and like to set new accents. At the same time, you enjoy administrative and organizational tasks
  • You have experience in the conception and implementation of personnel development measures and projects
  • They work independently, structured and reliable, like to take on responsibility and keep an overview even in hectic times

We offer you

  • A versatile and responsible activity with creative scope in an exciting working environment
  • A meaningful work, because the people you accompany make an important contribution to the mental health of the German population
  • Close cooperation in a dedicated HR team and with various stakeholders within the PDGR
  • Very attractive training opportunities, both internal and external
  • Flexible working hours, above-average social benefits, holiday credits of at least 30 days and the possibility of unpaid leave
