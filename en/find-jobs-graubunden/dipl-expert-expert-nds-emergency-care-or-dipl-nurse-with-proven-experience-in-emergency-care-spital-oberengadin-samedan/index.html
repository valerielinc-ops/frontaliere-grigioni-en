Full description

The Spital Oberengadin AG in Samedan, a company of the KSGR Group, is responsible for basic medical care and emergency care for the population and guests with the largest Spital Südbünden. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a colleague immediately or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • Triagulation of patients according to the Manchester Triage System
  • Ensuring safe, professionally competent initial care for patients of all age groups – from infants to the elderly – of all severity levels as well as different socio-cultural backgrounds with surgical, internistic and psychosocial emergency indications
  • Point-of-care testing in late and night service
  • Application of various gypsum applications
  • Care during and after small outpatient operations
  • Follow-up checks

Your profile

  • degree as an expert NDS Emergency Care or equivalent diploma with Swiss recognition or
  • Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH with demonstrable professional experience in interdisciplinary emergency care or with an interest in becoming familiar with this area
  • resilient and responsible personality with high quality awareness
  • Accurate, independent and organised working methods
  • Teamwork and flexibility (optimal willingness to provide services depending on seasonal load)
  • Appreciative attitude towards patients, relatives and employees
  • German in word and writing, Italian and English, other languages beneficial

Pensum: 80-100 %
