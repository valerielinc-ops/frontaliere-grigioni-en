Full description

Tofu is more than just a trend for you? The Hilcona Taste Factory is the competence center for vegan and vegetarian production with a future. In our modern Tofurei, you and your team produce high-quality tofu products and ensure that quality, hygiene and a smooth production process are guaranteed at all times. You like to take on professional responsibility and are ready for shift operations? We look forward to your application.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Working time model: 2-shift operation
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: German
  • Location: Hilcona AG, Riedlöserstrasse 7 7302 Landquart

Tasks

  • Operation and monitoring of our production facilities in the area
  • Ensuring a smooth production process in compliance with quality, hygiene and safety requirements
  • Recognize and correct simple faults as well as support for optimization of production processes
  • Implementation of cleaning and control work according to specifications
  • Collaboration in the training of new employees and active cooperation in the production team
  • Careful documentation of production data in the ERP system

Requirements

  • Completed training in the food industry, dairy technology or a similar specialty beneficial
  • Experience in the food industry, dairy industry or cheese factory
  • Experience in dealing with ERP systems is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing (at least level B2–C1)
  • Readiness for shift work
  • Teamwork as well as a reliable and independent working method
