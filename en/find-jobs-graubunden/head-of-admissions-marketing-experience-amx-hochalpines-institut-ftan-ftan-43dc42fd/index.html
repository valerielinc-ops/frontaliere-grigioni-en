The High Alpine Institute Ftan (HIF) is a traditional Swiss boarding school at 1,700 m above sea level with tailor-made educational paths (bilingual Swiss Matura, international IGCSE\-\&A\-Level\-programme), the renowned HIF Sports Academy and seasonal camps. We belong to Education in Motion (EiM) – a global educational family with over 12,000 people worldwide.

For this key position, we are looking for a strategically and operationally strong leader who positions HIF worldwide, wins and shapes the entire experience – with regular presence in Ftan, Engadin (Switzerland) and occasional international travel.

Head of Admissions, Marketing & Experience - AMX (80% to 100%)

Your mission – you move that with us

  • Global Enrollment: Strategic management of the entire admission process for Matura and international education programs – with clear, measurable enrollment and conversion goals.
  • Market Identification & Development: Identify and develop new source markets worldwide – in addition to the DACH region, Europe and Asia, also active in the Americas (North and South America) and the successor states of the former Soviet Union / Eastern Europe & Central Asia.
  • Agent network: Build, bring and/or support a viable international agent and advisor network (Educational Consultants).
  • Marketing\-Mix analog \& digital: Design and control integrated campaigns – educational fairs, events, print, roadshows as well as website, SEO/SEA, social media, content, PR and email marketing.
  • AI in the marketing of the digital economy: using artificial intelligence and modern digital tools (content creation, personalization, automation, predictive analyses) effectively and future-oriented.
  • Family counselling: personally, empathetically and competently accompany parents and children through the entire decision-making process (admission counselling, campus visits, trial days).
  • Experience\-Quality: Responsible for the quality of all touch points from first contact to open days to onboarding
  • Close integration with the HIF\ camps and the Sports Academy.
  • Team leadership \& CRM: Professional and disciplinary leadership of the AMX\ team
  • Ownership for the CRM (HubSpot), pipeline control and regular enrollment\ forecasting with the school management and eggs.

This is what you bring – your profile *

  • Multi-year, proven track record in marketing and admissions (ideally in the international school or premium education environment); relevant university degree.
  • Sovereign mastery of the entire marketing mix (analog \& digital) and deep understanding of the digital economy.
  • Practical familiarity with the use of AI in marketing and openness to new technologies.
  • Joy and sensitivity in the personal advice of parents and children. ? Proven strength in market identification; ideally an existing international agent network that you bring in – or the talent to build it quickly and sustainably.
  • International orientation
  • Experience/contacts in the Americas and/or the successor states of the former Soviet Union are a big plus.
  • leadership experience, team player mentality and structured working methods; adept use of CRM systems (HubSpot) and digital marketing tools.
  • Negotiation confident in German (incl. Swiss German\-understanding) and English (C1/C2\); Russian or Spanish are a strong advantage.
  • Readiness for occasional international and national travel as well as regular presence on our campus in Ftan

What we offer

  • Strategic key role with great creative leeway and visible impact in a global group (EiM: 12,000\+:innen).
  • Unique, family campus in Engadin – healthy alpine lifestyle, nature and sport right on the doorstep.
  • Flexible work design (presence in Ftan, remote options, travel phases).
  • School places for your own children (Matura or IGCSE/A\-level); Discounts on public transport and ski / mountain railway subscriptions, use of sports facilities

Applications of third-country nationals (not EU / EFTA)

Safeguarding \& Professionalism *

HIF is actively committed to the protection and welfare of children and adolescents. All employees are obliged to maintain clear professional boundaries and high standards of conduct. Employment is subject to thorough reference checks, background checks and further clarifications in accordance with international child protection guidelines.
