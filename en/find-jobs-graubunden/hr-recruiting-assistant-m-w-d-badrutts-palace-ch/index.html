Technical & Logistics Assistant (m/f/d) — Badrutt's Palace Hotel

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Your key responsibilities

  • You will play an active role in supporting the daily technical and logistical operations.

  • You will assist with the setup, maintenance, and transportation of equipment and materials, ensuring that everything is prepared for smooth daily operations.

  • A key part of your role will be transporting materials and supplies between the Badrutt's Palace Hotel and the Paradiso Mountain Club.

  • Your primary workplace will be the Paradiso Mountain Club, where you will carry out most of your day-to-day responsibilities.

  • You will ensure that vehicles, tools, and technical equipment are maintained and ready for use.

  • You will coordinate logistical tasks and support operational teams whenever needed.

  • You will follow all health and safety regulations and company procedures.

  • From time to time, you will also take on additional operational tasks assigned by your supervisor.

  • Whenever needed, you will provide operational support during the daily lunch service.


• What you bring to the job

For this diverse and dynamic position, you ideally bring:

  • High level of commitment, resilience, and flexibility.

  • Valid Category D1 driving licence (mandatory).

  • Ability to work independently as well as part of a team.

  • Previous experience in technical support, logistics, or a similar operational role is an advantage.

  • Affinity for mountain environments and snow.

  • High sense of responsibility.

  • Language skills: very good command of either Italian or German, with a good understanding of English


Our offer

  • Fixed-term seasonal contract starting in mid-November 2026, running until early April 2027, with the opportunity to return for future seasons.

  • 5-day working week, averaging 43.5 hours.

  • 5 weeks of holiday and 6 public holidays per year (pro rata).

  • 13th salary from the start date (pro rata temporis).

  • Accommodation available upon request in one of our attractive staff residences.

  • The opportunity to work in a prestigious environment with excellent development prospects.

If you are looking for an interesting, challenging, and varied role in a dynamic environment and would like to join a well-established team, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.


Recruitment Fraud Warning

All official communication is sent exclusively from email addresses ending in @badruttspalace.com. If you receive any request for payment or suspicious communication, please disregard it and contact us directly through our official website.
