Three locations. One team. Your future.

The PLAST Group unites Stüdli Plast AG in Romanshorn, Grischa Plast AG in Maienfeld and Küng Plast AG in Uznach. With around 76 employees and 64 machines, we combine many years of experience with modern plastic technology and innovative manufacturing solutions.

From large components and assembly to precision injection molding, toolmaking and 3K technology, to medical technology, micro injection molding and measurement technology, our three locations offer a versatile working environment.

As a certified group of companies, we stand for quality, innovation and continuous development. At the same time, we focus on modern workplaces, flexible working time models, short decision-making processes and a corporate culture in which our employees are valued as people and specialists.

Discover your possibilities at the PLAST Group – and shape the future with us.

Plastics technologist in tool maintenance (m/f/d)

Your tasks

  • Ensuring the perfect condition of our injection molding tools as well as their maintenance and maintenance
  • Assistance in production
  • Implementation of preventive maintenance and maintenance work on injection molding tools
  • Dismantling, cleaning, testing and assembly of injection moulding tools

Detection and correction of malfunctions and wear phenomena on tools

  • Support for tool repairs and optimization of existing tools
  • Assessment of tool damage and initiation of appropriate measures to restore functionality
  • Ensuring tool availability for a smooth production process
  • Documentation of the maintenance, repair and maintenance carried out
  • Close cooperation with production, setup and toolmaking
  • Participation in the continuous improvement of our tools and maintenance processes

Your profile

  • Completed training as a plastics technologist EFZ, polymechanic EFZ or in a comparable technical profession
  • Professional experience in plastic injection molding and ideally in tool maintenance
  • Good knowledge of the design and operation of injection molding tools
  • Experience in disassembly, cleaning, maintenance and assembly of tools of advantage
  • Technical understanding and strong craftsmanship
  • Independent, careful and structured working methods
  • Solution-oriented thinking and the ability to identify and fix technical problems
  • High quality and responsibility awareness
  • Teamwork, reliability and readiness
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

  • Flexible working time models and contemporary workplaces
  • Exciting and varied activity with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Flat hierarchies and short decision paths
  • Education and training opportunities
  • Attractive social benefits
  • Possibility to actively take responsibility and shape toolmaking
