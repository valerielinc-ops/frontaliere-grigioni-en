Deputy Team Leader Sales (m/f/d)

Since 1932 stands for high-quality watches, jewellery and personal advice in the old town of Chur. We are looking for a committed personality who actively sells and takes responsibility for a well-organized everyday sales routine.

Their tasks

  • They advise our customers personally and competently and maintain long-term customer relationships.
  • They support the sales management and, in their absence, take over the operational management of day-to-day business.
  • They conduct daily briefings, coordinate tasks and priorities and support employees in demanding customer meetings.
  • They keep an eye on open customer concerns and agreed feedback and ensure reliable processing by the responsible persons.
  • They coordinate customer appointments, pick-ups and handovers between sales, administration and workshops.
  • They pay attention to the quality of the CRM entries and the implementation of our consulting and service standards.
  • They work together with various internal departments and support customer events.

Your profile

  • Experience in high-quality sales, ideally with watches or jewelry.
  • Organisational capacity, initiative and careful working methods.
  • Enjoying personal customer relationships and a natural host mentality.
  • A committed attitude and the ability to guide and support employees in everyday life.
  • Initial leadership experience or the willingness to gradually assume responsibility.
  • Interest in watches, jewellery and crafts as well as safety in dealing with digital sales instruments.

We offer

  • A varied task with active sales, coordination and operational team leadership.
  • Clear responsibilities and direct cooperation with sales management.
  • An owner-managed company with short decision paths and space for your own ideas.
  • Renowned brands, its own goldsmith’s studio and a watchmaking workshop.
  • Opportunities to further develop your expertise and leadership skills.

We look forward to your application with CV and relevant certificates about.
