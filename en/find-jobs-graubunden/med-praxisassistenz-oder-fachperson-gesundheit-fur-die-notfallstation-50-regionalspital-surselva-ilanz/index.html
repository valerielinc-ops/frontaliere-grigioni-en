Full description

Med. Practice assistant or health specialist for the emergency department 50% immediately or by agreement Regionalspital Surselva AG provides the extended basic and emergency care for approximately 22,000 inhabitants and seasonally around 20,000 holiday guests in the tourism region of Bündner Oberland. Our approximately 320 employees treat with modern infrastructure (CT, MRI, IMC, etc.) about 2,600 inpatient and 16,000 outpatient patients annually in the clinics of surgery and orthopaedics/traumatology with neurosurgery and urology, internal medicine with cardiology, oncology, gastroenterology pneumology and rheumatology, gynecology and obstetrics, pediatrics and anesthesia. Regionalspital Surselva AG is awarded the “Swiss Olympic Medical Base approved by Swiss Olympic” label. Through the cooperation with the Center Hospital in Chur, an extended range of services can be offered.
