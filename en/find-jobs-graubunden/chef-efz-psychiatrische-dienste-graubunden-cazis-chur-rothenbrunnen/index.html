Full description

At the clinic Beverin in Cazis, we train learner cooks. Every day we prepare 250 meals for our patients, employees, guests and visitors. On occasions such as banquets and aperitifs, we pamper our guests with delicacies from the kitchen.

You will learn how to process and prepare fresh, seasonal foods. You take food, carefully prepare it for warm and cold dishes and sweets, and creatively direct it. Various cooking methods and techniques, creative preparation, the interaction between service and kitchen as well as the calculations of dishes, hygiene and environmental protection are part of your training.

Our basic attitude in your education

  • We strive for a learning climate characterized by mutual appreciation, tolerance and openness.
  • We offer you space to take on your role as a learner cook and learner cook.
  • We provide you with various forms of learning that promote individual, holistic and self-directed learning.
  • We have clear rules and we define the scope for action together.
  • You learn through practical work and reflection, in which we use leadership and training tools systematically.
  • We see learning in practice as a continuous and constructive process.
  • Through differentiated, constructive and well-founded feedback, we achieve learning success and promote your transfer thinking – from theory to practice.

Key points of your education

Duration and education Your apprenticeship lasts 3 years and takes place over three learning fields:

  • School education in compulsory education takes place on one school day per week at the Commercial Vocational School (GBC) in Chur.
  • The intercompany courses (ÜK) support your learning success by imparting basic and industry-specific competences.
  • The professional learning fields are made possible in the kitchen of the clinic Beverin in Cazis. During your apprenticeship, you will complete an external internship lasting several weeks in a hotel company.

Professional maturity We support you if you strive for the Berufsmaturität (BM) during your apprenticeship. Tell us your interest with the application.

Holidays During basic vocational training, you have five weeks of vacation per year.

Accommodation At the Cazis location, we offer you low-cost accommodation if a daily journey between home and work is associated with a large amount of time.

Our Requirements for You

  • You enjoy handling food and cooking
  • You have a good comprehension, are creative and willing to experiment
  • You have a good sense of taste and smell
  • You are capable of teamwork and act customer-oriented
  • You have hygiene awareness
  • You are resilient and responsible
  • You show willingness to work irregular hours

Perspectives According to Your Teaching

After completing your apprenticeship, you can work as a cook in the profession or aspire to one of the shortened additional apprentices as a dietitian cook, as a restoration specialist, as a hotel specialist, as a bakery confectioner or as a bakery confectioner. With the vocational examination to the head cook or the head chef or the gastro manager or the gastro manager is another way open.

With the higher specialist examination you will obtain a federal diploma to chef or chef or head of community gastronomy or head of community gastronomy.

The Higher School of Applied Sciences offers you the opportunity for further education as a qualified Hôtelierière-Restaurateurtrice HF or a qualified Hôtelier-Restaurateur HF.

At the University of Applied Sciences you can take the Bachelor of Science (FH) in Food Technology or in International Hospitality Management.

Selection procedure

After examining your application, you will be informed about the selection decision. If you are shortlisted, the training manager agrees on an interview and selection days. Through selection days you get an insight into everyday work and we get to know each other better. The selection days are organized by us and take place in the clinic Beverin in Cazis.

The employment decision is made by at least two people, usually by the training manager and the chef.

Your benefits at a glance

Training for the Swiss Federal Vocational Certificate (EBA) is possible depending on the prerequisites and in certain professions after consultation with the field of education.

We support you if you strive for the Berufsmaturität (BM) during your apprenticeship. Tell us your interest with the application.

As a competitive athlete, we provide you with special teaching conditions after consultation with the Office for Vocational Education and Training and your Sports Federation / Sports Club in order to create an ideal combination between vocational training and competitive sport. In the preliminary clarification, we take a good look at whether your sport is compatible with your chosen apprenticeship and the training goals.

5 weeks of vacation + 1 All our learners receive 5 weeks of holiday per year. If you work in the model of the 43-hour week, you even get 5 free days (compensation days). These are prepared during the 43-hour week throughout the year.

Introduction dates On the introductory days in August you will meet with all new learners at the clinic Beverin in Cazis. After the welcome by Josef Müller, CEO, you will get first insights into the PDGR and get job-specific information about your start of your apprenticeship. The day is rounded off with a joint aperitif in which your vocational trainer participates. Your parents and relatives are also invited.

The next two days all new learners spend together. We work on various topics in workshops so that you are ready to start your teaching.

Apprenticeship Day Get out of everyday life – the apprentice day is the annual highlight of our learners. Once a year you meet with all learners for a common activity. Washing gold in the Surselva, planting Tannen together with the WWF in the Prättigau or visiting the RhB workshops in Landquart are examples of the past apprenticeship days.

The day promotes the common exchange among the learners of the different professional groups and enables you to have a cross-professional experience.
