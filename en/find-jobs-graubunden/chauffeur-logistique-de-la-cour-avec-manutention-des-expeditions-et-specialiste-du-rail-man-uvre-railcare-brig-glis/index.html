Full description

Combined transport from a single source. As a young and innovative transport company, we connect the whole of Switzerland with our own trains and trucks and transport everyday goods from door to door. railCare AG is a subsidiary of Coop and employs around 600 people at 14 locations. Are you a chauffeur C/CE, have you already gained some experience in dispatching or in the office and are you looking for the next career step? With us you combine driving (40%) with coordination and planning (60%)

  • and we train you in the railway sector! Coordination tasks (approx. 60%)
  • Technical management of the team and ensuring smooth daily operations
  • Control of shunting maneuvers, dispatch of trains and execution of technical train inspections (including trips in accordance with FDV)
  • Central contact person for the control center, transport scheduling, network management and locomotive personnel
  • Monitoring of processes and equipment with quick, solution-oriented intervention in the event of disruptions
  • Active collaboration on new lifting and shunting concepts as well as constant process optimization Daily operational business (approx. 40)
  • Transshipment of swap containers with vehicles between trucks and railway wagons (horizontal transshipment)
  • Loading and unloading, preparing and moving swap containers on and off
  • On-time collections and deliveries according to customer specifications
  • Active assistance with general handling work on the site
  • Requirements
  • CH driving license cat (Monday to Saturday incl. Holidays) and good physical condition
  • Very good knowledge of spoken and written German is essential
  • Strong organizational talent, networked thinking as well as high reliability and customer orientation
