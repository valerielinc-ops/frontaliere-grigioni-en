Full description

Are you looking to become self-employed? As an established convenience and food service provider, Valora offers you a turnkey business model that has been tried and tested over many years and proven successful. We provide support beyond the opening of your store, so that you not only get off to a successful start, but also feel at home as a self-employed agency partner at our side in the long term. You can choose which of our first-class formats best suits you: avec, Press & Books or k kiosk (in high-traffic locations such as train stations, airports or petrol stations).

Tasks you’ll enjoy

  • Running one or more busy Valora stores as a self-employed business owner, on your own responsibility
  • Managing staff, including scheduling, recruitment, and employee training and development
  • Merchandise management (inventory, stock levels, orders)
  • Proactively advising customers and building / maintaining customer relationships
  • Working closely with suppliers and the responsible sales manager
  • Continuously optimizing processes and service quality in the store
  • Ensuring the store presents a professional image and implements sales promotion measures

What you bring

  • Vocational training as a retail specialist (EFZ) and/or relevant experience in retail, hospitality or catering
  • Interest in self-employment and managing one or more stores
  • Willingness—especially at the beginning—to invest a great deal of time and effort, and to persevere
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and action, team spirit and organizational talent
  • Enjoyment of working with people, and a strong customer and service orientation
  • At least CHF 30'000 (depending on the format) in equity to establish a GmbH and join as an agency partner

Benefits we offer

  • Turnkey store fit-out in a consistent brand corporate design, plus advertising and communication materials
  • Well-established locations in high-traffic areas
  • Cross-regional marketing measures
  • Advice on revenue and cost planning
  • Detailed onboarding and preparation for your future responsibilities
  • Comprehensive programs for training, education and professional development, as well as seminars and expert presentations
  • Central purchasing
  • Inventory management system and category management (structuring of product groups)
  • Attractive development prospects and the opportunity to take over several stores

Our contact details

Apply today, easily online, and become a self-employed entrepreneur! If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us: bewerbung.agp@valora.com
