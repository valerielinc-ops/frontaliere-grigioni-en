Full description

NDS HF experts can deal with rapidly changing situations and complex issues. They enjoy the independent and interdisciplinary nursing care of people of all ages, with different socio-cultural origins and different degrees of illness and injury. As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable, but for the region extremely important company. We maintain a collegial working atmosphere and enjoy the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos. | We are looking for agreement |

  • Recording the clinical patient situation and initiating immediate measures
  • Professional care and care of outpatient, inpatient patients in an emergency

  • Implementation of diagnostic and therapeutic measures such as point of care testing (POCT) or plaster and splint adjustments
