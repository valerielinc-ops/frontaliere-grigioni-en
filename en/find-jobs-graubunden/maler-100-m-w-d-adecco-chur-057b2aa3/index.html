Introduction

For our customer we are looking for a painter (m/f/d) for versatile applications in the field of renovation, maintenance and design. The activity includes classic painting works in the interior and exterior as well as the careful preparation of substrates and the professional execution of the works according to specifications. We are looking for a reliable person who works cleanly and brings craftsmanship. It is used in a professional working environment with clear processes and different areas of responsibility. The position is aimed at applicants who value practical work and enjoy working on surfaces professionally and achieve a clean result.

Tasks

  • They carefully prepare substrates and create the basis for high-quality painting work.
  • They carry out painting and painting work in the interior and exterior areas professionally and cleanly.
  • They properly cover work areas, furniture and components and secure the work area.
  • They carry out repair, renovation and maintenance work as specified.
  • They check surfaces for quality and, if necessary, carry out rework precisely.

Profile

  • They have craftsmanship and work carefully and reliably.
  • They bring with them experience or interest in painting work and surface finishing.
  • They work independently, structured and adhere to specified workflows.
  • They are physically resilient and have a good eye for clean work results.
  • Ideally, they have a good knowledge of German for secure communication in everyday work.

Advantages

  • Varied activity with different areas of work and changing tasks in the painting trade.
  • industry-standard remuneration, depending on commitment and qualification.
