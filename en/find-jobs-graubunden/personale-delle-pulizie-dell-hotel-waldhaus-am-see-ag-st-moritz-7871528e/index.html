Full description

We are currently looking for a motivated and reliable housekeeping team member to join our team.

Your Responsibilities

  • Clean and prepare guest rooms, bathrooms, and suites according to hotel standards.
  • Ensure guest rooms are spotless, comfortable, and ready for arrivals.
  • Change bed linen, replace towels, and replenish guest amenities.
  • Clean and maintain public areas, corridors, and other designated hotel spaces.
  • Report maintenance issues, damages, and lost property to the supervisor.
  • Handle cleaning products, equipment, and hotel supplies safely and responsibly.
  • Follow hygiene, cleanliness, and safety standards.
  • Assist with laundry and linen management.
  • Work closely with the housekeeping and reception teams to ensure smooth daily operations.

Your Profile

  • Previous housekeeping experience in a hotel is an advantage.
  • Strong attention to detail and a high standard of cleanliness.
  • Reliable, punctual, organized, and able to work independently.
  • Physically fit and comfortable with an active role.
  • Positive attitude and willingness to work as part of a team.
  • Flexibility to work weekends and public holidays according to the schedule.
  • Basic knowledge of German, English, Italian, or Portuguese is an advantage.

What We Offer

  • Full-time employment (100%) in a beautiful location in St. Moritz.
  • A friendly, international working environment.
  • A supportive and welcoming team.
  • Competitive salary based on experience and qualifications.
  • Opportunities to develop your skills in the hospitality industry.
