Full description

As a family-run, high-tech company with almost 19,000 employees at 71 locations worldwide, we are looking for forward thinkers with unconventional ideas and drive to join our team. Our company culture, which values collaboration and mutual trust, creates the ideal framework for boldly trying new things and questioning the status quo. Our technologies inspire people to develop and produce things that are currently unimaginable. Whether lasers, machine tools, EUV or electronics - TRUMPF is building technological worlds for future generations. Are you ready for new challenges?

Your tasks

  • Programming of customized software for laser marking systems
  • Implementation and organizational management of individual customer projects from the preparation of the specifications to acceptance by the customer
  • Creating offers with price calculation, delivery time and technical specifications
  • Maintaining a close exchange both with our customers and with our development
  • Technical contact for production, service and sales companies in support matters
  • Documentation and software testing
  • Support and maintenance of existing projects
  • Further development of customized software

What you bring

  • Studies in computer science (FH or university) or comparable training/practical experience in software development
  • Programming experience in C# with the Visual Studio development tools
  • Knowledge of machine controls and automation interfaces
  • Enjoy projects in direct cooperation with customers
  • Willingness to travel around 20%
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing (at least C1 level)
  • Good English skills in word and writing (at least B2 level)

What we offer

  • Respectful and appreciative corporate culture
  • Highly modern working environment with the latest technology
  • Early assumption of responsibility and varied tasks
  • Extensive training and further education offer
  • Actively promoted work-life balance
