Full description

True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

The Robotics Business Unit develops, produces and distributes pipetting robots to automate liquid handling processes for pharmaceuticals, the food industry and cell or DNA research.

You can do that

  • You will oversee and coordinate technical and organizational issues along the product life cycle from product launch to phase-out and termination.
  • You will analyze existing lifecycle processes and continuously develop them with regard to efficiency, transparency and standardization.
  • You build and develop comprehensive overviews of products, lifecycle status, activities and dependencies.
  • You identify relevant risks and the need for action at an early stage and initiate appropriate measures.
  • You will work on complex technical problems and coordinate the technical problem-solving process.
  • You will coordinate lifecycle activities between engineering, product management, operations, quality and other specialist areas involved.
  • You support product changes and ensure that their impact on products, processes, documentation and organization is considered holistically.

This is what you bring

  • Completed studies, for example in mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, system engineering or a comparable technical discipline
  • Strong systemic, analytical and process-oriented thinking
  • Ability to quickly grasp complex technical and organizational relationships and derive concrete solutions from them
  • Enjoying a versatile generalist role with technical issues and numerous interfaces
  • Experience with product lifecycle, engineering, change or product change processes is an advantage
  • Good understanding of data, key figures and meaningful overviews
  • Ability to bring different stakeholders together and independently advance cross-cutting topics
  • Structured, pragmatic and solution-oriented working method
  • Very good communication skills in German and English
