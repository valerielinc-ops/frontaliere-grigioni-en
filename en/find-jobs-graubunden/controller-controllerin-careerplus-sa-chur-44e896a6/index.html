An international trading company based in Chur provides business partners worldwide with high-quality products. A dynamic corporate culture characterized by appreciation, personal responsibility and continuous improvement forms the basis for successful cooperation across national borders. As an employer, the company combines the stability of an internationally networked organization with regional roots, attractive social benefits and diverse development opportunities. As a controller, you play a central role in the analysis of goods flows, the optimization of transport costs and the expansion of modern controlling instruments. You will have the opportunity to actively develop processes, advance digital solutions and expand your professional skills in a supportive working environment.

Your responsibilities with the new employer:

  • The analysis and control of logistics invoices is one of your core tasks
  • By systematically evaluating freight costs and flows of goods, you can identify potential savings and develop economically convincing optimization measures together with the responsible specialist departments.
  • For management and departments, you prepare relevant key figures, sound analyses and meaningful reports and accompany projects with your controlling expertise from planning to implementation
  • They actively shape the further development of the controlling system landscape, improve reporting structures and use digital instruments as well as automated data processes for sustainable efficiency increases.

You offer your new employer:

  • First to several years of professional experience in controlling form your professional basis for this position.
  • A targeted further training in controlling, business administration or business intelligence supports your professional development
  • Excel knowledge, a strong understanding of goods flows and freight costs as well as experience with dashboards, key figures systems and deviation analyses help you to assess complex issues in a well-founded manner
  • Impart your analytical way of thinking, your independent and efficient way of working and your communicative strength You understand complex results and meet internal contacts in a solution-oriented manner

You can also look forward to the following amenities

  • Flexible working hours and hybrid work models allow you to balance your everyday work and offer the opportunity to better reconcile work and private life
  • Attractive further education programs and an e-learning platform available around the clock promote your professional development and open up new perspectives in controlling and digital topics
  • Above-average social benefits and comprehensive health management with consulting services that are accessible at all times support your well-being and offer additional security in everyday work
  • Discounted food options as well as attractive conditions for sports and massage offers create additional added value and contribute to a pleasant working atmosphere
  • An appreciative corporate culture with respectful communication, direct cooperation and lived recognition offers you an environment where initiative and commitment are valued

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Graubünden?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Graubünden's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Graubünden's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
