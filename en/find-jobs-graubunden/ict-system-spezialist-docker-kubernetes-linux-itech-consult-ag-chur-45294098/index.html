ICT\-System\Specialist (m/f/d) \- Docker \| Kubernetes \| Linux

For a customer in a public environment, we are looking for an experienced personality with expert knowledge for the operation and further development of containerized specialist applications. You support the central IT office in the installation, integration and support of applications on an on-premises platform with Kubernetes, Docker and Rocky Linux. You work closely with suppliers and the application managers of the departments.

ICT\-System\-Specialist Container\-Platforms (m/f/d)\- Kubernetes\| Docker\| Linux\| Rancher

Tasks

  • Design, installation and maintenance of existing and new specialist applications on the container platform
  • Integration of new applications as well as technical support and coordination of suppliers and application managers
  • Installing operating system patches and application updates during the planned maintenance windows
  • Analysis and resolution of complex disruptions in 3rd Level Support
  • Preparation and maintenance of operational documentation, operations manuals and installation instructions

Experience

  • Expert knowledge in the construction and operation of Docker and Kubernetes under Linux
  • Very good knowledge in the installation and integration of containerized specialist applications
  • Experience with Rocky Linux and managing Kubernetes environments with Rancher
  • Experience in technical implementation and coordination of application integrations
  • Independent, reliable and service-oriented working method
  • Analytical and networked thinking as well as a structured, team-oriented way of working
  • Readiness for regular maintenance on Tuesday and Thursday evenings between 20:00 and 22:00
