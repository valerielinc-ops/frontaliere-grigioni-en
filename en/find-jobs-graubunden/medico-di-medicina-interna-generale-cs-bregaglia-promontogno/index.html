The Bregaglia Health Centre (CSB) is located in the Bregaglia Valley, about 4 km from the border with Italy. This regional health center gathers under the same roof a medical study, including first aid service (internal medicine consulting, specialist clinics, complete laboratory diagnostics, ultrasounds, radiology, alternative therapies), a small hospital for acute, a nursing home with department for people with dementia, a physiotherapy service and rescue service. The small medical team is responsible for the health and emergency care of the local population, numerous Italian frontier workers and international tourists.

Requirements

  • Flexibility and reliability
  • Ability to work in a small team
  • Good computer skills in the medical and welfare field
  • Italian or German language, with excellent knowledge of the other language
  • Experience in emergency and emergency service would be an advantage

We offer

  • A dynamic working environment
  • Good balance between work and private life (possibility of part-time)
  • Accommodation available
  • Support for vocational training and updating
