Full description

Con lungimiranza, spirito imprenditoriale e una visione d'insieme, contribuisci all'ulteriore espansione della RhB Immobilien AG e alla realizzazione di interessanti e variegati progetti di sviluppo territoriale, creando insieme ai collaboratori e ai partner un vero valore aggiunto per i Grigioni e la RhB.

Cosa puoi fare

  • Initiation, structuring and control of site and real estate developments in alpine, tourist and urban areas from potential analysis to building input
  • Analysis and further development of existing properties, including optimisation of use, renovation strategies, conversion and value-adding measures
  • Coordination of area, quarter planning and other planning law procedures as well as representation of interests vis-à-vis municipalities, authorities, landowners and other stakeholders
  • Independent management and control of construction projects in the SIA phases 11 to 33, including definition and monitoring of goals, qualities, deadlines, costs, risks and project organisation
  • Performing the client and owner representation, managing external planning and consulting teams and involving internal specialist departments and decision-making bodies
  • Development of usage, market and location analyses, investment cost calculations, profitability and scenario analyses and reliable business cases as well as creation of project strategies, decision bases and proposals for management and board of directors

Ecco perché sei la persona giusta’

  • Completed studies (university or technical college) in the fields of architecture, spatial or urban planning, civil engineering, real estate or real estate management
  • Knowledge of project management as well as an advantage in the client representation
  • Experience in public procedure monitoring

as well as in the development and control of complex real estate or site projects

  • Winning and engaging personality with communication and negotiating strength
  • Openness to new ideas, team players and interest in organizational development and development

Cosa ti offriamo’

  • 2. Class General Subscription
  • Free tickets and discounts on rail travel abroad
  • Paid mobile phone and subscription for business and private use
  • Flexible part-time models
  • Attractive social benefits and pension fund with very good benefits
  • Additional purchase of holidays
  • Possibility for internal and external training with financial support from RhB
