Full description

Member of the Board of Directors – Focus on Energy Industry and Transformation IBC is an independent public institution of the city of Chur. It supplies the city with electricity and water, and the city and several surrounding communities with gas, heat and cooling energy. In addition, IBC also offers services in the field of energy supply. In addition to the supply mandate, the focus is on the implementation of the "Energy Chur 2040 Master Plan". With our existing and new heating and energy network, we want to cover around 60 percent of Churer’s heat demand with CO2-free, renewable energy by 2040.

The Board of Directors is the highest executive body of IBC, and the management is subordinate to it. Reported to the city council, which leads the IBC with an ownership strategy.

To complement our strategic management body, we are looking for 1. June .2027 an entrepreneurial and implementation-strong energy specialist as a member of the Board of Directors.

Member of the Board of Directors – Focus on Energy Industry and Transformation

Their tasks

  • Strategic leadership: You are actively involved in shaping the long-term corporate strategy in the area of tension between security of supply, economic efficiency and the owner’s climate policy goals.
  • Infrastructure & Energy Transition: They accompany key investment decisions, in particular in the areas of renewable energies, district heating expansion, grid infrastructure and cross-sectoral cross-connection synergies.
  • Governance and supervision: You perform the non-transferable tasks of the Board of Directors, supervise the management and risk management (ICS) and contribute your expertise to the Board of Directors.
  • Stakeholder dialogue: You act as a competent contact person at the interface between companies, politics, administration and the public in the energy sector.

Your profile

  • Expertise: In-depth knowledge of the Swiss energy industry (electricity market, grid regulation/ElCom, heat supply) as well as proven practical experience in the transformation towards renewable energy systems.
  • Management experience: Several years of experience at management or board level, ideally in an infrastructure, energy or technology company.
  • Financial and governance competence: Secure assessment of investment accounts and financial statements according to Swiss GAAP FER.
  • Political sense: High understanding of the framework conditions of a public company, anchoring in the region and full independence (no conflicts of interest).
  • Personality: Analytically thinking, constructively critical team player personality with strong communication skills and the necessary time availability.
