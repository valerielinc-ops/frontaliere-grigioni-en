Job description Hello, we are KiMi. We have more than 30 daycare centers, hoarding rooms and lunch tables in six cantons. This obliges: We communicate clearly, have high demands and show attitude. And that benefits the whole KiMi team. Because: We take it personally, are familiar, think visionary and act professionally. So that children can discover the world together and carefree. Are you a children’s song expert, have a great name memory and know a few tricks on how to drum up many explorers? If you can answer all this with “yes” and are looking forward to accompanying children pedagogically every day, then you are right with us as a co-educator: in our location West. As a co-educator, you support the groups according to the guidance and guidelines of the group leadership. You help to accompany and support the children, are an important caregiver for the children and parents and take on the responsibility of the group in absences. What you get from us: A great, committed team that treats each other in an appreciative way, open communication, a secure job in a large network, training and promotion opportunities, flexible pens, individual working hours and a fair wage according to kibesuisse basics. In addition: free food, free KiMi clothing, many ZFV staff discounts and more. What you bring:

A big heart for children

  • A degree as specialist supervision (EFZ) or Infant education: or equivalent (attach SBFI recognition)
  • Good communication skills
  • Warmth, sense of responsibility, assertiveness

What do you mean? Are you there? Then send us your CV and your EFZ by mail. Increase your chance by attaching your motivation to us in a motivation letter. We look forward to seeing you.
