Introduction We offer you an exciting position in our Blatter's Arosa Hotel. With your heartfelt and committed commitment, you contribute to our high quality of service and inspire our guests every day anew. Your tasks

You are responsible for

  • Repairs in the house
  • Small maintenance of plants and machines as well as coordination with external craftsmen
  • The care of the environment and snow clearance in winter
  • Acceptance and delivery of supplies
  • Maintenance work during the intermediate season
  • Assistance with chauffeur services
  • General further work

Your profile

This is what we expect from you

  • A completed apprenticeship
  • Solid technical understanding and flexibility and resilience
  • A happy nature – a smile is simply part of it for you
  • Attention and a cool head, even in hectic situations
  • Very good knowledge of German

You can expect this from us

With us you can expect an interesting and varied working environment. As a private company, we attach great importance to a pleasant working atmosphere and long-term cooperation. Modern working time recording is a matter of course for us.

We offer

Take advantage of our all-round package

We will cover all expenses related to your entry and the required formalities. On request, you can rent an apartment or a new 1-room apartment from us. We are also happy to support you in the search for a suitable accommodation.

You also benefit from

  • Discount ski passes and free ski passes on selected days in winter
  • The Arosa All-Inclusive Card in Summer
  • Reduced room rates for your family members
  • A bonus if you refer professional colleagues to us

You work and live in one of the most beautiful tourist places in Switzerland. Whether summer or winter – You can expect a variety of sports and leisure opportunities right on your doorstep. Contact details For initial information you can call us and / or send us your complete documents.
