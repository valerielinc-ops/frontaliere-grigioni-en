Full description

Introduction We offer you an exciting position at Blatter’s Arosa Hotel. In this role you support our service team during the high season. With your charming appearance, your warm nature and your motivation, you contribute to maintaining our high quality and delighting our guests again and again. Your tasks

In the team, you are responsible for

  • Advice, ordering, serving guests
  • Assistance in breakfast, lunch and evening service
  • Setting and setting tables
  • Managing your own station and independently billing your station
  • Mise en Place for a smooth service process
  • Further work in the service area

Your profile

We expect you to

  • a completed apprenticeship and/or several years of experience in the (seasonal) hotel industry
  • Warm and cheerful nature – a smile is a matter of course for you
  • Fun at work and a pronounced service orientation
  • healthy flexibility and resilience, even when it gets hectic
  • Attention, understanding and a good feeling for our guests
  • Very good knowledge of German

We offer

You work in an interesting and varied environment. As a privately run hotel, we attach great importance to a pleasant working atmosphere and tend to a long-term cooperation. Modern working time recording is a matter of course for us.

In addition, you benefit from

  • Discount ski passes and free ski passes on selected days
  • Our all-round package, which covers all entrance and formality expenses
  • Discount room prices for family members
  • Bonus for placement of professional colleagues

They work and live in one of the most beautiful tourist places in Switzerland. Various sports opportunities are available in summer as well as in winter. Contact details For initial information, please call us. We look forward to your complete application documents.
