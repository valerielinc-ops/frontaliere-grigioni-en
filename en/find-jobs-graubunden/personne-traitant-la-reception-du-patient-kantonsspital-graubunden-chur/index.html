Full description

Start on January 1, 2027 or by appointment as a case officer: in patient admission 80 - 100%

Our reception and patient admission are an important point of contact for our patients, for relatives and visitors. Our experienced team supports each other and works together to enable our patients to run smoothly both in the outpatient and in the inpatient area.

Are you an established personality and have organizational skills? Perfect, then you belong to us!

These are your tasks

  • You provide competent information for internal and external inquiries
  • You collect and maintain patient master data in the systems and are in exchange with health insurance companies and insurance companies
  • You take planned and emergency entrances and coordinate them with the appropriate offices

You bring that with you

  • A commercial education with experience in health care or a completed basic medical education
  • Good MS-Office knowledge and ideally experience in SAP-R/3, Italian and English skills are a plus
  • A reliable, open personality with pleasure in dealing with people as well as readiness for weekend assignments and irregular working hours

Can you do something that others can’t? You make the difference? Then you belong to us!
