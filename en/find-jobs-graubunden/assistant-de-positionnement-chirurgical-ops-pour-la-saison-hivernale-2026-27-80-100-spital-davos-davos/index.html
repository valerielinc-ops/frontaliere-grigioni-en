Full description

Spital Davos has a varied and lively surgical operation with general surgery, orthopedics with a focus on traumatology, gynecology/obstetrics, and neuro-surgery. As a regional/acute hospital with emergency service, nursing home, and Spitex, we are responsible for the medical basic provision for the Davos population and guests. However, the well-being of our employees is equally important to us. Working with us means being part of a small but important operation for the region, with a collegial work environment, appreciating the proximity to nature and the numerous sports and leisure opportunities in Davos. ## Tasks

  • Preparation of the patient and technical equipment for the surgical operation
  • Execution of surgical positions according to the requirements of the surgeon and anesthesia
  • Assistance in the admission and discharge of the patient
  • Assistance in X-ray examinations ##
  • Requirements
  • Completed professional training and work experience
  • Knowledge in the medical field and specialized course in surgical positioning
  • also welcome are cross-trainers
  • Experience in the field of surgical positioning is an advantage
  • Flexibility, commitment, and technical skill
  • Communication and team skills
  • Willingness to work shifts and weekends
  • Very good German language skills, other languages are supportive in daily exchanges ##
  • What we offer
  • Challenging and interesting tasks in a collegial, motivated team
  • An exciting environment in which your solid expertise and skills, as well as your responsible and independent working style, are valuable
  • Attractive employment conditions and social benefits; hospital-owned daycare until school age
  • Diverse sports and leisure offer in the highest city in Europe ## Contact
