Med. Praxisassistent/in (MPA) 80-100%, General. Medicine, chur Location: Scaletta Medical Center The Scaletta Medical Center in Chur is the medical center of the MCM-Group. In addition to the outpatient primary care by currently 3 family doctors, the offer in special areas is also continuously expanded. The new, spacious and inviting premises in the immediate vicinity of the train station include a total of 6 offices for family doctors, specialists and therapies. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an MED. PRAXISASISTENT/PRAXISASISTENT 80%-100 % You can expect a varied and versatile activity in a motivated environment with modern structures and development opportunities.

Their tasks

  • Office hours organisation incl. Reception and care of patients
  • Assistance in medical treatments (e.g. wound care, bandage change, blood sampling, lung function test)
  • Working in the laboratory, on the digital X-ray and support of the practice pharmacy
  • Administrative activities (ordering, performance recording, billing and insurance clearance)

Your profile

  • Completed training as MPA (EFZ), with laboratory and X-ray knowledge
  • Good computer skills
  • Knowledge of foreign languages (English and/or Italian) is advantageous
  • Kind, empathetic and well-groomed appearance
  • Resilience and teamwork – retaining even in hectic situations You have the overview
  • Motivation to actively participate in the further development of our center

We offer you

  • An appreciative and collegial working atmosphere
  • A modern, well-equipped workplace
  • Qualified training and support by a motivated team
  • Varied tasks with development possibilities
  • 5 weeks holiday
