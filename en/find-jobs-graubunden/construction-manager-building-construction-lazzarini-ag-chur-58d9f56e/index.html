Construction manager in building construction

You keep track, think ahead and take responsibility?

For our team Real Estate Total Service (IGS) in Chur, we are looking for an experienced person with a passion for construction projects. You actively participate in various projects and successfully implement them together with a dedicated project team.

Your tasks

  • Independent organisation and management of the construction site
  • Cost calculation, tendering, submission and participation in awards, supplementary management
  • Management and control of deadlines, costs and quality
  • Coordination of entrepreneurs, specialist planners and other project participants
  • Management and logging of construction meetings
  • Acceptance and defect management
  • Construction administration and project documentation

What is important to us You like to take action, communicate openly and clearly and take responsibility. With your entrepreneurial thinking, you recognize opportunities at an early stage, develop solutions and successfully advance projects together with the team.

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as construction manager, technician, draughtsman, architecture or a comparable qualification
  • At least 5 years of experience in construction management in Swiss building construction
  • Good knowledge of Swiss construction practice and relevant standards
  • Experience with Bauad and MS\Office\ programs
  • A structured and forward-looking way of working

What we offer you *

  • Various projects and different building owners
  • High personal responsibility and scope for design
  • An innovative environment for the further development of modern BIM processes up to the paperless construction site
  • Collaboration in project teams (project manager – construction manager – draftsman) with short decision paths
  • Flexible working hours and possibility for home office
  • Modern work equipment and company vehicle
  • Professional and personal training and development opportunities
  • A working environment in which cooperation, reliability and initiative count
