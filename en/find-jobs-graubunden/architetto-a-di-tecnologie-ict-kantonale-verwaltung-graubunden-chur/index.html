Full description

Are you ready to help develop the digital transformation? As an IT service and competence center of the cantonal administration of Graubünden, we support our internal customers with a secure basic infrastructure and advanced applications. Together with you, we are advancing the digital transformation of the canton of Graubünden. As the Office for Computer Science, we are looking for an ICT technology architect immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • You observe and assess new technologies in and related to administration, currently with a focus on artificial intelligence and cloud services
  • You will develop technical target architectures and define architectural principles, standards and technological guidelines for our administration
  • You will advise our departments on the selection of suitable technologies and platforms
  • You will help develop our Microsoft Azure platform and SaaS/PaaS services
  • You will analyze existing application landscapes and develop strategies for their modernization
  • You ensure the compatibility of innovation, information security, data protection, operations and governance

Requirements

  • A university degree in computer science, business informatics or business administration with a further education in AI, cloud architectures or IT security
  • Several years of professional experience in the areas of cloud computing, SaaS/PaaS and hybrid architectures or IT security
  • Experience in the modernization and migration of applications and platforms
  • Understanding architecture frameworks, governance and security requirements
  • Communication strength and a structured, team-oriented way of working
  • Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is advantageous
