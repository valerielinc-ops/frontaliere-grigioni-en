Helicopter pilot / helicopter pilot 100%

The Swiss rescue service Rega brings professional medical assistance from the air to patients in Switzerland and worldwide. It is a non-profit and private foundation supported by more than 3.7 million patrons.

For our crew in Samedan/GR we are looking for 1\. September 2027 a motivated personality that strengthens our team. In this versatile field of application, you can fully contribute your passion for helicopter air rescue.

These activities inspire you

  • Performing rescue and relocation flights in visual and instrument flight (day / ) in domestic and border countries
  • Ensure safe and smooth flight operations as a crew member
  • Willingness to work irregular hours (picking services, 24 hours)
  • Maintaining contact with partners and participating in expert committees

That sets them apart.

  • Completed vocational training or higher education
  • Valid EASA\ license as a professional helicopter pilot with extension for night flight (NIT) and CH\ mountains (MOU) as well as proven flight experience (\> 2,500 hours, of which 2,000 are on turbine helicopters)
  • Where possible:

+ NVIS Helicopter IFR EASA + HESLO 2 + Experience in mountain rescue + Flight rating on a twin-engine helicopter

  • Reliable, independent, team-capable and physically and psychologically resilient personality with a pronounced service orientation
  • Mother tongue German as well as good communication in English, Italian is advantageous
  • Other requirements:

+ Valid emergency assistance card + Strong technical understanding + Good user knowledge in Microsoft 365 + up to 45 years + Place of residence in the region of the Engadin Rega\ base

What you can look forward to

It awaits you a varied, demanding and exciting task in rescue aviation. We offer you optimal equipment in the field of rescue, medical and flight technology as well as regular training and further education. Would you like to contribute your experience and skills in a future-oriented, exciting environment and actively participate in the further development of Rega? Then you are exactly right in our team spirit environment. Do you have questions? Then do not hesitate to contact us. We look forward to your application by 16\ at the latest. November 2026.
