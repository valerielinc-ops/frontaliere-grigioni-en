Backup \& Storage Engineer (m/f/d)

IT Switzerland AG *

With more than 1,800 IT specialists in nine countries, we develop and promote new business models within the \ group. To this end, we digitize company processes and are pioneers in innovation. To this end, we are continuously investing in training and further education, modern systems, infrastructure and perhaps soon in you.

You bring in-depth experience in the backup environment and are happy to take responsibility for the safe and reliable operation of business-critical systems. With us, you are responsible for the conception, operation and further development of our global backup infrastructure. You develop standards further and bring your knowledge to storage and server virtualization in the context of data backup. Variety, scope for design and a direct impact are guaranteed.

Does that sound like an exciting task for you? Then welcome to IT Switzerland AG at the location in Chur!

YOUR TASKS

  • Responsibility for the design, operation and further development of our global backup infrastructure
  • Ensuring stable, secure, high-performance and highly available enterprise-level backup operations
  • Definition, implementation and further development of backup standards, policies and processes
  • Support and further development of storage and server virtualization in the context of data protection
  • Documentation of the systems as well as participation in overarching IT\ projects and concepts

Your profile

  • Completed education or study in the IT\ area
  • Sound and verifiable experience in the backup environment
  • Very good knowledge of backup technologies and architectures
  • Experience with Commvault is beneficial
  • Solid knowledge in storage and server virtualization
  • A sense of responsibility and a structured and independent working method
  • Experience with agile methods as well as project management or collaboration tools
  • Joy of teamwork, initiative and participation
  • Readiness for occasional travel

We offer you

A very independent, varied but also responsible and challenging activity in an international market environment and a cooperative corporate culture. The promotion and further training of our employees is a matter of concern to us and you benefit from very good employment conditions, flexibility, an excellent working atmosphere and very good social benefits.
