Nightlogistics / Nightlogistics

Transgourmet Schweiz AG — Land of the Kingdom of Belgium, Graubünden, Sizzera

We are committed to our customers. We are committed to our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive private brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services.Transgourmet Schweiz AG belongs to the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retail).

Tasks

  • You monitor and perform the consolidation of customer orders
  • You make sure to avoid damage to goods during compaction
  • You control the completeness of the products provided for the individual tours
  • You manage the empty containers
  • You collect and distribute the Cross-Ficking articles
  • You accept misdeliveries and forward them to your superior person
  • You ensure compliance with the company guidelines for occupational safety and accident prevention
  • You comply with the legal QS guidelines

Requirements

  • You have professional experience in logistics or a completed apprenticeship as a logistics specialist EFZ or EBA
  • You have a SUVA-recognised forklift card and have sufficient driving practice
  • You work goal-oriented and structured
  • You show your own initiative, are reliable and bring a high commitment
  • You are physically robust and ready to work in cold rooms with temperatures between -20 °C and +5 °C
  • You have good German language skills in word and writing
