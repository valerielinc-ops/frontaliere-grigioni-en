Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the cantonal administration Graubünden together with us.

The Office for Prisoners (AJV) Graubünden is a department of the Department of Justice, Security and Health of the Canton of Graubünden. The AJV is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the execution of the various types of detention. In Graubünden, the execution of penalties and measures is thus regulated as a uniform topic and holistic task, in which different professions work interdisciplinary with people who have become delinquent in order to prevent further delinquency.

The prison (JVA) Realta serves the open prison of men according to the Swiss Penal Code.
