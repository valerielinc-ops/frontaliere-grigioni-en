Full description

As a family-run, high-tech company with almost 19,000 employees at 71 locations worldwide, we are looking for forward thinkers with unconventional ideas and drive to join our team. Our company culture, which values collaboration and mutual trust, creates the ideal framework for boldly trying new things and questioning the status quo. Our technologies inspire people to develop and produce things that are currently unimaginable. Whether lasers, machine tools, EUV or electronics - TRUMPF is building technological worlds for future generations. Are you ready for new challenges?

Your tasks

  • Preference calculation for flat-bed laser machines, laser markers and associated assemblies
  • Advice in all customs matters and support of any customs inspections
  • Analysis of tax risks in series transactions
  • Flexible use for varying tasks and projects within the team (e.g. export control, tariffs, supplier declarations, master data maintenance)
  • Close cooperation with purchasing, logistics, production and external parties
  • Regular participation in training according to the current political and economic situation

Your profile

  • Commercial apprenticeship or similar degree completed
  • Ideally, further training in foreign trade with corresponding professional experience
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and high personal responsibility
  • Proactive, independent and exact working method
  • Conceptual and analytical skills
  • Very good knowledge of German, good knowledge of English in word and writing
  • Secure handling of MS Office and ERP systems (SAP, GTS)
  • Teamwork and enjoyment of international cooperation

What we offer

  • Modern working environment
  • Early assumption of responsibility and varied tasks
  • Extensive training and further education offer
  • Respectful and appreciative corporate culture
  • Various employee benefits
  • Work where others go on holiday
