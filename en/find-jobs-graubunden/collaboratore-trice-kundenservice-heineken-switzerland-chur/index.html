Employee: in customer service at Heineken Switzerland (Calanda Brewery) in Chur, Canton of Graubünden, Switzerland. As part of the HEINEKEN Group, the world’s second largest brewery group, HEINEKEN Switzerland employs around 800 people at several locations in Switzerland.
