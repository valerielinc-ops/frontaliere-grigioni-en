Full description

As a member of our physio team, you accompany our outpatient and inpatient patients on their way back to everyday life and help them to restore or maintain their ability to move. As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees.
