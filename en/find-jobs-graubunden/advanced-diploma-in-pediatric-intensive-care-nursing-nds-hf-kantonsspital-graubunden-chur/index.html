Full description

We are looking for Dipl. Nursing specialists HF for the two-year postgraduate degree program Dipl. Expert / Expert Intensive care HF for pediatrics 90 - 100%

In the postgraduate studies to / to the Dipl. Expert / Expert Intensive Care NDS HF you care for premature and newborn children up to older children with serious or life-threatening illnesses and injuries. You provide for the physical and psychological well-being and safety of the children.

Dealing with complex, rapidly changing situations and understanding children and their families in exceptional moments are among the daily challenges of the specialists. This requires a high degree of flexibility, rapid and yet prudent reaction.

These are your tasks

  • You carry out the care of patients in a situational manner and use technical aids and devices in a targeted and professional manner
  • This training provides technical and pathophysiological knowledge as well as performance-oriented action. In addition, psychosocial, ethical and family-related foundations are significant
  • You complete the second and third semester at the Children's Hospital in Zurich

You bring that with you

  • Dipl. Nursing specialist HF or a foreign diploma in nursing recognised by the State Secretariat for Education, Research and Innovation (SBFI)
  • Experience in a pediatric acute department
  • Depending on neonatology or paediatric experience, an early job start is desired
