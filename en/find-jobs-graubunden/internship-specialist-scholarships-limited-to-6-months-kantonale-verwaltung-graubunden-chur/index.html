The Department of Education, Culture and Environmental Protection comprises five departments with a wide field of activity. The scholarships department is affiliated to the Finance & Controlling department of the department secretariat. The department secretariat is the department's staff office. It supports the government council in running the department as well as in leadership, communication and public relations issues. The office space is centrally located in Chur.
