dasteam ag can look back on more than 39 years of existence on the Swiss recruitment services market and we are one of the leading national recruitment agencies with a total of 28 branches in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein. Due to our high customer orientation, a well-developed network, wealth of experience and openness to new, unconventional ideas, we always try to offer optimal services for companies and job seekers.

For our customer in Domat/Ems, we are looking for a committed personality as a chauffeur\*euse C / CE and farm logistics operator.

Job description ##

  • Pure variety: food and non-food deliveries, pick up returns, customer contact, tour conclusion.
  • Assuming responsibility: You load your vehicle at temperature, secure the load and deliver to our customers on time.
  • Direct customer contact: You bring the goods not only to the customer, but directly to the intended rooms (cooler, freezer).
  • Team spirit: We all pull on the same rope. A strong team, short distances, uncomplicated bosses.

We are expecting ##

  • Driving licence Cat C / CE
  • Experience as a chauffeur/euse (ideally in the food sector)
  • Physical fitness and joy of packing
  • Good knowledge of German (verbal & writing)
  • Willingness to work also on Saturdays (seasonal business)
  • Personal initiative, reliability and pleasure in customer contact
