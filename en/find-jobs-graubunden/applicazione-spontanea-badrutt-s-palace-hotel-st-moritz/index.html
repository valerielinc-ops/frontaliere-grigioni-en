Full description

Your key responsibilities

As a Chef de Partie, you will play a key role in ensuring the highest culinary standards and delivering an exceptional dining experience. Your main responsibilities will include:

  • Preparing, cooking, and presenting high-quality dishes within your assigned kitchen section.

  • Ensuring consistent quality through the careful selection, preparation, and handling of fresh ingredients.

  • Supporting the development of seasonal menus and contributing creative ideas to enhance our culinary offering.

  • Maintaining the highest standards of food hygiene, safety, and cleanliness in accordance with HACCP regulations.

  • Working closely with the Executive Chef, Sous Chefs, and the wider kitchen team to ensure a smooth and efficient operation.

  • Assisting in the training and support of junior kitchen team members when required.

  • Managing your section efficiently while maintaining excellent quality, organization, and attention to detail.

• What you bring to the job

  • Completed professional training as a Chef or an equivalent qualification in the culinary field.

  • Previous experience as a Chef de Partie, ideally in a luxury hotel or fine dining environment.

  • Passion for high-quality cuisine with strong attention to detail and presentation.

  • Excellent organizational skills and the ability to work efficiently under pressure.

  • Sound knowledge of HACCP guidelines and food safety standards.

  • Strong communication skills and the ability to work effectively as part of a team.

  • Good command of Italian and English.

Our offer

  • Fixed-term seasonal position from late November 2026 to early April 2027, with the opportunity to return for future seasons.

  • Average 5-day work week with 43.5 hours per week.

  • 5 weeks of annual vacation and 6 public holidays per year.

  • 13th-month salary from the start of employment (pro rata).

  • Accommodation available upon request in one of our modern staff residences.

  • Meals available in our new, modern staff restaurant.

  • An attractive working environment with excellent opportunities for professional growth and development.

Are you passionate about culinary excellence and looking for an exciting opportunity as a Chef de Partie in a luxury hotel environment? We look forward to receiving your complete online application and to welcoming you to our team.
