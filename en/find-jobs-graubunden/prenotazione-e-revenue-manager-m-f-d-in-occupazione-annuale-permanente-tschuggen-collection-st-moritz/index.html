Full description

The Carlton Hotel in St. Moritz is a 5-star superior luxury hotel in the Bündner Bergen, designed by Carlo Rampazzi. As one of the 4 houses of "The Tschuggen Collection AG", our house offers 60 individually designed suites and rooms, plus stylish restaurants - including the gourmet highlight Da Vittorio with 18 points Gault Millau & 2 Michelin stars and the Grand Restaurant, a tribute to nature and the culinary delicacies of the Engadine - as well as an impressive 1 200 m2 spa. We value sustainability, are Green Globe certified and are members of Leading Hotels of the World, Swiss Deluxe Hotels, Forbes Travel Guide and American Express Fine Hotels & Resorts.
