Full description

COMPETENT AND CORDIAL With more than 600 employees at Schiers Hospital, the three retirement homes Schiers, Jenaz and Klosters, Spitex, Living with Service, the crèche, the parent counselling service and the Klosters Medical Centre, the Flury Foundation ensures comprehensive and integrated health care in Prättigau. Our hospital in Schiers is a modern primary care acute care hospital with an interdisciplinary ward with 41 beds for the clinics of surgery, medicine, gynaecology and obstetrics, an outpatient day clinic, the emergency department and the certified Intermediate Care Station (IMC). We are looking for an employee for patient admission and reception as of 1 April 2027 or by appointment. Patient registration 80%

Your responsibilities will include

  • First point of contact for patients, relatives and visitors
  • Administrative admission of inpatients and outpatients
  • Recording and processing of patient data in SAP/INES
  • Obtaining and processing cost reimbursement commitments from the cost bearers
  • Clarifications with internal and external service providers
  • Operation of the switchboard
  • Information at the reception as an internal and external contact person
  • Cash management, incl. billing

Your profile

  • Commercial training with professional experience in the healthcare sector or medical practice assistant
  • Experienced handling of MS Office programs, experience with a hospital information system/SAP

advantageous

  • They appreciate a varied job, flexible working hours and feel comfortable in a lively hospital environment.
