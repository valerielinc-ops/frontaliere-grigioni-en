Assembly / Freight forwarder (m/f/d)

Three locations. One team. Your future.

The PLAST Group unites Stüdli Plast AG in Romanshorn, Grischa Plast AG in Maienfeld and Küng Plast AG in Uznach. With around 76 employees and 64 machines, we combine many years of experience with modern plastic technology and innovative manufacturing solutions.

From large components and assembly to precision injection molding, toolmaking and 3K technology, to medical technology, micro injection molding and measurement technology, our three locations offer a versatile working environment.

As a certified group of companies, we stand for quality, innovation and continuous development. At the same time, we focus on modern workplaces, flexible working time models, short decision-making processes and a corporate culture in which our employees are valued as people and specialists.

Discover your possibilities at the PLAST Group – and shape the future with us.

Their tasks

  • Assembly of plastic components and assemblies according to specifications
  • Packaging and shipping preparation of products
  • Coordination and implementation of transport
  • Ensuring quality and meeting deadlines
  • Support for the management of inputs and outputs

Your profile

  • Completed training or experience in assembly, logistics or forwarding
  • Craftsmanship and technical understanding
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Teamwork, reliability and independent working methods
  • Category B driving licence is advantageous

We offer

  • A modern and versatile working environment in an innovative company
  • Attractive employment conditions and flexible working hours
  • Flat hierarchies and short decision paths
  • Individual training opportunities
  • An appreciative corporate culture

Application information If you are interested in this exciting position, we look forward to receiving your application via this website or our website.

Please send us your CV and a short cover letter explaining your motivation and suitability for this position.

We look forward to meeting you.

With friendly greetings

Oliver Gutknecht.
