Full description

Start by agreement as ICT Application Manager: in 80 - 100%

From 1 January 2027, ICT Samedan with its eight employees will be part of the ICT department of the Graubünden Canton Hospital. In this function, you support the specialist areas on site at the Samedan Hospital with a focus on business applications and participate in exciting projects. In this way, you contribute to ensuring that our patients receive the best possible administrative service and are treated in the usual quality.

These are your tasks

  • You will supervise, support and develop our specialist applications and ensure their smooth operation
  • You will introduce new applications together with internal departments, suppliers and the cantonal hospital Graubünden and accompany tests and rollouts
  • You work in ICT projects, take over sub-project management and create documentation and IT concepts

You bring that with you

  • An education in the computer science environment or a comparable qualification or professional experience in the hospital (e.g. in care) with understanding of clinical processes, high IT affinity and interest in specialist applications as well as digitalization
  • Enjoying applications and ICT specialist applications and a structured and independent working method
  • Team spirit, communication strength and motivation to move us forward together

Can you do something that others can’t? You make the difference? Then you belong to us!
