Full description

Your surroundings

The Rothenbrunnen Home Centre offers a total of 62 residential spaces, spread over four residential groups in Rothenbrunnen and the outdoor residential groups in Chur and Roveredo. Your future job will be in a residential community for adults with mental disabilities. The entire environment and the field of activity are consistently aimed at enabling residents to participate in all areas of life. On site, there are also supervised job offers in the ARBES workshop.

Their tasks

  • Bringing the UN CRPD to life: They closely monitor how the UN CRPD is implemented in daily practice and provide valuable suggestions for further development
  • Supporting participation: They actively accompany people with disabilities in everyday life and in social life
  • Sensitize and advise: They provide expert advice as well as recipients on the rights and implementation of the UN CRPD.
  • Accompanying projects: They support the development of new offers and actively help with training
  • Representing interests: You are committed and strongly committed to the interests and rights of people with disabilities

Our requirement profile

  • You bring your own disability experience, which you profitably incorporate into your work
  • You are committed to the topic of inclusion and the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities is close to your heart
  • You have professional experience and have a clear connection to social institutions or institutions
  • You are a positive and communicative personality and openly approach people

We offer you

  • You can expect a versatile and meaningful task that offers you deep insights into all areas of our organization.
  • They work in an accessible environment and benefit from flexible working hours.
  • You work closely with the management of specialist development, who is always supportive and advisory.
