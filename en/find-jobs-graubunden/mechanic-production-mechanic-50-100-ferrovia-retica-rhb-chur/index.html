Full description

In questo ruolo di grande responsabilità, ti occuperai di garantire la disponibilità della nostra infrastruttura in a delle regioni più suggestive della Svizzera. Sarai direttamente responsabile del funzionamento senza interruzioni dei nostri sistemi di sicurezza ferroviaria.

Cosa puoi fare

  • Manufacture of wheel discs or bandages on CNC and conventional equipment, drawn or sampled
  • Independent setup and operation of the modern machine park
  • Processing of the wheelset profile on the reprofiling installation (Safop)
  • Removal and pressing of wheel discs on axles
  • Removal and shrinkage of bandages by means of induction methods

Ecco perché sei la persona giusta’

  • Completed training as a polymechanic or production mechanic
  • Experience with the programming system TopSolid or willingness to learn it
  • Experience in CNC editing and programming
  • Resilient, team-oriented and polyvalently usable

  • Flexible part-time models
  • Attractive social benefits and pension fund with very good benefits
  • Additional purchase of holidays
  • Possibility for internal and external training with financial support from RhB
