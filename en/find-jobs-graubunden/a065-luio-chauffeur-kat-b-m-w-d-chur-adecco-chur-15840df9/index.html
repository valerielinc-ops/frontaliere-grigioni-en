Full description

Introduction

For a customer in the field of transport and logistics, we are looking for a reliable driver Cat. B. It is a position with practical driving and delivery tasks in daily operation. Our customer attaches importance to punctual execution, careful handling of vehicles and a structured way of working. As an experienced HR service provider, Adecco supports tailor-made mediation and accompanies the entire process professionally. The job is suitable for people who like to travel, take responsibility and do their tasks independently and reliably. A later takeover by the customer is planned and can take place if appropriate.

Tasks

  • They carry out journeys with category B vehicles reliably and on schedule.
  • You take over delivery and transport tasks according to the specifications of the customer operation.
  • They shall check the vehicle for proper and safe condition before departure.
  • They handle transport goods, vehicles and working materials carefully.
  • They consistently comply with the applicable traffic rules and internal work instructions.

Profile

  • have a valid category B driving licence;
  • They work reliably, punctually and take responsibility in daily use.
  • They bring an independent and careful way of working.
  • They have a high degree of concentration and orientation in road transport.
  • They are customer-oriented and communicate clearly and objectively in everyday work.

Advantages

  • Possibility to take over by the customer with suitable cooperation and performance.
  • Assistance from an experienced HR service expert throughout the process.
