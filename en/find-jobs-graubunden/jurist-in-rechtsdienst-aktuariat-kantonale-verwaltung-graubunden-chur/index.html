Full description

The child and adult protection authority KESB is there for people in need. Those who need help and support can count on KESB. There is no day like the other. In our interdisciplinary team, we actively shape a secure future for vulnerable adults, families, children and young people.

We are looking for KESB Graubünden, Engadin/Südtäler branch, as of 1 January. March 2027 or by agreement a lawyer.

Tasks

  • Support and advice to the members of the authority as well as to assist persons in substantive and formal legal matters (child and adult protection law, marriage and inheritance law, land transactions, insurance law, international private law)
  • Independent processing of assigned procedures, including transactions requiring approval (Art. 416 ZGB)
  • Preparation of draft decisions in the assigned procedures
  • Actuarial activity for the Authority
  • Active participation in various project groups (e.g. practice finding, legal revisions)
  • Minutes at hearings

Requirements

  • University degree in law
  • Professional experience, preferably in legal practice or in court and solid knowledge of private law
  • Independent, resilient, flexible and network-thinking personality
  • Very good oral and written expression in German, knowledge of Italian is desired, further foreign language skills are advantageous
  • Prudent, efficient and precise working methods
  • High willingness to provide services and teamwork in interdisciplinary service
  • Proven social skills and empathy towards people in difficult life situations
