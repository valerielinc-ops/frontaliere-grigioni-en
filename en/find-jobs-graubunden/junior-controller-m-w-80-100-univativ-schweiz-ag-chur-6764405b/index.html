For our healthcare client, we are looking for a numerical and motivated personality as Junior Controller (m/f) 80\-100%.

Our diversity is your chance! Whether IT, economics or engineering – we open doors for you in a wide range of specialist areas and industries. With us you will find more than just a job: exciting projects, individual development opportunities and real responsibility – whether you are studying, have just finished or have your first professional experience.

What to expect

  • Participation in cost accounting for various areas of health care and social services
  • Support in the preparation of monthly cost and performance reports
  • Preparation and evaluation of ad\-hoc\ analyses as well as business evaluations
  • Preparation of key figures and basis for decision-making for departments and management
  • Participation in the optimization of existing controlling processes and reporting instruments
  • Support for cross-divisional projects and administrative controlling tasks

What you should bring

  • Completed commercial apprenticeship (EFZ) as well as ongoing or completed business studies (FH/HF/Uni))
  • Strong affinity for numbers and interest in financial and business connections
  • First practical experience in finance, accounting or controlling beneficial, but not a requirement
  • Analytical, structured and independent working method
  • Good MS\Office\ knowledge, especially Excel, as well as pleasure in dealing with data
  • Motivated, willing to learn and team-oriented personality with an interest in long-term development in controlling
  • Negotiable knowledge of German in word and writing

What we offer you

  • Entry opportunity into controlling with attractive professional development prospects
  • Working with an experienced and well-qualified team of five
  • Versatile tasks with insight into different areas of the company
  • Structured familiarisation and support in professional development
  • Flexible work design with Homeoffice\ possibilities by arrangement
  • Progressive employment conditions, above-average social benefits and at least 30 days of holiday
  • Meaningful activity in a stable and socially relevant environment
