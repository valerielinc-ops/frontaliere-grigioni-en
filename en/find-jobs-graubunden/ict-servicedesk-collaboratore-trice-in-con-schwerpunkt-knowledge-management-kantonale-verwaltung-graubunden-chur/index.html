Full description

Are you ready to help develop the digital transformation? As an IT service and competence center of the cantonal administration of Graubünden, we support our departments with a secure basic infrastructure and advanced applications. Together with you, we will advance the digital transformation of the canton of Graubünden.

ICT customer service is exactly your thing? We as the Office for Computer Science are looking for an ICT service desk employee with a focus on knowledge management.

Tasks

  • You create, maintain and revise instructions and checklists and prepare technical content in an understandable and addressable manner.
  • You ensure that knowledge from the service desk and the specialist areas is recorded in a structured manner, processed and made usable for employees and customers. For this you work closely with the specialist areas.
  • You develop knowledge management in the service desk, analyze recurring support requests and derive optimizations for documentation and support knowledge.
  • You will process support requests and trouble reports and forward more complex cases to the level support team.

Requirements

  • You have completed an education or training in computer science or bring several years of experience in 1st-level support.
  • You have a very good written expression and can convey technical connections in an understandable and addressable manner. Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is an advantage.
  • You have experience with Microsoft 365 as well as a broad technical understanding.
  • You work in a structured, quality-conscious and team-oriented manner and are characterized by a high level of customer and service orientation.
