Full description

Start by appointment as a clinical assistant: in radiology 90 - 100%

Empathetic, flexible and organizationally strong? Perfect! Then you are exactly right with us.

The X-ray Institute is equipped with all the modalities of modern radiology and provides radiological care both for the cantonal hospital Graubünden as a center hospital and for a large part of southeast Switzerland in the ambulatory area.

These are your tasks

  • You coordinate appointments and prepare patients for CT, MR and ultrasound examinations
  • You inform, accompany and monitor patients before, during and after the examinations
  • Assist in radiological examinations and keep an eye on the stock of consumables and the department pharmacy

You bring that with you

  • You are a medical assistant:
  • You work reliably and independently, act service-oriented and act friendly at all times
  • Your appreciative attitude and team spirit enrich our everyday work

Can you do something that others can’t? You make the difference? Then you belong to us!

  • Gastronomic offer: All four locations of the cantonal hospital Graubünden offer a wide range of catering options. With us you can eat varied, balanced and healthy.
