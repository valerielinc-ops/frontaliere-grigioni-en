Full description

The Bündner Spital- und Heimverband is the industry association of the Bündner health institutions. In the field of outpatient care, the Spitex conference is committed to the professional development, quality assurance and networking of Spitex organizations. As of January 1, 2027, we are creating a new specialist centre for Spitex development and are looking for a structured and initiative personality. Care expert MScN (40 – 60%) It awaits you a versatile and future-oriented function with great creative leeway and development potential.

Their tasks

  • Professional advice and support of member organisations in nursing and quality-related issues
  • Support in the implementation of quality specifications and development topics
  • Development of tools, guidelines and model concepts as well as support for their implementation in practice
  • Initiation and management of projects, processing and coordination of common development topics
  • Management of working groups and experience exchange vessels
  • Conduct of training, workshops and webinars
  • Promoting knowledge transfer and best practices between organisations
  • Network work and representation of nursing topics towards relevant stakeholders

Requirements

  • Master of Science in Nursing (MScN) with experience in Spitex
  • Experience in quality development and project work
  • Networked and future-oriented thinking
  • Knowledge of Italian is an advantage

We offer modern employment conditions with flexible working hours. An open-minded board and a well-coordinated, small team will support you. For information, the Chairman of the Spitex Conference, Johanna Weiss, is at your disposal Tel. 081 257 09 56. Your application is directed to Johanna Weiss info@bsh-gr.ch.
