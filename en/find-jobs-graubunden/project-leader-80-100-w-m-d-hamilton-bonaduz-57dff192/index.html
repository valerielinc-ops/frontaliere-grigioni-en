Company Hamilton AG

True to the vision -We drive innovation to improve people-s lives- Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

The Robotics Business Unit develops, produces and distributes pipetting robots to automate liquid handling processes for pharmaceuticals, the food industry and cell or DNA research.

*You can do that

As a Project Leader, you shape the future of our automation solutions.

You take responsibility for extensive development projects and successfully implement them according to the product specifications and the backlog.

You will tackle challenges of a professional nature as well as in cooperation with various teams in a proactive and solution-oriented manner.

Your focus is on complex, cross-location projects, where you keep an overview, set priorities and ensure smooth processes.

You bring that with you

  • Several years of experience in project management and in the management of hybrid project teams within a matrix organization
  • Practice in at least one of the following areas: automation technologies, product development or life science applications
  • Experience in agile project management
  • Good language skills in German and English
  • Experience in using Jira desirable
