Full description

Our Hotel Stätzerhorn offers 33 beautiful, modern rooms, a restaurant, a lobby, and 2 conference rooms with 70-80 seats each. Our family-run, cozy, child-friendly 3-star hotel is centrally located in the middle of the bike/hiking and skiing area Lenzerheide/Arosa. We are looking for an established person in the service area with experience and knowledge of the means of payment, the checkout system and the a-la-carte service at lunch and evening, as well as banquet and half board service. Late service is also part of the employment relationship. Characteristics of the person:

  • self-contained, reliable and resilient
  • honesty
  • joy at work
  • friendly dealings with the guests
  • neat appearance
  • cleanliness
  • knowledge of German
  • weekend is also worked
  • food and lodging can possibly be provided (work dress: white blouse / white shirt, black pants or jupe) If you meet all the points, we look forward to your application.
