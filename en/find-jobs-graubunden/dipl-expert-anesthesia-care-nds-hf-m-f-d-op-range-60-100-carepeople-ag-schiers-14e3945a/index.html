Full description

Are you looking for an exciting professional perspective in which your expertise in anesthesia care is valued on a daily basis? Then here is the ideal opportunity! From now on or after agreement, we are looking for a committed and professionally experienced personality as a Dipl. Expert anesthesia care NDS HF (m/f/d) for the surgical area in a regionally well-established acute care hospital. You can expect versatile and responsible work in a highly modern working environment.

Requirement profile ##

  • Completed further education as a Dipl. Expert anesthesia care NDS HF (SRC recognition is required for degrees obtained abroad)
  • Several years of experience in the field of anesthesia care is advantageous
  • High level of expertise and responsibility
  • Flexibility, resilience and strong teamwork
  • Enjoying an interdisciplinary collaboration and willingness to take responsibility

Tasks ##

  • Independent and professional implementation of anesthesia care during operations and interventions
  • Ensuring comprehensive patient monitoring throughout the perioperative process
  • Collaboration with doctors and interdisciplinary teams for optimal patient care
  • Preparation and follow-up of anesthesiological measures and devices
  • Ensuring the highest quality and hygiene standards

  • Exciting, varied activity in a modern and technically advanced environment
  • Attractive employment conditions and targeted further development opportunities
  • An appreciative and collegial team that warmly welcomes you
  • Individual support and support during the entire application process by your personal recruitment consultant
