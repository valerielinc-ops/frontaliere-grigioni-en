Put to the Point

As a key function, you ensure the proper functioning of our acceptance, the storage, shipping and delivery, as well as the handover of military equipment in your assigned area of expertise.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-15484
  • Place of work: Barrackenstrasse 118, 7000 Chur

You can make this contribution

  • Responsible for the allocation of material for repair and inspection and check the condition and functionality of the material stored above and below ground and ammunition
  • Instruct and train the troops and specialists about the treatment and maintenance of the material mainly in German
  • Leading contact person for customers and troop craftsmen
  • Responsible for the proper management of the work and persons involved in the delivery and return of army material and ammunition to the troops in compliance with the specified dates
  • Remove, store and re-invent the material and ammunition in a professional manner and provide it in accordance with existing regulations and instructions

This makes you unique

  • Vocational apprenticeship as logistician/in EFZ
  • Pronounced service concept in contact with and in advising customers, for example the troop
  • Frequent and conscientious work and organisational skills to support a smooth operation
  • Category B, BE C, (CE and F), good SAP knowledge

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the material, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
