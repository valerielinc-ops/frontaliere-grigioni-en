Porter Housekeeping (m/f/d) Flims By 15.11.2026 or by agreement we are looking for a new host / -in for our porter Pensum: 80-100% from mid-November Admission: from mid-November 2026 or by arrangement Our traditional family business stands for warmth, passion and personal responsibility. With us, not only professional competence counts, but above all personality – an open appearance, joy in dealing with people and the proverbial heart in the right place. Our guests are as diverse as our everyday life: from marriage proposals and weddings to incentive travelers to family holidays. We don't get bored. In the summer and winter season, you work together with around 50 dedicated and charming hosts. Several times in succession we were voted the 75 best winter hotels and the 100 best holiday hotels in Switzerland. We see these awards as an incentive to continuously question and redefine our service. Our goal is to always surprise our guests with a real WOW moment. Their tasks Every host is equally important to us. Whether in front of or behind the scenes, we all contribute to the guestwow.

You are responsible for

  • the support of our porter team on three to four days a week
  • Active cooperation in all cleaning work
  • support on floors (e.g. room cleaning or laundry logistics)
  • maintenance of outdoor facilities (e.g. gardening, snow clearance depending on the season)
  • Acquisition of driving services incl. Guest transport
  • the establishment and dismantling of seminars and events, including technology
  • the construction and conversion of public areas according to event specifications

That sets them apart.

  • A warm host personality with a well-groomed appearance and real pleasure in the profession
  • A valid driving licence (mandatory)
  • Good German skills as well as basic English skills are advantageous
  • A positive attitude to life and fun working in a team
  • Technical knowledge
  • Performing simple repairs

  • A family working atmosphere with real mutual appreciation

  • Further education opportunities directly in the hotel – we grow together with our team
  • German courses on offer as well as active support in your personal and professional development
