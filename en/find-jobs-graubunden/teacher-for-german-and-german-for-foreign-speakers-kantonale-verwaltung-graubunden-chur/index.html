Full description

The Bundner Kantonsschule is an important educational institution with around 1,000 students from all three cantonal languages. As a department of the Office for Higher Education, it offers secondary education courses at the long- and short-term high school as well as at the technical, commercial and computer schools. As a teacher for German and German for foreign languages, you teach learners at the secondary school as well as at the technical, commercial and computer science schools.

We are looking for our school at 1. August 2027 a teacher for German and German for foreign speakers.

Tasks

  • Teaching in the subject area German and German for foreign speakers at the secondary school as well as at the technical, commercial and computer science school
  • Organization and implementation of project weeks, class events and excursions in a team with other teachers
  • Taking over various work within the student body
  • Participation in development projects of the comprehensive school

Requirements

  • Completed university studies in German as well as a teaching diploma for Maturity schools
  • Additional qualification in German as a second language (DaZ) at secondary level II desired
  • Flexible, initiative and team capable personality who likes to work with young people. In addition, we want a communicative person who takes responsibility and likes to be involved in school life.
  • As a teacher at the trilingual school, knowledge of other cantonal languages round off your profile
